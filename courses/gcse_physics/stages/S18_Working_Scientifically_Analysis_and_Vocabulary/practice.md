# S18_Working_Scientifically_Analysis_and_Vocabulary - Practice: Working scientifically: analysis, evaluation and scientific language

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Plot a line graph of these values of distance against time: 0 s gives 0 m, 2 s gives 6, 4 s gives 12, 6 s gives 18, 8 s gives 24. Describe the pattern with figures and calculate the speed.
2. Five repeat readings for a current are 0.32, 0.34, 0.33, 0.51 and 0.33 A. Identify the anomaly, calculate the mean without it and the range.
3. Write a conclusion for an experiment where the extension of a spring increases in proportion to the mass added.
4. Convert 2500 g to kg, 0.45 A to milliamps, and write 0.000035 m in standard form and in micrometres.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
