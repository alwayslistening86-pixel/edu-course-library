# S11_Momentum - Practice: Momentum, conservation of momentum and changes in momentum

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Calculate the momentum of a 0.45 kg football moving at 25 m/s and a 5000 kg lorry at 12 m/s.
2. A 1.5 kg trolley at 2.0 m/s hits a stationary 0.5 kg trolley and they join. Calculate their common velocity.
3. A 4.0 kg rifle fires a 0.020 kg bullet at 400 m/s. Calculate the recoil velocity of the rifle.
4. Explain, using change in momentum, how an airbag reduces the force on a driver's head in a crash.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
