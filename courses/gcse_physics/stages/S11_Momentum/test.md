# S11_Momentum - Test: Momentum, conservation of momentum and changes in momentum

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) State the equation for momentum. b) Calculate the momentum of a 2.0 kg trolley at 4.0 m/s. c) State the law of conservation of momentum.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A 60 kg passenger decelerates from 15 m/s to rest in 0.10 s, and in another crash in 0.50 s. Calculate the force in each case and explain the difference.'
