# S13_Electromagnetic_Waves_Lenses_and_Radiation - Practice: The electromagnetic spectrum, lenses, visible light, and infrared emission and absorption (required practicals 9 and 10)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Put the EM waves in order of wavelength, longest first, and give one use of each.
2. Explain why UV, X-rays and gamma rays are more dangerous than radio waves.
3. Draw a ray diagram for a convex lens forming a magnified virtual image and calculate the magnification of an image 6.0 cm high from an object 2.0 cm high.
4. Explain why a matt black kettle cools faster than a shiny one.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
