# S17_Working_Scientifically_Thinking_and_Experiments - Practice: Working scientifically: scientific thinking and experimental skills

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a hypothesis and list independent, dependent and control variables for an investigation into how the length of a wire affects its resistance.
2. Give two ethical questions science cannot answer alone and how evidence can still inform each.
3. Write a risk assessment line for using a low-voltage power supply with a heating element in water.
4. A student times 20 swings of a pendulum as 19.3, 19.5 and 21.6 s. Identify the anomaly, calculate the mean of the good results and suggest two improvements.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
