# S17_Working_Scientifically_Thinking_and_Experiments - Test: Working scientifically: scientific thinking and experimental skills

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) State what is meant by a control variable. b) A pupil measures the length of a spring extension with a ruler marked in centimetres. Suggest a way to get a more precise measurement. c) Explain why results are repeated.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Plan an investigation into how the angle of a ramp affects the speed of a trolley at the bottom, and evaluate one limitation.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S17_Working_Scientifically_Thinking_and_Experiments` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S18_Working_Scientifically_Analysis_and_Vocabulary.
