# S04_Density_Heating_and_States_of_Matter - Practice: Density, changes of state, internal energy, specific heat capacity and latent heat (required practicals 1, 2 and 5)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A stone of mass 210 g displaces 30 cm3 of water. Calculate its density in g/cm3 and kg/m3.
2. Calculate the energy needed to raise 1.2 kg of copper (c = 385 J/kg degree C) from 20 to 100 degrees Celsius.
3. Calculate the energy needed to boil away 0.05 kg of water at 100 degrees Celsius (L = 2 260 000 J/kg).
4. Sketch and explain a heating curve for ice at -10 degrees Celsius to steam.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
