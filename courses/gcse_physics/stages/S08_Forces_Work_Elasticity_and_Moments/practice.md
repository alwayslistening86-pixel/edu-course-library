# S08_Forces_Work_Elasticity_and_Moments - Practice: Vectors, contact and non-contact forces, weight, work done, springs and moments (required practical 6)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Calculate the weight of a 65 kg person on Earth and on the Moon (1.6 N/kg).
2. A force of 40 N pushes a box 5.0 m along the floor in the direction of the force. Calculate the work done and the energy transferred to the thermal store if friction is the only opposing force.
3. A spring extends by 6.0 cm when a 3.0 N force is applied. Calculate its spring constant in N/m and the energy stored.
4. Calculate the force needed at 0.60 m from the pivot to balance a 90 N weight at 0.20 m on the other side of a see-saw.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
