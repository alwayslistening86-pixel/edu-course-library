# S10_Newtons_Laws_Stopping_Distance_and_Practical - Practice: Newton's three laws, stopping distance and braking (required practical 7)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A 900 kg car accelerates at 2.0 m/s2. Calculate the resultant force. If the driving force is 2400 N, find the resistive force.
2. Explain, using Newton's first law, why passengers move forward when a car brakes suddenly.
3. Identify the pair of forces in Newton's third law when a person stands on the ground.
4. A car travelling at 15 m/s has thinking distance 9 m and braking distance 20 m. Find the stopping distance and list three factors that could increase each part.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
