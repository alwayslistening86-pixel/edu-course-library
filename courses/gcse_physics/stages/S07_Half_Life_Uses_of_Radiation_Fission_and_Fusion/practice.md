# S07_Half_Life_Uses_of_Radiation_Fission_and_Fusion - Practice: Half-life, contamination, background radiation, uses of radiation, fission and fusion

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A source has an activity of 640 Bq and a half-life of 20 minutes. Find the activity after 1 hour.
2. Explain the difference between contamination and irradiation and which is worse for an alpha source, and why.
3. Explain why technetium-99m (half-life 6 hours, gamma emitter) is used as a medical tracer.
4. Describe how a chain reaction occurs in a nuclear reactor and how it is controlled.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
