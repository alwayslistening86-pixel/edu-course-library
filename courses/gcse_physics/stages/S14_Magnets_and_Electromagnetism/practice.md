# S14_Magnets_and_Electromagnetism - Practice: Magnets, magnetic fields, electromagnets, the motor effect, motors and loudspeakers

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Sketch the magnetic field pattern around a bar magnet and between two attracting poles.
2. List three ways to increase the strength of an electromagnet.
3. Higher: a wire 0.15 m long carries 8.0 A perpendicular to a field of 0.40 T. Calculate the force and use Fleming's left-hand rule to say the direction if the field is into the page and the current is to the right.
4. Higher: explain why a motor coil rotates continuously and why a split-ring commutator is needed.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
