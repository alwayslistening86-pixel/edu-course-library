# S09_Motion_Speed_Velocity_and_Acceleration - Practice: Distance, displacement, speed, velocity, distance-time and velocity-time graphs and acceleration

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A cyclist travels 4.5 km in 15 minutes. Calculate the average speed in m/s.
2. Draw a distance-time graph for a journey of 20 m in 10 s, a rest for 5 s and then back to the start in 10 s, and calculate the speed on each section.
3. A car accelerates uniformly from 5 m/s to 25 m/s in 8 s. Calculate the acceleration and the distance covered.
4. Explain why a skydiver reaches terminal velocity, sketching a velocity-time graph.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
