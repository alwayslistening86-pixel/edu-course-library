# S06_Atoms_and_Radioactive_Decay - Practice: The atom, isotopes, models of the atom, radioactive decay and nuclear equations

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. An atom has 11 protons, 12 neutrons and 11 electrons. State its atomic number, mass number and element, and describe an isotope.
2. Explain how the results of the alpha scattering experiment show that the plum pudding model is wrong.
3. Complete: polonium-210 (84 protons) emits an alpha particle; state the daughter nucleus mass and atomic numbers.
4. Compare alpha, beta and gamma radiation in a table of nature, range in air and ionising power.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
