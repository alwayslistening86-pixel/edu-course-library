# S01_Energy_Stores_Transfers_and_Resources - Practice: Energy stores, transfers, power, efficiency and energy resources

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A 1.5 kg book is lifted through 2.0 m. Calculate the gain in gravitational potential energy. If it is dropped, find its speed just before it hits the floor (ignore air resistance).
2. Calculate the energy needed to heat 2.0 kg of water from 20 to 70 degrees Celsius (specific heat capacity 4200 J/kg degree C).
3. A motor transfers 500 J of electrical energy and lifts a load gaining 350 J of gravitational potential energy. Calculate the efficiency and state where the remainder goes.
4. Compare wind power and gas-fired power for reliability, cost and environmental impact.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
