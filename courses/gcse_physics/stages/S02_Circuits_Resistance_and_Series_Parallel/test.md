Calculate the current. c) Explain what happens to an LDR resistance in bright light.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A 4 ohm and a 12 ohm resistor are in parallel across a 6 V cell. Calculate the current in each branch, the total current, and the total resistance.'
