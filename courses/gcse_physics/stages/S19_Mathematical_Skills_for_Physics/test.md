# S19_Mathematical_Skills_for_Physics - Test: Mathematical requirements for GCSE Physics

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Write 45 000 000 in standard form. b) Calculate the mean of 12.3, 12.5 and 12.4 s. c) Rearrange v = f lambda to find f.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A velocity-time graph rises uniformly from 0 to 12 m/s in 6 s, then stays constant for 4 s. Calculate the acceleration and the total distance travelled by finding the area.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S19_Mathematical_Skills_for_Physics` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass. This was the last stage: the cumulative exam is now unlocked once every stage test is passed.
