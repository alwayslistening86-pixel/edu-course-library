# S19_Mathematical_Skills_for_Physics - Practice: Mathematical requirements for GCSE Physics

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write 0.000 035 m in standard form and in micrometres, and 4.2 x 10 to the power 5 J in kilojoules.
2. Rearrange P = V I to make I the subject and calculate the current for a 2300 W kettle at 230 V.
3. Plot speed against time from data, draw the line of best fit, and find the acceleration and the distance travelled from the area.
4. Draw a tangent to a distance-time curve at 3 s and calculate the speed at that instant.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
