# S05_Gases_and_Fluid_Pressure - Practice: Gas particles, gas pressure, pressure in fluids and atmospheric pressure

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain in terms of particles why the pressure in a sealed can of gas rises when it is heated.
2. A 600 N person stands on two feet, each of area 0.02 m2. Calculate the pressure on the ground.
3. Higher: a gas at 100 kPa and volume 0.030 m3 is compressed to 0.010 m3 at constant temperature. Calculate the new pressure.
4. Explain why a diver at 20 m feels more pressure than one at 5 m, and calculate the difference in pressure (density 1000 kg/m3, g = 9.8 N/kg).

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
