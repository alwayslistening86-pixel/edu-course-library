# GCSE Physics (AQA 8463) — separate science, Foundation and Higher tier - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.

## Format
Two timed papers at the learner's tier, each 1 hour 45 minutes and 100 marks, matching AQA's real structure: Paper 1 covers topics 4.1 to 4.4 (energy, electricity, particle model of matter, atomic structure) and Paper 2 covers topics 4.5 to 4.8 (forces, waves, magnetism and electromagnetism, space physics). Each paper mixes multiple-choice, structured, closed short-answer and open-response questions, with about 30 per cent of marks on mathematical skills and 15 per cent on working scientifically and required practicals.

## Example structure
Paper 1 draws on S01-S07 and Paper 2 draws on S08-S16. Include at least one required-practical-based question per paper (S02, S04, S08, S10, S12, S13) and questions using S17-S19 skills. At Higher tier include Higher-only content: p = h rho g, pressure of gases, half-life ratios, resolving forces, momentum, F = BIl, transformers and black-body radiation, plus evaluation questions.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly. A pass requires consistent performance across all topics under timed conditions, with calculations set out step by step with units, and extended answers using correct scientific terminology.

## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete (for that tier).
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name which stage(s) broke down under mixed/timed conditions, offer targeted review or a fresh retry paper.
