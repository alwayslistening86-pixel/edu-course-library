## Test items
1. An employee with 18 months' service is dismissed without notice and without any justification such as gross misconduct. Which claim is most likely available to them? Choose every correct option.
   A. Unfair dismissal only, since they have under two years' service
   B. Wrongful dismissal (breach of contract for lack of notice), available regardless of length of service
   C. Redundancy pay only
   D. No claim is available to any employee with under two years' service
2. Explain the difference between wrongful dismissal and unfair dismissal. [4 marks]
3. Which is a protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010? Choose every correct option.
   A. Political party membership
   B. Disability
   C. Postcode
   D. Salary level
4. Under UK GDPR/the Data Protection Act 2018, personal data must be: Choose every correct option.
   A. kept forever in case it is needed later
   B. processed lawfully, fairly and transparently, and kept no longer than necessary
   C. shared with any third party on request
   D. exempt from these rules if the business is small
5. Redundancy occurs when: Choose every correct option.
   A. an employee is dismissed for poor performance
   B. the job itself disappears, e.g. through business closure or reduced need for that type of work
   C. an employee resigns voluntarily
   D. an employee is dismissed for gross misconduct

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [4] B4 wrongful dismissal is a common law/contractual claim that the employer breached the contract of employment, typically by dismissing without proper notice or without contractual justification, available to any employee regardless of service length, with damages generally limited to the notice-period shortfall; unfair dismissal is a statutory claim (normally requiring two years' continuous service) that the dismissal was for no potentially fair reason or followed no fair process, with different remedies (reinstatement, re-engagement, or a statutory compensation award).
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
