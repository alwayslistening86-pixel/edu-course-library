# LAW_S05_Law_in_the_Professional_Context - Practice: Law in the professional context: data protection and employment law

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. An individual is told when, where and exactly how to do their work by the business, must do the work personally with no right to send a substitute, and the business is obliged to keep offering them regular work which they are obliged to accept. Explain what this suggests about their employment status. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 the high degree of control, the requirement for personal service, and the mutuality of obligation together strongly suggest an employment relationship (rather than genuine self-employment), regardless of what label the parties may have given the arrangement.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
