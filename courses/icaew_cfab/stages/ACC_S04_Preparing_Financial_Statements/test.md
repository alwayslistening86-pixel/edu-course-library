# ACC_S04_Preparing_Financial_Statements - Test: Preparing financial statements

## How to run this

## Test items
1. A sole trader has revenue of £95,000, cost of sales of £58,000 and operating expenses of £19,000. Prepare a summary statement of profit or loss showing gross profit and profit for the year. [3 marks]
2. Which statement reports an entity's financial performance over a period, rather than its position at a point in time? Choose every correct option.
   A. Statement of financial position
   B. Statement of profit or loss
   C. None; both report a point in time
   D. Notes to the accounts only
3. A partnership agreement gives Partner A a salary of £10,000 and shares remaining profit 60:40 (A:B). Profit for the year before appropriation is £40,000. Calculate the total profit share for each partner. [4 marks]
4. In a limited company's financial statements, a dividend paid to shareholders is: Choose every correct option.
   A. deducted as an expense in the statement of profit or loss
   B. deducted directly from retained earnings, not as an expense
   C. added to revenue
   D. ignored entirely
5. Current assets are £60,000 and current liabilities are £40,000. Calculate the current ratio and comment briefly on what it suggests. [3 marks]
6. Which best describes ROCE? Choose every correct option.
   A. Gross profit divided by revenue
   B. Operating profit divided by capital employed (equity plus non-current liabilities)
   C. Current assets divided by current liabilities
   D. Net profit divided by cost of sales

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] M1 gross profit = 95,000 - 58,000 = £37,000; A1 profit for the year = 37,000 - 19,000 = £18,000; B1 correct structure: gross profit is revenue less cost of sales, and profit for the year is gross profit less all other operating expenses, in that order.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] M1 remaining profit after salary = 40,000 - 10,000 = £30,000; M1 A's share of remainder = 30,000 x 0.60 = £18,000; A1 A total = 10,000 + 18,000 = £28,000; A1 B total = 30,000 x 0.40 = £12,000.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [3] M1 current ratio = 60,000/40,000 = 1.50:1; A1 = 1.50:1; B1 current assets exceed current liabilities, suggesting the business can cover its short-term obligations from short-term resources, though the ratio alone does not show how quickly those assets convert to cash.
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
