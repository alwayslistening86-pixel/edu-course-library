# BTF_S01_Business_Organisations_and_Stakeholders - Practice: Business organisations, stakeholders and objectives

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the main advantage and main disadvantage a sole trader gives up by incorporating as a limited company. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 advantage gained: limited liability, so personal assets are protected beyond the amount invested in shares; disadvantage: loss of simplicity/privacy and increased regulatory burden (e.g. filing accounts at Companies House, corporation tax rather than income tax, statutory accounting and audit requirements as the company grows).

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
