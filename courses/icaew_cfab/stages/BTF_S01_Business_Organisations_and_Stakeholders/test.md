## Test items
1. Which type of business organisation gives its owners limited liability but cannot offer its shares to the public? Choose every correct option.
   A. Sole trader
   B. Ordinary partnership
   C. Private limited company
   D. Public limited company
2. Using Mendelow's power/interest matrix, a stakeholder with high power and high interest should be: Choose every correct option.
   A. given minimal effort
   B. kept informed only
   C. kept satisfied only
   D. managed closely as a key player
3. A local community group has low power over a factory's planning decisions but high interest in its environmental impact. According to Mendelow's matrix, how should the factory's management treat this stakeholder, and why might that classification change over time? [3 marks]
4. Which is a disadvantage of an ordinary (non-LLP) partnership compared with a limited company? Choose every correct option.
   A. Partners have unlimited liability for partnership debts
   B. A partnership cannot have more than one owner
   C. A partnership must always be audited
   D. A partnership cannot employ staff
5. Which is most likely to be a connected (rather than purely internal or purely external) stakeholder? Choose every correct option.
   A. An employee
   B. A shareholder
   C. The government
   D. A pressure group

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: D (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B2 low power/high interest stakeholders should be kept informed; B1 the classification could change if the group gains power, e.g. by organising a well-publicised campaign or gaining local political backing, which would move it towards a key player requiring closer management.
4. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
