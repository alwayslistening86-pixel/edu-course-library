## Test items
1. A dispute between two companies over an unpaid invoice would normally be resolved as: Choose every correct option.
   A. a criminal prosecution
   B. a civil claim
   C. neither, as commercial disputes cannot go to court
   D. an ECHR claim
2. Which court hears the most serious criminal (indictable) offences, often with a jury? Choose every correct option.
   A. The County Court
   B. The Magistrates' Court
   C. The Crown Court
   D. The Chancery Division
3. Explain two advantages of arbitration over full court litigation for a commercial contract dispute. [4 marks]
4. Under the Human Rights Act 1998, UK courts must: Choose every correct option.
   A. ignore the European Convention on Human Rights entirely
   B. interpret UK legislation compatibly with Convention rights where possible
   C. always prioritise EU law over UK statute
   D. apply the Convention only to criminal cases
5. ICC Incoterms are used mainly to: Choose every correct option.
   A. set UK criminal sentencing guidelines
   B. define when risk and cost pass between buyer and seller in international trade
   C. regulate UK company audits
   D. set UK income tax rates

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] B4 any two of: arbitration is typically faster and cheaper than full litigation; it is private/confidential, unlike most court proceedings; the parties can choose an arbitrator with relevant commercial/technical expertise; it can be enforced internationally under conventions that make cross-border enforcement more straightforward than a foreign court judgment.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
