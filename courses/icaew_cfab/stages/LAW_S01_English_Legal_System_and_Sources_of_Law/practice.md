# LAW_S01_English_Legal_System_and_Sources_of_Law - Practice: The English legal system and sources of law

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference in the standard of proof between a civil claim and a criminal prosecution, and why that difference exists. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 a civil claim is decided on the balance of probabilities (more likely than not); a criminal prosecution must be proved beyond reasonable doubt, a much higher standard, reflecting the far more serious consequences (a criminal conviction, potentially imprisonment) of getting it wrong.
