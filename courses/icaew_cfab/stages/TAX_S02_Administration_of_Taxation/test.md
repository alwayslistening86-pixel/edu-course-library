## Test items
1. PAYE is best described as: Choose every correct option.
   A. a self-assessment return filed annually by every employee
   B. the system by which an employer deducts income tax and employee NIC at source and remits it, with employer NIC, to HMRC
   C. a tax that applies only to the self-employed
   D. a penalty regime for late filing
2. The online self-assessment filing deadline for an individual's tax return, and the balancing payment deadline, both normally fall on: Choose every correct option.
   A. 31 October following the tax year end
   B. 5 April at the end of the tax year
   C. 31 January following the tax year end
   D. 31 July following the tax year end
3. Explain why HMRC's penalty for an inaccuracy in a tax return caused by a deliberate and concealed understatement is higher than for an inaccuracy caused by a genuine lack of reasonable care. [3 marks]
4. A company's accounting period ends 31 December. By when is its corporation tax normally due (assuming it is not one of the largest companies)? Choose every correct option.
   A. 31 December (immediately)
   B. 31 January (the following month)
   C. 9 months and 1 day after the accounting period end, i.e. 1 October
   D. 12 months after the accounting period end
5. Which is a consequence of failing to keep adequate tax records, even if the return figures later prove correct? Choose every correct option.
   A. No consequence; only incorrect figures are penalised
   B. A penalty can be charged for inadequate record-keeping itself
   C. The taxpayer is automatically exempt from future enquiries
   D. The tax year is extended

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B3 the penalty regime is risk/culpability-based: deliberate and concealed behaviour reflects a conscious decision to under-declare tax and then actively hide it, which is far more culpable than an innocent mistake made despite taking reasonable care, so the penalty scales with the taxpayer's behaviour, not just the size of the error.
4. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
