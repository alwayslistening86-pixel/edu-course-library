# TAX_S02_Administration_of_Taxation - Practice: Administration of taxation

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the online self-assessment filing deadline and the balancing payment deadline for an individual's 2026/27 tax return. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] A1 both fall on 31 January following the end of the tax year, i.e. 31 January 2028 for the 2026/27 tax year; B1 the online filing deadline and the balancing payment deadline are the same date, unlike the earlier 31 October deadline that applies only to paper returns.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
