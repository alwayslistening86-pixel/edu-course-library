## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)

[3] B3 the audit committee oversees financial reporting and the external auditor relationship, areas where executive directors (who prepared the financial statements and are responsible for the figures being scrutinised) would face a self-review or self-interest threat if they oversaw themselves; independent NEDs provide objective oversight free of that conflict.
