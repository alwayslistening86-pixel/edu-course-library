## Test items
1. A non-executive director's role is best described as: Choose every correct option.
   A. running the business day-to-day like an executive director
   B. providing independent oversight and challenge, without day-to-day management responsibility
   C. auditing the company's financial statements personally
   D. representing only the interests of one major shareholder
2. Which board committee is primarily responsible for setting executive pay? Choose every correct option.
   A. Audit committee
   B. Nomination committee
   C. Remuneration committee
   D. Risk committee
3. An employee discovers a serious safety breach that their manager instructs them not to report. Outline a structured approach they could take to resolve this ethical dilemma. [4 marks]
4. Under the UK Corporate Governance Code's 'comply or explain' approach, a listed company that does not comply with a provision of the Code must: Choose every correct option.
   A. be automatically delisted
   B. explain its reasons for departing from the provision
   C. face no consequence or disclosure requirement
   D. always be fined by the FRC
5. Which best describes a corporate code of ethics? Choose every correct option.
   A. A legally binding statute applicable to all UK businesses
   B. An organisation's own statement of expected standards of behaviour for its staff
   C. A document required only of sole traders
   D. A synonym for the statement of financial position

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
3. 4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
