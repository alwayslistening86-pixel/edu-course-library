# LAW_S02_Contract_Agency_and_Negligence - Practice: Contract, agency and negligence

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A supplier offers to sell goods for £5,000. The buyer replies agreeing, but asking for delivery a week earlier than proposed. Explain whether a contract has been formed. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 no, not yet - the buyer's reply changing a term (the delivery date) is a counter-offer, not an unqualified acceptance; a counter-offer destroys the original offer, and no contract exists until the supplier accepts the buyer's counter-offer (or the buyer accepts the original terms unconditionally).

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
