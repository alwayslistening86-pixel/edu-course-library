## Test items
1. Which UK tax year applies to an individual's income tax for the year 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027? Choose every correct option.
   A. The 2025/26 tax year
   B. The 2026/27 tax year
   C. The calendar year 2026
   D. The financial year to 31 March 2027
2. Which is an example of legitimate tax avoidance rather than tax evasion? Choose every correct option.
   A. Deliberately failing to declare cash income to HMRC
   B. Contributing to an ISA to shelter savings income from tax within the statutory allowance
   C. Falsifying invoices to reduce reported profit
   D. Failing to register for VAT despite exceeding the threshold
3. A tax adviser suspects, based on information a client has provided, that the client has deliberately concealed income from HMRC in prior years. Explain the adviser's likely obligation. [3 marks]
4. Which body's guidance, while influential, is not itself a source of law? Choose every correct option.
   A. An Act of Parliament
   B. A court judgment (case law)
   C. HMRC guidance and manuals
   D. Secondary legislation
5. Which is a social justice objective commonly pursued through UK tax policy? Choose every correct option.
   A. A flat tax rate applying equally to all income levels
   B. A progressive income tax structure, where the average tax rate rises with income
   C. Abolishing all allowances
   D. Taxing only imports

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B3 concealing income from HMRC is tax evasion, a criminal offence and a predicate offence for money laundering purposes; the adviser has a legal duty to consider making a suspicious activity report (via the firm's MLRO) under the money laundering reporting regime, in addition to the ethical requirement not to become knowingly involved in facilitating the evasion.
4. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
