# TAX_S01_Objectives_Types_of_Tax_and_Ethics - Practice: Objectives, types of tax and ethics

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference between tax avoidance and tax evasion, and state which is illegal. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 tax avoidance is legally arranging one's affairs to minimise tax within the law (e.g. using a tax-efficient savings allowance) and is not illegal, though aggressive/contrived avoidance can still be challenged by HMRC; tax evasion is illegally failing to pay tax legally due (e.g. concealing income) and is a criminal offence.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
