## Test items
1. Which component of audit risk is the only one the auditor can directly control? Choose every correct option.
   A. Inherent risk
   B. Control risk
   C. Detection risk
   D. Business risk
2. A balance involves a complex, judgemental estimate of a warranty provision. Explain whether this increases inherent risk, and why. [3 marks]
3. A monthly bank reconciliation is an example of which type of control? Choose every correct option.
   A. Preventative
   B. Detective
   C. Authorisation
   D. Physical
4. Which is a principle of segregation of duties? Choose every correct option.
   A. The same person should authorise, execute and record a transaction for efficiency
   B. Authorisation, custody/execution and recording of a transaction should be split across different people
   C. Segregation of duties applies only to cash transactions
   D. Segregation of duties eliminates the need for any other control
5. Explain why an internal audit function typically reports to an audit committee rather than to operational management. [3 marks]
6. Which is a business risk that could translate into audit risk? Choose every correct option.
   A. The auditor choosing a small sample size
   B. Loss of a major customer raising going concern doubt
   C. The auditor's own staff turnover
   D. The audit fee being agreed in advance

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. [3] B3 yes, it increases inherent risk, because complex, judgemental estimates are inherently more susceptible to misstatement (through error or management bias) than simple, objectively verifiable balances such as cash, even before considering the entity's controls.
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [3] B3 reporting to an audit committee (rather than the managers whose work it reviews) preserves the internal audit function's independence and objectivity, so its findings on control weaknesses are not filtered or suppressed by the very managers it may need to criticise.
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
