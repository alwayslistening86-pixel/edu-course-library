# ASR_S02_Risk_Internal_Controls_and_Information_Flows - Practice: Risk, internal controls and information flows

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain why segregation of duties between the person who authorises a payment and the person who records it in the ledger reduces the risk of undetected fraud. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 if one person could authorise, execute and record a payment alone, they could make an unauthorised payment and conceal it in the records; splitting these roles across different people means a fraud would usually require collusion between two or more people, which is harder to arrange and more likely to be uncovered.
