# ACC_S03_Adjustments_and_Correction_of_Errors - Test: Adjustments to accounting records and correction of errors

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of the certification's items (code-output, multiple-select and short code-writing). Give all the items at once, with no hints and no running of code until every answer is in. Then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. The trial balance fails to balance by £180 (credits exceed debits). A suspense account is opened, with the £180 shortfall entered as a debit to make the trial balance balance. It is later found that a £180 discount allowed was omitted entirely from the discounts allowed account (but correctly recorded, as a credit, in the receivables ledger). Prepare the correcting journal entry. [3 marks]
2. Which error would NOT cause the trial balance to fail to balance? Choose every correct option.
   A. A transaction posted with a debit but no corresponding credit
   B. An error of principle (a capital item posted as an expense, both sides equally)
   C. A one-sided posting to a ledger account
   D. A debit posted twice with no corresponding second credit
3. A business pays £2,400 for insurance on 1 October, covering the 12 months to 30 September next year. Its year end is 31 December. Calculate the prepayment to carry forward. [3 marks]
4. An irrecoverable (bad) debt of £500 is written off. What is the correct double entry? Choose every correct option.
   A. Debit Receivables £500, Credit Irrecoverable Debts Expense £500
   B. Debit Irrecoverable Debts Expense £500, Credit Receivables £500
   C. Debit Cash £500, Credit Receivables £500
   D. No entry is needed until the customer confirms non-payment
5. Rent of £6,000 for the year has been paid, but at the year end £1,500 of rent relating to next year has already been paid in advance. State the rent expense for the year and the amount, if any, shown in the statement of financial position, and where. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] M1 Debit Discounts Allowed £180 (the missing expense); A1 Credit Suspense £180 (clears the debit balance on the suspense account); B1 after posting, the suspense account balance is nil.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] M1 months used by year end = 3 (Oct-Dec); M1 months prepaid = 9; A1 prepayment = 2,400 x 9/12 = £1,800.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [3] M1 rent expense for the year = 6,000 - 1,500 = £4,500; A1 the £1,500 prepaid is shown as a current asset (prepayment) in the statement of financial position; B1 correct reasoning: the prepayment relates to a future period, so it is deferred out of this year's expense and carried forward as an asset.
