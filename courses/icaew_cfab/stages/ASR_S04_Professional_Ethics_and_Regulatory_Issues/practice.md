# ASR_S04_Professional_Ethics_and_Regulatory_Issues - Practice: Professional ethics and regulatory issues

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. An audit firm is also asked to prepare the bookkeeping and draft financial statements for the same client it audits. Identify the threat this creates and one possible safeguard. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B2 this creates a self-review threat, because the audit team would then be auditing figures the firm itself prepared; B1 a safeguard is using a completely separate team (with no involvement in the audit) to perform the bookkeeping/accounts preparation work, with appropriate reviews, or, for a public interest entity, declining to provide the service alongside the audit at all.

## How to run it
