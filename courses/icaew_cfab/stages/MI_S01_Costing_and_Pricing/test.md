## Test items
1. Unit cost is £30. Calculate the selling price using (a) a 50% mark-up on cost and (b) a target margin of 20% of the selling price. [4 marks]
2. Which costing method is required for valuing inventory in external financial statements under IAS 2? Choose every correct option.
   A. Marginal costing
   B. Absorption costing
   C. Throughput accounting
   D. Activity-based costing only
3. A 15% price rise causes a 25% fall in quantity demanded. Calculate the price elasticity of demand, state whether demand is elastic or inelastic, and state what is likely to happen to total revenue. [4 marks]
4. A supplying division's full cost per unit is £50. Using cost plus 30%, what is the transfer price? Choose every correct option.
   A. £50.00
   B. £65.00
   C. £15.00
   D. £80.00
5. Which is a variable cost from the perspective of total cost behaviour? Choose every correct option.
   A. Factory rent
   B. Direct material, where total cost rises in proportion to output
   C. A fixed annual insurance premium
   D. A supervisor's fixed monthly salary

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 (a) price = 30 x 1.50; A1 = £45.00; M1 (b) price = 30/(1-0.20); A1 = £37.50.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] M1 elasticity = -25%/15% = -1.67; A1 |-1.67| > 1, so demand is elastic; B1 total revenue is likely to fall, since the proportionate fall in quantity exceeds the proportionate rise in price; B1 correct reasoning: elasticity is judged by the absolute value of the ratio, with a value greater than 1 meaning quantity is proportionately more responsive than price.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
