## Test items
1. The principle that a registered company is a legal person separate from its shareholders was established in: Choose every correct option.
   A. Donoghue v Stevenson
   B. Salomon v Salomon & Co Ltd
   C. Caparo Industries v Dickman
   D. R v Ghosh
2. A company defaults on a loan secured by a floating charge over its inventory. Explain what happens to the charge at that point, and where floating-charge holders typically rank in a liquidation compared with fixed-charge holders. [3 marks]
3. A person sets up and signs a contract on behalf of a company that has not yet been incorporated. Who is generally liable on that pre-incorporation contract? Choose every correct option.
   A. The company, once incorporated, automatically and exclusively
   B. No one, since the contract is automatically void
   C. The promoter personally, unless the contract provides otherwise or it is later validly adopted
   D. Companies House
4. Which insolvency procedure is specifically aimed at trying to rescue the company as a going concern, or otherwise achieve a better result for creditors than an immediate liquidation? Choose every correct option.
   A. Liquidation
   B. Administration
   C. A share buyback
   D. A rights issue
5. Under the Companies Act 2006 director's duty to promote the success of the company, a director must have regard to a range of factors beyond short-term shareholder return, including: Choose every correct option.
   A. only the interests of the largest shareholder
   B. the likely long-term consequences, employees' interests, and the company's impact on the environment and community
   C. only maximising this year's dividend
   D. nothing beyond strict legal compliance

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [3] B2 on default, the floating charge crystallises into a fixed charge over the assets then held by the company; B1 fixed-charge holders rank ahead of floating-charge holders in the statutory order of priority in a liquidation.
3. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
