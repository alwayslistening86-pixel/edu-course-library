# LAW_S03_Company_and_Insolvency_Law - Practice: Company and insolvency law

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference between an ordinary resolution and a special resolution, giving an example of a decision each would typically be used for. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 an ordinary resolution needs a simple majority (over 50%) of votes cast, e.g. to appoint a director; a special resolution needs at least 75% of votes cast, used for more significant constitutional changes, e.g. altering the company's articles of association.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
