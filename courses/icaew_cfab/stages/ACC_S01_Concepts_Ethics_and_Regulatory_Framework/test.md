## Test items
1. Which are the two fundamental qualitative characteristics of useful financial information under the Conceptual Framework? Choose every correct option.
   A. Relevance
   B. Comparability
   C. Faithful representation
   D. Timeliness
2. A business buys equipment for £5,000 cash. What is the effect on total assets? Choose every correct option.
   A. Increases by £5,000
   B. Decreases by £5,000
   C. No change - one asset increases as another decreases by the same amount
   D. Cannot be determined
3. A business receives a rent invoice of £1,200 for the current quarter but has not yet paid it. Under the accrual basis, is an expense recognised this quarter, and what is recorded in the statement of financial position? [3 marks]
4. Which body issues IFRS Sustainability Disclosure Standards? Choose every correct option.
   A. The IASB
   B. The Financial Reporting Council
   C. The ISSB
   D. HMRC
5. Which accounting concept means the same accounting policies should normally be applied from one period to the next? Choose every correct option.
   A. Going concern
   B. Consistency
   C. Prudence
   D. Money measurement
6. Explain the difference between the going concern concept and the business entity concept. [4 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: A, C (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B1 yes, £1,200 rent expense is recognised this quarter, because the cost has been incurred regardless of when it is paid; B2 a payable (accrual) of £1,200 is recorded as a current liability until it is paid.
4. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
6. [4] B2 going concern assumes the business will continue trading for the foreseeable future, so assets are valued on a continuing-use basis rather than a forced-sale basis; B2 the business entity concept treats the business as separate from its owner(s) for accounting purposes, so the owner's personal assets/liabilities are not included in the business's financial statements.
