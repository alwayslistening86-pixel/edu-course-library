# ACC_S01_Concepts_Ethics_and_Regulatory_Framework - Practice: Accounting concepts, ethics and the regulatory framework

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A business pays £900 cash for insurance covering the next 12 months, starting this month. Under the accrual basis, how much of this £900 is an expense of the current month, and how is the rest treated? [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 one month's cost = 900/12 = £75 is the current month's expense; A1 the remaining £825 is a prepayment (an asset) carried forward to future months.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
