## Practice items
1. Explain one advantage and one risk of a business automating its bank reconciliation process using RPA. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B2 advantage: faster, more consistent processing, freeing staff time for exception investigation and judgement-based work; B1 risk: if the automated rules are wrong, or the underlying data feed is corrupted, errors could be applied consistently and at scale before being noticed, so the automated output still needs review/control.
