# MI_S04_Management_Decision_Making - Test: Management decision making: breakeven, limiting factor and investment appraisal

## How to run this

## Test items
1. Selling price is £60/unit, variable cost is £35/unit, and fixed costs are £75,000. Budgeted sales are 4,000 units. Calculate the breakeven point and the margin of safety in units. [4 marks]
2. Product X earns contribution of £15/unit and needs 3 labour hours/unit; Product Y earns contribution of £22/unit and needs 4 labour hours/unit. Labour hours are the scarce resource. Rank the two products and state which should be produced first. [4 marks]
3. Which investment appraisal method(s) account for the time value of money? Choose every correct option.
   A. Payback period
   B. Net present value
   C. Accounting rate of return
   D. None of these
4. A project costs £20,000 and generates net cash inflows of £8,000 a year for 3 years. At 10%, discount factors are 0.909, 0.826, 0.751. Calculate the NPV and state whether the project should proceed. [5 marks]
5. The Internal Rate of Return (IRR) is: Choose every correct option.
   A. the payback period expressed as a percentage
   B. the discount rate at which NPV equals zero
   C. always equal to the cost of capital
   D. only relevant to accounting rate of return

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 contribution = 60-35 = £25/unit; M1 breakeven = 75,000/25 = 3,000 units; A1 = 3,000 units; A1 margin of safety = 4,000 - 3,000 = 1,000 units.
2. [4] M1 X contribution/hour = 15/3 = £5.00; M1 Y contribution/hour = 22/4 = £5.50; A1 Y should be produced first, since its contribution per scarce labour hour (£5.50) exceeds X's (£5.00); B1 correct method: with a single scarce resource, products are ranked by contribution per unit of the scarce resource, not by contribution per unit of output.
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. [5] M1 PV yr1 = 8,000x0.909 = £7,272; M1 PV yr2 = 8,000x0.826 = £6,608; M1 PV yr3 = 8,000x0.751 = £6,008; A1 NPV = -20,000 + 19,888 = £-112; A1 positive NPV, so the project should proceed at a 10% cost of capital.
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
