# MI_S05_Technology_Data_and_Ethics_in_Decision_Making - Test: Technology, data and ethics in decision making

## Test items
1. Which is an example of secondary data? Choose every correct option.
   A. A survey the business designed and ran itself this month
   B. Government-published trade statistics reused by the business
   C. A stopwatch time study conducted on the factory floor
   D. A focus group run by the business
2. Which chart type is most appropriate for showing a trend in monthly sales over two years? Choose every correct option.
   A. Pie chart
   B. Line graph
   C. Bar chart comparing unrelated categories
   D. None; trends cannot be charted
3. A manager omits an adverse variance from a report to protect their department's bonus, while every other figure in the report is accurate. Explain which ethical principle this breaches and why. [3 marks]
4. A spreadsheet's greatest single risk, compared with a well-controlled manual system, is often: Choose every correct option.
   A. It cannot perform arithmetic accurately
   B. A single formula error can propagate silently through an entire model
   C. It cannot be checked by anyone other than its creator
   D. It is always slower than manual calculation
5. Which is most likely to introduce data bias into a forecast? Choose every correct option.
   A. Using a large, representative dataset
   B. Training a model only on data from an unusually favourable period
   C. Cross-checking data against an independent source
   D. Documenting the data's source clearly

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B3 this breaches integrity and objectivity: selectively omitting a genuine, relevant result to create a misleadingly favourable impression is a form of dishonesty even though no individual figure is falsified, because it deceives the report's users about the department's true performance.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
