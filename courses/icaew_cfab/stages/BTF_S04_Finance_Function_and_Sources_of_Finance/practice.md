# BTF_S04_Finance_Function_and_Sources_of_Finance - Practice: The finance function and sources of finance

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A company borrows £1,500,000 at 5% interest per year. Calculate the annual interest cost, and state one advantage a share issue would have over this loan. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 1,500,000 x 0.05; A1 = £75,000; B1 a share issue would have no fixed annual interest cost and no repayment obligation, unlike the loan (though it dilutes existing shareholders' ownership and control).

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
