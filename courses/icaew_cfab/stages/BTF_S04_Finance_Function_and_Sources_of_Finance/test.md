## Test items
1. Which is a short-term source of finance? Choose every correct option.
   A. A 10-year bank loan
   B. A share issue
   C. A bank overdraft
   D. Retained earnings
2. A business borrows £900,000 at 4.5% interest. Calculate the annual interest cost, and explain one risk of instead relying heavily on extended trade credit to fund the same need. [3 marks]
3. Which is a disadvantage of raising finance through a new share issue, compared with a bank loan? Choose every correct option.
   A. It creates a fixed legal obligation to repay
   B. It dilutes existing shareholders' percentage ownership and control
   C. It always costs more in interest than a loan
   D. It cannot be used by a limited company
4. Which best describes the role of the treasury function? Choose every correct option.
   A. Preparing statutory financial statements only
   B. Managing cash, funding and financial risk
   C. External audit of the financial statements
   D. Setting the corporation tax rate
5. Which user is most likely to need frequent, detailed internal management accounts rather than the annual statutory financial statements? Choose every correct option.
   A. A prospective shareholder researching the company for the first time
   B. HMRC assessing corporation tax
   C. An operations manager deciding whether to approve extra overtime this month
   D. A credit rating agency

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. [3] M1 900,000 x 0.045; A1 = £40,500; B1 relying heavily on extended trade credit risks damaging supplier relationships, losing early-payment discounts, and suppliers tightening or withdrawing credit terms if payment is persistently delayed.
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
