## Test items
1. Under the Bribery Act 2010, a commercial organisation can be strictly liable for failing to prevent bribery by an associated person. What is the only available defence? Choose every correct option.
   A. That the organisation did not personally benefit
   B. That the organisation had adequate procedures in place to prevent bribery
   C. That the bribery occurred outside the UK
   D. There is no defence available under any circumstances
2. Which offence under the Fraud Act 2006 involves dishonestly making a false representation intending to make a gain or cause a loss? Choose every correct option.
   A. Fraud by failing to disclose information
   B. Fraud by false representation
   C. Fraud by abuse of position
   D. Insider dealing
3. An employee reports to a regulator, reasonably and in good faith, that their employer is illegally dumping hazardous waste, and is then dismissed as a result. Explain the legal protection potentially available to them. [3 marks]
4. Gaining unauthorised access to a company's computer system is an offence under which Act? Choose every correct option.
   A. The Fraud Act 2006
   B. The Computer Misuse Act 1990
   C. The Bribery Act 2010
   D. The Companies Act 2006
5. 'Tipping off' a client that a money laundering report has been made about them is: Choose every correct option.
   A. good client service
   B. a criminal offence
   C. required by the ICAEW Code of Ethics
   D. only a disciplinary, not a criminal, matter

## Answer key (for the tutor only)

2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)

4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
