# BTF_S02_External_Environment - Test: The external environment of business (PESTLE)

## How to run this

## Test items
1. In PESTLE analysis, a change in the corporation tax rate is which type of factor? Choose every correct option.
   A. Social
   B. Political
   C. Technological
   D. Environmental
2. Interest rates rise from 3% to 5.5% on a business's £800,000 variable-rate borrowing. Calculate the extra annual interest cost, and state which macroeconomic factor this illustrates. [3 marks]
3. A good with many close substitutes and no urgent need typically has: Choose every correct option.
   A. inelastic demand
   B. elastic demand
   C. zero demand regardless of price
   D. perfectly inelastic supply
4. In Porter's Five Forces, a market with very low barriers to entry has a high: Choose every correct option.
   A. threat of substitute products
   B. bargaining power of buyers only
   C. threat of new entrants
   D. level of government regulation, always
5. Which is a social/demographic factor rather than an economic one? Choose every correct option.
   A. Inflation
   B. Interest rates
   C. An ageing population
   D. Exchange rates

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [3] M1 800,000 x (0.055-0.03); A1 = £20,000; B1 this illustrates the effect of interest rates (monetary policy) on a business's finance costs.
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
