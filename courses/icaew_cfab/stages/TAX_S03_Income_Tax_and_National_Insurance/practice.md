# TAX_S03_Income_Tax_and_National_Insurance - Practice: Income tax and national insurance contributions

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Calculate the 2026/27 income tax liability for an individual with total income (all non-savings) of £45,000 (below the personal allowance taper threshold). [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] M1 personal allowance = £12,570 (full, since income is below £100,000); M1 taxable income = 45,000 - 12,570 = £32,430; M1 this is entirely within the basic rate band (£37,700), taxed at 20%; A1 tax = 32,430 x 20% = £6,486.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
