## Test items
1. Which type of evidence is generally most reliable? Choose every correct option.
   A. Oral evidence from client staff
   B. Written evidence generated internally by the client
   C. Written evidence obtained directly by the practitioner from an independent external source
   D. A verbal assurance from the finance director
2. A test of controls is used to: Choose every correct option.
   A. measure a balance's monetary value directly
   B. check whether a control actually operated as designed
   C. replace the need for any substantive testing in every case
   D. confirm a legal opinion
3. Explain what sampling risk is and how it can be reduced, though not eliminated. [3 marks]
4. A written management representation is obtained: Choose every correct option.
   A. instead of any other available evidence, whenever convenient
   B. only where no other sufficient appropriate evidence is reasonably expected to exist
   C. only for cash balances
   D. only when the client refuses to cooperate
5. A junior team member becomes aware of facts suggesting possible fraud by a senior manager. They should: Choose every correct option.
   A. say nothing until the audit is complete
   B. resolve the matter alone to avoid concerning colleagues
   C. refer the matter to a more senior/experienced colleague such as the engagement partner
   D. confront the manager directly and alone

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B3 sampling risk is the risk that a conclusion based on a sample differs from the conclusion that would have been reached by testing the entire population, because the sample happens not to be representative; a larger, more carefully and representatively selected sample reduces sampling risk, but cannot eliminate it entirely short of testing 100% of the population.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
