# ASR_S03_Obtaining_Assurance_Evidence - Practice: Obtaining assurance evidence

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Rank, from most to least reliable, evidence obtained: (i) an oral explanation from the client's finance assistant, (ii) a bank confirmation letter received directly from the bank, (iii) a copy invoice printed from the client's own system. Explain your ranking. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 (ii) is most reliable (written, from an independent external source, obtained directly); (iii) is next (written, but generated internally by the client); (i) is least reliable (oral, and internally generated).

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
