# S14_Vitiating_Factors - Practice: Vitiating factors: mistake, misrepresentation, duress and undue influence

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish common mistake from mutual mistake. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 common mistake: both parties share the same fundamental mistaken belief about the contract (e.g. the subject matter no longer exists); B2 mutual mistake: the parties are at genuine cross-purposes, each meaning something different, so no true agreement was reached on an objective view.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
