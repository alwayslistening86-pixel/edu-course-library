# S18_Conveyancing_Start_and_Title - Practice: The conveyancing transaction overview, taking instructions, and deducing/investigating title

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. List the three parts of the register of title for registered land and what each contains. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 property register: describes the land and rights benefiting it (e.g. easements); B1 proprietorship register: names the registered proprietor(s) and states the class of title; B1 charges register: lists encumbrances burdening the land (e.g. mortgages, restrictive covenants).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
