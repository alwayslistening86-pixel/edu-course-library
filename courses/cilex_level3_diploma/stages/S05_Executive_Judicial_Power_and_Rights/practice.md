# S05_Executive_Judicial_Power_and_Rights - Practice: Executive power, judicial power, judicial review, and civil liberties/human rights

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish individual from collective ministerial responsibility. [2 marks]
2. State the three grounds for judicial review. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 individual: a minister personally accountable to Parliament for their department/own conduct; B1 collective: all ministers must publicly support Cabinet decisions or resign.
2. [3] B1 illegality; B1 procedural unfairness; B1 irrationality (Wednesbury unreasonableness).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
