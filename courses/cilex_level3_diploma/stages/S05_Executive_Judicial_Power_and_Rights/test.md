## Test items
1. Explain the effect of sections 3, 4 and 6 of the Human Rights Act 1998. [6 marks]
2. A prisoner is refused a religious dietary requirement by a prison (a public authority) with no proper consideration of their Article 9/8 Convention rights, and the refusal is based on a prison policy that on its face appears to conflict with the relevant statute. Using IRAC, advise on the prisoner's options. [12 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B2 s3: courts must read and give effect to legislation compatibly with Convention rights so far as possible; B2 s4: where that is not possible, a higher court may make a declaration of incompatibility, which does not invalidate the legislation but signals Parliament should act; B2 s6: it is unlawful for a public authority to act incompatibly with Convention rights, giving individuals a direct route to challenge such acts.
2. [12] Mark using issue-rule-application-conclusion (IRAC): identify the issue(s), state the rule precisely with its authority, apply it to the facts given, and reach a supported conclusion. Credit: issue = a public authority act potentially incompatible with Convention rights, and potential incompatibility between the policy and statute; rule = s6 HRA 1998 makes it unlawful for a public authority to act incompatibly with Convention rights, giving a direct claim/judicial review route; s3 requires legislation to be interpreted compatibly with Convention rights where possible; judicial review grounds (illegality/procedural unfairness) may also apply if the policy misapplies the statute or the process was unfair; application = the refusal with no proper consideration of Art 8/9 rights is a good s6 challenge, and the court should first try to interpret the statute compatibly under s3 before considering a declaration of incompatibility under s4; conclusion = the prisoner can bring judicial review on human-rights and possibly illegality grounds, seeking a quashing order and/or declaration; a s4 declaration of incompatibility is a last resort if s3 interpretation is not possible.
