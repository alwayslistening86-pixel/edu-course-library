# S12_Consideration_Intention_and_Capacity - Practice: Formation of a valid contract: consideration, intention to create legal relations and capacity

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the rule in Pinnel's Case (affirmed in Foakes v Beer) about part payment of a debt, and name one recognised exception. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 part payment of a debt is not, without more, good consideration for a promise to forgo the balance, so the creditor can still sue for the rest; B2 exception: payment at the creditor's request in a different form, earlier than the due date, at a different place, or with something additional given, or a genuine composition agreement between the debtor and multiple creditors -- credit any one.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
