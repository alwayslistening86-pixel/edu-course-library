# S03_Legal_Services_and_Regulation - Test: The provision and regulation of legal services

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in CILEX's style: short knowledge items (some multiple-select, mirroring the MCQ element of F1-F4) plus at least one scenario/problem question requiring issue-rule-application-conclusion (IRAC) reasoning -- identify the legal issue(s) a set of facts raises, state the applicable rule with its authority (statute section or case), apply it precisely to the facts, and reach a supported conclusion or piece of client advice. Give the whole test at once, with no hints. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain the personal responsibilities a legal services trainee has for client money and client data. [6 marks]
2. A CILEX trainee at a firm notices a large client payment has been recorded against the wrong file, and separately is asked by a friend outside work about a client's confidential matter. Using IRAC, explain how the trainee should respond to each situation. [10 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 client money must be kept separate from the firm's own money and handled per CILEX Regulation's accounts outcomes/SRA Accounts Rules; B1 client data must be collected, stored and used in compliance with the Data Protection Act 2018 and UK GDPR; B1 awareness of cybersecurity risk in handling client data/money; B1 compliance with AML regulations including client due diligence; B1 the trainee bears personal responsibility for their own compliance, not just the firm's; B1 must recognise the limits of their own authority and escalate concerns to a supervisor rather than act alone.
2. [10] Mark using issue-rule-application-conclusion (IRAC): identify the issue(s), state the rule precisely with its authority, apply it to the facts given, and reach a supported conclusion. Credit: issue = a client-money handling error, and a potential confidentiality breach; rule = client money must be accurately allocated and kept separate under the applicable accounts rules, and confidentiality/disclosure duties under the professional Code of Conduct prohibit disclosing client matters outside authorised purposes; application = the misallocated payment is a compliance issue the trainee should flag to a supervisor/the firm's accounts function promptly rather than correct informally or ignore, given the limits of their own authority; the friend's request must be refused, since confidentiality is owed to the client regardless of the setting; conclusion = escalate the payment error to a supervisor and decline to discuss the client matter with the friend, citing professional confidentiality.
