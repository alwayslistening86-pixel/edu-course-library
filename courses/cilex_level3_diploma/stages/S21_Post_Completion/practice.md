# S21_Post_Completion - Practice: Post-completion: sale-side and purchase-side steps, tax and registration

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State two post-completion steps on the purchase side. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 submitting an SDLT (England) or LTT (Wales) return and paying tax due within the applicable deadline; B2 applying to HM Land Registry to register the transfer/mortgage using the priority period from the pre-completion search; B2 reporting to the client/lender once registration is complete -- any 2 for full marks.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
