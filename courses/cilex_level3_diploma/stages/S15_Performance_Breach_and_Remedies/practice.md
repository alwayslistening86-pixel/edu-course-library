# S15_Performance_Breach_and_Remedies - Practice: Performance, termination, breach and frustration; causation and remedies for breach

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the test for whether a contract is discharged by frustration. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B3 after formation, without either party's fault, an unforeseen/unforeseeable event occurs making performance impossible, illegal, or radically different from what was originally contemplated (not merely more difficult or expensive).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
