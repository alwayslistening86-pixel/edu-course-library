# S22_Personal_Impact_and_Teamwork - Test: Personal impact, working in a team, and setting professional standards

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in CILEX's style: short knowledge items (some multiple-select, mirroring the MCQ element of F1-F4) plus at least one scenario/problem question requiring issue-rule-application-conclusion (IRAC) reasoning -- identify the legal issue(s) a set of facts raises, state the applicable rule with its authority (statute section or case), apply it precisely to the facts, and reach a supported conclusion or piece of client advice. Give the whole test at once, with no hints. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A trainee overhears a colleague making comments that repeatedly single out another team member's religion in a mocking way. What is the most appropriate first step for the trainee to take? Choose every correct option.
   A. Ignore it, since it is not the trainee's place to intervene in colleagues' conversations
   B. Join in to avoid seeming awkward in front of the team
   C. Raise the concern appropriately, e.g. with a supervisor or through the firm's reporting channels, recognising this may amount to unlawful discrimination/harassment related to a protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010
   D. Confront the colleague publicly and aggressively in front of the team
2. Explain the purpose of CPD and appraisal, and how they connect to a trainee's duty to recognise the limits of their own competence and authority. [6 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. [6] B2 CPD is the ongoing, structured maintenance and development of professional knowledge and skills, required to keep practitioners competent as law and practice evolve; B2 appraisal is a structured, periodic review of performance and development needs, providing feedback and agreeing goals; B2 both processes reinforce the wider professional obligation for a trainee to recognise the limits of their own competence and authority: appraisal surfaces gaps or training needs, and CPD is the mechanism for actively closing them, together supporting a trainee's judgement about when a matter is beyond their current competence and a supervisor should be involved.
