# S20_Exchange_and_Completion - Practice: Reporting and exchange, preparing for completion, and completion itself

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. What pre-completion search confers priority protection for the buyer's application to register, and what does that priority period protect against? [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B2 the official search of the register with priority (OS1 for whole, OS2 for part of the registered title); B1 it protects the buyer's application to register against any adverse entry made by a third party during the priority period between the search and the buyer's own registration application.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
