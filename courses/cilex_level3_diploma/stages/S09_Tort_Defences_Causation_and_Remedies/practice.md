# S09_Tort_Defences_Causation_and_Remedies - Practice: Defences, causation and remedies in tort

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish general damages from special damages in a personal injury claim. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 general damages compensate non-quantifiable loss such as pain, suffering and loss of amenity, assessed using guideline/comparable figures; B1 special damages compensate precisely quantifiable loss up to trial, such as medical expenses and lost earnings.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
