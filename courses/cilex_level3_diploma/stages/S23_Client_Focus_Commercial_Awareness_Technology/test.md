## Test items
1. A trainee uses a generative AI tool to help draft a client letter summarising a point of law, including a case citation the tool supplied. What is the correct approach before sending the letter? Choose every correct option.
   A. Send it as drafted, since AI tools are reliable enough not to need checking
   B. Independently verify the legal content and check the case citation is real and correctly describes the law, since the trainee remains professionally responsible for the accuracy of the final work product
   C. Add a disclaimer to the client that the letter was AI-generated and send it unchecked
   D. Refuse to use any AI assistance under any circumstances
2. Explain why 'internal clients' matter as much as external clients to good client-first practice. [6 marks]
3. A trainee is asked to draft a straightforward letter using the firm's automated document assembly system, which pulls in standard clauses based on answers to a short questionnaire. Explain the risks of simply sending the generated letter without further review, and what the trainee should do instead. [8 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [6] B2 an internal client is a colleague (e.g. a supervising fee earner) who relies on a trainee's work, such as research, drafting or administrative tasks, to serve the external client properly; B2 poor service to an internal client (unreliable, unclear or late work) ultimately undermines the service the external client receives, since the fee earner cannot properly advise or act without dependable support; B2 the same principles of responsiveness, clarity, reliability and proactive flagging of problems that apply to external clients should therefore apply equally when working for internal clients.
3. [8] B2 automated document assembly can generate documents efficiently but risks producing content that does not accurately reflect the specific client's facts or intentions if the questionnaire answers were incomplete, ambiguous or wrongly interpreted by the system, or if a standard clause is not actually appropriate for this client's circumstances; B2 over-reliance without human checking risks sending inaccurate, irrelevant or even harmful advice to the client, exposing the client and the firm to risk; B2 the trainee should carefully review the generated document against the client's actual instructions/facts before sending, checking for accuracy, relevance and any clauses that do not fit the specific matter; B2 if anything is unclear or the trainee is unsure whether the generated content is appropriate, they should escalate to a supervisor rather than send it on trust, consistent with the general professional duty to recognise the limits of their own competence.
