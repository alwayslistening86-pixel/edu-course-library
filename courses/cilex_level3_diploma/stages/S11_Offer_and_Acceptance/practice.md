# S11_Offer_and_Acceptance - Practice: Formation of a valid contract: offer and acceptance

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference between an offer and an invitation to treat, with one example of each. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 an offer is a statement of terms made with the intention to be bound as soon as accepted (e.g. Carlill-style unilateral advertisement, or a bid at auction); B2 an invitation to treat is an indication of willingness to negotiate/receive offers, not itself acceptable (e.g. goods in a shop window, per Boots; an advertisement for the sale of goods, per Partridge v Crittenden).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
