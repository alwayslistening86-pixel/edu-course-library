# S10_Contract_Outline - Practice: Contract law in outline: privity, professional/consumer contracts, illegality

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the two limbs of s1(1) of the Contracts (Rights of Third Parties) Act 1999 under which a third party may enforce a contract term. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 s1(1)(a) the contract expressly provides that the third party may enforce the term; B2 s1(1)(b) the term purports to confer a benefit on the third party, unless on the true construction of the contract the parties did not intend it to be enforceable by them.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
