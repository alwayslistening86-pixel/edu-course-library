# S02_The_English_Legal_System - Practice: The English legal system: courts, appeals, legal personality, ADR

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name the court of first instance and the first appeal route for (a) an either-way offence sent to the Crown Court and (b) a £6,000 civil debt claim. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 (a) Crown Court first instance, appeal to the Court of Appeal (Criminal Division); B2 (b) County Court (small claims track, as under £10,000), appeal to a Circuit Judge/High Court then Court of Appeal (Civil Division).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
