# S16_Property_Foundations - Practice: Organisations, underpinning land law, documents, co-ownership and regulatory matters

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish a beneficial joint tenancy from a tenancy in common, and state what happens to each on the death of one co-owner. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 joint tenancy: each owns the whole notionally together with no distinct share; on death, the right of survivorship passes the whole beneficial interest automatically to the survivor(s), outside the will; B2 tenancy in common: each owns a distinct, quantifiable share; on death, that share does not pass by survivorship but forms part of the deceased's estate, passing under their will or the intestacy rules.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
