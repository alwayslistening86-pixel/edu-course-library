# S13_Content_of_a_Contract - Practice: The content of a contract: terms, implied terms, exemption clauses

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the two common-law tests used to decide whether a term should be implied in fact. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 the business efficacy test -- the term is necessary to make the contract work commercially; B2 the 'officious bystander' test -- the term is so obvious that, if an officious bystander suggested it, both parties would testily agree it goes without saying.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
