# CILEX Level 3 Diploma in Law and Practice (CPQ Foundation Stage) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
A representative paper covering each of the six modules: several short knowledge items (including multiple-select) per module, one IRAC-style scenario question per law module (F1-F4), a short client communication/drafting task (F5) and a short legal-research task with citation (F6). Questions are original, in CILEX's style; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage-test items. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest to reach full coverage of all six modules.

## 6 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [F1] A local council refuses a market trader's licence renewal without giving any reason or a chance to respond, relying on a discretion granted by statute. The trader wants to challenge the decision. Explain, using IRAC, whether judicial review is available and what remedy might be sought. [12 marks]
2. [F2] A shop customer slips on a spillage that had been on the floor for 40 minutes before staff cleaned it. Using IRAC, advise on the customer's claim in negligence and under the Occupiers' Liability Act 1957. [12 marks]
3. [F3] A trader advertises a laptop in a shop window at a clearly mismarked price. A customer 'accepts' by handing over cash at the till before the trader notices the error. Using IRAC, explain whether a contract has been formed. [10 marks]
4. [F4] A married couple bought their home as beneficial joint tenants. One dies without a will, survived by their spouse and one adult child. Using IRAC, explain who owns the house and what, if anything, passes under the intestacy rules. [10 marks]
5. [F5] Draft a short client care letter opening paragraph for a new residential conveyancing client, and list the other essential contents a compliant client care letter needs. [10 marks]
6. [F6] A client asks whether a 2019 Court of Appeal decision on a point of contract law is still good law. Describe, step by step, how you would research this using primary and secondary sources, and how you would cite the case correctly. [10 marks]

[12] Scenario items are marked on an issue-rule-application-conclusion (IRAC) basis: identify every legal issue the facts raise; state the applicable rule precisely, naming the statute section or case authority; apply the rule to the specific facts given (not a restatement of the rule in the abstract); and reach a supported conclusion or piece of client advice. Marks are lost for a correct rule with no application to the facts, and for a conclusion unsupported by the reasoning above it. ## Grading
Mark knowledge items and scenario items by the matching mark scheme (knowledge items: a mark per accurate, specific point; scenario items: IRAC, per the note above). A pass needs at least 50% of the marks overall and at least 40% on each of the six modules. Report the total, the percentage per module, and the weakest topic areas. This is the course's own standard; CILEX's real pass marks for F1-F6 are not published as fixed percentages the same way, so this course does not claim to reproduce them.
