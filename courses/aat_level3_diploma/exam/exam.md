# AAT Level 3 Diploma in Accounting - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Four mini-papers in sequence, one per unit: Business Awareness items from S01-S05; Financial Accounting: Preparing Financial Statements items from S06-S16; Management Accounting Techniques items from S17-S24; Tax Processes for Businesses items from S25-S29. Mix multiple-choice, numeric-entry and short written/journal/financial-statement items reflecting each unit's real question mix, in non-sequential order within each unit. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank across all four units; the tutor writes further items in the same proportions and style to build a complete set of four mini-papers.

## 11 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Business Awareness] Which of these is a fundamental ethical principle relating to acting diligently and in accordance with applicable technical and professional standards? Choose every correct option.
   A. Integrity
   B. Professional competence and due care
   C. Confidentiality
   D. Objectivity
2. [Business Awareness] A business receives a large, one-off consultancy fee that arrives one month before the year end but relates mostly to work still to be done next year. Identify which fundamental accounting principle is most relevant, and explain how it should be applied. [2 marks]
3. Under IAS 16, which of these should be excluded from the cost of a non-current asset? Choose every correct option.
   A. Delivery costs
   B. Installation costs
   C. Repairs after the asset is brought into use
   D. Costs of testing the asset is working properly
4. [Financial Accounting] A machine costs £48,000, has an estimated residual value of £3,000 and a useful life of 5 years. Calculate the annual straight-line depreciation charge. [2 marks]
5. [Financial Accounting] A sole trader's revenue is £180,000 and cost of sales is £117,000. Calculate the gross profit and the gross profit margin (%). [3 marks]
6. [Management Accounting] Which method of inventory valuation, in a period of rising prices, gives the highest closing inventory value? Choose every correct option.
   A. FIFO
   B. LIFO
   C. AVCO
   D. They are always identical
7. [Management Accounting] Selling price is £60/unit, variable cost is £35/unit, and fixed costs are £75,000. Calculate the contribution per unit and the breakeven point in units. [3 marks]
8. [Management Accounting] Standard material usage for 500 units is 4kg/unit at £3/kg. Actual usage was 2,150kg. Calculate the material usage variance and state whether it is adverse or favourable. [3 marks]
9. [Tax Processes] A UK business's taxable turnover for the previous 12 months first exceeds which figure, triggering compulsory VAT registration? Choose every correct option.
   A. £85,000
   B. £88,000
   C. £90,000
   D. £100,000
10. [Tax Processes] A VAT-registered business has standard-rated sales (VAT-exclusive) of £54,000 and standard-rated purchases (VAT-exclusive) of £21,000 in a VAT period. Calculate the VAT payable to HMRC (standard rate 20%). [3 marks]
11. [Tax Processes] An employee earns £2,200 in a month (2026/27, monthly personal allowance £1,047.50, monthly NIC primary threshold £1,047.50, employee NIC 8% up to the monthly upper earnings limit of £4,189.17). Calculate their employee Class 1 NIC for the month. [2 marks]

## Answer key for the ready-made items (tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [2] B1 the accruals principle; B1 only the portion of the fee earned by the year end should be recognised as this year's income, with the rest deferred (accrued/prepaid income) to the period it relates to.
3. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
4. [2] M1 (48,000-3,000)/5; A1 = £9,000/year.
5. [3] M1 gross profit = 180,000-117,000 = £63,000; M1 margin = 63,000/180,000; A1 = 35%.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
7. [3] M1 contribution = 60-35 = £25/unit; A1 breakeven = 75,000/25; A1 = 3,000 units.
8. [3] M1 standard usage = 500 x 4 = 2,000kg; M1 usage variance (in kg) = 2,000-2,150 = 150kg adverse, valued at standard price 150 x £3; A1 = £450 adverse.
9. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
10. [3] M1 output VAT = 54,000 x 20% = £10,800; M1 input VAT = 21,000 x 20% = £4,200; A1 VAT payable = £6,600.
11. [2] M1 (2,200-1,047.50) x 8%; A1 = £92.20.

## Grading
Mark each unit separately against `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric`, then report an overall percentage. A pass needs at least 70% on each of the four units individually AND at least 70% overall -- mirroring AAT's real, uniform 70% pass mark, which applies per unit (each unit assessment is itself separately pass/fail at 70%), not a lower per-component minimum.

## Outcome
- **Pass:** record `exam_status: "passed"`. The course is complete.
- **Not yet:** leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the weakest unit and topic areas, offer targeted review, and retry with fresh papers.
