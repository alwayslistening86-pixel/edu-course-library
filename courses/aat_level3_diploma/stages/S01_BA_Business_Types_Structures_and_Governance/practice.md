# S01_BA_Business_Types_Structures_and_Governance - Practice: Business types, the legal framework, stakeholders, structure and governance

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State two features that distinguish a private limited company from a sole trader. [2 marks]
2. Which business structure has at least one partner with liability capped at their investment and no management role? Choose every correct option.
   A. a limited partnership
   B. a sole trader
   C. an LLP
   D. a PLC

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 separate legal personality; B1 limited liability for owners; B1 ownership (shareholders) and management (directors) are typically separate; B1 must file accounts at Companies House -- any 2 for full marks.
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
