# S08_FA_Non_Current_Asset_Acquisition_and_Disposal - Practice: Authorising capital expenditure, capital vs revenue expenditure, and recording acquisitions/disposals

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A non-current asset cost £12,000 and has accumulated depreciation of £9,000. It is sold for £2,500 cash. Calculate the gain or loss on disposal, stating which it is. [3 marks]
2. Which of these should be classified as revenue expenditure, not capitalised? Choose every correct option.
   A. Repairs to keep an existing machine running
   B. The purchase price of a new machine
   C. Delivery costs of a new machine
   D. Installation costs of a new machine

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 carrying amount = 12,000 - 9,000 = £3,000; M1 proceeds £2,500 are less than carrying amount £3,000; A1 loss on disposal of £500.
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
