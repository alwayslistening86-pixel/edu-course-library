# S21_MA_Budgeting_and_Variance_Analysis - Practice: Preparing budgets and calculating/interpreting variances

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Budgeted sales are 3,000 units; required closing inventory is 250 units; opening inventory is 300 units. Calculate the production budget. [2 marks]
2. Standard material cost is £5.00/kg. Actual price paid was £5.30/kg for 2,000kg used. Calculate the material price variance, stating whether adverse or favourable. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 3,000 + 250 - 300; A1 = 2,950 units.
2. [2] M1 (5.00-5.30) x 2,000; A1 = £600 adverse.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
