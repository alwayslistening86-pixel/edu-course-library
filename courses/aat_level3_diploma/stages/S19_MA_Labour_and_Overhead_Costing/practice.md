# S19_MA_Labour_and_Overhead_Costing - Practice: Labour costing and overhead allocation/absorption

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. An employee's basic rate is £12.00/hour, standard week 35 hours. They work 39 hours, with hours beyond 35 paid at double time. Calculate their total gross pay for the week. [3 marks]
2. A cost centre's budgeted overhead is £36,000 and budgeted machine hours are 4,000. Calculate the overhead absorption rate per machine hour. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 basic pay = 35 x 12.00 = £420.00; M1 overtime = 4 x (12.00 x 2) = 4 x 24.00 = £96.00; A1 total = £516.00.
2. [2] M1 36,000/4,000; A1 = £9.00 per machine hour.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
