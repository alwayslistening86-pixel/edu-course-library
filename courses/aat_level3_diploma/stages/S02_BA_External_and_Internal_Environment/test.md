## Test items
1. A government announces a new tax on single-use plastic packaging. Identify two PESTLE categories this could be classified under, explaining each. [3 marks]
2. Explain how a high barrier to entry in a market affects the level of competition a business faces. [2 marks]
3. Which best describes the 'social' dimension of the triple bottom line? Choose every correct option.
   A. Fair treatment of employees, customers and the community
   B. Minimising resource use and emissions
   C. Remaining financially viable
   D. Maximising shareholder dividends
4. Explain why an accountant's public interest duty is relevant to sustainability. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 Political -- a change in government tax policy; B1 Legal -- a new legal requirement businesses must comply with; B1 (either) Environmental -- driven by environmental/sustainability concerns -- full marks for any 2 of the 3 well explained.
2. [2] B1 a high barrier to entry (e.g. large capital investment needed, strong regulation, established brand loyalty) makes it harder for new competitors to enter the market; B1 so existing businesses in the market face less competition than in a market with low barriers to entry.
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
4. [2] B1 the public interest duty means acting for the wider public benefit, not only an employer's/client's narrow short-term interest; B1 this increasingly means considering the sustainability implications of the advice and information an accountant provides, not just its short-term financial effect.
