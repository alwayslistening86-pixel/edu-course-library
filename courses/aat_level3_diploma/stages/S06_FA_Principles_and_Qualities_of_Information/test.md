## Test items
1. Explain why lenders are considered a primary user of financial statements, and what they use the accounts to assess. [2 marks]
2. Explain the going concern principle, and state what should happen if it is not appropriate to apply it. [2 marks]
3. Which enhancing qualitative characteristic means different, independent observers could reach consensus that information faithfully represents what it claims to? Choose every correct option.
   A. Verifiability
   B. Comparability
   C. Timeliness
   D. Understandability
4. Explain the materiality principle, using an example of an item that might be material for a small business but immaterial for a large one. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 lenders provide finance (loans) to the business and need to decide whether to lend, and whether to continue lending; B1 they use the accounts to assess whether the business can repay the loan with interest, i.e. its liquidity and solvency.
2. [2] B1 going concern assumes the business will continue operating for the foreseeable future, so assets/liabilities are valued on that basis; B1 if this assumption is not appropriate (e.g. the business is expected to cease trading), the accounts should be prepared on a different basis (e.g. a break-up basis) and this should be disclosed.
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
4. [3] B1 an item is material if its omission or misstatement could reasonably be expected to influence users' decisions; B1 materiality depends on the size and nature of the item relative to the business, not a fixed amount; B1 example, e.g. a £2,000 error would likely be material for a small sole trader but immaterial for a large PLC.
