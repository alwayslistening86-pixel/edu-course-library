# S25_TAX_UK_Tax_System_and_VAT_Principles - Practice: The UK tax system and the principles of VAT

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Which of these is the annual piece of primary legislation that enacts a Budget's tax changes? Choose every correct option.
   A. The Finance Act
   B. The Companies Act
   C. The VAT regulations
   D. The Partnership Act
2. State the difference between tax avoidance and tax evasion. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
2. [2] B1 tax avoidance is legally arranging affairs to minimise tax (though it can still be challenged if it exploits the law's intent); B1 tax evasion is illegally not paying tax that is legally due, e.g. concealing income -- and is a criminal offence.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
