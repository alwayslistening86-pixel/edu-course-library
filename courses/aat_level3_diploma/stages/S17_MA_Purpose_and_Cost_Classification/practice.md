# S17_MA_Purpose_and_Cost_Classification - Practice: The purpose of management accounting and classifying costs

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Which cost stays the same in total, regardless of the level of activity, within a relevant range? Choose every correct option.
   A. A fixed cost
   B. A variable cost
   C. A semi-variable cost
   D. A direct cost
2. Classify a factory supervisor's salary by element (direct/indirect) and by behaviour (fixed/variable), explaining your answer. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
2. [2] B1 indirect -- cannot be traced to a single product/cost unit, it is shared across everything the supervisor oversees; B1 fixed -- normally does not change with the level of output produced.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
