# S09_FA_Depreciation - Practice: Calculating and recording depreciation: straight-line and diminishing balance

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A machine costs £40,000, has a residual value of £4,000 and a useful life of 6 years. Calculate the annual straight-line depreciation charge. [2 marks]
2. An asset costs £18,000 and is depreciated at 25% reducing balance. Calculate the depreciation charge for year 1 and the carrying amount at the end of year 1. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 (40,000-4,000)/6; A1 = £6,000/year.
2. [2] M1 year 1 depreciation = 18,000 x 25% = £4,500; A1 carrying amount = 18,000 - 4,500 = £13,500.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
