# S23_MA_Short_Term_Decision_Making - Practice: Breakeven and CVP analysis, limiting factors and relevant costing

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Selling price £25/unit, variable cost £15/unit, fixed costs £30,000. Calculate the breakeven point in units. [2 marks]
2. A product's absorbed cost to make is £22/unit, of which £5/unit is unavoidable fixed overhead. An external supplier offers £19/unit. Calculate the relevant cost of making, and state which option is cheaper. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 contribution = 25-15 = £10; A1 breakeven = 30,000/10 = 3,000 units.
2. [2] M1 relevant cost of making = 22 - 5 = £17/unit; A1 making is cheaper (£17 relevant cost vs £19 to buy in).

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
