# S14_FA_Partnership_Financial_Statements - Practice: Preparing the partnership's appropriation account, statement of profit or loss and statement of financial position

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Partners C and D have fixed capital of £20,000 and £10,000, interest on capital 4% p.a., no salaries, PSR 1:1. Net profit for appropriation is £24,000. Calculate each partner's total share. [3 marks]
2. Partner E's current account opens at £500 (credit), plus interest on capital £600 and profit share £9,000, less drawings £7,200. Calculate the closing balance. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 interest: C = 20,000 x 4% = £800; D = 10,000 x 4% = £400 (total £1,200); M1 residual = 24,000 - 1,200 = £22,800, split 1:1 = £11,400 each; A1 C's total = 800+11,400 = £12,200; D's total = 400+11,400 = £11,800.
2. [2] M1 500 + 600 + 9,000 - 7,200; A1 = £2,900.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
