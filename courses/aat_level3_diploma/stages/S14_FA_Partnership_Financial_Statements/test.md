# S14_FA_Partnership_Financial_Statements - Test: Preparing the partnership's appropriation account, statement of profit or loss and statement of financial position

## Test items
1. Partners F and G have fixed capital of £50,000 and £25,000, interest on capital 6% p.a., F receives a salary of £6,000, PSR 2:1. Net profit for appropriation is £42,000. Calculate the amount allocated in total to each partner, showing your workings. [5 marks]
2. Partner H's current account opens at £1,200 (credit). H is entitled to interest on capital of £900 and a residual profit share of £14,600, and has taken drawings of £10,000 during the year. Calculate H's closing current account balance. [2 marks]
3. Which of these normally changes only when a partner formally injects or withdraws capital, not with the period's profit or drawings? Choose every correct option.
   A. The capital account
   B. The current account
   C. The appropriation account
   D. The statement of profit or loss
4. Explain the difference in purpose between a partner's capital account and their current account. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 interest on capital: F = 50,000 x 6% = £3,000; G = 25,000 x 6% = £1,500 (total £4,500); M1 residual profit = 42,000 - 4,500 - 6,000 = £31,500; M1 split 2:1: F = 31,500 x 2/3 = £21,000; G = 31,500 x 1/3 = £10,500; A1 total to F = 3,000 + 6,000 + 21,000 = £30,000; total to G = 1,500 + 10,500 = £12,000; B1 check: 30,000 + 12,000 = £42,000, agreeing with net profit for appropriation.
2. [2] M1 1,200 + 900 + 14,600 - 10,000; A1 = £6,700.
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
4. [3] B1 the capital account holds the partner's originally invested/agreed capital, normally fixed and changed only by a formal injection or withdrawal of capital; B1 the current account is a running balance of the partner's share of profit (interest, salary, residual share) less drawings, changing every period; B1 the current account reflects what is currently owed to/by the partner beyond their fixed capital stake.
