# S10_FA_Accruals_Prepayments_and_Receivables - Practice: Accruals, prepayments, irrecoverable debts and allowances for doubtful receivables

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A business pays £2,400 on 1 October for 12 months' rent in advance, with a 31 December year end. Calculate the prepayment and the expense for the period. [3 marks]
2. A general allowance for doubtful receivables is required at 3% of receivables of £90,000. The opening allowance was £2,400. Calculate the increase or decrease in the allowance charged to profit. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 3 months (Oct-Dec) relate to this period, 9 months are prepaid; M1 prepayment = 2,400 x 9/12 = £1,800; A1 expense for the period = 2,400 - 1,800 = £600.
2. [3] M1 new allowance = 90,000 x 3% = £2,700; M1 change = 2,700 - 2,400; A1 increase of £300 charged as an expense.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
