# S26_TAX_VAT_Registration_and_Deregistration - Practice: VAT registration and deregistration

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. What is the current UK VAT registration threshold? Choose every correct option.
   A. £90,000
   B. £85,000
   C. £88,000
   D. £100,000
2. State one reason a business below the VAT registration threshold might still choose to register voluntarily. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
2. [1] B1 any reasonable reason, e.g. to reclaim input VAT on its costs, or to appear more credible to VAT-registered customers expecting a VAT invoice.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
