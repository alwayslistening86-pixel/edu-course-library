# S24_MA_Cash_Management - Practice: Cash flow forecasting and managing cash surpluses and deficits

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A month's opening cash balance is £3,200. Receipts are £28,000 and payments are £25,600. Calculate the closing balance. [2 marks]
2. Current assets are £45,000 (inventory £15,000) and current liabilities are £30,000. Calculate the current ratio and the quick ratio. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 3,200 + 28,000 - 25,600; A1 = £5,600.
2. [2] M1 current ratio = 45,000/30,000 = 1.5:1; A1 quick ratio = (45,000-15,000)/30,000 = 1.0:1.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
