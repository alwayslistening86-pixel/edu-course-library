# S16_FA_Incomplete_Records - Practice: Preparing accounting records from incomplete information, mark-up/margin, and reasonableness

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Payables control account: opening balance £15,000, payments to suppliers £62,000, discounts received £900, closing balance £13,400. Calculate credit purchases for the year. [3 marks]
2. Cost of sales is £40,000 and margin is 20%. Calculate the revenue. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 total debits (money 'leaving' the payables balance) = 62,000 + 900 + 13,400 = £76,300; M1 credit purchases = 76,300 - 15,000; A1 = £61,300.
2. [2] M1 gross profit = 40,000 x 20%/(1-20%) -- or, using margin correctly, revenue = cost of sales/(1-margin) = 40,000/0.80; A1 = £50,000.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
