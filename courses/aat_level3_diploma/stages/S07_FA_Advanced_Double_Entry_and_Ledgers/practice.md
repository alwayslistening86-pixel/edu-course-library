# S07_FA_Advanced_Double_Entry_and_Ledgers - Practice: The accounting equation, ledger classification, books of prime entry and period end routines

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A business has liabilities of £27,000 and equity of £58,000. Calculate its total assets. [2 marks]
2. Which of these is a memorandum (subsidiary) ledger, not part of the double-entry system? Choose every correct option.
   A. The individual receivables ledger
   B. The receivables ledger control account
   C. The VAT control account
   D. The general ledger

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 assets = liabilities + equity = 27,000 + 58,000; A1 = £85,000.
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
