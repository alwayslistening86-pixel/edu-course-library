## Test items
1. A business has assets of £94,000 and equity of £61,000. Calculate its total liabilities. [2 marks]
2. Classify each of the following: (a) a five-year bank loan; (b) purchased software licences; (c) a bank overdraft. [3 marks]
3. Which book of prime entry is used to record depreciation and the correction of errors? Choose every correct option.
   A. The journal
   B. The cash book
   C. The purchases daybook
   D. The discounts received daybook
4. Explain the difference in purpose between the receivables ledger control account and the individual receivables (memorandum) ledger. [3 marks]
5. State two things that should be checked or verified as part of financial period end routines, before the period is closed off. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] M1 liabilities = assets - equity = 94,000 - 61,000; A1 = £33,000.
2. [3] B1 (a) non-current liability; B1 (b) intangible non-current asset; B1 (c) current liability.
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
4. [3] B1 the receivables ledger control account is a general ledger account, part of the double-entry system, summarising in total what all credit customers owe; B1 the individual receivables ledger holds one memorandum account per customer, not part of double-entry, used for credit control/reference; B1 reconciling the two helps identify errors, since the control account total should equal the sum of the individual balances.
5. [2] B1 general ledger balances reconciled to relevant sources (e.g. bank account to bank statement, control accounts to memorandum ledgers); B1 whether transactions recorded are genuine, valid and in the correct period; B1 that balances have been correctly carried down/closed off according to their classification -- any 2 for full marks.
