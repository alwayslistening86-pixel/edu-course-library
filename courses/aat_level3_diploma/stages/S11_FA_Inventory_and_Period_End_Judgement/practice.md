# S11_FA_Inventory_and_Period_End_Judgement - Practice: Recording inventory under IAS 2, and the judgement needed for period end adjustments

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. An inventory item cost £1,200. It can now be sold for £1,450 after £150 of further costs to complete and sell it. Calculate the item's NRV and state the value it should be included at. [2 marks]
2. IAS 2 requires inventory to be valued item by item at the lower of cost and NRV, meaning: Choose every correct option.
   A. a below-cost item cannot be offset against an above-cost item
   B. all inventory items are simply totalled at cost
   C. only the most valuable items are written down
   D. NRV is always used regardless of cost

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 NRV = 1,450 - 150 = £1,300; A1 valued at the lower of cost (£1,200) and NRV (£1,300), so £1,200 (cost).
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)

One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
