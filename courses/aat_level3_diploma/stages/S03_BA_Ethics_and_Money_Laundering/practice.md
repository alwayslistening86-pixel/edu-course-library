# S03_BA_Ethics_and_Money_Laundering - Practice: The ethical code, ethical conflicts and money laundering

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Which fundamental ethical principle is most directly threatened by accepting a large personal gift from a client in exchange for favourable treatment? Choose every correct option.
   A. Objectivity
   B. Confidentiality
   C. Professional competence and due care
   D. Professional behaviour
2. Name the three stages of money laundering. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
2. [3] B1 placement; B1 layering; B1 integration.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
