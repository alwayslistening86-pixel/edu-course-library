# S05_BA_Information_Visualisation_and_Communication - Practice: Information requirements, big data, visualisation and communication

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Which of the 'five Vs' of big data refers to how trustworthy the data is? Choose every correct option.
   A. Veracity
   B. Volume
   C. Velocity
   D. Variety
2. State which chart type best suits showing a trend in monthly sales over a year, and why. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
2. [2] B1 a line chart; B1 because it clearly shows change/trend over a continuous time period.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
