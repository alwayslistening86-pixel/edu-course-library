## Test items
1. Explain how the information needs of an operational-level employee differ from those of a strategic (board) level decision-maker. [3 marks]
2. List the five Vs of big data. [3 marks]
3. A finance team wants to communicate key monthly figures quickly to non-technical department managers. Which is most appropriate? Choose every correct option.
   A. A software dashboard summarising the key figures visually
   B. A raw export of the full general ledger
   C. A 40-page detailed financial report
   D. No communication -- managers do not need this information
4. Explain why professional scepticism should be applied to information drawn from big data sources. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 operational: detailed, frequent, mostly internal information for day-to-day tasks; B1 strategic: highly summarised information for long-term decisions; B1 strategic decisions often also draw on external, not just internal, sources.
2. [3] B1 volume; B1 variety; B1 velocity; B1 veracity; B1 value -- any 3 for full marks.
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
4. [2] B1 big data can vary widely in veracity/quality, and a low-quality or unreliable source can lead to a wrong conclusion if accepted uncritically; B1 professional scepticism means questioning the source and reliability of the data, not just accepting an analysis at face value.
