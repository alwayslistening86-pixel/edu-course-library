# A-level Psychology (AQA 7182): Introductory Topics, Psychology in Context, and Relationships/Schizophrenia/Aggression - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Paper 1 covers Social Influence, Memory, Attachment and Psychopathology (S01-S17), four 24-mark sections. Paper 2 covers Approaches (S18-S23), Biopsychology (S24-S30) and Research Methods (S31-S40), worth 24, 24 and 48 marks respectively. Paper 3 covers Issues and Debates (S41-S43, compulsory, 24 marks) plus Relationships (S44-S46), Schizophrenia (S47-S49) and Aggression (S50-S52), 24 marks each. Questions are original, in AQA's style, at real tariffs (1-16 marks); write fresh ones rather than reusing stage-test items. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest to fill each paper to its real 96-mark structure.

## 10 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1] Outline two variables that were found to affect the level of conformity in Asch's research. [4 marks]
2. [Paper 1] Discuss Bowlby's monotropic theory of attachment. [16 marks]
3. [Paper 1] A therapist is using cognitive behaviour therapy to treat a client with depression. Explain, referring to Ellis's ABC model, how the therapist might challenge the client's irrational belief that a single mistake at work means they are a complete failure. [4 marks]
4. Which of the following is a negative symptom of schizophrenia? Choose every correct option.
   A. avolition
   B. a delusion
   C. a hallucination
   D. disorganised speech that is fluent but incoherent
5. [Paper 2] Explain the difference between the somatic nervous system and the autonomic nervous system. [3 marks]
6. [Paper 2] Discuss the psychodynamic approach in psychology. [16 marks]
7. [Paper 2] A researcher measures the correlation between hours of sleep and reaction time in 12 participants, and wants to test the significance of the relationship using an appropriate statistical test. The data for both variables is at the ordinal level. Name the correct inferential test, and justify your choice. [3 marks]
8. [Paper 3] Outline two ways in which culture bias can affect the diagnosis of schizophrenia. [4 marks]
9. [Paper 3, Relationships] Evaluate Rusbult's investment model of romantic relationships. [16 marks]
10. [Paper 3, Aggression] Discuss the frustration-aggression hypothesis as an explanation of aggression. [16 marks]
