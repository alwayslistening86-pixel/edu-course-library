## Test items
1. Describe all four techniques of the original cognitive interview. [4 marks]
2. [9-mark style] Discuss the cognitive interview as a way of improving the accuracy of eyewitness testimony. [9 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 report everything -- report every detail, even seemingly trivial or unconfident ones; B1 reinstate the context -- mentally recreate the environmental and emotional context of the original event; B1 reverse the order -- recall events in a different chronological order to disrupt schema-driven expectations and reveal dishonest accounts; B1 change perspective -- recall from another person's viewpoint, again to disrupt expectation-driven recall.
2. [9] Mark out of 9 using AQA's levels of response (AO1 knowledge + AO3 discussion/evaluation): Level 3 (7-9) accurate, coherent, effective discussion, showing sound knowledge and understanding; Level 2 (4-6) evident knowledge and understanding, discussion is less thorough/effective or slightly muddled in places; Level 1 (1-3) knowledge is limited, discussion is barely evident or absent, answer may just be a list of points. 0 marks: no relevant content. Credit accurate description of the four (or enhanced CI) techniques and their psychological rationale, and effective evaluation such as: research support for improved recall accuracy compared with a standard interview (e.g. Kohnken's meta-analysis found the CI produced more correct details, though also somewhat more incorrect ones, than standard interviews); the CI being time-consuming and requiring extensive specialist training, limiting real-world police use; and the difficulty of using some components with vulnerable witnesses (e.g. very young children).
