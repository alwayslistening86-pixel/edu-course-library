# S16_Depression - Practice: Depression

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give one cognitive characteristic of depression. [1 mark]
2. Outline the three components of Beck's negative triad. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 any one of poor concentration/difficulty making decisions, absolutist/black-and-white thinking, negative or hopeless beliefs about self/world/future, briefly and accurately described.
2. [3] B1 negative view of the self (e.g. 'I am worthless'); B1 negative view of the world (e.g. 'no one understands me'); B1 negative view of the future (e.g. 'nothing will ever improve').

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
