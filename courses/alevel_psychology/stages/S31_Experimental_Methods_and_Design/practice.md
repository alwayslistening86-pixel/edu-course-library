# S31_Experimental_Methods_and_Design - Practice: Experimental methods and experimental design

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between a field experiment and a natural experiment. [2 marks]
2. Give one limitation of using an independent groups design. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 field experiment: conducted in a natural setting, but the researcher still directly manipulates the IV; B1 natural experiment: studies an IV that occurs naturally/independently of the researcher, who does not manipulate it.
2. [2] B1 participant variables (individual differences between people in different groups) are not controlled, potentially confounding the results; B1 (or) requires more participants overall than repeated measures, briefly explained.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
