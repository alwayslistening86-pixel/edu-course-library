## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 learning theory ('cupboard love') proposes attachment forms because the caregiver is associated with/provides food; B1 Harlow found infant monkeys preferred the non-feeding cloth surrogate for comfort over the feeding wire surrogate; B1 suggesting contact comfort, not feeding, is central to attachment, directly undermining learning theory's central claim.
2. [3] B1 the infant's primary attachment relationship forms a mental representation/template of what relationships are generally like; B1 this template is carried forward and used to understand and form later relationships; B1 giving early attachment quality a lasting influence on, e.g., the person's own later parenting or romantic relationships.
