# S49_Treating_Schizophrenia - Practice: Treating schizophrenia, and the interactionist approach

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain how typical antipsychotics are thought to reduce positive symptoms of schizophrenia. [2 marks]
2. Give one difference between a token economy and CBT for psychosis. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 they act as dopamine antagonists, binding to but not stimulating dopamine (D2) receptors; B1 blocking normal dopamine activity and so reducing dopamine transmission, consistent with the dopamine hypothesis.
2. [2] B1 a token economy uses operant conditioning (reinforcing desirable behaviour with exchangeable tokens) to manage behaviour/functioning, rather than addressing underlying beliefs; B1 CBT works directly on the patient's own thoughts/beliefs (e.g. challenging delusional beliefs, developing coping strategies), a cognitive rather than purely behavioural approach.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
