2. Evaluate drug therapy as a treatment for schizophrenia. [8 marks]
3. A behavioural technique used in institutional settings, where desirable behaviour is reinforced with exchangeable tokens, is called: Choose every correct option.
   A. a token economy
   B. cognitive behavioural therapy
   C. family therapy
   D. the double bind

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 schizophrenia develops from the combination of a genetic/biological vulnerability (diathesis) and sufficient environmental stress; B1 Meehl's original version proposed a single specific genetic vulnerability ('schizotaxia') interacting with chronic stress; B1 modern versions recognise multiple possible diatheses (genetic, prenatal, early trauma) combining with different kinds of stressor; B1 explains the model's practical implication -- combining biological and psychological treatment is likely more effective than either alone.
