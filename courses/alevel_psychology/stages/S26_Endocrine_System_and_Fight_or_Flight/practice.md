# S26_Endocrine_System_and_Fight_or_Flight - Practice: The endocrine system and the fight-or-flight response

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain why the pituitary gland is often called the 'master gland'. [2 marks]
2. Name the gland that releases adrenaline in response to a perceived threat. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 it controls the release of hormones from most other endocrine glands in the body; B1 itself regulated by the hypothalamus.
2. [1] B1 the adrenal gland (specifically the adrenal medulla).
