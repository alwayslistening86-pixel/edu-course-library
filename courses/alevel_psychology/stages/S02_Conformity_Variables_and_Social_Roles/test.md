## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 conformity increased when the line-judgement task was made harder (lines more similar in length); B1 explanation: a harder/more ambiguous task makes participants less certain of their own judgement; B1 so they rely more on others for information (informational social influence).
2. [6] B1 mock prison at Stanford, emotionally stable male volunteers randomly assigned to guard or prisoner roles; B1 prisoners 'arrested', given uniforms/numbers removing individual identity; guards given uniforms/mirrored sunglasses and told to keep order; B1 study stopped after six days (planned as two weeks); B1 guards became increasingly brutal/authoritarian, devising degrading punishments; B1 prisoners became increasingly passive and distressed, some released early; B1 conclusion: people readily conform to assigned social roles, supporting a situational explanation of behaviour.
