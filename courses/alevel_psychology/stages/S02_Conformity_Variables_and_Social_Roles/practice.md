# S02_Conformity_Variables_and_Social_Roles - Practice: Variables affecting conformity, and conformity to social roles

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline what Asch found when just one confederate broke unanimity by giving the correct answer. [2 marks]
2. Give one criticism of Zimbardo's Stanford Prison Experiment. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 conformity to the wrong answer dropped sharply; B1 this was because the dissenting confederate gave the participant social support/broke the group's unanimous agreement.
2. [2] B1 identifies a valid criticism (e.g. lack of experimental control/Zimbardo's dual role, demand characteristics, ethical issues -- psychological harm/limited informed consent); B1 briefly explains why this is a weakness.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
