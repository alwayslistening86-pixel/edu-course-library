# S44_Evolutionary_Explanations_and_Attraction - Practice: Evolutionary explanations and factors affecting attraction

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain what is meant by anisogamy, and how it relates to evolutionary explanations of partner preferences. [3 marks]
2. Outline social penetration theory's account of self-disclosure in relationship development. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 anisogamy is the fundamental difference in size and number of male (small, numerous) versus female (larger, far fewer) gametes; B1 combined with women's greater minimum biological investment in offspring; B1 this is argued to have shaped different evolutionary mating strategies/preferences for men and women (e.g. women being more selective, men prioritising indicators of fertility).
2. [2] B1 as a relationship develops, partners reveal increasingly broad and deep (more intimate) self-disclosure to each other; B1 this is a reciprocal process, and appropriately-timed disclosure increases intimacy/attraction.
