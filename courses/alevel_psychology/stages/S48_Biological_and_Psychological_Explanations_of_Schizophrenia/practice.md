# S48_Biological_and_Psychological_Explanations_of_Schizophrenia - Practice: Biological and psychological explanations of schizophrenia

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give the approximate concordance rate for schizophrenia found in Gottesman's research for MZ twins and for DZ twins, and state what this suggests. [3 marks]
2. Outline Bateson's double bind theory of schizophrenia. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 MZ (identical) twins: approximately 48%; B1 DZ (non-identical) twins: approximately 17%; B1 the much higher MZ than DZ concordance suggests a substantial genetic contribution to schizophrenia, though concordance well below 100% shows genetic factors alone cannot fully account for it.
2. [2] B1 a child who repeatedly receives contradictory messages from a parent (e.g. verbal affection alongside non-verbal rejection) may develop a confused, internally contradictory understanding of reality/relationships; B1 reflected later in disorganised thinking/behaviour resembling schizophrenic symptoms.

One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
