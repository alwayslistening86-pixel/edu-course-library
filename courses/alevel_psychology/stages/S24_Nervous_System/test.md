## Test items
1. Outline the two main divisions of the nervous system, and the subdivisions of each. [6 marks]
2. A rapid, automatic withdrawal of the hand from a hot surface, controlled by the spinal cord without conscious brain processing, is an example of: Choose every correct option.
   A. a spinal reflex
   B. sympathetic arousal
   C. operant conditioning
   D. the parasympathetic response

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 central nervous system (CNS): brain and spinal cord; B1 peripheral nervous system (PNS): connects CNS to the rest of the body; B1 PNS divides into the somatic nervous system (voluntary muscle movement, sensory information) and the autonomic nervous system (involuntary organ/gland function); B1 the autonomic nervous system further divides into the sympathetic nervous system (dominant in stress/arousal, 'fight-or-flight') and the parasympathetic nervous system (dominant at rest, 'rest and digest'); B1 correctly notes the spinal cord's role in reflexes; B1 correct example of sympathetic/parasympathetic opposing effects (e.g. heart rate).
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
