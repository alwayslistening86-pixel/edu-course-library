# S24_Nervous_System - Practice: The nervous system

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between the somatic and autonomic nervous systems. [2 marks]
2. Give one function of the parasympathetic nervous system. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 somatic nervous system: governs voluntary, conscious muscle movement and carries sensory information to the CNS; B1 autonomic nervous system: governs involuntary, automatic functioning of internal organs and glands.
2. [1] B1 returns the body to its resting state after arousal (e.g. reduces heart rate) / 'rest and digest' function, briefly and accurately described.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
