## Test items
1. Ten participants complete a memory test, scoring the following number of words recalled out of a possible 10: 4, 9, 6, 9, 5, 7, 8, 9, 3, 10. Calculate the mean and the median, showing your working, and identify the mode. [4 marks]
2. The standard deviation of the same data set (mean 7) is approximately 2.40. Explain what this standard deviation tells us about the spread of scores, and give one strength of using standard deviation rather than the range to describe this spread. [3 marks]
3. Describe the shape of a positively skewed distribution, and explain the relative positions of the mean, median and mode within it. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 mean: (4+9+6+9+5+7+8+9+3+10)/10 = 70/10; A1 mean = 7; M1 median: sort the scores (3,4,5,6,7,8,9,9,9,10) and average the two middle values (7+8)/2; A1 median = 7.5, and the mode is 9 (the most frequently occurring score).
2. [3] B1 a standard deviation of about 2.40 shows scores are, on average, spread about 2.40 points away from the mean of 7, i.e. a moderate spread rather than all scores being tightly clustered; B1 correctly notes this reflects the whole data set's spread, not just the two most extreme values; B1 strength: standard deviation uses every value in the data set, giving a more precise, sensitive measure of spread than the range, which uses only the highest and lowest values.
3. [3] B1 most values cluster toward the lower end of the distribution, with a 'tail' of extreme high values stretching to the right; B1 the mean is pulled up by this tail, so the mean is higher than the median; B1 the median is in turn higher than the mode, giving the order mode < median < mean.
