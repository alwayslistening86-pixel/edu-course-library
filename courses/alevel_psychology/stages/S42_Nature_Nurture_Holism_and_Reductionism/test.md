## Test items
1. Outline the four levels of explanation in psychology, from most holistic to most reductionist, with an example of each. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by epigenetics, and how this concept relates to the nature-nurture debate. [3 marks]
3. [9-mark style] Discuss holism and reductionism as explanations of behaviour. [9 marks]
