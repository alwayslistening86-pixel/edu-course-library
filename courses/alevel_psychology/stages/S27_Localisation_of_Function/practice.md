# S27_Localisation_of_Function - Practice: Localisation of function and hemispheric lateralisation

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name the brain area associated with speech production, and describe one effect of damage to it. [2 marks]
2. Outline what Sperry's split-brain research found when an object was shown only to a patient's left visual field. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 Broca's area; B1 damage produces Broca's aphasia -- slow, non-fluent, laboured speech, though comprehension is largely intact.
2. [2] B1 the patient could not verbally name the object (since it was processed only by the right hemisphere, which in most people lacks speech capacity); B1 but could often still select/point to it correctly with the left hand.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
