## Test items
1. Outline what happens in the intra-psychic and dyadic phases of Duck's model of relationship breakdown. [4 marks]
2. Outline the absorption-addiction model of parasocial relationships, describing its three levels. [4 marks]
3. In the reduced cues theory of virtual relationships, the reduction of non-verbal cues is argued to lead to: Choose every correct option.
   A. more blunt, deindividuated and disinhibited communication
   B. more intense, rapid intimacy than face-to-face relationships
   C. the complete absence of self-disclosure
   D. an increase in physical attractiveness cues

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 intra-psychic phase: begins once a partner privately reaches a threshold of dissatisfaction ('I can't stand this any more'), reflecting privately without voicing concerns to the partner; B1 weighing the pros/cons of staying versus leaving; B1 dyadic phase: begins once the threshold 'I would be justified in withdrawing' is reached, and the dissatisfied partner confronts their partner directly; B1 discussing the relationship together, which may lead to repair attempts or a mutual recognition the relationship cannot be saved.
2. [4] B1 entertainment-social level: mild, common involvement, following a celebrity as a source of enjoyable social conversation; B1 intense-personal level: more obsessive, personal involvement, e.g. seeing the celebrity as a soulmate/intimate friend; B1 borderline-pathological level: extreme, potentially uncontrollable behaviours; B1 correctly notes the levels reflect a progressively increasing degree of psychological dysfunction.
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
