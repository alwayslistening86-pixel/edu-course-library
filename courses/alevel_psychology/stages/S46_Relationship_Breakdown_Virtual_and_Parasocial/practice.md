# S46_Relationship_Breakdown_Virtual_and_Parasocial - Practice: Relationship breakdown, virtual relationships and parasocial relationships

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name Duck's four original phases of relationship breakdown, in order. [4 marks]
2. Distinguish between the reduced cues theory and the hyperpersonal model of virtual relationships. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B1 B1 B1 B1 each for intra-psychic phase, dyadic phase, social phase, grave-dressing phase, in the correct order.
2. [2] B1 reduced cues theory: CMC lacks non-verbal cues present face-to-face, leading to more blunt, deindividuated communication and less intimacy; B1 hyperpersonal model: CMC can allow more intense, rapid intimacy, partly through selective self-presentation and idealisation of the other person.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
