## Test items
1. Outline the process of classical conditioning, referring to Pavlov's research. [4 marks]
2. Give one strength and one limitation of the behaviourist approach. [4 marks]
3. In Skinner's operant conditioning research, an electric shock that stops when a rat presses a lever is an example of: Choose every correct option.
   A. negative reinforcement
   B. positive reinforcement
   C. punishment
   D. classical conditioning

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 an unconditioned stimulus naturally produces an unconditioned response (e.g. food producing salivation); B1 a neutral stimulus is repeatedly paired with the unconditioned stimulus (e.g. a bell paired with food); B1 the neutral stimulus becomes a conditioned stimulus producing a conditioned response alone (e.g. salivating at the bell); B1 correctly attributed to Pavlov's research with dogs.
2. [4] B2 strength: e.g. use of controlled laboratory experiments gives high internal validity/scientific credibility, or useful real-world application (e.g. systematic desensitisation, token economies), explained; B2 limitation: e.g. environmental determinism, largely ignoring free will/cognitive processes, or limited generalisability of animal research to complex human behaviour, explained.
