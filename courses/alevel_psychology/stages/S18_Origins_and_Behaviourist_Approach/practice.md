# S18_Origins_and_Behaviourist_Approach - Practice: The origins of psychology and the behaviourist approach

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Define introspection, and name the psychologist most associated with it. [2 marks]
2. Distinguish between positive reinforcement and negative reinforcement. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 introspection: systematically analysing/recording one's own conscious mental processes as they occur; B1 correctly attributed to Wundt.
2. [2] B1 positive reinforcement: receiving a desirable consequence for a behaviour, making it more likely to recur; B1 negative reinforcement: a behaviour that removes/avoids an unpleasant consequence, also making it more likely to recur.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
