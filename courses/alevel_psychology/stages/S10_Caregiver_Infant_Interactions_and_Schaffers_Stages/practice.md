# S10_Caregiver_Infant_Interactions_and_Schaffers_Stages - Practice: Caregiver-infant interactions, Schaffer's stages, and the father's role

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between reciprocity and interactional synchrony. [2 marks]
2. Name Schaffer's four stages of attachment development, in order. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 reciprocity: a turn-taking pattern where caregiver and infant respond to each other's signals; B1 interactional synchrony: caregiver and infant mirror/coordinate each other's actions and emotions simultaneously.
2. [4] B1 B1 B1 B1 each for asocial stage, indiscriminate attachment stage, specific attachment stage, multiple attachments stage, in the correct order.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
