# S14_Definitions_of_Abnormality - Practice: Definitions of abnormality

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name the four definitions of abnormality specified by AQA. [4 marks]
2. Give one limitation of using deviation from social norms as a definition of abnormality. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B1 B1 B1 B1 each for statistical infrequency, deviation from social norms, failure to function adequately, deviation from ideal mental health.
2. [2] B1 social norms vary across cultures and change over historical time; B1 correctly explained, e.g. risk of pathologising behaviour that simply differs from the majority, or historical/cultural bias in what counted as 'deviant'.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
