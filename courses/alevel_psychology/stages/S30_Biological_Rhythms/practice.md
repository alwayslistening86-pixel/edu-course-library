# S30_Biological_Rhythms - Practice: Biological rhythms

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between an infradian and an ultradian rhythm, giving one example of each. [4 marks]
2. Name the main endogenous pacemaker for the circadian sleep-wake cycle, and state its location. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 infradian rhythm: lasts longer than 24 hours, e.g. the menstrual cycle (or SAD); B2 ultradian rhythm: lasts less than 24 hours, recurring more than once a day, e.g. the cycle of sleep stages.
2. [2] B1 the suprachiasmatic nucleus (SCN); B1 located in the hypothalamus.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
