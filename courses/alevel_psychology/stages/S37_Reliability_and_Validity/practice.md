# S37_Reliability_and_Validity - Practice: Reliability and validity

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain what is meant by test-retest reliability. [2 marks]
2. Distinguish between face validity and concurrent validity. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 giving the same test/measure to the same participants on two separate occasions; B1 comparing/correlating the two sets of scores -- a strong positive correlation indicates good reliability.
2. [2] B1 face validity: a subjective, 'at a glance' judgement of whether a measure appears to measure what it claims to; B1 concurrent validity: comparing a new measure's scores against an existing, already validated measure of the same construct, taken at around the same time.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
