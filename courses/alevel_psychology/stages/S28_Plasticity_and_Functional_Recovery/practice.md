# S28_Plasticity_and_Functional_Recovery - Practice: Plasticity and functional recovery

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Define synaptic pruning. [2 marks]
2. Give one mechanism of functional recovery after brain trauma. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 the process by which rarely-used synaptic connections are weakened and eventually eliminated; B1 while connections used more often are strengthened, refining the brain's neural structure.
2. [2] B1 names a mechanism (axonal sprouting, denervation supersensitivity, or recruitment of homologous areas); B1 briefly and accurately explains it.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
