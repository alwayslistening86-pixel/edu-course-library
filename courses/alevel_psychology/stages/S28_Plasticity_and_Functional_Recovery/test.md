## Test items
1. Describe Maguire et al.'s research into the brains of London taxi drivers, and explain what it shows about plasticity. [4 marks]
2. [8-mark style] Discuss factors affecting functional recovery of the brain after trauma. [8 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 used MRI scans to compare taxi drivers, who must learn London's complex street layout, with a matched control group; B1 taxi drivers had significantly larger posterior hippocampi (associated with spatial navigation/memory); B1 hippocampal volume correlated positively with time spent as a taxi driver; B1 shows extensive, sustained learning can produce structural (physical) changes in the brain, i.e. plasticity.
