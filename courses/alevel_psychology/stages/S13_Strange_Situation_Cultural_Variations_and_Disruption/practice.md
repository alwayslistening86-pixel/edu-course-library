# S13_Strange_Situation_Cultural_Variations_and_Disruption - Practice: The Strange Situation, cultural variations, disruption of attachment, and later relationships

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give the approximate proportion of infants classified as securely attached in Western samples, and outline their typical reunion behaviour. [2 marks]
2. Outline what Van Ijzendoorn and Kroonenberg's meta-analysis found about variation within versus between cultures. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 approximately 60-75%; B1 typical reunion behaviour: easily comforted/settled at reunion with the caregiver.
2. [2] B1 variation in attachment type within a single country/culture was greater than variation between different countries/cultures; B1 suggesting factors other than broad national culture (e.g. social class, specific childcare practices) strongly influence attachment type.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
