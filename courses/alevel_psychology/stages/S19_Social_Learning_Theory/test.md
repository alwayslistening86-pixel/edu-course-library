2. Describe the procedure and findings of Bandura, Ross and Ross's Bobo doll study. [6 marks]
3. [16-mark style] Discuss social learning theory as an explanation of human behaviour. [16 marks]

[3] B1 an observer is more likely to imitate a model's behaviour if they see the model being reinforced/rewarded for it (and less likely if punished); B1 learning occurs through observing the consequences experienced by someone else, not through the observer's own direct reinforcement; B1 correctly cites Bandura's finding that children who saw the aggressive model rewarded imitated more aggression than those who saw the model punished.
