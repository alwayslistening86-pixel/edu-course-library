## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 the mind is compared to a computer, treated as an information processor; B1 in terms of input, storage/coding and output processes; B1 this influenced the development of computer models used to test hypotheses about human cognitive processing.

[8] Mark out of 8 using AQA's levels of response (AO1 knowledge + AO3 discussion/evaluation): Level 4 (7-8) knowledge is accurate and reasonably detailed; discussion/evaluation is effective; answer is clear and coherent.
