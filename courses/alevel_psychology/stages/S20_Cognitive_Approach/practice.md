# S20_Cognitive_Approach - Practice: The cognitive approach

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Define what is meant by a 'schema' in the cognitive approach. [2 marks]
2. Outline what is meant by cognitive neuroscience. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 a package/cluster of ideas and information developed through experience; B1 acting as a mental framework that helps organise and interpret incoming information.
2. [2] B1 the field combining cognitive and biological approaches, studying the neurological/biological structures underpinning cognitive processes; B1 made possible by modern brain-scanning technology.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
