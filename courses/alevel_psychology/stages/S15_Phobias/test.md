## Test items
1. Explain how operant conditioning maintains a phobia once it has been acquired, according to the two-process model. [4 marks]
2. Describe the procedure of systematic desensitisation. [4 marks]
3. [16-mark style] Discuss the behavioural approach to treating phobias, referring to systematic desensitisation and/or flooding. [16 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 avoiding the phobic stimulus reduces/removes the anxiety the person would otherwise feel; B1 this reduction in an unpleasant feeling is negatively reinforcing; B1 the avoidance behaviour, and so the phobia, is therefore strengthened/persists; B1 the person never gets the opportunity to learn the stimulus is not actually as dangerous as feared.
2. [4] B1 the client is taught relaxation techniques; B1 an anxiety hierarchy is constructed, ranking feared situations from least to most frightening; B1 the client is gradually exposed to each step while remaining relaxed, based on counterconditioning/reciprocal inhibition (relaxation and fear cannot coexist); B1 progression to the next step only occurs once the client is relaxed at the current one, continuing until the phobic stimulus itself no longer produces anxiety.
3. [16] Mark out of 16 using AQA's levels of response for a 16-mark 'Discuss ...' essay (AO1 knowledge + AO3 discussion/evaluation, no fixed AO1/AO3 split): Level 4 (13-16) knowledge is accurate and generally well detailed; discussion/evaluation is thorough and effective; the answer is clear, coherent, focused and well structured, using appropriate psychological terminology throughout. Level 3 (9-12) knowledge is evident but there are some inaccuracies/a lack of detail; discussion/evaluation is mostly effective and shows some depth; the answer is mostly clear and organised, with generally appropriate terminology. Level 2 (5-8) knowledge is limited, and mostly focused on description; discussion/evaluation is limited, superficial or lacks focus; the answer lacks a logical structure, with some use of psychological terminology. Level 1 (1-4) knowledge is very limited or muddled; evaluation/discussion, if present at all, is barely discernible or inappropriate; the answer lacks any clear structure; terminology is used inaccurately or not at all. 0 marks: no relevant content. Credit accurate description of SD (relaxation training, anxiety hierarchy, counterconditioning/reciprocal inhibition) and/or flooding (prolonged exposure, extinction), and discussion including research support for effectiveness, SD's better tolerability versus flooding's speed but higher unpleasantness/dropout risk and ethical/consent considerations, limited effectiveness for phobias without a clear behavioural component, and the possibility of symptom substitution.
