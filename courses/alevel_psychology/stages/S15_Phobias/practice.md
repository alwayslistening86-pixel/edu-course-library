# S15_Phobias - Practice: Phobias

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give one behavioural characteristic of a phobia. [1 mark]
2. Outline how a phobia would be acquired through classical conditioning. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 any one of panic, avoidance, endurance, briefly and accurately described.
2. [3] B1 a neutral stimulus is paired with a frightening/traumatic unconditioned stimulus that naturally produces an unconditioned fear response; B1 through this association, the previously neutral stimulus becomes a conditioned stimulus; B1 which now alone produces a conditioned fear response, i.e. the phobia.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
