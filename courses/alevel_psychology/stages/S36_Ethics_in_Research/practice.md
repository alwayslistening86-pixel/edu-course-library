# S36_Ethics_in_Research - Practice: Ethics in research

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain what is meant by 'the right to withdraw'. [2 marks]
2. Give one alternative to full informed consent, and explain when it might be used. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 participants can leave the study at any point, and withdraw their data even retrospectively after taking part; B1 without needing to give a reason and without penalty.
2. [2] B1 presumptive consent (asking a similar group of people, not the actual participants, whether they would find the study acceptable), or parental/guardian consent for children; B1 used when full informed consent is not possible, e.g. because deception is needed or participants are too young.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
