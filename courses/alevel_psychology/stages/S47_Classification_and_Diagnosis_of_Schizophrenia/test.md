## Test items
1. Explain how culture bias can affect the reliability and validity of a schizophrenia diagnosis. [4 marks]
2. [8-mark style] Discuss issues surrounding the reliability and validity of diagnosing schizophrenia. [8 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 rates of schizophrenia diagnosis vary substantially by ethnicity within the same country; B1 this may reflect genuine cultural differences in symptom expression being misinterpreted by clinicians from a different cultural background, rather than a genuine difference in the underlying rate of the disorder; B1 accurate example (e.g. culturally normal reports of hearing ancestral voices being misread as a hallucination); B1 this reduces both reliability (clinicians from different cultural backgrounds may reasonably disagree) and validity (raises doubt over whether diagnosis accurately identifies the real underlying condition).

Credit accurate knowledge of co-morbidity, symptom overlap, culture bias and gender bias, and how each affects reliability (consistency of diagnosis) and/or validity (whether the diagnosis identifies a genuine, meaningful condition); discussion should reach a reasoned view on how seriously these issues undermine confidence in schizophrenia diagnosis, and/or note improvements from more standardised, detailed diagnostic criteria over time.
