# S32_Other_Research_Methods - Practice: Other research methods: observation, self-report, case studies, content analysis

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between event sampling and time sampling in observational research. [2 marks]
2. Give one limitation of an unstructured interview compared with a structured interview. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 event sampling: recording every instance of a specific pre-defined behaviour, whenever it occurs; B1 time sampling: recording behaviour only at set, regular time intervals.
2. [2] B1 identifies a valid limitation (e.g. harder to replicate/compare across participants, or requires more interviewer skill, or takes longer to analyse); B1 briefly and accurately explained relative to a structured interview's fixed question set.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
