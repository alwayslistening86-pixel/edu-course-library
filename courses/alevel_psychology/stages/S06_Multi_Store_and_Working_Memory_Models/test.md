## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 three separate, linear stores: sensory register, STM and LTM; B1 STM coding mainly acoustic; B1 LTM coding mainly semantic; B1 STM limited capacity (7+/-2) and limited duration (about 18 seconds without rehearsal); B1 LTM potentially unlimited capacity and duration; B1 rehearsal transfers information from STM to LTM.
2. [3] B1 HM lost the ability to form new long-term memories following brain surgery (anterograde amnesia); B1 but retained a normal short-term memory; B1 suggesting STM and LTM are genuinely separate stores, as the multi-store model proposes.
