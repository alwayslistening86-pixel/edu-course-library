# S25_Neurons_and_Synaptic_Transmission - Practice: Neurons and synaptic transmission

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give one structural or functional difference between sensory and motor neurons. [2 marks]
2. Explain the role of the myelin sheath. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 identifies a valid difference (e.g. sensory neurons carry impulses from receptors to the CNS with long dendrites/short axons, motor neurons carry impulses from the CNS to effectors with short dendrites/long axons, or direction of travel); B1 accurately and clearly explained.
2. [2] B1 a fatty covering on the axon that insulates it; B1 speeding up transmission of the electrical impulse, with the nodes of Ranvier allowing the impulse to 'jump' along the axon.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
