# S35_Variables_Control_and_Demand_Characteristics - Practice: Variables, control, demand characteristics and pilot studies

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between an extraneous variable and a confounding variable. [2 marks]
2. Explain what a pilot study is and why researchers conduct one. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 extraneous variable: any variable other than the IV that could affect the DV, but does not necessarily differ systematically between conditions; B1 confounding variable: an extraneous variable that varies systematically alongside the IV, making it impossible to know whether the IV or the confound caused any observed difference.
2. [2] B1 a small-scale trial run of an investigation conducted before the main study; B1 to test and refine the procedure/materials/instructions in advance, identifying and fixing potential problems before committing resources to the full study.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
