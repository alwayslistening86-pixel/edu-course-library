## Test items
1. Explain how the theory of evolution by natural selection is used by the biological approach to explain behaviour. [3 marks]
2. Evaluate the biological approach to psychology. [6 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 a genetically determined behaviour that enhances survival and reproductive success will be naturally selected and passed to future generations; B1 the biological approach therefore explains some modern human behaviours as adaptations that increased ancestral humans' reproductive success; B1 accurate example (e.g. attachment, aggression, or mate preferences).
2. [6] Mark out of 6 using AQA's levels of response (typically an 'outline and evaluate' question, AO1 + AO3): Level 3 (5-6) accurate and organised knowledge, with effective, coherent evaluation; Level 2 (3-4) knowledge is evident but not always accurate/organised, evaluation is present but not fully effective; Level 1 (1-2) knowledge is basic/muddled, evaluation is weak or missing. 0 marks: no relevant content. Credit points such as: use of objective, scientific methods (brain scanning, genetic analysis) giving strong scientific credibility; effective biological treatments developed from this approach (e.g. drug therapy); the criticism of biological determinism, underplaying free will/cognitive/environmental influence; and the limitation that twin studies cannot fully separate genetic from shared-environment effects.
