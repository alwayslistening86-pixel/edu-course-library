# S21_Biological_Approach - Practice: The biological approach

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between genotype and phenotype. [2 marks]
2. Explain what a higher concordance rate for a trait in MZ twins compared with DZ twins is taken to suggest. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 genotype: an individual's actual genetic makeup/genetic code; B1 phenotype: the observable characteristics resulting from the interaction of genotype and environment.
2. [2] B1 suggests a genetic contribution to the trait; B1 because a purely environmental cause would be expected to produce similar concordance regardless of genetic relatedness.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
