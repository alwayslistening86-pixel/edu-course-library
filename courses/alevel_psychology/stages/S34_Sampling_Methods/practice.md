# S34_Sampling_Methods - Practice: Sampling methods

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain what is meant by a target population and a sample. [2 marks]
2. Give one strength and one limitation of stratified sampling. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 target population: the entire group of people a researcher is interested in and wishes to generalise findings to; B1 sample: the smaller group actually selected/tested, intended to represent that population.
2. [2] B1 strength: designed to produce a sample proportionally reflecting the target population's composition on the chosen characteristics, generally good for generalisability; B1 limitation: the chosen strata may not capture every characteristic relevant to the research question, and it is more time-consuming to organise.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
