# S51_Social_Psychological_Explanations_of_Aggression - Practice: Social psychological explanations of aggression

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain what is meant by 'displacement' in the frustration-aggression hypothesis. [2 marks]
2. Give two mechanisms proposed to produce deindividuation. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 since the true source of frustration is often too powerful/unavailable to attack directly; B1 aggression may instead be redirected toward a safer, more available substitute target.
2. [4] B2 B2 any two of: reduced private self-awareness (less internal reflection on personal values/standards), reduced public self-awareness (feeling anonymous/unidentifiable to others), resulting shift toward following the group's immediate situational norms, each briefly and accurately explained.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
