1. Explain how the frustration-aggression hypothesis was revised by Berkowitz to address criticism of its original strong version. [4 marks]
2. [8-mark style] Discuss social learning theory as an explanation of human aggression. [8 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 the original hypothesis proposed frustration always inevitably produces aggression, and aggression is always caused by frustration; B1 Berkowitz's revision proposed frustration instead produces a readiness/drive for aggression, not aggression itself directly; B1 whether this readiness is actually expressed depends on situational factors, including the presence of aggressive cues (e.g. the sight of a weapon, the 'weapons effect'); B1 acknowledging aggression can also occur without frustration, and frustration does not always lead to aggression.
