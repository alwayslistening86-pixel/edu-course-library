## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 male volunteers believed they were in a study on punishment and learning, rigged to become the 'teacher'; B1 instructed by an experimenter in a lab coat to give a confederate 'learner' increasingly severe (fake) electric shocks for wrong answers, 15V-450V; B1 standardised verbal prods used if the teacher hesitated (e.g. 'the experiment requires that you continue'); B1 accurate reference to the outcome (65% continued to 450V) or to at least one situational variable tested.
2. [4] B1 the agentic state is when a person sees themselves as the agent of another's wishes, no longer feeling personally responsible for their actions; B1 contrasted with the autonomous state, where a person feels personally responsible, guided by their own conscience; B1 the shift between the two is called agentic shift; B1 binding factors (e.g. not wanting to disrupt the procedure) keep a person in the agentic state despite distress.
