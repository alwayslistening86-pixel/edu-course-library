# S03_Explanations_for_Obedience - Practice: Milgram's research and situational explanations for obedience

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. What percentage of participants in Milgram's original study continued to give shocks up to the maximum 450V? [1 mark]
2. Explain why obedience fell when Milgram moved the study from Yale University to a run-down office building. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 65%.
2. [2] B1 the less prestigious location reduced the perceived legitimacy of the authority figure/study; B1 correctly links this to the 'location' variable and reduced obedience (to 47.5%).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
