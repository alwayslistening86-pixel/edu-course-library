# S23_Humanistic_Approach_and_Comparison - Practice: The humanistic approach and comparison of approaches

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline what is meant by 'self-actualisation' in the humanistic approach. [2 marks]
2. Distinguish between conditions of worth and unconditional positive regard. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 the innate desire/motivation to achieve one's full personal potential; B1 representing the highest level of psychological growth, at the top of Maslow's hierarchy of needs.
2. [2] B1 conditions of worth: love/approval given only conditionally, when a child meets certain expectations; B1 unconditional positive regard: acceptance and support given regardless of what a person says or does.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
