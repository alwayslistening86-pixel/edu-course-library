# S41_Gender_Culture_Free_Will_and_Determinism - Practice: Gender bias, cultural bias, and free will versus determinism

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between alpha bias and beta bias, with an example of each. [4 marks]
2. Explain the difference between hard determinism and soft determinism. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 alpha bias: exaggerates/overstates differences between men and women, e.g. some evolutionary explanations of relationships overstating fixed, innate sex differences; B2 beta bias: ignores/minimises real differences between men and women, e.g. early fight-or-flight research using predominantly male samples but generalising findings to everyone.
2. [2] B1 hard determinism: all behaviour is entirely caused by internal/external forces beyond the individual's control, so free will is an illusion; B1 soft determinism: behaviour is generally caused by internal/external factors, but the individual retains some meaningful degree of conscious control/choice.
