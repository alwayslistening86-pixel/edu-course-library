# Accounting & Finance (BSc Hons) — Open University module-framework structure, QAA Accounting Benchmark graded - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
A single comprehensive paper drawing questions from every stage, weighted roughly evenly across the three blocks (intermediate financial reporting/law; financial management/management accounting foundations; advanced financial accounting/governance-audit/advanced financial management-management accounting/taxation), plus one extended integrative professional-report question. Questions are original; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage tests. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 5 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. A machine costs £60,000, has a residual value of £10,000, and a 5-year useful life, depreciated straight-line. After 2 years, it is revalued to £48,000. Calculate the revaluation surplus recognised, and state where it is recognised. [5 marks]
2. Parent acquires 80% of Subsidiary for £640,000; Subsidiary's identifiable net assets at acquisition (fair value) are £700,000. Using the proportionate NCI method, calculate goodwill. [3 marks]
3. Initial investment £250,000; cash inflows £80,000 a year for 4 years; cost of capital 9%. Calculate the NPV and state whether the project should be accepted. [5 marks]
4. A company's taxable total profits are £180,000 for 2026/27. Calculate its corporation tax liability, showing the marginal relief calculation. [4 marks]
5. [Integrated case, extended answer] A UK trading company is deciding whether to lease or buy a new machine under IFRS 16, and separately whether an ongoing legal claim against it requires a provision under IAS 37. The finance director wants to keep both off the statement of financial position to protect the company's gearing covenant ahead of a bank refinancing. Write a short report: (a) explain how IFRS 16 requires the lease to be treated regardless of the FD's preference; (b) explain the IAS 37 recognition criteria that determine whether the legal claim needs a provision; (c) identify the governance/ethical concern raised by the FD's stated motivation. [20 marks]

## Answer key for the ready-made items (tutor only)
1. [5] M1 annual depreciation = (60,000-10,000)/5 = £10,000; M1 carrying amount after 2 years = 60,000 - (2x10,000) = £40,000; A1 revaluation surplus = 48,000-40,000 = £8,000; B1 recognised in other comprehensive income; B1 not in profit or loss, unless reversing a previous downward revaluation charged there.
2. [3] M1 NCI at acquisition = 20% x 700,000 = £140,000; M1 goodwill = 640,000 + 140,000 - 700,000; A1 = £80,000.
3. [5] M1 annuity factor = (1-1.09^-4)/0.09 = 3.2397; M1 PV of inflows = 80,000 x 3.2397 = £259,178; A1 NPV = 259,178-250,000 = £9,178; A1 positive; B1 accept, since NPV > 0 at the company's cost of capital.
4. [4] M1 tax before relief = 180,000 x 25% = £45,000; M1 marginal relief = 3/200 x (250,000-180,000) = £1,050; A1 corporation tax payable = 45,000-1,050 = £43,950; B1 correct reasoning that profits fall between the small profits and main rate thresholds, so marginal relief applies.
5. [20] Mark out of 20 using this course's undergraduate accounting/finance-report levels (adapted from the QAA Accounting benchmark's description of graduate analytical and professional-judgement skill): Level 1 (1-5) largely descriptive, states relevant rules/figures but does not apply them to the specific facts; Level 2 (6-10) applies the relevant technical material with some development, but limited integration across topics or limited critical judgement; Level 3 (11-15) a well-structured response that correctly applies technical material to the specific facts, integrates more than one topic area, and reaches a supported conclusion or recommendation; Level 4 (16-20) a sophisticated, professional-standard response that applies technical material accurately and completely, exercises real professional judgement on ambiguous or contestable points, and communicates a clear, well-reasoned recommendation as a qualified accountant/finance professional would to a client or board. Indicative technical content: (a) under IFRS 16, with limited exceptions (short-term/low-value asset leases), a lessee must recognise a right-of-use asset and a lease liability regardless of preference -- 'keeping it off the balance sheet' is not an available accounting policy choice for a lease within IFRS 16's scope; (b) a provision is recognised only where there is a present obligation from a past event, an outflow is probable, and the amount can be estimated reliably -- if any of these is not met (e.g. outflow is only possible, not probable), it is a contingent liability disclosed in the notes rather than a recognised provision, and the accounting answer must follow the actual facts/legal advice, not the FD's preferred financial statement outcome; (c) the FD's stated motivation (deliberately structuring/interpreting accounting treatment to protect a covenant ahead of a bank refinancing) is a self-interest/management-bias threat to the fundamental principles of integrity and objectivity, and is precisely the kind of override risk external audit, a strong audit committee and professional scepticism exist to catch -- accounting treatment must follow the applicable standards' actual requirements and the genuine facts, not be reverse-engineered from a desired financial outcome.

## Grading
Mark short and calculation answers against the mark schemes; mark the extended integrative answer by levels (technical accuracy and correct application to the specific facts; integration across more than one technical area; professional judgement and a reasoned, appropriately qualified recommendation). A pass needs at least 60% of the marks overall and a genuine attempt at every block. Report the total, the percentage per block and the weakest topic areas.

## Outcome
- **Pass:** record `exam_status: "passed"`. The course is complete.
- **Not yet:** leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the weakest topic areas, offer targeted review, and retry with fresh questions.
