# S06_Management_Accounting_I_Costing_and_Budgeting - Practice: Management accounting I: costing and budgeting

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Fixed production overhead £80,000, budgeted output 4,000 units. Production is 4,200 units, sales are 4,000 units. State whether absorption or marginal costing reports higher profit, and by how much. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 absorption costing reports the higher profit, by 200 units x (80,000/4,000) = £4,000; B1 correct reasoning: the fixed overhead carried forward in the 200 units of closing inventory under absorption costing is expensed in full under marginal costing.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
