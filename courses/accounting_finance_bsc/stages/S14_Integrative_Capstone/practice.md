# S14_Integrative_Capstone - Practice: Integrative capstone

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name the four broad technical areas of this degree that an integrated professional scenario is most likely to draw on. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 any three of: financial reporting/financial accounting, audit and governance, financial management, management accounting, taxation; B1 explanation that a real business decision typically touches more than one of these areas at once, which is why they need to be considered together rather than in isolation.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
