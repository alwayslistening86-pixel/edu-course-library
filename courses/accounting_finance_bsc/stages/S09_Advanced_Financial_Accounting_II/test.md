# S09_Advanced_Financial_Accounting_II - Test: Advanced financial accounting II

Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Parent owns 40% of Associate C, investment initially £200,000. Associate's profit for the year is £60,000; Associate pays total dividends of £15,000. Calculate the closing investment carrying amount, and state where the parent's share of the associate's profit is recognised. [4 marks]
2. A foreign subsidiary's net assets are $1,500,000 at the year end (closing rate $1.30/£) and its profit for the year is $300,000 (average rate $1.25/£). Translate both figures into sterling. [3 marks]
3. A bond is issued for proceeds of £90,000 against a £100,000 nominal value, coupon 4% (£4,000 a year), effective interest rate 6%. Calculate the year 1 finance cost, the amount added to the carrying amount, and the closing carrying amount. [4 marks]
4. Profit attributable to ordinary shareholders is £450,000; weighted average shares in issue are 1,500,000. Calculate basic EPS. Share options, if exercised, would add 100,000 shares for no change in profit. Calculate diluted EPS. [4 marks]
5. Which financial asset would normally be measured at amortised cost under IFRS 9? Choose every correct option.
   A. A government bond held to collect its contractual coupon and principal payments, with SPPI cash flows
   B. A trading investment in listed equity shares bought to sell for a short-term profit
   C. An equity investment with no fixed contractual cash flows
   D. A bond held to collect contractual cash flows that are solely payments of principal and interest

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 share of profit = 40% x 60,000 = £24,000; M1 share of dividend = 40% x 15,000 = £6,000; A1 closing carrying amount = 200,000 + 24,000 - 6,000 = £218,000; B1 the parent's share of the associate's profit is recognised as investment income within the parent's own (consolidated) profit or loss, not merely disclosed in a note.
2. [3] M1 net assets translated at closing rate = 1,500,000/1.30 = £1,153,846; A1 profit translated at average rate = 300,000/1.25 = £240,000; B1 the resulting translation exchange difference is recognised in other comprehensive income, not profit or loss.
3. [4] M1 finance cost = 90,000 x 6% = £5,400; M1 amount added = 5,400 - 4,000 = £1,400; A1 closing carrying amount = 90,000 + 1,400 = £91,400; B1 the bond is measured at amortised cost because it is held to collect contractual cash flows that are solely payments of principal and interest (the SPPI test).
4. [4] M1 basic EPS = 450,000/1,500,000 = 30.0p; M1 diluted EPS = 450,000/(1,500,000+100,000); A1 = 28.12p; B1 diluted EPS is lower, correctly showing the dilutive effect of the options if exercised.
5. Correct: A, D (exactly these options, no others)
