# S04_Law_for_Accounting_and_Finance - Practice: Law for accounting and finance

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A supplier breaches a minor, non-essential term of a supply contract. Is the buyer entitled to terminate the contract, and what remedy is available? [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 no, a breach of a warranty (a minor/non-essential term) does not entitle the innocent party to terminate the contract; B1 the buyer's remedy is damages only, to compensate the loss actually caused by the breach.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
