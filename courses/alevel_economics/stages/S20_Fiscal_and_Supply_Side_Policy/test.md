# S20_Fiscal_and_Supply_Side_Policy - Test: Fiscal policy and supply-side policies

## Test items
1. Explain how automatic stabilisers reduce fluctuations in the economic cycle. [4 marks]
2. Distinguish between a cyclical and a structural budget deficit. [2 marks]
3. Evaluate the use of supply-side policies to reduce unemployment. [25 marks]
4. Explain what the Laffer curve suggests about the relationship between tax rates and tax revenue. [3 marks]
5. Explain what is meant by crowding out. [2 marks]
6. Which are market-based supply-side policies? Choose every correct option.
   A. reducing income tax rates
   B. increasing spending on training
   C. deregulation of markets
   D. privatisation

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 in a recession, tax revenue falls automatically and benefit spending rises; B1 supporting disposable income and AD; B1 in a boom, tax revenue rises and benefit spending falls; B1 restraining AD, without any policy decision.
2. [2] B1 cyclical: caused by the economic cycle (e.g. a recession lowers tax revenue); disappears in recovery; B1 structural: persists even at full employment/over the cycle.
3. [25] Level 5 (21-25): analysis of interventionist (education, training, reducing structural unemployment and immobility) and market-based policies (incentives from lower taxes and benefits, labour market flexibility), linked to LRAS and the natural rate; balanced evaluation (time lags, ineffective against cyclical unemployment, costs, equity, the type of unemployment) reaching a supported judgement. Level 4 (16-20): good analysis, some evaluation. Level 3 (11-15): some analysis, limited evaluation. Levels 1-2 (1-10): mainly knowledge, little analysis.
4. [3] B1 as rates rise from zero, revenue initially increases; B1 beyond a certain rate, higher rates reduce revenue; B1 because of disincentives to work/increased avoidance and evasion.
5. [2] B1 increased government borrowing raises interest rates (or uses up resources); B1 reducing private sector investment and consumption.
6. Correct: A, C, D (exactly these options, no others)
