## Test items
1. Explain the difference between normal and supernormal profit, and the significance of supernormal profit in a competitive market. [4 marks]
2. A firm faces the demand curve P = 80 - 2Q. Calculate TR at Q = 10, 20 and 30, and state the output that maximises total revenue. [4 marks]
3. Explain, with examples, what is meant by creative destruction. [3 marks]
4. Explain why a price-taking firm's average revenue equals its marginal revenue. [2 marks]
5. Explain two ways technological change can affect a firm's costs. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 normal profit: the minimum needed to keep resources in their current use (covers opportunity cost); B1 supernormal: profit in excess of normal; B1 in a competitive market it signals profitability and attracts new firms; B1 entry increases supply and competes the supernormal profit away.
2. [4] B1 TR(10) = 600; B1 TR(20) = 800; B1 TR(30) = 600; B1 maximum at Q = 20 (where MR = 80 - 4Q = 0).
3. [3] B1 innovation creates new products, firms or methods; B1 which replace and destroy existing ones; B1 e.g. streaming replacing DVD rental/digital cameras replacing film.
4. [2] B1 it can sell any quantity at the market price; B1 so each extra unit adds the same revenue as the price (AR = MR = P).
5. [2] B1 automation raises productivity, lowering average costs (LRAC shifts down); B1 but the initial investment raises fixed costs (or: new materials lower variable costs).
