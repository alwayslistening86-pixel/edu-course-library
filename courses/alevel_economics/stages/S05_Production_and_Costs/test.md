# S05_Production_and_Costs - Test: Production, specialisation, returns and costs

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: short calculations and explanations, a data-response or essay question, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below (levels-style for longer answers) and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A firm's total costs for 0-6 units of output are: 0 units £80, 1 units £110, 2 units £130, 3 units £145, 4 units £168, 5 units £200, 6 units £246. Calculate the marginal cost of the 4th unit, the average cost at 5 units, and the output at which average variable cost is lowest. [4 marks]
2. Explain the difference between diminishing returns and decreasing returns to scale. [4 marks]
3. Explain two internal economies of scale a large supermarket chain might gain. [4 marks]
4. Explain why a firm's short-run average cost curve is U-shaped. [3 marks]
5. Explain how specialisation and the division of labour increase labour productivity, and one disadvantage. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 MC of 4th unit = £23; B1 AC at 5 = £40; M1 AVC = (TC - 80)/Q: 30.0, 25.0, 21.7, 22.0, 24.0, 27.7; A1 lowest at 3 units.
2. [4] B1 diminishing returns is a short-run concept; B1 adding more of a variable factor to a fixed factor eventually reduces marginal product; B1 decreasing returns to scale is long-run; B1 increasing all factors in proportion raises output less than proportionately.
3. [4] B1 purchasing: bulk buying; B1 lower price per unit from suppliers; B1 financial: borrow more cheaply; B1 as banks see less risk (or technical, managerial, marketing with explanation).
4. [3] B1 initially AFC falls as fixed costs are spread over more output; B1 and marginal returns may be increasing; B1 eventually diminishing returns raise MC and AVC, so AC rises.
5. [3] B1 workers become more skilled at a single task; B1 no time lost switching tasks/use of specialised machinery; B1 disadvantage: boredom lowers motivation (or over-dependence, higher labour turnover).
