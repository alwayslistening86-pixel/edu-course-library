## Test items
1. A firm cuts the price of its product from £20 to £18 and sales rise from 1,000 to 1,150 a week. Calculate the PED and the change in total revenue, and explain the relationship. [4 marks]
2. Explain two factors that determine the price elasticity of demand for petrol. [4 marks]
3. Distinguish between a movement along a supply curve and a shift of the supply curve, giving a cause of each. [4 marks]
4. Explain why the price elasticity of supply of wheat is lower in the short run than in the long run. [3 marks]
5. A supermarket finds the YED for organic food is +1.8. Explain what this means and one implication for the supermarket. [3 marks]
6. If demand is price inelastic, which are true? Choose every correct option.
   A. a price rise increases total revenue
   B. the absolute value of PED is less than 1
   C. a price cut increases total revenue
   D. the percentage change in quantity is smaller than the percentage change in price

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 %ΔQ = +15%, %ΔP = -10%; A1 PED = -1.5; B1 TR rises from £20,000 to £20,700; B1 demand is price elastic, so the percentage rise in quantity exceeds the percentage fall in price, increasing revenue.
2. [4] B1 few close substitutes for most drivers; B1 so demand is price inelastic; B1 time period: in the long run drivers can switch to electric cars/public transport; B1 so demand becomes more elastic over time (or: necessity for many; proportion of income).
3. [4] B1 movement: caused only by a change in the good's own price; B1 e.g. price rises, quantity supplied extends; B1 shift: caused by a change in a non-price determinant; B1 e.g. a rise in wages shifts supply to the left.
4. [3] B1 crops take time to grow (a growing season), so output can't respond quickly; B1 in the long run, farmers can plant more land/switch crops/invest; B1 so quantity supplied responds more to price over time.
5. [3] B1 normal good and a luxury (income elastic); B1 a 1% rise in income raises demand by 1.8%; B1 e.g. stock more organic produce as incomes rise, but sales fall sharply in a recession.
6. Correct: A, B, D (exactly these options, no others)
