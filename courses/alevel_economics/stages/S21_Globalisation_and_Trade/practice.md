# S21_Globalisation_and_Trade - Practice: Globalisation and trade

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working or a full paragraph before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Export prices rise by 5% and import prices by 10% (from a base of 100). Calculate the new terms of trade index. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 105/110 x 100 = 95.5.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
