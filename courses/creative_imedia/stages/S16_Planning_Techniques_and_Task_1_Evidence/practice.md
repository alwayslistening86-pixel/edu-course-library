# S16_Planning_Techniques_and_Task_1_Evidence - Practice: Planning techniques and evidencing Task 1

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. For the practice brief 'Fernhill Bakery', make a mind map of keywords from the brief and a small mood board (digital or physical). Tell your tutor what each part is for.
2. Produce three concept sketches for a logo and choose one, writing two sentences on why (in your own words).
3. Write a justification paragraph for one element of your chosen concept in the form element, what it says, client fit, audience fit.
4. Create a visualisation diagram and asset table for a leaflet for the practice brief, then swap with your tutor who will say what a third party could not tell from it (facts only, not what to change).

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
