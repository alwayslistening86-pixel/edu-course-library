# S16_Planning_Techniques_and_Task_1_Evidence - Test: Planning techniques and evidencing Task 1

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Practice brief (original): 'Ridgeway Bikes, a mid-range bike repair shop, wants a visual identity and a flyer for teenagers and commuters.' Produce (a) a labelled design concept for the visual identity (or a described sketch), (b) a justification of two elements covering client and audience, and (c) a layout diagram outline with a partial asset table. The tutor grades against the strand criteria: how suitable is the concept, does the justification give how and why for client and audience, and are the layout and assets detailed enough to be used by someone else.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S16_Planning_Techniques_and_Task_1_Evidence` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S17_Image_Editing_Tools_and_Techniques.
