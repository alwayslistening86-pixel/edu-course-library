# S01_Media_Industry_Sectors_Products_and_Job_Roles - Practice: The media industry: sectors, products and job roles

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Place these products in every sector that could use them: a podcast, an animated advert, a website, a comic. Give one reason for each placement.
2. Choose a role from each group (creative, technical, senior) and explain what each contributes to making a mobile game trailer.
3. A three-person start-up is producing a short promotional film. Explain why one team member might take on several roles.
4. Name two roles that belong mainly to one production phase and two that span several. Say which phases.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
