# S04_Media_Codes - Practice: Media codes: how meaning, impact and engagement are created

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe a camera angle, a lighting choice and a sound choice and say the effect of each on the audience.
2. A poster for a fun-run for young families uses a rounded, colourful font. Explain how typography and colour work together to engage the audience.
3. Explain the difference between meaning, impact and engagement, using a single product as an example.
4. Choose a genre (horror or comedy). List four codes you would use and explain how the combination creates the right mood.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
