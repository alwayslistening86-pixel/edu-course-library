## Test scenario
Timed mini-paper (about 25 minutes, 25 marks, original questions): 'Green Roots, a garden centre, wants a 90-second promotional video for its website and social media aimed at young families.' Section A style: a) State two sectors of the media industry (2). b) Identify the type of file compression used by JPEG (1). c) State two segmentation categories (2). Section B style: d) Explain two ways the video could be adapted to suit young families (4). e) Explain one legal issue the company must consider when filming customers (3). f) Analyse how file format and resolution affect how well the video will work on mobile phones (6). g) Discuss the view that a workplan is essential for this project, reaching a judgement (5).
