# S09_Distribution_Platforms_and_Media - Test: Distribution platforms and media

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario (8 marks): 'A museum wants an interactive guide with images, audio and short videos for visitors. Options are a mobile app, a website and a memory stick handed out at the entrance.' a) Identify which platform or media you would recommend and give two reasons (4). b) Explain one disadvantage of your choice (2). c) Explain how your choice affects the file formats used for the images and videos (2).
