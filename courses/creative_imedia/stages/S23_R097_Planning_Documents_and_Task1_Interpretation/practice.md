# S23_R097_Planning_Documents_and_Task1_Interpretation - Practice: Planning documents for interactive media, and interpreting a client brief

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Wireframe a second screen of the booking product and label at least four features.
2. Add three more rows to your asset table from the lesson, including file type and resolution for each.
3. Draw a hierarchy diagram for an e-learning app with a home screen, three lesson screens and a quiz screen.
4. Write two sentences interpreting a brief for "an app for dog owners to find local dog-friendly cafes" and explaining who it appeals to and why.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
