# S02_Purpose_Style_and_Client_Briefs - Practice: Purpose, style, content and layout, and client requirements

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. List the five purposes and give an everyday product for each. Then pick one product with two purposes and explain both.
2. A brief says: 'A friendly, informal website to promote our community bakery to families, ready in two weeks.' Underline every keyword and say which requirement each one is.
3. Explain two ways a tight timescale and a fixed client ethos could constrain planning and production.
4. Explain the difference between a formal written brief and a negotiated brief and say one advantage of each.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
