# S03_Audience_Segmentation_and_Research - Practice: Audience segmentation, research methods and data

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. For each of the eight segmentation categories give one example group and one way a product designer could respond to it.
2. A company wants a fitness app for over-60s. Explain two ways the audience characteristics would influence the design.
3. Compare online surveys and focus groups. Give one advantage and one disadvantage of each.
4. Classify each as primary or secondary and qualitative or quantitative: (a) a newspaper article on gaming trends; (b) a survey result showing 68% prefer dark mode; (c) an interview quote about why a user dislikes adverts.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
