Practice brief (continuing the charity sign-up website): a) Describe the order you would build the site's four screens in, and why (5). b) Describe your saving/versioning approach during the build (5). c) Describe how you would export the finished site and two things you would check afterwards (5). d) List the separate component parts you would evidence for strand 2a, and state the technical skill shown in each (5). This tests understanding, not a finished build.
