# S08_Regulation_Classification_and_Health_and_Safety - Practice: Regulation, classification, health and safety

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. State what the ASA, Ofcom, BBFC and PEGI each do, with one product for each.
2. A studio wants its film to be rated 12A rather than 15. Explain the impact on the production and one reason it might want this.
3. List four hazards for a night-time outdoor shoot and one control for each.
4. Explain the difference between a risk assessment and a location recce and why both are useful before filming in a shopping centre.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
