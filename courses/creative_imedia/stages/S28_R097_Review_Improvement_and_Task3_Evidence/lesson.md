# S28_R097_Review_Improvement_and_Task3_Evidence - Lesson: Reviewing fitness for purpose, constraints, further development and evidencing Task 3

## Goal
The learner reviews a finished product's fitness for purpose, explains constraints on quality, proposes feasible improvements and further development, and evidences Task 3's two strands.

## Syllabus items taught here
- R097-3.1b - Reviewing fitness for purpose: judge strengths and weaknesses against the brief, client, audience, accessibility and audio-visual quality
- R097-3.2a - Constraints and improvements: explain how time, resources, hardware, software, skills, budget and legislation limit quality and propose feasible improvements
- R097-3.2b - Further development opportunities: propose scope, extra media, interactivity or product changes linked to client requirements and repeat business
- R097-T3a - Task 3 strand 3a: test plan and review of effectiveness: know how to evidence comprehensive technical testing and a critical review for client and audience
- R097-T3b - Task 3 strand 3b: recommendations for improvement and further development: know how to prioritise and explain improvements and further developments

## How to teach this
Ask the learner to review something they made earlier in the course and answer honestly: 'What's actually wrong with it, and why is it like that rather than better?' Use this to model that a good review names real weaknesses and real, specific reasons, not vague praise or vague blame.

#### R097-3.1b Reviewing fitness for purpose
Reviewing means judging the finished product's strengths and weaknesses against the original brief, the client's needs, the audience, accessibility, and audio-visual/technical quality - not simply describing what was built. A strong review is balanced (states genuine strengths and genuine weaknesses) and specific (names which screen, asset or feature and why it does or does not work), referencing the brief and audience directly. A review that only says "it looks good" or lists features built, without judgement against the brief, does not meet Level 2 standard. Activity: write one genuine strength and one genuine weakness of the booking product, each tied to a specific brief requirement.

#### R097-3.2a Constraints and improvements
Constraints that limit a real product's quality include time, available resources, hardware, software, the maker's own skills, budget and legislation (for example copyright or accessibility law). A good review explains which constraints actually affected the specific product made, not generic ones, and proposes improvements that are feasible - achievable with slightly more time, skill or resource - rather than an unrelated wish list. Linking each proposed improvement to a specific constraint or weakness found in review shows understanding, not just imagination. Activity: name one constraint that limited the booking product and one feasible improvement it explains.

#### R097-3.2b Further development opportunities
Further development goes beyond fixing weaknesses: proposing genuine extensions such as wider scope, additional media, more interactivity, or new product features, explicitly linked to client requirements and the chance of repeat business or an ongoing relationship with the client. This differs from an "improvement" (fixing something that falls short) - development proposes growing the product for future value. Activity: propose one further-development idea for the booking product that would make the leisure centre want to commission more work.

#### R097-T3a Task 3 strand 3a: test plan and review of effectiveness
Strand 3a (10 marks) asks for both the technical test plan evidence (from S27) and a critical review of effectiveness for the client and audience, brought together as one piece of evidence. MB3 evidence shows comprehensive testing covering functionality, technical properties and multimedia, plus a balanced, specific review judged against the brief and audience; MB1 evidence shows minimal testing and a superficial or missing review. Activity: combine the test plan drafted in S27 with a short written review paragraph and check both are present and specific.

#### R097-T3b Task 3 strand 3b: recommendations for improvement and further development
Strand 3b (4 marks) asks the learner to prioritise and explain recommended improvements and further development opportunities - not just list them, but say which matters most and why, linked to the review's findings. MB3 evidence prioritises sensibly and explains reasoning; MB1 evidence lists ideas with no prioritisation or justification. Activity: take the improvement and development ideas already drafted and state which one should be done first and why.
