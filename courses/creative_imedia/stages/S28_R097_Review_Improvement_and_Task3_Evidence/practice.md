# S28_R097_Review_Improvement_and_Task3_Evidence - Practice: Reviewing fitness for purpose, constraints, further development and evidencing Task 3

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a balanced review paragraph (one strength, one weakness) for a product of your choice, tied to its intended audience.
2. Name a constraint and a feasible improvement linked to it, for a project you know.
3. Propose one further-development idea, explicitly linked to a client wanting more work in future.
4. Prioritise two improvement ideas and explain in one sentence why one should come first.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
