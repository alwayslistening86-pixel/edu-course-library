# S13_R094_Overview_and_Visual_Identity - Practice: R094 overview: what visual identity is and how it is designed

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain in your own words three purposes of a visual identity and give a real brand example for one.
2. For a fictional business, 'Fernhill Bakery' (a family bakery selling traditional bread), list a possible name, slogan and logo idea and explain how each communicates the business.
3. Explain how colour and typography would differ for an economy supermarket and a high-end jeweller.
4. Explain why a visual identity that clashes with the desired brand identity is not fit for purpose.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
