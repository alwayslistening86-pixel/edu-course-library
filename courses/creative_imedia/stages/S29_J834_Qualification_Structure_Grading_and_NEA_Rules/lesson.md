# S29_J834_Qualification_Structure_Grading_and_NEA_Rules - Lesson: The J834 qualification as a whole: structure, performance objectives, grading and NEA rules

## Goal
The learner explains how J834 is structured and graded overall, what the performance objectives assess, the rules governing NEA work, and how the NEA units link back to R093.

## Syllabus items taught here
- J834-STRUCT - Qualification structure, units, GLH and the terminal assessment rule: know the mandatory and optional units, weighting, 120 GLH and that the exam must be taken in the final series
- J834-PO - Performance objectives PO1 to PO4 and their weightings: know what recall, application, analysis/evaluation and skills demonstration mean and where each is assessed
- J834-GRADE - Grading: UMS, unit grades and overall grades Level 1 Pass to Level 2 Distinction*: calculate unit and overall boundaries and read the performance descriptors
- J834-NEA - NEA rules: independence, authenticity, feedback, referencing and AI use: follow the rules for supervised, independent, referenced work and evidence portfolios
- J834-SYN - Synoptic links between R093 and the NEA units: link each NEA topic area back to its R093 topic areas

## How to teach this
Zoom out from individual units to the whole qualification: 'You've now met three units in detail - how do OCR add them up into one final grade, and what stops someone else doing your coursework for you?' This stage exists to join up everything taught in S01-S28 into the bigger picture the exam board actually certificates.

#### J834-STRUCT Qualification structure, units, GLH and the terminal assessment rule
J834 is made up of one mandatory externally examined unit (R093, 60 GLH), one mandatory NEA unit (R094, 30 GLH) and one optional NEA unit chosen from five (R095-R099, each 30 GLH) - 120 GLH in total for the full Level 1/2 Cambridge National. This build teaches R093, R094 and the optional unit R097, as carried over from the original learner's choice. A key rule (the "terminal assessment rule") is that the R093 exam must be taken in the learner's final assessment series, after all NEA units are complete, so grading always reflects the most current standard of work. Activity: state, in the learner's own words, why OCR requires the exam to be sat last rather than first.

#### J834-PO Performance objectives PO1 to PO4 and their weightings
J834 assesses four performance objectives: PO1 recall knowledge and understanding, PO2 apply knowledge and understanding, PO3 analyse and evaluate, and PO4 demonstrate creative and technical skills. R093 (the exam) mainly assesses PO1-PO3 through Section A recall and Section B application, analysis and evaluation. R094 and R097 (the NEA units) mainly assess PO2 and PO4 through applying knowledge to a client brief and demonstrating practical creative and technical skill, though PO3 also appears in review and justification strands (for example R097 Task 3). Activity: for each performance objective, name one stage or strand from this course that directly assesses it.

#### J834-GRADE Grading: UMS, unit grades and overall grades Level 1 Pass to Level 2 Distinction*
Each unit is marked out of its raw marks (R093: 70; R094: 50; R097: 70) and converted to a Uniform Mark Scale score out of a fixed unit maximum (R093: 80 UMS; R094: 50 UMS; R097: 70 UMS; 200 UMS maximum overall for this combination), which lets results from different series be compared fairly. The three unit UMS scores are summed, and the total is read against grade boundaries: Distinction* at Level 2 needs at least 180 total UMS, Distinction 160, Merit 140, Pass 120; Level 1 grades are Distinction 100, Merit 80, Pass 60; below 60 is unclassified. Activity: given example unit UMS scores of 60 (R093), 40 (R094) and 55 (R097), add them and state the resulting overall grade.

#### J834-NEA NEA rules: independence, authenticity, feedback, referencing and AI use
NEA work (R094 and R097) must be the learner's own, independent, authentic work: teachers may give general feedback and clarify the brief and marking criteria, but must not tell the learner what to do next, correct their work in detail, or provide a bank of ready-made assets or solutions. Any sources, images or ideas taken from elsewhere must be referenced/acknowledged, and using generative AI to produce work that is then presented as the learner's own is malpractice, since it is not the learner's authentic evidence of skill. Teachers authenticate the work with a signed statement confirming it is the learner's own. Activity: give an example of acceptable general feedback and an example of unacceptable, too-specific feedback for the same piece of NEA work.

#### J834-SYN Synoptic links between R093 and the NEA units
J834's NEA units are deliberately synoptic with R093: the industry, audience, client brief, planning, legal/regulatory and distribution knowledge taught for R093's exam (Topic Areas 1-4, taught in S01-S12 of this course) underpins the decisions learners must justify in R094 and R097's planning and review strands - for example explaining audience appeal (R097 strand 1a) draws directly on R093's audience segmentation content (R093-2.3), and explaining legal/licensing choices for assets draws on R093's IP content (R093-3.4.2). Learners who treat R093 as separate from the NEA units miss marks available for this cross-unit reasoning. Activity: name one R093 topic area and explain how it directly supports a specific strand of R094 or R097.
