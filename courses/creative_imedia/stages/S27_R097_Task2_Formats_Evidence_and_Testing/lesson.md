# S27_R097_Task2_Formats_Evidence_and_Testing - Lesson: Justifying formats for Task 2, and testing the finished product

## Goal
The learner justifies the formats and properties used for saved components and the final export, and plans and carries out technical testing of a finished product.

## Syllabus items taught here
- R097-T2b - Task 2 strand 2b: creating the final product: know how to evidence effective build skills, conventions with creativity, and fitness for purpose
- R097-T2c - Task 2 strand 2c: appropriate formats and properties: know how to justify saved component formats and the final export format and properties
- R097-3.1a - Testing and checking technical properties: use test plans, checklists, success criteria, functionality tests, retests and multimedia checks

## How to teach this
Ask the learner: 'A product works perfectly but was saved as a huge, uncompressed file that won't upload to the school website - is that finished?' Use this to show that Task 2's later strands are about correctness of format and thorough checking, not just building something that technically runs once.

#### R097-T2b Task 2 strand 2b: creating the final product
Strand 2b (16 marks, the largest strand in R097) asks the learner to evidence the finished, assembled product itself: effective build skills across the whole product, conventions followed with genuine creativity layered on top, and clear fitness for the stated purpose and audience. MB3 evidence is a fully working, polished product where every planned element (navigation, content, interactivity) functions correctly and consistently; MB1 evidence is a partial or malfunctioning build. This strand is marked on the whole product, so a strong start that is not finished cannot reach the top band. Activity: check the booking product against a simple checklist (every screen present, every link works, every planned asset included) and note any gaps.

#### R097-T2c Task 2 strand 2c: appropriate formats and properties
Strand 2c (6 marks) asks the learner to justify, in writing, the file formats and properties chosen for saved components and the final exported product - for example why an image was saved as a particular format, why the export format suits the target platform, and why file sizes are appropriate for how the product will be delivered. This is the reasoning layer on top of the saving/exporting work done in S26, similar to how strand 1c sat on top of planning. Activity: write two sentences justifying the export format chosen for the booking product against its intended platform.

#### R097-3.1a Testing and checking technical properties
Testing uses a test plan or checklist stating what will be tested, expected results and actual results, covering functionality (every link, button and form works), technical properties (assets display and play at the right size/quality) and multimedia checks (audio and video play correctly on the target device). Faults found are retested after fixing, and the whole test process is recorded as evidence, not just a final "it works" statement. A test plan with success criteria set in advance (rather than written up afterwards to match whatever happened) is stronger evidence. Activity: draft a five-row test plan for the booking product covering at least one link, one form and one media asset.

## Explicitly not here
Reviewing fitness for purpose in depth, constraints and further development (S28), Task 3 evidence (S28).
