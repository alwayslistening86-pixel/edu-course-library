# S15_Graphic_Properties_Licences_and_Permissions - Practice: Technical properties of graphics, licences and permissions

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why a logo saved as JPEG might look poor on a coloured web page and what format solves this.
2. Explain the difference between RGB and CMYK and when to use each.
3. For each asset say who owns it and what permission you need: a photograph the client supplied; a well-known drinks brand's logo; an image from a free stock site; a photo of a friend you took yourself.
4. Design the column headings for an asset table for a two-page leaflet.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
