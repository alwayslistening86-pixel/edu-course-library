## Test scenario
Practice brief: 'You are creating a leaflet and website banner for Fernhill Bakery. The assets are the client's logo (supplied as a PNG), a photo of bread from a stock library, a photograph you took, and an image found via an internet search.' a) Explain whether bitmap or vector is better for the logo used on a shop sign and on a website (4). b) Explain how colour mode and resolution should differ for the leaflet and the banner (4). c) Explain the licence and permission position of each of the four assets and how you would record it (8).
