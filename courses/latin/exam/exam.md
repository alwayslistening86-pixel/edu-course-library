# GCSE Latin (Eduqas/WJEC C990) — Language, Literature (Livy and Virgil) and Roman Festivals and Worship - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.
## Format
This cumulative exam mirrors Eduqas GCSE Latin's real three-component structure and is sat as one combined session covering all three papers in miniature; no real past-paper material is used anywhere below.

**Component 1: Language (worth 50% of the qualification in the real qualification; modelled here at reduced length).**
Section A: a short unseen Latin passage (accidence and syntax drawn only from items taught in stages L01-L06) followed by (a) comprehension questions answered in English, where reasonable paraphrase earns full credit, and (b) a Latin-to-English translation of part of the same or a companion passage, marked by weighting finite verbs and comparatives/superlatives more heavily, per LAT-GR-comprehension-technique. Section B: a short choice between (a) an English-to-Latin translation using only first-conjugation verbs and first/second-declension nouns, or (b) a grammar/syntax analysis task identifying case, tense, mood and clause type in given sentences, per LAT-C1-section-b.

**Component 3A: Latin Literature and Sources (worth 25%; modelled here at reduced length).**
One original passage-based question set on Livy's Hannibal Crosses the Alps (drawing on any of passages CONTEXT/A-H) requiring narrative recall, explanation of Hannibal's characterisation, and identification of at least one named stylistic feature (ablative absolute, gerund/gerundive, neuter plural adjective as noun, result clause, or unfulfilled condition) with its effect. One equivalent original question set on Virgil's Hercules and Cacus episode (drawing on any of passages CONTEXT/A-I) requiring narrative recall and identification of at least one named verse technique (emphatic word order, sound effect, or Homeric epithet) with its effect. No verbatim quotation of either text beyond a few words is used or expected in answers; paraphrase and analysis are what is assessed.

**Component 3B: Latin Sources for Topics — Topic 8: Roman Festivals and Worship (worth 25%; modelled here at reduced length).**
One original source-based question set presenting a short invented scenario (an inscription, an extract paraphrasing an ancient author such as Ovid, or a description of an archaeological find) requiring the learner to identify the festival, deity, priesthood or practice concerned, explain its ritual detail accurately, and evaluate what that specific type of source can and cannot establish, per LAT-C3B-T8-sources.
