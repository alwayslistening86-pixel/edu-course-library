# O02_Death_Rites_Lifecycle_and_Divination - Practice: Parentalia and Lemuria, life-cycle rituals, sacrifice, death and afterlife beliefs, and divination

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the contrast in mood between the Parentalia and the Lemuria and describe one specific ritual action from each.
2. Describe the bulla and lunula and explain what each marked in a Roman child's life.
3. Describe the steps of an animal sacrifice from leading the animal to the altar to the examination of the exta.
4. Explain two different Roman beliefs about what happens after death, citing the term di manes.
5. Distinguish augury from haruspicy and explain the difference between a fastus and a nefastus day.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
