# M02_Livy_Alps_Ascent_Descent_and_Style - Practice: Livy — the Alpine ascent, the landslide, the fire-and-vinegar solution, and Livy's style

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why the local tribes and the terrain are both described as threats in passage E.
2. Retell Hannibal's speech at the summit and explain what rhetorical effect it has on the reader as well as the troops.
3. Describe exactly what physical obstacle blocks the descent and why it cannot simply be marched around.
4. Explain the fire-and-vinegar technique step by step and what it shows about Hannibal as a leader.
5. Give one example each of an ablative absolute, a gerund/gerundive and a neuter plural adjective used as a noun, and explain the effect of each.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
