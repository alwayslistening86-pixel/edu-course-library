# M01_Livy_Hannibals_Career_and_Omens - Practice: Livy — Hannibal's early career, character and the prophetic dream

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain in two or three sentences why Hamilcar's expansion into Spain set up the war Livy narrates.
2. Retell the oath scene in your own words, including who initiated it and why Livy includes it.
3. List two virtues and two vices Livy gives Hannibal in the character sketch, and explain why Livy includes both.
4. Explain the dream's two stages (the instruction, and what Hannibal sees when he disobeys it) and what it foreshadows.
5. Describe the trick used to get the elephants across the Rhone and what happened to the elephants that panicked.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
