# O03_Temples_Deities_Priesthoods_Household_and_Sources - Practice: Temples, deities, priesthoods, household religion, and working with ancient sources

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Name three temples from this stage and state each one's specific religious or civic function.
2. Match each of Saturn, Vesta, Bona Dea, the Lares and the Penates to the correct sphere of responsibility.
3. Explain the difference between a flamen and a pontifex, and between an augur and a haruspex.
4. Describe the lararium and explain what a household's daily religious routine involved.
5. Take one named literary source, one inscription and one piece of archaeological evidence from this stage and explain, for each, one thing it can and one thing it cannot tell a historian about Roman religion.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
