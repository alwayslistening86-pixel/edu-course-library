# O01_Roman_Religion_Nature_and_Major_Festivals - Practice: The nature of Roman religion, and the Lupercalia, Bona Dea, Saturnalia and Vestalia

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain in your own words why "correct action" mattered more than "correct belief" in Roman religion, using the term pax deorum.
2. Describe what the Luperci did at the Lupercalia and why women wanted to be struck by the februa.
3. Explain why the Bona Dea rites are hard for historians to reconstruct reliably, using the Clodius Pulcher scandal as an example.
4. Describe the role reversal that took place during the Saturnalia and explain one modern scholarly interpretation of it.
5. Explain the Vestal Virgins' role at the Vestalia and why an extinguished sacred flame mattered so much.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
