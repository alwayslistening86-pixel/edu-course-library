# N02_Virgil_Combat_Cult_and_Future_Rome - Practice: Virgil — Hercules exposes the cave, the combat, the cult of Hercules, the Salii's hymn and future Rome

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe how Hercules exposes the cave and explain why Virgil describes it in such exaggerated terms.
2. Retell the fight and death of Cacus, noting how Hercules wins.
3. Explain what the Ara Maxima is and why its founding matters to Virgil's Roman audience.
4. Summarise two of the deeds recounted in the Salii's hymn.
5. Explain what the tour of future Rome achieves for Virgil's readers, and give one example each of emphatic word order, a sound effect and a Homeric epithet.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
