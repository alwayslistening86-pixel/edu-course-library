# N01_Virgil_Alliance_Banquet_and_Cacus_Lair - Practice: Virgil — Aeneas's alliance with Evander, the banquet, and Cacus's lair

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain in your own words why Virgil includes a Hercules story inside a poem about Aeneas.
2. Explain the basis on which Aeneas and Evander agree to become allies.
3. Explain why Evander narrating the Cacus myth during a religious feast matters for how Virgil presents the story.
4. Describe Cacus's cave and list two details Virgil uses to make him monstrous.
5. Explain exactly how Cacus disguises the cattle theft and how it is eventually discovered.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
