# L04_Nonfinite_Forms_and_Subjunctive - Practice: Non-finite forms and the subjunctive

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give the present infinitive of regō and audiō, and state each verb's conjugation from it.
2. Form the present participle of amō and the perfect passive participle of regō, and translate each.
3. Give three positive imperative commands, one from each of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd conjugations.
4. Form the imperfect and pluperfect subjunctive of amō and regō.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
