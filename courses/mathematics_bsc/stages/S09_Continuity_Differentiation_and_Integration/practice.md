# S09_Continuity_Differentiation_and_Integration - Practice: Continuity, differentiation and integration

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question or proof in full before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Use the IVT to show cos x = x has a solution in (0, pi/2). [3 marks]
2. Verify Rolle's theorem applies to f(x)=x^2-4x+3 on [1,3] and find the guaranteed c. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 lets f(x)=cos x - x, continuous; A1 f(0)=1>0, f(pi/2)=-pi/2<0; A1 by IVT there is c in (0,pi/2) with f(c)=0, i.e. cos c = c.
2. [3] M1 f(1)=0, f(3)=0, continuous and differentiable everywhere; A1 hypotheses satisfied; A1 f'(x)=2x-4=0 gives c=2, in (1,3).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt (including a full proof, not just an answer) before offering a hint; then walk through the model solution and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
