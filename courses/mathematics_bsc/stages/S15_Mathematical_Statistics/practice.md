# S15_Mathematical_Statistics - Practice: Mathematical statistics

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question or proof in full before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Find the MLE of lambda for an exponential distribution given a single observation x (density f(x;lambda)=lambda e^{-lambda x}). [3 marks]
2. Is the sample variance formula sum(x_i-xbar)^2/(n-1) or /n unbiased for the population variance? State which and why briefly. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 l(lambda)=ln(lambda)-lambda x; M1 dl/dlambda = 1/lambda - x =0; A1 lambda-hat = 1/x.
2. [2] M1 recalls dividing by n-1 (Bessel's correction) gives the unbiased estimator; A1 dividing by n underestimates the population variance on average (biased).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt (including a full proof, not just an answer) before offering a hint; then walk through the model solution and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
