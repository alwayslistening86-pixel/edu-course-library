# S04_Groups_and_Subgroups - Practice: Groups and subgroups

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question or proof in full before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Verify that (Z_5 \ {0}, x) (nonzero residues mod 5 under multiplication) is a group, by writing out its Cayley table and identifying the identity and each inverse. [4 marks]
2. List the elements of the subgroup of S_3 generated by the transposition (1 2). [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] M1 closure checked from the table; A1 identity is 1; M1 inverses read off (2<->3, 4<->4); A1 associativity holds since it is inherited from integer multiplication mod 5.
2. [2] M1 computes powers of (1 2): (1 2)^1=(1 2), (1 2)^2=e; A1 subgroup {e, (1 2)}, order 2.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt (including a full proof, not just an answer) before offering a hint; then walk through the model solution and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
