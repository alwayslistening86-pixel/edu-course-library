# S03_Probability_and_Statistical_Inference - Practice: Probability and statistical inference

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question or proof in full before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. X~B(10,0.3). Find E[X], Var(X), and P(X=3) (to 4 d.p.). [4 marks]
2. Calls arrive at a helpline at a Poisson rate of 3 per 10 minutes. Find P(no calls in a given 10-minute period). [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B1 E[X]=3; B1 Var(X)=2.1; M1 uses P(X=3)=C(10,3)(0.3)^3(0.7)^7; A1 = 0.2668.
2. [2] M1 uses P(X=0)=e^(-3); A1 = 0.0498.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt (including a full proof, not just an answer) before offering a hint; then walk through the model solution and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
