# S07_Eigenvalues_Diagonalisation_and_Inner_Product_Spaces - Practice: Eigenvalues, diagonalisation and inner product spaces

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question or proof in full before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Find the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of A=[[5,4],[1,2]]. [5 marks]
2. Are u=(1,2,-1) and v=(3,-1,1) orthogonal (Euclidean inner product)? [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [5] M1 char poly (5-lambda)(2-lambda)-4=0; A1 lambda^2-7lambda+6=0, lambda=1,6; M1 solves (A-I)v=0 for lambda=1; A1 eigenvector (say) (-1,1); A1 for lambda=6, (A-6I)v=0 gives eigenvector (4,1).
2. [2] M1 computes u.v=3-2-1=0; A1 yes, orthogonal since the dot product is 0.
