#### 3.2.2 All cells arise from other cells
Within multicellular organisms, not all cells keep the ability to divide. Eukaryotic cells that do divide have a cell cycle: interphase (DNA replication and organelle synthesis; the longest stage), mitosis (nuclear division) and cytokinesis. Mitosis produces two genetically identical daughter cells for growth and repair: prophase (chromosomes condense and become visible as two chromatids joined at a centromere; nuclear envelope breaks down; spindle forms), metaphase (chromosomes line up at the equator, attached to spindle fibres by their centromeres), anaphase (centromeres divide; chromatids pulled to opposite poles), telophase (chromosomes uncoil; nuclear envelopes reform). Cancer results from uncontrolled cell division (mutations in genes controlling the cycle); many cancer treatments target the cell cycle (e.g. blocking DNA replication or spindle formation). Prokaryotes divide by binary fission: replication of the circular DNA and plasmids, then division of the cytoplasm. Viruses do not divide: they inject their nucleic acid into a host cell, whose machinery replicates the virus particles.

#### RP2 Required practical 2: stained root-tip squashes, identifying the stages of mitosis with an optical microscope and calculating a mitotic index
**Method:** cut the tip (5 mm) of a root (e.g. garlic or onion, where cells are dividing); soften in warm hydrochloric acid (breaks down the tissue so cells separate and stops division); stain (e.g. toluidine blue or acetic orcein) to make chromosomes visible; squash under a coverslip (spreads cells into a single layer) without sliding it. View at low then high power; count cells in each stage. **Mitotic index** = number of cells with visible chromosomes (in mitosis) ÷ total number of cells observed.

#### MS 0.3 Use ratios, fractions and percentages
Ratios and percentages. *Example:* 32 of 400 cells in mitosis: mitotic index = 0.08 (8%). If the cell cycle lasts 20 hours, the time spent in a stage = proportion of cells in that stage x 20 h: 32/400 x 20 h = 1.6 h in mitosis.
