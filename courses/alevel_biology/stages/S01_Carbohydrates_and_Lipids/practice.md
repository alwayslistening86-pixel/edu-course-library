# S01_Carbohydrates_and_Lipids - Practice: Monomers, polymers, carbohydrates and lipids

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name the bond formed between two monosaccharides and the type of reaction that forms it. [2 marks]
2. Describe how to test a solution for a non-reducing sugar. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 glycosidic bond; B1 condensation.
2. [3] B1 negative Benedict's test first (stays blue); B1 heat with dilute HCl, then neutralise with sodium hydrogencarbonate; B1 heat with Benedict's: brick-red (or orange/yellow/green) precipitate.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
