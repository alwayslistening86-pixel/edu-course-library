# S01_Carbohydrates_and_Lipids - Test: Monomers, polymers, carbohydrates and lipids

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain how the structure of cellulose makes it suited to its function in plant cell walls. [4 marks]
2. Explain two ways in which the structure of glycogen makes it a good energy store in animals. [4 marks]
3. Describe the structure of a triglyceride and explain why triglycerides are good energy stores. [4 marks]
4. Explain how phospholipids form a bilayer in water. [2 marks]
5. Give the monosaccharides that make up lactose and sucrose. [2 marks]
6. Which statements about starch are correct? Choose every correct option.
   A. It is a polymer of α-glucose
   B. Amylose is branched
   C. It is insoluble in water
   D. It turns iodine solution blue-black

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 made of β-glucose; B1 long, straight, unbranched chains; B1 chains held together by many hydrogen bonds; B1 forming microfibrils that give strength (resist bursting).
2. [4] B1 highly branched; B1 so many ends for enzymes to hydrolyse rapidly, releasing glucose quickly; B1 insoluble; B1 so it does not affect water potential (or: compact, so a lot stored in a small space).
3. [4] B1 glycerol and three fatty acids; B1 joined by ester bonds (condensation); B1 many C-H bonds release a lot of energy when oxidised; B1 insoluble so no effect on water potential (or low mass per unit energy).
4. [2] B1 hydrophilic phosphate heads face the water on both sides; B1 hydrophobic fatty acid tails face inwards, away from water.
5. [2] B1 lactose: glucose and galactose; B1 sucrose: glucose and fructose.
6. Correct: A, C, D (exactly these options, no others)
