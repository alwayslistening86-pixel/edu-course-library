# S17_Stimuli_Receptors_Heart_Rate - Test: Survival and response, receptors and control of heart rate

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain how a Pacinian corpuscle produces a generator potential when pressure is applied. [3 marks]
2. Explain why rods give greater sensitivity but lower visual acuity than cones. [4 marks]
3. Describe how an increase in blood CO2 during exercise leads to an increase in heart rate. [4 marks]
4. Explain how IAA causes a shoot to grow towards light. [3 marks]
5. Explain the importance of the delay at the atrioventricular node. [2 marks]
6. In a choice chamber, 18 woodlice moved to the damp side and 6 to the dry side. Suggest why a chi-squared test is used, and describe the behaviour shown. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 pressure deforms the lamellae and the neurone membrane; B1 stretch-mediated sodium ion channels open; B1 Na+ diffuse in, depolarising the membrane (generator potential).
2. [4] B1 several rods connect to a single bipolar neurone; B1 so generator potentials combine (spatial summation) to reach threshold in dim light; B1 each cone connects to its own bipolar neurone; B1 so the brain can distinguish light from separate points (higher acuity).
3. [4] B1 chemoreceptors (aorta/carotid arteries) detect the fall in pH; B1 send impulses to the medulla; B1 more impulses along the sympathetic nerve to the SAN; B1 SAN discharges more frequently, increasing heart rate.
4. [3] B1 IAA moves to the shaded side of the shoot; B1 where it stimulates cell elongation; B1 so the shaded side grows faster and the shoot bends towards the light.
5. [2] B1 allows the atria to finish contracting and empty; B1 before the ventricles contract.
6. [2] B1 to test whether the difference from an even (12:12) distribution is significant, rather than due to chance; B1 woodlice move to/prefer damp conditions (e.g. by kinesis: moving faster and turning less in dry air).
