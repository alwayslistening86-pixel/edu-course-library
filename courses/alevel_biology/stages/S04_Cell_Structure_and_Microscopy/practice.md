# S04_Cell_Structure_and_Microscopy - Practice: Eukaryotic and prokaryotic cells, viruses and methods of studying cells

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A mitochondrion 2.5 μm long appears 50 mm long in a micrograph. Calculate the magnification. [2 marks]
2. Give two features of prokaryotic cells that are not found in eukaryotic cells. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 50 000 μm ÷ 2.5 μm; A1 x20000.
2. [2] B1 circular DNA free in the cytoplasm (no nucleus), not associated with proteins; B1 murein/peptidoglycan cell wall (or: no membrane-bound organelles).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
