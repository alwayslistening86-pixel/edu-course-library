# S18_Nerve_Impulses_and_Synapses - Test: Nerve impulses and synaptic transmission

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Describe how a resting potential is established. [3 marks]
2. Describe the events at a cholinergic synapse that lead to an action potential in the postsynaptic neurone. [5 marks]
3. Explain why myelinated neurones conduct impulses faster than non-myelinated ones. [3 marks]
4. An insecticide inhibits acetylcholinesterase. Suggest its effect on insect muscles. [2 marks]
5. Explain how temporal summation can lead to an action potential. [2 marks]
6. Which events happen during depolarisation of an axon membrane? Choose every correct option.
   A. voltage-gated Na+ channels open
   B. Na+ diffuse into the axon
   C. K+ diffuse into the axon
   D. the inside becomes positive relative to the outside

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 sodium-potassium pump moves 3 Na+ out and 2 K+ in by active transport; B1 membrane more permeable to K+ (more K+ channels open), so K+ diffuses out; B1 the inside becomes negative relative to the outside (about -70 mV).
2. [5] B1 action potential causes Ca2+ channels to open, Ca2+ diffuses into the presynaptic knob; B1 vesicles fuse with the presynaptic membrane, releasing acetylcholine; B1 ACh diffuses across the cleft and binds to receptors; B1 Na+ channels open, Na+ diffuses in, depolarising the postsynaptic membrane; B1 if threshold is reached, an action potential is generated.
3. [3] B1 myelin insulates the axon; B1 depolarisation only at nodes of Ranvier; B1 so impulses jump from node to node (saltatory conduction).
4. [2] B1 ACh isn't broken down and stays bound to receptors; B1 postsynaptic membrane stays depolarised, so muscles contract continuously (spasm/paralysis).
5. [2] B1 a single presynaptic neurone releases neurotransmitter repeatedly in quick succession; B1 the combined effect reaches the threshold in the postsynaptic neurone.
6. Correct: A, B, D (exactly these options, no others)
