# S03_Nucleic_Acids_ATP_Water_Ions - Test: DNA and RNA, replication, ATP, water and inorganic ions

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Describe the process of semi-conservative DNA replication. [5 marks]
2. In Meselson and Stahl's experiment, predict the result after two generations in 14N if replication were conservative, and state the actual result. [2 marks]
3. Explain why ATP is suited to be the immediate energy source in cells. [3 marks]
4. Explain how two properties of water are important to living organisms. [4 marks]
5. A DNA sample contains 22% thymine. Calculate the percentage of guanine. [2 marks]
6. State the role of iron ions and of sodium ions in the body. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] B1 DNA helicase breaks hydrogen bonds between bases; B1 both strands act as templates; B1 free nucleotides attracted to complementary bases (A-T, C-G); B1 DNA polymerase joins adjacent nucleotides; B1 forming phosphodiester bonds by condensation; each new molecule has one original strand.
2. [2] B1 conservative: one quarter heavy, three quarters light (no intermediate); B1 actual: half intermediate, half light.
3. [3] B1 releases energy in small, manageable amounts; B1 hydrolysis is a single-step reaction, so energy is released quickly; B1 can be rapidly resynthesised (or: phosphorylates other molecules making them more reactive).
4. [4] B1 high latent heat of vaporisation; B1 evaporation (sweating) cools with little water loss; B1 cohesion (hydrogen bonds between molecules); B1 allows water to be pulled up xylem as a column (or high heat capacity buffers temperature; solvent).
5. [2] M1 A = 22%, so A + T = 44%; A1 G = (100 - 44)/2 = 28%.
6. [2] B1 iron: part of haemoglobin, binding oxygen; B1 sodium: co-transport of glucose/amino acids into cells.
