# S26_Practical_Skills_Data_and_Essay - Practice: Practical skills, data analysis, statistics and the synoptic essay

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State which statistical test you would use to see whether (a) the mean heights of two groups of plants differ significantly, (b) there is a correlation between soil pH and species number. [2 marks]
2. Outline a plan for the essay title 'The importance of hydrogen bonds in living organisms'. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 (a) Student's t-test; B1 (b) Spearman's rank correlation coefficient.
2. [4] B1 B1 B1 B1 any four relevant topics, e.g. water properties (cohesion, heat capacity), DNA base pairing and replication, protein secondary/tertiary structure and enzymes, cellulose microfibrils, tRNA structure, transpiration.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
