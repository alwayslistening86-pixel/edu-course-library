# S26_Practical_Skills_Data_and_Essay - Test: Practical skills, data analysis, statistics and the synoptic essay

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Two samples of limpet shell heights gave means of 23.4 mm (SD 1.2) and 25.1 mm (SD 2.9). A t-test gives t = 1.65; the critical value at p = 0.05 is 2.10. What do you conclude, and why do the standard deviations matter? [3 marks]
2. A student investigates the effect of pH on amylase. Identify the independent and dependent variables and describe how to control temperature and enzyme concentration. [4 marks]
3. A measuring cylinder reads to the nearest 1 cm^3. Calculate the percentage uncertainty in a volume of 25 cm^3. [1 mark]
4. Explain why a strong correlation between two variables does not show that one causes the other. [2 marks]
5. Write a plan (not the full essay) for the title 'The importance of proteins in living organisms', naming at least four topics and the key point you'd make in each. [5 marks]
6. Which data should be displayed as a histogram? Choose every correct option.
   A. the frequency of leaf lengths grouped into 5 mm classes
   B. mean rates of reaction at five temperatures
   C. the number of each blood group in a class
   D. the frequency of body masses grouped into 2 kg classes

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 t is less than the critical value; B1 so there is no significant difference (accept the null hypothesis; the difference is probably due to chance); B1 the large SD of the second sample shows wide spread/overlap of the data.
2. [4] B1 independent: pH; B1 dependent: rate of starch breakdown (e.g. time to disappearance of blue-black with iodine); B1 temperature: thermostatically controlled water bath; B1 enzyme: same volume and concentration of amylase solution in each test.
3. [1] B1 ±0.5/25 x 100 = 2%.
4. [2] B1 a third factor may affect both; B1 causation needs a controlled experiment (changing one variable while keeping others constant).
5. [5] B1 B1 B1 B1 four relevant, varied topics at A-level depth (e.g. enzymes and activation energy; haemoglobin and oxygen transport; antibodies and the immune response; membrane carrier proteins/co-transport; actin and myosin; transcription factors; receptors in synapses; rubisco); B1 each linked explicitly to the title.
6. Correct: A, D (exactly these options, no others)
