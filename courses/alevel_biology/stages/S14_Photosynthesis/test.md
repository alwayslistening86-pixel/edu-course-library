# S14_Photosynthesis - Test: Photosynthesis

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Describe how ATP is made in the light-dependent reaction by chemiosmosis. [4 marks]
2. Explain what happens to the concentrations of GP and RuBP when the light is switched off. [4 marks]
3. In required practical 8, explain why the chloroplast extract is kept cold and in an isotonic buffer, and what the colour change of DCPIP shows. [3 marks]
4. Describe photolysis and state what happens to each product. [3 marks]
5. A grower raises CO2 concentration in a glasshouse. Explain why the yield may not increase further above a certain level. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 excited electrons from chlorophyll pass along the electron transfer chain; B1 energy released pumps protons into the thylakoid space; B1 protons diffuse back through ATP synthase (down their gradient); B1 providing energy to make ATP from ADP + Pi.
2. [4] B1 GP rises and RuBP falls; B1 no ATP or reduced NADP from the light-dependent reaction; B1 so GP can't be reduced to TP; B1 and RuBP can't be regenerated, while CO2 still combines with the remaining RuBP.
3. [3] B1 cold reduces enzyme activity that damages chloroplasts; B1 isotonic prevents chloroplasts bursting/shrinking by osmosis; B1 DCPIP turning from blue to colourless shows it has accepted electrons (dehydrogenase activity/light-dependent reaction).
4. [3] B1 light splits water: 2H2O → 4H+ + 4e- + O2; B1 electrons replace those lost by chlorophyll; B1 protons used to reduce NADP (and oxygen diffuses out/used in respiration).
5. [2] B1 another factor becomes limiting (light intensity or temperature); B1 so the rate of photosynthesis can't increase further.
