# S09_Digestion_and_Transport_in_Animals - Practice: Digestion, absorption and mass transport in animals

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Heart rate is 72 beats min^-1 and stroke volume 70 cm^3. Calculate cardiac output in dm^3 min^-1. [1 mark]
2. Name the enzymes that hydrolyse starch to glucose, stating where each acts. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 5.04 dm^3 min^-1.
2. [2] B1 amylase (in mouth/small intestine) hydrolyses starch to maltose; B1 maltase, membrane-bound on the ileum epithelium, hydrolyses maltose to glucose.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
