# S09_Digestion_and_Transport_in_Animals - Test: Digestion, absorption and mass transport in animals

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain the advantage of the Bohr effect during exercise. [3 marks]
2. Describe how tissue fluid is formed and returned to the circulatory system. [4 marks]
3. Explain what causes the atrioventricular valves to close during the cardiac cycle. [2 marks]
4. Explain the roles of bile salts and micelles in the digestion and absorption of lipids. [4 marks]
5. Explain why the curve for fetal haemoglobin lies to the left of the adult curve. [2 marks]
6. Relate the structure of an artery to its function. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 more CO2 is produced by respiring muscles; B1 lowers haemoglobin's affinity for oxygen (curve shifts right); B1 more oxygen is unloaded to the muscles.
2. [4] B1 high hydrostatic pressure at the arteriole end forces fluid out; B1 large plasma proteins remain, giving blood a lower water potential; B1 at the venule end water returns by osmosis; B1 excess tissue fluid drains into the lymph.
3. [2] B1 ventricles contract (ventricular systole); B1 ventricular pressure exceeds atrial pressure, pushing the valves shut (preventing backflow).
4. [4] B1 bile salts emulsify lipids into small droplets; B1 increasing the surface area for lipase; B1 micelles carry monoglycerides and fatty acids to the epithelial cells; B1 where they diffuse across the membrane (lipid-soluble).
5. [2] B1 fetal haemoglobin has a higher affinity for oxygen; B1 so it can load oxygen from the mother's blood in the placenta, where pO2 is low.
6. [3] B1 thick muscular wall withstands/maintains high pressure; B1 elastic tissue stretches and recoils, smoothing flow and maintaining pressure; B1 folded endothelium allows stretching (narrow lumen maintains pressure).
