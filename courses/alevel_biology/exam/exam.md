# A-level Biology (AQA 7402) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Three papers under timed conditions: Paper 1 covers topics 1-4 (S01-S13), with 15 marks of extended response; Paper 2 covers topics 5-8 (S14-S25), with a 15-mark comprehension question on an unseen passage; Paper 3 covers all topics: 38 marks of structured questions including practical techniques, 15 marks of critical analysis of given experimental data (S26 and the required practicals), and one 25-mark essay chosen from two titles that draw on several topics. Questions are original; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage tests. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 14 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1] Describe how the structure of the alveolar epithelium and its blood supply make gas exchange efficient. [4 marks]
2. [Paper 1] Explain how the structure of DNA is related to its functions. [5 marks]
3. [Paper 1] A student measured the water potential of potato tissue. Cylinders gained 6.2% mass in 0.20 mol dm^-3 sucrose and lost 3.8% in 0.40 mol dm^-3. Estimate the sucrose concentration with the same water potential as the tissue, assuming a straight line. [2 marks]
4. [Paper 1] Explain why vaccination programmes protect even people who are not vaccinated. [2 marks]
5. [Paper 1] Calculate the index of diversity for a sample: species A 18, B 12, C 6, D 4. [2 marks]
6. [Paper 2] Explain how an action potential is transmitted along a myelinated axon faster than along a non-myelinated one. [3 marks]
7. [Paper 2] 49% of a population can roll their tongue (dominant allele R). Use the Hardy-Weinberg equation to calculate the percentage of heterozygotes. [3 marks]
8. [Paper 2] Explain why the light-independent reaction stops in the dark. [3 marks]
9. [Paper 2] Describe the role of the kidney in the response to dehydration. [4 marks]
10. [Paper 2] Comprehension-style: a passage states that 'siRNA drugs can silence a faulty gene'. Explain how. [3 marks]
11. [Paper 3] Critical analysis: a study found that people who drank more coffee had lower rates of type II diabetes (r = -0.42, n = 2000). Evaluate the claim that coffee prevents diabetes. [4 marks]
12. [Paper 3] Describe how to prepare and observe a root-tip squash to calculate a mitotic index. [5 marks]
13. [Paper 3] Essay (25 marks): 'The importance of ATP in living organisms.' Write the essay under timed conditions (about 35 minutes). [25 marks]
14. [Paper 3] Which statistical test is appropriate to compare the observed ratio of offspring phenotypes with a 3:1 expected ratio? Choose every correct option.
   A. chi-squared test
   B. Student's t-test
   C. Spearman's rank correlation
   D. none: only a mean is needed

## Grading
Mark every question against a written mark scheme. Mark the essay out of 25 by levels, as AQA does (five levels: 1-5 up to 21-25), judged on the range of relevant topics, the depth and accuracy of A-level content, links to the title, and freedom from errors and irrelevance. A pass needs at least 50% of the 260 marks overall and at least 40% on each paper. Report the total, the percentage per paper and the weakest topic areas. AQA's grade boundaries change every series, so the course does not claim a predicted grade.

## Outcome
- **Pass:** record `exam_status: "passed"`. The course is complete.
- **Not yet:** leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the weakest topic areas, offer targeted review, and retry with fresh papers.
