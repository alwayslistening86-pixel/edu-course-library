# FA_S10_Statement_of_Cash_Flows_and_Incomplete_Records - Test: Statement of cash flows and incomplete records

## How to run this

## Test items
1. Which of these is classified as a financing activity in a statement of cash flows? Choose every correct option.
   A. Cash paid to purchase a new machine
   B. Cash received from issuing new shares
   C. Cash paid to suppliers for inventory
   D. Depreciation charged for the year
2. A sole trader's opening net assets are £64,000 and closing net assets are £81,000. During the year, capital introduced was £5,000 and drawings were £22,000. Calculate the profit for the year. [4 marks]
3. Opening trade payables are £14,000. Purchases on credit during the year are £92,000 and payments made to suppliers are £89,000. Calculate closing trade payables. [3 marks]
4. A business selling at a 20% margin (not mark-up) has revenue of £50,000. Which is correct? Choose every correct option.
   A. Cost of sales is £40,000
   B. Gross profit is £12,500 and cost of sales is £37,500
   C. Gross profit is £10,000 and cost of sales is £40,000
   D. Cost of sales is £50,000
5. A trader knows closing inventory was destroyed in a fire and cannot be counted. Sales for the year were £180,000, the trader always prices at a 40% margin, opening inventory was £22,000, and purchases were £115,000. Estimate the value of the inventory destroyed. [5 marks]
6. Why can a profitable business still run out of cash? Choose every correct option.
   A. Profit and cash are always identical, so this cannot happen
   B. Profit can be tied up in growing inventory, receivables, or debt repayments that don't appear in the statement of profit or loss
   C. Depreciation is a cash outflow that reduces cash directly
   D. Only loss-making businesses can run out of cash

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [4] M1 opening net assets + capital introduced - drawings + profit = closing net assets; M1 64,000 + 5,000 - 22,000 + profit = 81,000; A1 profit = 81,000 - 64,000 - 5,000 + 22,000; A1 = £34,000.
3. [3] M1 opening + purchases - payments = closing; M1 14,000 + 92,000 - 89,000; A1 = £17,000.
4. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
5. [5] M1 cost of sales = sales x (1 - margin) = 180,000 x 0.60 = £108,000; M1 goods available for sale = opening inventory + purchases = 22,000 + 115,000 = £137,000; A1 closing inventory (before the fire) = 137,000 - 108,000; A1 = £29,000, the estimated value destroyed; B1 correct method: since margin is expressed on sales (not cost), cost of sales = sales x (1 - margin), not sales / (1 + margin).
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
