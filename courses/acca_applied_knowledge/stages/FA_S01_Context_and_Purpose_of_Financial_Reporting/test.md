## Test items
1. Which financial statement shows an entity's financial position at a single point in time? Choose every correct option.
   A. Statement of profit or loss
   B. Statement of financial position
   C. Statement of cash flows
   D. Statement of changes in equity
2. Which stakeholders are most directly interested in whether a business can repay a loan and its interest? Choose every correct option.
   A. Lenders
   B. Employees only
   C. Suppliers only
   D. The general public only
3. A company has assets of £340,000 and equity of £210,000. Calculate its liabilities. [2 marks]
4. Who sets IFRS Accounting Standards? Choose every correct option.
   A. Individual company directors
   B. The International Accounting Standards Board (IASB)
   C. Each company's external auditor
   D. Shareholders at the AGM
5. Explain the difference between the role of company directors and the role of an external auditor in relation to a company's financial statements. [4 marks]
6. Which is an example of a liability? Choose every correct option.
   A. Cash at bank
   B. Trade payables
   C. Inventory
   D. A motor vehicle owned by the business

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
3. [2] M1 Liabilities = Assets - Equity = 340,000 - 210,000; A1 = £130,000.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [4] B2 directors are responsible for keeping proper accounting records and preparing financial statements that give a true and fair view, and for safeguarding assets and preventing/detecting fraud and error; B2 the external auditor independently examines those financial statements and expresses an opinion on whether they give a true and fair view, but does not prepare them and gives reasonable, not absolute, assurance.
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
