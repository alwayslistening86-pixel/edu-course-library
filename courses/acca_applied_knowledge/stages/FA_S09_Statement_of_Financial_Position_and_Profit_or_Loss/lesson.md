# FA_S09_Statement_of_Financial_Position_and_Profit_or_Loss - Lesson: Statement of financial position and statement of profit or loss

## Goal
The learner prepares a basic statement of financial position and statement of profit or loss (and other comprehensive income) for a sole trader/simple company from a trial balance and adjustments, identifies required disclosure notes, and treats events after the reporting period.

## Syllabus items taught here
- FA.G1 - Statement of financial position
- FA.G2 - Statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income
- FA.G3 - Disclosure notes
- FA.G4 - Events after the reporting period

## How to teach this
Ask: 'You have a full trial balance and a list of year-end adjustments (depreciation, accruals, closing inventory). What order do you work through them in?' Have the learner predict the result of every example before running it, and run code for real whenever they can.

#### FA.G1 Statement of financial position
The **statement of financial position** presents, at the reporting date: **non-current assets** (at carrying amount - cost less accumulated depreciation/amortisation), then **current assets** (inventory, trade receivables, prepayments, cash - normally listed in increasing order of liquidity, cash last, or per local convention), giving total assets; **equity** (share capital, share premium, retained earnings, for a company; capital account for a sole trader); **non-current liabilities** (e.g. long-term loans); and **current liabilities** (trade payables, accruals, tax payable, the current portion of any loan) - with total equity + total liabilities equalling total assets, confirming the accounting equation.

#### FA.G2 Statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income
The **statement of profit or loss** presents, for the period: revenue, less cost of sales (opening inventory + purchases - closing inventory, for a trader), giving gross profit; less operating expenses (distribution costs, administrative expenses, including depreciation and any allowance-for-receivables movement); less finance costs (loan interest); giving profit for the period. **Other comprehensive income** captures items IFRS require outside profit or loss (e.g. a revaluation surplus on property), combined with profit for the period to give total comprehensive income - not examined in depth at this level, but the learner should recognise the heading. *Worked example*: revenue £180,000; opening inventory £14,000; purchases £96,000; closing inventory £18,000; operating expenses £42,000; finance costs £3,000. Cost of sales = 14,000 + 96,000 - 18,000 = £{14000 + 96000 - 18000:,}; gross profit = 180,000 - {14000 + 96000 - 18000:,} = £{180000 - (14000 + 96000 - 18000):,}; profit for the period = {180000 - (14000 + 96000 - 18000):,} - 42,000 - 3,000 = £{(180000 - (14000 + 96000 - 18000)) - 42000 - 3000:,}.

#### FA.G3 Disclosure notes
**Disclosure notes** accompany the financial statements to give users information not evident from the statements themselves: accounting policies applied (e.g. the depreciation method and rates used, inventory valuation method), a breakdown/movement schedule of non-current assets (cost, additions, disposals, accumulated depreciation), details of provisions and contingent liabilities (FA.D9), related party transactions, and post-reporting-period events (FA.G4). Notes are an integral part of the financial statements, not optional extra reading.

#### FA.G4 Events after the reporting period
**Events after the reporting period** (IAS 10) are events occurring between the reporting date and the date the financial statements are authorised for issue. **Adjusting events** provide further evidence of conditions that existed *at* the reporting date (e.g. a customer's insolvency shortly after the year end confirming a receivable was already irrecoverable at that date; a court case settled after the year end confirming a present obligation existed at the year end) - the financial statements are adjusted to reflect them. **Non-adjusting events** relate to conditions arising *after* the reporting date (e.g. a fire destroying a warehouse after the year end, a major acquisition announced after the year end) - not adjusted for, but disclosed by note if material, since users need to know about them even though they don't change the figures at the reporting date itself.

## Explicitly not here
The statement of cash flows and incomplete records are FA_S10.
