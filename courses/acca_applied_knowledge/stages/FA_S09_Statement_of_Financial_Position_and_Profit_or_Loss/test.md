# FA_S09_Statement_of_Financial_Position_and_Profit_or_Loss - Test: Statement of financial position and statement of profit or loss

## How to run this

## Test items
1. Revenue is £260,000, opening inventory £21,000, purchases £142,000, closing inventory £26,000, operating expenses £58,000, and finance costs £4,500. Prepare the statement of profit or loss down to profit for the period. [5 marks]
2. Which of these appears within current assets on the statement of financial position? Choose every correct option.
   A. A long-term bank loan
   B. Trade receivables
   C. Share capital
   D. Accumulated depreciation on a building
3. A customer that owed the business money at the year end is declared insolvent one month after the year end, before the financial statements are authorised for issue. This is: Choose every correct option.
   A. a non-adjusting event, disclosed by note only
   B. an adjusting event, since it provides evidence of conditions existing at the reporting date
   C. irrelevant and requires no action
   D. always ignored unless the amount is immaterial
4. A warehouse is destroyed by fire two weeks after the year end, but before the financial statements are authorised for issue. Explain how this should be treated under IAS 10, and why. [3 marks]
5. Which of these would normally be disclosed in the notes to the financial statements rather than shown as a separate line in the primary statements? Choose every correct option.
   A. Total revenue for the period
   B. The depreciation method and rates applied to non-current assets
   C. Total assets
   D. Profit for the period
6. In the accounting equation as reflected in the statement of financial position, total assets equal: Choose every correct option.
   A. total liabilities only
   B. total equity only
   C. total equity plus total liabilities
   D. total revenue less total expenses

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 cost of sales = 21,000 + 142,000 - 26,000 = £137,000; A1 gross profit = 260,000 - 137,000 = £123,000; M1 less operating expenses 58,000; M1 less finance costs 4,500; A1 profit for the period = £60,500.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. [3] B1 this is a non-adjusting event, since the fire is a condition arising after the reporting date, not evidence of a condition that already existed at that date; B2 the statement of financial position and statement of profit or loss for the year are not adjusted for it, but if material it is disclosed by note (nature of the event and an estimate of its financial effect), since users need to know about it even though it doesn't change the year-end figures themselves.
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
6. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
