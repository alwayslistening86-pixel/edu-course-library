# FA_S03_Sales_Purchases_and_Cash - Test: Sales, purchases and cash

## How to run this

## Test items
1. A trade discount is: Choose every correct option.
   A. deducted before the transaction is recorded, so only the net amount is recorded
   B. recorded as a separate expense
   C. the same as sales tax
   D. only available on early payment
2. Goods with a list price of £8,000 are purchased subject to a 12.5% trade discount, and sales tax is then charged at 20% on the discounted price. Calculate the amount recorded as purchases and the sales tax on the transaction. [3 marks]
3. Under the imprest system for petty cash, the float is: Choose every correct option.
   A. allowed to run down over time with no reimbursement
   B. topped up periodically by exactly the amount spent, restoring the fixed level
   C. increased every month regardless of spending
   D. abolished once a business exceeds a certain size
4. A petty cash imprest is £200. At the end of the month, vouchers total £164 and cash remaining is counted at £30. Calculate the amount needed to restore the imprest to £200, and state whether there is a shortfall or surplus against the vouchers, and by how much. [4 marks]
5. Sales tax (VAT) collected by a business on its sales is: Choose every correct option.
   A. revenue of the business
   B. an expense of the business
   C. collected on behalf of, and owed to, the tax authority
   D. always irrecoverable
6. A settlement (early payment) discount differs from a trade discount in that it is: Choose every correct option.
   A. always deducted before the sale is recorded, regardless of payment date
   B. only relevant to inventory valuation, not sales
   C. conditional on prompt payment, and only reflected if/when actually taken
   D. never allowed under IFRS
