## Test items
1. Which conflict resolution approach involves working together to find a solution meeting both parties' underlying interests? Choose every correct option.
   A. Avoidance
   B. Accommodation
   C. Collaboration
   D. Competition
2. Explain why a difficult, sensitive message (such as a disciplinary matter) is usually best communicated face-to-face rather than by email. [3 marks]
3. Which of these are common sources of workplace conflict? Choose every correct option.
   A. Competition for scarce budget/resources
   B. Unclear or overlapping responsibilities
   C. Perfectly clear and consistent communication
   D. Personality clashes
4. A personal development plan (PDP) is best described as: Choose every correct option.
   A. a legal requirement for all employees
   B. a record of an individual's identified development goals, actions and timescales, reviewed periodically
   C. a synonym for a job description
   D. a one-off document never revisited
5. Explain what is meant by 'noise' in the communication process, giving one example. [2 marks]
6. Avoidance as a conflict resolution style is most appropriate when: Choose every correct option.
   A. the issue is serious and urgent
   B. the issue is trivial or the timing is poor, and it is unlikely to resurface
   C. the relationship with the other party does not matter at all
   D. both parties have equally strong, opposing, important interests

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. [3] B3 a rich, face-to-face channel allows tone of voice and body language to convey nuance and empathy, allows immediate two-way feedback so misunderstanding can be corrected at once, and reduces the risk of the message being misread as overly blunt or impersonal, which a written channel with no immediate feedback loop cannot offer.
3. Correct: A, B, D (exactly these options, no others)
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [2] B1 noise is anything that distorts or interferes with a message between sender and receiver, so it is understood less accurately than intended; B1 example: technical jargon the receiver doesn't understand, a poor phone line, a noisy open-plan office, or a poorly timed message delivered when the receiver is distracted.
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
