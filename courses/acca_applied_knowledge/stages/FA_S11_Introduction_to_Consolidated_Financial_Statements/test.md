## Test items
1. Consolidated financial statements combine a parent and its subsidiary by: Choose every correct option.
   A. adding the parent's ownership percentage of the subsidiary's figures only
   B. adding 100% of the subsidiary's assets, liabilities, income and expenses, with non-controlling interest shown separately
   C. showing the subsidiary only as a single investment line, never combining figures
   D. using the equity method
2. A parent acquires 75% of a subsidiary for £510,000 cash. At acquisition, the subsidiary's identifiable net assets have a fair value of £600,000, and NCI is measured at its proportionate share of net assets. Calculate NCI at acquisition and goodwill. [4 marks]
3. A shareholding of 25%, with a seat on the investee's board and participation in its policy-making, is most likely to be classified as: Choose every correct option.
   A. a subsidiary, requiring full consolidation
   B. an associate, accounted for using the equity method
   C. a simple financial asset investment, at fair value only
   D. a joint venture, requiring proportionate consolidation
4. An investor holds a 40% stake in an associate, carried at £120,000 at the start of the year. During the year the associate makes a profit of £60,000 and pays a dividend, of which the investor receives £8,000. Calculate the carrying amount of the investment at the year end under the equity method. [4 marks]
5. Non-controlling interest represents: Choose every correct option.
   A. the parent's own share of the subsidiary
   B. the portion of a subsidiary's equity not owned by the parent
   C. goodwill arising on consolidation
   D. the associate's share of the investor's profit

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [4] M1 NCI = 600,000 x 25% = £150,000; A1 = £150,000; M1 goodwill = 510,000 + 150,000 - 600,000; A1 = £60,000.
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. [4] M1 share of associate's profit = 60,000 x 40% = £24,000, added to the carrying amount; M1 dividend received of £8,000 is deducted from the carrying amount (not double-counted as separate income); A1 year-end carrying amount = 120,000 + 24,000 - 8,000; A1 = £136,000.
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
