## Test items
1. A division has operating profit of £120,000 and capital employed of £600,000; the company's cost of capital is 10%. Calculate the division's ROCE and residual income. [4 marks]
2. Which is a weakness of using ROCE alone to evaluate divisional managers? Choose every correct option.
   A. It is too complex to calculate
   B. A high-ROCE division may reject a project that earns more than the cost of capital because it would dilute the division's average ROCE
   C. It never uses profit
   D. It ignores capital employed entirely
3. Which are perspectives in Kaplan and Norton's Balanced Scorecard? Choose every correct option.
   A. Financial
   B. Customer
   C. Competitor pricing only
   D. Internal business process
4. Explain the difference between cost reduction and simple, unplanned cost cutting. [3 marks]
5. Which is most likely to be a non-financial performance indicator? Choose every correct option.
   A. Gross profit margin
   B. On-time delivery percentage
   C. Residual income
   D. Return on capital employed

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 ROCE = 120,000/600,000 x 100 = 20%; A1 = 20%; M1 RI = 120,000 - (600,000 x 0.10); A1 = £60,000.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. Correct: A, B, D (exactly these options, no others)
4. [3] B3 cost reduction is a planned, systematic effort to permanently lower cost without impairing the essential function/quality the customer values (e.g. value analysis, process redesign); unplanned cost cutting is an across-the-board or reactive reduction that risks damaging quality, staff morale or long-term capability, for a possibly temporary saving.
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
