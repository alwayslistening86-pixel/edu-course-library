# FA_S02_Accounting_Principles_and_Double_Entry_Systems - Test: Accounting principles and double-entry systems

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of the certification's items (code-output, multiple-select and short code-writing). Give all the items at once, with no hints and no running of code until every answer is in. Then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Which accounting concept requires the same accounting treatment to be applied to similar items from one period to the next? Choose every correct option.
   A. Materiality
   B. Consistency
   C. Prudence
   D. Going concern
2. Which are the two fundamental qualitative characteristics of useful financial information under the IASB's Conceptual Framework? Choose every correct option.
   A. Relevance
   B. Timeliness
   C. Faithful representation
   D. Understandability
3. A business receives £6,000 cash from a customer in full settlement of an outstanding trade receivable. State the double entry. [2 marks]
4. Which of these correctly states the rule for recording an increase in an expense? Choose every correct option.
   A. Credit the expense account
   B. Debit the expense account
   C. Expenses are never recorded in the ledger
   D. Debit the income statement directly, bypassing the ledger
5. A business buys a delivery van for £18,000, paying £5,000 cash immediately and the rest by taking out a loan. Journalise this transaction. [3 marks]
6. The concept that financial statements are prepared on the assumption a business will continue operating for the foreseeable future is: Choose every correct option.
   A. prudence
   B. materiality
   C. going concern
   D. substance over form

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: A, C (exactly these options, no others)
3. [2] B1 Dr Cash £6,000 (asset increases); B1 Cr Trade receivables £6,000 (asset decreases).
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [3] B1 Dr Non-current assets (van) £18,000; B1 Cr Cash £5,000; B1 Cr Loan payable £13,000.
6. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
