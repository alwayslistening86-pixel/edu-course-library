# BT_S08_Professional_Ethics - Test: Professional ethics

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of the certification's items (code-output, multiple-select and short code-writing). Give all the items at once, with no hints and no running of code until every answer is in. Then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A trainee accountant is asked by a manager to review figures they themselves originally prepared, creating a risk of insufficient challenge. This is an example of a: Choose every correct option.
   A. self-interest threat
   B. self-review threat
   C. advocacy threat
   D. intimidation threat
2. Which of these threatens the fundamental principle of confidentiality? Choose every correct option.
   A. Using client information obtained professionally for personal share trading
   B. Failing to keep up to date with a new accounting standard
   C. Being unduly influenced by a close friendship with a client
   D. Publicly criticising the profession in a way that damages its reputation
3. An accountant is asked by their employer to record a sale in this financial year when the goods will not actually be delivered until after the year end, to help meet a sales target. Identify which fundamental principle(s) this threatens and briefly explain why. [4 marks]
4. Which best distinguishes professional self-regulation (e.g. by ACCA) from external statutory regulation? Choose every correct option.
   A. They are exactly the same thing
   B. Professional bodies set and enforce standards for their own members; statutory regulators enforce legal requirements more broadly, both aiming to protect public trust
   C. Statutory regulators have no power to fine or sanction
   D. Professional bodies cannot discipline their members
5. Outline the steps an accountant should take when facing a serious ethical dilemma that cannot be resolved by discussion with their immediate manager. [4 marks]
6. A corporate code of ethics is most likely to be effective when: Choose every correct option.
   A. it is a lengthy document circulated once at induction and never mentioned again
   B. senior management visibly follow it and breaches have real consequences
   C. it exists only to satisfy external reviewers
   D. it applies only to junior staff

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] B2 integrity (recording a transaction that has not genuinely occurred, or in the wrong period, is dishonest); B2 (or) professional competence and due care/professional behaviour (financial statements must be prepared in accordance with applicable standards; going along with the request also risks the accountant's own professional reputation and standing).
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [4] B1 clarify the facts and identify which fundamental principle(s) and which type of threat is involved; B1 consult the organisation's own internal procedures (e.g. an ethics/compliance function, a whistleblowing line, or a more senior manager); B1 consider seeking advice from their professional body or independent legal advice if unresolved internally; B1 if the matter remains unresolved and is serious, consider declining further association with the matter, which may include resignation as a last resort.
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
