# BT_S08_Professional_Ethics - Practice: Professional ethics

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Which fundamental principle is most directly breached by an accountant who lets a close personal friendship with a client influence their professional judgement? Choose every correct option.
   A. Confidentiality
   B. Objectivity
   C. Professional competence and due care
   D. Professional behaviour
2. Name the five fundamental principles of ACCA's Code of Ethics and Conduct. [5 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [5] B1 integrity; B1 objectivity; B1 professional competence and due care; B1 confidentiality; B1 professional behaviour.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
