# MA_S04_Costing_Methods_and_Alternative_Principles - Practice: Costing methods and alternative principles

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Fixed overhead absorption rate is £6/unit. Production is 2,000 units and sales are 1,750 units. By how much, and in which direction, does absorption costing profit differ from marginal costing profit? [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 inventory rises by 2,000 - 1,750 = 250 units; M1 difference = 250 x £6; A1 absorption costing profit is £1,500 higher than marginal costing profit, because £1,500 of fixed overhead is carried forward in closing inventory rather than expensed.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
