# FA_S05_Non_Current_Assets_Depreciation_and_Intangibles - Test: Non-current assets, depreciation and intangibles

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of the certification's items (code-output, multiple-select and short code-writing). Give all the items at once, with no hints and no running of code until every answer is in. Then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A vehicle costs £22,000 and is depreciated at 25% per year on the reducing balance basis. Calculate the depreciation charge and carrying amount at the end of years 1 and 2. [5 marks]
2. A machine costing £35,000 has accumulated depreciation of £21,000 when it is sold for £11,000. Calculate the profit or loss on disposal. [3 marks]
3. Which of these is capitalised as part of the cost of a new machine, rather than expensed? Choose every correct option.
   A. Staff training on how to use the machine
   B. General factory overheads for the period
   C. Delivery and installation costs to bring the machine into use
   D. A repair carried out two years after purchase to fix normal wear and tear
4. Development expenditure is capitalised as an intangible asset only when: Choose every correct option.
   A. management simply decides it should be
   B. strict IAS 38 criteria are met, including technical feasibility and probable future economic benefit
   C. the amount spent exceeds £100,000
   D. it relates to pure/basic research
5. A patent is purchased for £45,000 with a useful life of 9 years, and amortised on a straight-line basis. Calculate the annual amortisation charge and the carrying amount after 4 years. [4 marks]
6. Research costs (as distinct from qualifying development costs) are: Choose every correct option.
   A. always capitalised as an intangible asset
   B. always expensed as incurred
   C. capitalised only if the amount is material
   D. amortised over the research project's expected life

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 year 1 depreciation = 22,000 x 0.25 = £5,500; A1 year 1 carrying amount = £16,500; M1 year 2 depreciation = 16,500 x 0.25 = £4,125; A1 year 2 carrying amount = £12,375; B1 correct method: reducing balance applies the rate to the carrying amount each year, not the original cost.
2. [3] M1 carrying amount = 35,000 - 21,000 = £14,000; M1 compare with proceeds £11,000; A1 loss on disposal = 14,000 - 11,000 = £3,000 (a loss, since proceeds are below carrying amount).
3. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [4] M1 annual amortisation = 45,000/9 = £5,000; A1 after 4 years, accumulated amortisation = 4 x 5,000 = £20,000; A1 carrying amount = 45,000 - 20,000 = £25,000; B1 correct method: an intangible asset with a finite useful life is amortised on a systematic basis (straight-line here) over that life.
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
