# BT_S02_Micro_Social_Technological_Environmental_and_Competitive_Factors - Practice: Microeconomic, social, technological, environmental and competitive factors

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Which market structure has many small firms selling an identical product, with no individual firm able to influence the market price? Choose every correct option.
   A. Monopoly
   B. Oligopoly
   C. Perfect competition
   D. Monopolistic competition
2. Name the Porter's Five Forces element illustrated by: 'a new budget airline finds it very expensive to lease aircraft and secure airport landing slots, discouraging it from entering the market.' [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. [1] B1 the threat of new entrants (here, kept low by high barriers to entry).

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
