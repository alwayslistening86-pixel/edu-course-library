# BT_S01_Business_Purpose_Stakeholders_and_Political_Legal_Macro_Factors - Practice: Business purpose, stakeholders, and political/legal/macroeconomic factors

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A group of individuals trading together, sharing profits, where an ordinary partner has unlimited liability for the firm's debts, is a: Choose every correct option.
   A. sole trader
   B. partnership
   C. private limited company
   D. public limited company
2. Using Mendelow's power/interest matrix, name the stakeholder category for (a) a large institutional shareholder with significant voting power who closely monitors the company, and (b) a member of the public with no power over the company and little interest in it. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [2] B1 (a) key players (high power, high interest - manage closely); B1 (b) minimal effort (low power, low interest).

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
