## Test items
1. Which is the defining feature of limited liability? Choose every correct option.
   A. Owners cannot be sued
   B. Owners' loss is limited to the amount they invested
   C. The business pays no tax
   D. The business cannot fail
2. Which of these are connected stakeholders (as opposed to internal or external)? Choose every correct option.
   A. Employees
   B. Suppliers
   C. Customers
   D. The local community
3. A local council is considering a large new factory near a residential area. Identify one internal, one connected and one external stakeholder in this situation, giving a reason for each classification. [3 marks]
4. Which of these is a political rather than a legal factor? Choose every correct option.
   A. Minimum wage legislation
   B. A change of government policy on trade tariffs
   C. Data protection law
   D. Unfair dismissal law
5. A central bank raises interest rates sharply. Explain two likely effects on a business that relies on bank borrowing. [4 marks]
6. Which stage of the trade/business cycle is characterised by falling output, rising unemployment and falling business confidence? Choose every correct option.
   A. Boom
   B. Recovery
   C. Recession
   D. Peak

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: B, C (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B1 internal: the factory's own employees/management (part of the organisation); B1 connected: a key supplier or a lender financing the build (a direct, contractual relationship); B1 external: local residents/the community (affected, but with no direct/contractual relationship).
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [4] B2 higher cost of servicing existing variable-rate/new debt, reducing profit and possibly investment; B2 (or) reduced consumer spending as mortgage/credit costs rise, lowering demand for the business's products; credit any two developed, relevant points (e.g. exchange rate appreciation making exports less competitive).
6. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
