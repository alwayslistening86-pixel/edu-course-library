# MA_S02_Data_Analysis_and_Statistical_Techniques - Practice: Data analysis and statistical techniques

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A firm wants to survey opinions across its 3 factories, which employ very different numbers of staff. It samples staff randomly within each factory, in proportion to that factory's headcount. Name this sampling method and explain why it was chosen over simple random sampling. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 stratified sampling; B1 ensures every factory (stratum) is fairly represented in proportion to its size, which pure random sampling might not achieve by chance.
