# FA_S08_Trial_Balance_Errors_and_Suspense_Accounts - Practice: Trial balance, correction of errors and suspense accounts

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or writes first, then runs it to check. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A £600 cash purchase of stationery was correctly credited to Cash but debited to Motor vehicles instead of Stationery expense. State the type of error and whether the trial balance would still balance. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 error of principle (posted to the wrong type/class of account); B1 the trial balance would still balance, since equal debit and credit entries of £600 were both made, just to the wrong account.

## How to run it
One item at a time. For output questions the learner commits to a prediction before running anything. Offer a worked explanation only after an attempt.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
