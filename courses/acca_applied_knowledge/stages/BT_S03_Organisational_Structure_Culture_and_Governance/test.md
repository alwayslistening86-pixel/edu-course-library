## Test items
1. Which best describes the informal organisation? Choose every correct option.
   A. The officially published organisation chart
   B. Unofficial relationships and communication networks that exist alongside the formal structure
   C. A legal requirement for public companies only
   D. A synonym for organisational culture
2. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of a tall organisational structure compared with a flat one. [4 marks]
3. Which is a defining feature of role culture in Handy's typology? Choose every correct option.
   A. Power concentrated in one individual
   B. A bureaucracy of defined jobs, procedures and hierarchy
   C. Flexible project teams organised around tasks
   D. The organisation exists to serve a small number of experts
4. Explain why separating the roles of Chair and Chief Executive is regarded as good corporate governance practice. [3 marks]
5. Which of these are examples of sustainable/ESG business practice? Choose every correct option.
   A. Publishing a sustainability report alongside the financial statements
   B. Reducing supply chain carbon emissions
   C. Maximising short-term profit regardless of environmental impact
   D. Fair treatment of employees and local communities
6. A large retailer decentralises pricing decisions to individual store managers rather than setting one national price from head office. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of this decentralisation. [4 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [4] B2 advantage: narrow spans of control allow closer supervision and clearer, more specialised reporting lines (any valid developed point); B2 disadvantage: more layers slow decision-making and communication, and add management cost (any valid developed point).
3. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
4. 5. Correct: A, B, D (exactly these options, no others)
