## Test items
1. Which function is primarily responsible for the statutory financial statements produced for shareholders and external users? Choose every correct option.
   A. Treasury
   B. Financial accounting
   C. Internal audit
   D. Tax
2. Which are examples of financial crime? Choose every correct option.
   A. Fraud
   B. Money laundering
   C. A legitimate, disclosed tax planning arrangement
   D. Bribery
3. Explain why internal audit is usually structured to report to an audit committee (those charged with governance) rather than to the finance director it may be reviewing. [3 marks]
4. Management accounting information, compared with statutory financial statements, is typically: Choose every correct option.
   A. required by law to follow accounting standards
   B. produced only annually
   C. tailored to internal decision-making needs, with no fixed external format
   D. aimed solely at shareholders
5. Explain the difference between fraud and money laundering. [3 marks]
6. Which body typically sets a company's mandatory accounting standards for its statutory financial statements? Choose every correct option.
   A. The company's own management
   B. An accounting standard-setter (e.g. the IASB, via applicable national adoption)
   C. The sales department
   D. There is no such body

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: A, B, D (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B3 reporting to an audit committee/governance body, rather than to the operational management whose controls it reviews, preserves internal audit's independence and objectivity, so its findings are not influenced by the very managers being assessed.
4. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
5. [3] B3 fraud is dishonestly obtaining a financial advantage or causing loss through deception (the underlying wrongdoing that often generates illicit proceeds); money laundering is the separate process of disguising the origin of proceeds already obtained (often through crime such as fraud) so they appear to come from a legitimate source.
6. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
