# BT_S06_Leadership_Management_and_Teams - Test: Leadership, management, teams and motivation

## Test items
1. Which leadership style involves the leader making decisions alone with little consultation? Choose every correct option.
   A. Democratic
   B. Laissez-faire
   C. Autocratic
   D. Situational
2. Which are recognised risks of group working? Choose every correct option.
   A. Groupthink
   B. Social loafing
   C. Guaranteed higher-quality decisions than an individual working alone
   D. Diffusion of individual responsibility
3. Explain the difference between Maslow's hierarchy of needs and Herzberg's two-factor theory as explanations of workplace motivation. [4 marks]
4. In Tuckman's model, at which stage is a team working most effectively towards its goal, with roles and ways of working well established? Choose every correct option.
   A. Forming
   B. Storming
   C. Norming
   D. Performing
5. Explain why SMART objectives are recommended for a staff appraisal. [3 marks]
6. Which is an example of off-the-job training? Choose every correct option.
   A. Learning a new till system by using it during a normal shift
   B. Shadowing a colleague while doing the actual job
   C. Attending an external management development course
   D. Receiving informal tips from a colleague during the working day

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
2. Correct: A, B, D (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] B2 Maslow proposes a hierarchy of needs (physiological through to self-actualisation) that must generally be satisfied roughly in order before higher-level needs motivate strongly; B2 Herzberg instead splits factors into hygiene factors (whose absence causes dissatisfaction but whose presence does not strongly motivate, e.g. pay) and motivators (which actively drive satisfaction and motivation, e.g. achievement and recognition), rather than a strict hierarchy.
4. Correct: D (exactly these options, no others)
5. [3] B3 objectives that are Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound give a clear, agreed, objective basis against which actual performance can later be assessed, reducing ambiguity and disagreement, and making the next review meaningfully comparable to what was agreed.
6. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
