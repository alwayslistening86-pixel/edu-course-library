# ACCA Applied Knowledge: Business and Technology, Management Accounting, Financial Accounting (BT/FBT, MA/FMA, FA/FFA) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Three mini-papers in sequence, each in the paper's own real proportions: BT items from BT_S01-BT_S08; MA items from MA_S01-MA_S08; FA items from FA_S01-FA_S12. Mix single-select, multiple-response and short-calculation/number-entry items reflecting Section A and Section B question styles, in non-sequential order within each paper. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank across all three papers; the tutor writes further items in the same proportions and style to build a complete paper.

## 16 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [BT] Which type of business organisation gives its owners limited liability but cannot offer its shares to the public? Choose every correct option.
   A. Sole trader
   B. Ordinary partnership
   C. Private limited company
   D. Public limited company
2. [BT] Using Mendelow's power/interest matrix, a stakeholder with high power and high interest should be: Choose every correct option.
   A. given minimal effort
   B. kept informed only
   C. kept satisfied only
   D. managed closely as a key player
3. [BT] Which internal control splits authorisation, custody and recording of a transaction across different people? Choose every correct option.
   A. Physical controls
   B. Segregation of duties
   C. Arithmetic controls
   D. Blockchain
4. [BT] Explain the difference between leadership and management. [4 marks]
5. [BT] Which fundamental ethical principle is most directly threatened when an accountant reviews figures they themselves originally prepared? Choose every correct option.
   A. Confidentiality (self-interest threat)
   B. Objectivity (self-review threat)
   C. Professional competence (advocacy threat)
   D. Professional behaviour (familiarity threat)
6. [MA] Total cost is £30,000 at 5,000 units and £38,000 at 9,000 units. Use the high-low method to find the variable cost per unit and the fixed cost. [3 marks]
7. [MA] Which inventory valuation method is not permitted under IAS 2? Choose every correct option.
   A. FIFO
   B. AVCO
   C. LIFO
   D. Standard cost
8. [MA] Budgeted sales are 6,000 units, desired closing inventory is 500 units, opening inventory is 300 units. Calculate the production budget. [2 marks]
9. [MA] Which appraisal method(s) account for the time value of money? Choose every correct option.
   A. Payback period
   B. Net present value
   C. Accounting rate of return
   D. None of these
10. [MA] Operating profit is £96,000 and capital employed is £480,000. Calculate ROCE. [2 marks]
11. [FA] Which best states the accounting equation? Choose every correct option.
   A. Assets = Liabilities - Equity
   B. Assets = Liabilities + Equity
   C. Assets + Liabilities = Equity
   D. Equity = Assets + Liabilities
12. [FA] Opening inventory nil. Purchases: 300 units @ £5.00, then 400 units @ £5.50. 500 units are issued. Calculate the closing inventory value using FIFO. [2 marks]
13. [FA] A machine costs £28,000, has a residual value of £3,000 and a 5-year useful life. Calculate the annual straight-line depreciation charge. [2 marks]
14. [FA] Under IAS 37, a provision is recognised when: Choose every correct option.
   A. an outflow is merely possible
   B. there is a present obligation from a past event, an outflow is probable, and the amount can be reliably estimated
   C. the amount cannot be estimated at all
   D. the obligation depends wholly on a future event outside the entity's control
15. [FA] Revenue is £120,000 and gross profit is £45,000. Calculate the gross profit margin. [2 marks]
16. [FA] Current assets are £150,000 (including inventory £60,000) and current liabilities are £100,000. What is the quick (acid test) ratio? Choose every correct option.
   A. 1.50:1
   B. 0.90:1
   C. 0.60:1
   D. 2.50:1
