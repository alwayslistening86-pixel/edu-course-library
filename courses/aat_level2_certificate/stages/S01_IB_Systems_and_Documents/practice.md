# S01_IB_Systems_and_Documents - Practice: Business documents, prime-entry books, coding and bookkeeping systems

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A business has assets of £24,000 and liabilities of £9,750. Calculate its equity (capital). [2 marks]
2. Which document is sent by a supplier listing all invoices, credit notes and payments on an account, and the resulting balance? Choose every correct option.
   A. a statement of account
   B. a remittance advice
   C. a petty cash voucher
   D. a credit note

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 24,000 - 9,750; A1 £14,250.
2. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
