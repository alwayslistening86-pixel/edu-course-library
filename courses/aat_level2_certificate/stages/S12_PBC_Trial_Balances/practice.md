# S12_PBC_Trial_Balances - Practice: Trial balances

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A trial balance's debit column totals £35,600 and its credit column totals £35,600. What does this confirm (and what does it NOT guarantee)? [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)

[2] B1 it confirms that total debits equal total credits, which supports (but does not prove) double entry was applied correctly; B1 it does not catch errors not disclosed by the trial balance (e.g. errors of commission, omission, principle, original entry, reversal or compensating errors -- see PBC3.2).

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
