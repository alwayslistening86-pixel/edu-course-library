## Test items
1. Explain the five fundamental principles of ethics for accounting technicians. [5 marks]
2. Explain the trade-off a business might face between sustainability and profit, giving an example. [3 marks]
3. Explain why an accounting technician has a public interest duty, and give one consequence of behaving unethically. [3 marks]
4. Which fundamental principle of ethics means not disclosing information acquired professionally without proper authority? Choose every correct option.
   A. confidentiality
   B. objectivity
   C. integrity
   D. professional behaviour

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] B1 integrity: being straightforward and honest; B1 objectivity: not allowing bias or conflict of interest to override professional judgement; B1 professional competence and due care: maintaining knowledge/skill and acting diligently; B1 confidentiality: not disclosing or misusing information acquired professionally; B1 professional behaviour: complying with relevant laws/regulations and avoiding conduct that discredits the profession.
2. [3] B1 more sustainable practices can cost more (e.g. lower-emission materials, cleaner production processes), reducing short-term profit; B1 but they may protect long-term profitability, reputation, or regulatory compliance; B1 valid example given (e.g. switching to more expensive recycled materials).
3. [3] B1 accountants hold positions of trust, and people beyond their immediate employer/client (e.g. investors, lenders, tax authorities) rely on the integrity of financial information they produce; B1 this means the profession's duty extends to the wider public interest, not just the employer/client; B1 valid consequence, e.g. disciplinary action by AAT, legal consequences, reputational or financial damage.
4. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
