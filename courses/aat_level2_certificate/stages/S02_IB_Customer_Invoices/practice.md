# S02_IB_Customer_Invoices - Practice: Customer invoices, credit notes and books of prime entry

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A customer orders goods with a list price of £400 and a 10% trade discount. Calculate the discounted net, the VAT at 20% and the invoice total. [3 marks]
2. An invoice is raised for net £800, VAT £160 (total £960), with a 2% PPD available if paid within 14 days. The customer pays in time. Calculate the credit note's discount, its VAT and its total. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 net = 400 x 0.9 = £360; A1 VAT = 360 x 0.2 = £72; A1 total = £432.
2. [3] M1 discount = 800 x 0.02 = £16; M1 VAT on discount = 16 x 0.2 = £3.20; A1 credit note total = £19.20.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
