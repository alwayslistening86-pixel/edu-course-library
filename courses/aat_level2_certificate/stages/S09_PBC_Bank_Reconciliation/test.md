# S09_PBC_Bank_Reconciliation - Test: Bank reconciliation

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AAT's own style: numeric gap-fill calculations, journal/ledger-entry questions and multiple-choice items, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints and a calculator allowed (as in the real assessment). Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain the difference between an unpresented cheque and an outstanding lodgement, and how each is treated when reconciling the bank statement balance to the cash book. [4 marks]
2. A cash book shows a balance of £2,340.00. The bank statement reveals interest received of £18.60 and bank charges of £40.00, neither yet recorded in the cash book. Calculate the updated cash book balance. [3 marks]
3. A bank statement shows a closing balance of £3,890.20. There is an unpresented cheque of £520.00 and an outstanding lodgement of £275.50. Calculate the reconciled balance that should match the cash book. [3 marks]
4. Which payment method typically reduces the bank balance on the same day the payment is made? Choose every correct option.
   A. a Faster Payment
   B. a cheque
   C. a standing order set up but not yet due
   D. none of these -- all reduce the balance later

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 an unpresented cheque is paid out by the business but not yet cleared by the bank; B1 it is deducted from the bank statement balance in the reconciliation; B1 an outstanding lodgement is paid in by the business but not yet shown on the statement; B1 it is added to the bank statement balance in the reconciliation.
2. [3] M1 2,340.00 + 18.60 - 40.00; A1 £2,318.60; B1 interest received added, bank charges deducted.
3. [3] M1 3,890.20 - 520.00 + 275.50; A1 £3,645.70; B1 unpresented cheque deducted, outstanding lodgement added.
4. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
