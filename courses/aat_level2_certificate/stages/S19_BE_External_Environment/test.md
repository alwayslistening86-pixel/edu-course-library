## Test items
1. Explain how a rise in UK interest rates might affect a small business that has a bank loan, and consumer spending more generally. [3 marks]
2. The pound sterling weakens against the US dollar. Explain the effect on a UK business that exports goods to the USA, and on a UK business that imports raw materials priced in US dollars. [4 marks]
3. Explain one benefit and one disadvantage of international trade for a UK business. [2 marks]
4. Which principle of an effective tax system means the taxpayer should know how much, when and how to pay? Choose every correct option.
   A. certain
   B. equitable
   C. convenient
   D. efficient

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 a business with a loan faces higher interest payments, increasing its costs; B1 higher rates also make saving more attractive and borrowing (including mortgages) more expensive for consumers, tending to reduce consumer spending; B1 lower consumer spending can reduce demand for the business's products, compounding the effect of higher borrowing costs.
2. [4] B1 the exporter's goods become cheaper for US buyers (in dollar terms), likely increasing demand for its exports; B1 this benefits the exporting business; B1 the importer must pay more pounds to buy the same quantity of dollar-priced materials, increasing its costs; B1 this disadvantages the importing business -- a weaker pound helps exporters and hurts importers.
3. [2] B1 benefit, e.g. access to larger markets, or access to cheaper/better-quality inputs, or increased competition driving efficiency; B1 disadvantage, e.g. exposure to foreign competition, exchange rate risk, or exposure to policy/events in other countries.
4. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
