# S22_BE_Finance_Function - Test: The finance function

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AAT's own style: numeric gap-fill calculations, journal/ledger-entry questions and multiple-choice items, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints and a calculator allowed (as in the real assessment). Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage to a business of outsourcing part of its finance function. [2 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by organisational solvency and describe one way the finance team helps maintain it. [3 marks]
3. Explain why effective communication by the finance team matters to the rest of the organisation. [2 marks]
4. Which of these is an example of the finance function serving an external stakeholder? Choose every correct option.
   A. filing statutory accounts at Companies House
   B. producing an internal budget for the sales department
   C. recruiting a new finance assistant
   D. setting an internal expenses policy

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 advantage, e.g. reduced cost, or access to specialist expertise; B1 disadvantage, e.g. reduced direct control, or data-security/confidentiality concerns.
2. [3] B1 solvency means the business can pay its debts as they fall due; B1 the finance team helps maintain it, e.g. through cash flow monitoring, or credit control (chasing customer payments), or managing when supplier payments are made; B1 clear link made between the finance team's activity and maintaining solvency.
3. [2] B1 financial information is only useful in decision-making if non-finance colleagues can actually understand it; B1 clear communication ensures management information/budgets are used effectively by the departments that receive them.
4. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
