## Test items
1. Explain why an accounting technician should assess whether a source of information is valid before using it in their work. [3 marks]
2. Explain two risks to a business of an employee sharing information inappropriately on social media. [2 marks]
3. Explain what is meant by prioritising workload, giving an example of how a finance employee might decide what to do first. [2 marks]
4. Which of these is an example of a primary source of information for a business? Choose every correct option.
   A. a customer survey the business conducts itself
   B. a published industry report summarising market trends
   C. a competitor's annual report
   D. a general news article about the economy

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 an invalid source (inaccurate, biased, outdated or unverifiable) could lead to incorrect conclusions or decisions being made; B1 relying on such a source in professional work risks breaching the fundamental principle of professional competence and due care; B1 checking validity (reliability, currency, credibility of origin) protects the quality and trustworthiness of the technician's own work.
2. [2] B1 reputational damage to the business from an ill-judged or unprofessional post; B1 breach of client/employer confidentiality, or legal risk (e.g. defamation, data protection breach) -- any two valid risks.
3. [2] B1 prioritising means deciding the order in which tasks are tackled, based on factors such as deadline, importance, or dependency on other tasks/people; B1 valid example, e.g. completing a task with an imminent deadline before a lower-priority task with no immediate deadline.
4. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
