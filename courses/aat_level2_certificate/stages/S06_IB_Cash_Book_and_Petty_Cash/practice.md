# S06_IB_Cash_Book_and_Petty_Cash - Practice: The cash book, petty cash book and recurring entries

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A cash book's receipts total £1,240.00 and its payments total £980.75. Calculate the balance carried down. [2 marks]
2. A petty cash float (imprest) is £150.00. Vouchers for the period total £62.40. Under the imprest system, how much is reimbursed at the end of the period, and what is the float restored to? [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 1,240.00 - 980.75; A1 £259.25.
2. [2] B1 £62.40 is reimbursed (the exact amount spent); B1 the float is restored to £150.00.

## How to run it
One item at a time. Let the learner finish a full attempt (workings shown) before revealing the answer; walk through any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
