## Test items
1. A customer's debt of £480.00 (including VAT at 20%) is written off as irrecoverable. Calculate the net (expense) amount and the VAT element, and state the journal entry (accounts, debit or credit, and amounts). [5 marks]
2. Explain why the VAT element of an irrecoverable debt is debited to the VAT control account when the debt is written off. [2 marks]
3. List three items recorded through the wages control account when processing payroll. [3 marks]
4. Which book of prime entry is used to record opening entries, irrecoverable debt write-offs and payroll transactions? Choose every correct option.
   A. the journal
   B. the sales daybook
   C. the cash book
   D. the purchases daybook

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 net = 480.00 / 1.20 = £400.00; A1 VAT = 480.00 - 400.00 = £80.00; B1 debit Irrecoverable Debts Expense £400.00; B1 debit VAT control account £80.00; B1 credit Receivables Control Account £480.00.
2. [2] B1 VAT was originally charged (output VAT, a credit) on the original sale, but since the debt will never be collected from the customer, that VAT can be reclaimed from HMRC; B1 debiting the VAT control account reduces the net amount owed to HMRC accordingly.
3. [3] B1 gross pay; B1 income tax; B1 employer's and/or employees' National Insurance Contributions; B1 employer's and/or employees' pension contributions; B1 voluntary deductions -- any 3 for full marks.
4. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
