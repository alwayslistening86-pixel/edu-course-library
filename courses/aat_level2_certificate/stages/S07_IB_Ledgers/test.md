## Test items
1. Explain the purpose of the receivables ledger control account, and how it relates to the individual customer accounts in the receivables ledger. [3 marks]
2. A supplier's account in the payables ledger has credit entries totalling £5,600 and debit entries totalling £2,300. Calculate the balance carried down and state whether it is a debit or credit balance. [2 marks]
3. Which ledger holds the receivables and payables control accounts? Choose every correct option.
   A. the general ledger
   B. the receivables ledger
   C. the payables ledger
   D. the cash book

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 the control account is a single general ledger account summarising, in total, what all credit customers owe, and is part of the double-entry system; B1 the individual customer accounts in the receivables ledger are memorandum accounts (not part of double-entry) giving the detail behind that total; B1 the two should agree in total, which is checked by reconciliation.
2. [2] M1 5,600 - 2,300 = £3,300; A1 credit balance (credits exceed debits, as expected for a liability/amount owed to a supplier).
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
