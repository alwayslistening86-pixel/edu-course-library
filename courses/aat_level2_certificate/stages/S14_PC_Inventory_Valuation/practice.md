# S14_PC_Inventory_Valuation - Practice: Inventory valuation: FIFO, LIFO and AVCO

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner works each calculation or entry in full before checking the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Opening inventory is 60 units at £4.00 each; 40 more units are received at £6.00 each; 70 units are issued. Calculate the FIFO issue cost. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 60 units at £4.00 = £240; M1 10 units at £6.00 = £60; A1 issue cost = £300.
