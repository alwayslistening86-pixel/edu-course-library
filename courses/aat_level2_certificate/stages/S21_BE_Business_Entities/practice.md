## Goal

## Practice items
1. State whether a sole trader has limited or unlimited liability, and what this means. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 unlimited liability; B1 the owner is personally liable for all business debts, putting personal assets at risk.
