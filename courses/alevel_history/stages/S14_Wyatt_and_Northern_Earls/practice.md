# S14_Wyatt_and_Northern_Earls - Practice: Wyatt's Rebellion, 1554, and the Northern Earls' Revolt, 1569-70

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give the immediate trigger for Wyatt's Rebellion (1554) and for the Northern Earls' Revolt (1569-70). [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 Wyatt's Rebellion: Mary I's announced marriage to Philip II of Spain; B1 Northern Earls' Revolt: the aim of restoring Catholicism and marrying Mary, Queen of Scots to the Duke of Norfolk to create an alternative succession claim.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
