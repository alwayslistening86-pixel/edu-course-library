## Test items
1. Explain how population growth affected English society across the sixteenth century. [6 marks]
2. 'The 1590s represented a serious crisis for Elizabethan government.' Assess the validity of this view, using the interpretations debate you have studied. [20 marks]
3. Which of the following was a direct concession forced from Elizabeth I in the parliamentary session of 1601? Choose every correct option.
   A. withdrawal of some royal monopolies
   B. acceptance of a named successor
   C. toleration of Catholic worship
   D. abolition of recusancy fines
