# S05_Economy_Society_Culture_and_Interpretations - Practice: Trade; the changing structure of society; cultural change; the 1589-1603 'crisis of government' debate

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two effects of the dissolution of the monasteries, 1536-40, on English society. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 transferred land to lay owners (often gentry), creating a class with a vested interest in the religious settlement; B2 ended monastic almsgiving/hospitality relied on by some poor (any second valid effect, e.g. loss of an educational/charitable provider).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
