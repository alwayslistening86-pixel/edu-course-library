## Test items
1. Explain why popular resistance to Tudor rule declined after 1570. [6 marks]
2. 'Justices of the Peace were more important than the nobility in maintaining Tudor control of the country.' Assess the validity of this view. [20 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 growing reach and effectiveness of local government (JPs, lords lieutenant, militias); B1 destruction of the last aristocratic leadership capable of large-scale rebellion after the Northern Earls' Revolt (1569-70); B1 decades of established Tudor rule normalised obedience/legitimacy; B1 more systematic poor relief gave local elites and some of the poor a stake in order rather than rebellion; B1 no single issue after 1570 (religion, succession, economic distress) combined as explosively as in 1536 or 1549; B1 government learned from earlier risings and responded faster/more effectively.
