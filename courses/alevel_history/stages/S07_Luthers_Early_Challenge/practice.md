# S07_Luthers_Early_Challenge - Practice: The Ninety-Five Theses; the shaping of Luther's beliefs; excommunication

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the importance of the printing press to the impact of the Ninety-Five Theses. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 Luther intended an academic debate sent through normal channels; B1 the theses were printed and translated without his control, spreading across Germany within weeks; B1 this gave the dispute a scale and speed the Church had no established way of containing, turning a local challenge into a national/European sensation.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
