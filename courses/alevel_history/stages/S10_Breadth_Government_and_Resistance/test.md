## Test items
1. Explain two ways in which Tudor governments' methods of maintaining control developed between 1485 and 1603. [6 marks]
2. 'Religious grievance was the most dangerous type of grievance behind Tudor rebellions, 1485-1603.' Assess the validity of this view. [20 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B3 growth and professionalisation of regional councils, especially the Council of the North after 1537; B3 the steadily expanding administrative/judicial role of Justices of the Peace; B3 the Elizabethan lords lieutenant giving faster, more direct county-level military mobilisation than earlier reliance on noble retainers alone; B3 growing government speed and effectiveness in responding to risings by the later sixteenth century compared with 1485 -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [20] Mark out of 20 using Edexcel's generic levels for extended writing: Level 1 (1-4) generalised/descriptive; Level 2 (5-8) limited explanation/analysis or mostly narrative with some analytical comment; Level 3 (9-12) explains relevant factors with some support but uneven coverage, reaching a partially supported judgement; Level 4 (13-16) well-organised, explains a range of factors with good evidence and a supported judgement; Level 5 (17-20) fully focused, weighs a range of factors with precise, wide-ranging evidence, sustained analysis and a clearly substantiated judgement. Credit: religious risings (the Pilgrimage of Grace, the Western Rebellion, the Northern Earls' Revolt) as the largest in scale and hardest to satisfy by concession, since they could unite disparate social groups behind a single cause; against/alongside this, political/dynastic grievance combined with sustained foreign state backing (Warbeck, Tyrone) proved equally or more durable and dangerous, while purely economic grievance (Kett's) mobilised huge numbers but was suppressed relatively quickly without external backing; a strong answer weighs danger by scale, duration and government response across more than one case rather than asserting a single type is always most dangerous.
