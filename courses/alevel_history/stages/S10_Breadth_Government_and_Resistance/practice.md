# S10_Breadth_Government_and_Resistance - Practice: Breadth: government methods of control and the typology of rebel grievance, 1485-1603

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give one example each of a rebellion primarily driven by (a) economic/social grievance and (b) religious grievance. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 (a) Kett's Rebellion, 1549 (enclosure/economic grievance); B1 (b) the Pilgrimage of Grace, 1536-37, or the Western Rebellion, 1549, or the Northern Earls' Revolt, 1569-70 (any valid religious rising).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
