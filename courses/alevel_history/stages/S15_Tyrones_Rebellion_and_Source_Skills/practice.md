# S15_Tyrones_Rebellion_and_Source_Skills - Practice: Tyrone's Rebellion, 1594-1603, and Paper 3 source-evaluation technique

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two elements combined in the causes of Tyrone's Rebellion, 1594-1603. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 Gaelic Irish political autonomy and resistance to intensifying English administrative/cultural control; B1 Catholic religious solidarity against Protestant England, reinforced by direct Spanish military backing (any two valid elements).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
