# S16_Synthesis_and_Exam_Technique - Practice: Synoptic comparison across 1485-1603, and exam technique for all three papers

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name one Tudor rebellion whose failure owed most to lack of sustained foreign backing, and one whose danger owed most to having secured it. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 e.g. Kett's Rebellion (1549), purely domestic, defeated relatively quickly; B1 e.g. Perkin Warbeck's conspiracy (with Scotland) or Tyrone's Rebellion (with Spain), far more dangerous/sustained (any valid, correctly matched pair).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
