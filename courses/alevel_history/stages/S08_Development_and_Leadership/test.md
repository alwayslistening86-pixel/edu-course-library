1. Explain the significance of the Augsburg Confession (1530) for the Lutheran movement. [6 marks]
2. 'Luther's response to the Peasants' War, 1525, did more to shape the future of the Lutheran movement than any of his theological writings.' Assess the validity of this view. [20 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 drafted by Melanchthon as the first definitive, systematic statement of Lutheran belief; B1 presented to Charles V at the Diet of Augsburg, giving Lutheranism formal, public standing before the Emperor; B1 deliberately moderate/conciliatory tone aimed to minimise the apparent gap with Catholic doctrine; B1 became the foundational doctrinal document around which Lutheran territories and later the Schmalkaldic League could unite; B1 helped distinguish 'respectable' Lutheranism from more radical reform movements in the eyes of princes; B1 its failure to secure reconciliation with Charles V also showed the limits of compromise, hardening the religious division.
