# S08_Development_and_Leadership - Practice: The Diet of Worms; radicalism and the Peasants' War; Lutheran beliefs and practice; Melanchthon

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain why Luther condemned the Peasants' War of 1525. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 he feared association with social revolution would destroy his movement's political protection/respectability; B1 he distinguished spiritual freedom (his message) from social/political revolt; B1 he needed the support of princes, who would not tolerate a movement linked to peasant rebellion.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
