# S09_Spread_Survival_and_Exam_Technique - Practice: Failed negotiations; the princes and the Schmalkaldic League; Charles V and the Peace of Augsburg; the Papacy's response

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the significance of the Schmalkaldic League (1531). [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 a defensive military/political alliance of Lutheran princes and cities; B1 transformed Lutheranism into an organised, armed political bloc rather than scattered territorial movements; B1 forced Charles V to treat Lutheranism as a serious political/military power, shaping his strategy for two decades.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
