# S19_Transition_Metal_Chemistry - Practice: Colour, variable oxidation states, catalysts and aqueous ions

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give the colours of the vanadium species as VO2^+ is reduced by zinc in acid to V^2+, with oxidation states. [2 marks]
2. What is seen when NaOH(aq) is added dropwise then in excess to a solution of Al^3+ ions? [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 yellow (+5) → blue (+4) → green (+3) → violet (+2); B1 all oxidation states correct.
2. [2] B1 white precipitate; B1 dissolves in excess to a colourless solution.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
