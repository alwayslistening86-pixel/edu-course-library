# S15_Kp - Test: Kp for homogeneous gaseous equilibria

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator, the Periodic Table and the data sheet. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. 2SO2(g) + O2(g) ⇌ 2SO3(g). At equilibrium the mixture contains 0.40 mol SO2, 0.20 mol O2 and 1.40 mol SO3 at a total pressure of 500 kPa. Calculate Kp and its units. [5 marks]
2. For the SO3 equilibrium above (exothermic forward), state the effect on the value of Kp of (a) increasing the total pressure and (b) increasing the temperature. [2 marks]
3. Hydrogen and iodine: H2(g) + I2(g) ⇌ 2HI(g), Kp = 48.0 at 730 K. At equilibrium p(H2) = p(I2) = 15.0 kPa. Calculate p(HI), and explain why Kp has no units here. [3 marks]
4. Define partial pressure. [1 mark]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 total 2.00 mol, mole fractions 0.20, 0.10, 0.70; M1 partial pressures 100, 50, 350 kPa; M1 Kp = 350^2/(100^2 x 50); A1 0.245; B1 kPa^-1.
2. [2] B1 (a) no change; B1 (b) decreases.
3. [3] M1 p(HI)^2 = 48.0 x 15.0^2; A1 103.9 kPa; B1 pressure units cancel (2 on top, 2 below).
4. [1] B1 the pressure a gas in a mixture would exert if it alone occupied the whole volume (= mole fraction x total pressure).
