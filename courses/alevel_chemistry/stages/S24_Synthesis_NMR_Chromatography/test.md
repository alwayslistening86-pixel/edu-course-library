# S24_Synthesis_NMR_Chromatography - Test: Organic synthesis, NMR spectroscopy and chromatography

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator, the Periodic Table and the data sheet. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Predict the 1H NMR spectrum of propanone and of propanal (number of peaks, splitting and integration ratio). [4 marks]
2. Give three reasons why TMS is used as the standard in NMR spectroscopy. [3 marks]
3. Suggest a two-step synthesis of ethanoic acid from ethene, with reagents and conditions for each step. [4 marks]
4. Describe how to purify an impure organic solid by recrystallisation and test its purity. [5 marks]
5. Explain how gas chromatography separates a mixture and how GC-MS identifies the components. [3 marks]
6. A compound C4H8O2 shows a singlet (3H) at 3.7 ppm, a quartet (2H) at 2.3 ppm and a triplet (3H) at 1.1 ppm. Identify it. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 propanone: one peak; B1 singlet (6H); B1 propanal: three peaks; B1 triplet (3H), quintet/multiplet (2H) and triplet (1H, CHO) in a 3:2:1 ratio.
2. [3] B1 gives a single, intense signal (12 equivalent H) upfield of most others; B1 inert/non-toxic; B1 volatile, so easily removed (δ defined as 0).
3. [4] B1 step 1: steam with a phosphoric acid catalyst → ethanol; B1 300 °C, 60-70 atm; B1 step 2: acidified potassium dichromate(VI); B1 excess oxidant, heat under reflux → ethanoic acid.
4. [5] B1 dissolve in the minimum volume of hot solvent; B1 filter hot to remove insoluble impurities; B1 cool to crystallise, filter under reduced pressure; B1 wash with cold solvent and dry; B1 melting point: sharp and matching the data-book value.
5. [3] B1 components partition between the gas mobile phase and the stationary phase; B1 different affinities give different retention times; B1 each component then enters the mass spectrometer, whose spectrum (molecular ion, fragments) identifies it.
6. [2] B1 CH3CH2 group adjacent to a carbon with no H, and an isolated CH3 on O; B1 methyl propanoate, CH3CH2COOCH3.
