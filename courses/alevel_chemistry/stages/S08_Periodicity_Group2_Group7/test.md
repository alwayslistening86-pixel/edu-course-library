# S08_Periodicity_Group2_Group7 - Test: Periodicity, Group 2 and the halogens

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator, the Periodic Table and the data sheet. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain why the melting point of silicon is much higher than that of phosphorus (P4). [3 marks]
2. Describe what is seen when concentrated sulfuric acid is added to solid sodium bromide, and write an equation for the redox reaction. [4 marks]
3. A solution gives a cream precipitate with acidified silver nitrate, which dissolves in concentrated but not dilute ammonia. Identify the ion and explain why nitric acid is added first. [3 marks]
4. Write an equation for chlorine with cold dilute sodium hydroxide, give the oxidation states of chlorine in the products, and name the type of reaction. [3 marks]
5. Explain why barium sulfate can be swallowed for X-ray imaging even though soluble barium compounds are toxic. [2 marks]
6. Describe a test to show a solution contains ammonium ions. [2 marks]
7. Down Group 2 from Mg to Ba, which increase? Choose every correct option.
   A. atomic radius
   B. solubility of the hydroxides
   C. first ionisation energy
   D. solubility of the sulfates

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 silicon is macromolecular/giant covalent; B1 many strong covalent bonds must be broken; B1 phosphorus is simple molecular, only weak van der Waals forces are overcome.
2. [4] B1 misty fumes (HBr); B1 orange/brown fumes (Br2); B1 colourless gas SO2 (choking); B1 2HBr + H2SO4 → Br2 + SO2 + 2H2O.
3. [3] B1 bromide, Br-; B1 nitric acid removes carbonate (or hydroxide) ions; B1 which would also form a precipitate with Ag+.
4. [3] B1 Cl2 + 2NaOH → NaCl + NaClO + H2O; B1 -1 and +1; B1 disproportionation.
5. [2] B1 BaSO4 is insoluble; B1 so barium ions are not absorbed into the blood.
6. [2] B1 add NaOH(aq) and warm; B1 gas turns damp red litmus paper blue.
7. Correct: A, B (exactly these options, no others)
