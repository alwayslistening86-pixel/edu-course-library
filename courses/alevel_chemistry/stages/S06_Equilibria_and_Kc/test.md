## Test items
1. 0.50 mol of PCl5 is placed in a 2.0 dm^3 flask and heated: PCl5 ⇌ PCl3 + Cl2. At equilibrium 0.30 mol of Cl2 is present. Calculate Kc and give its units. [4 marks]
2. For 2SO2 + O2 ⇌ 2SO3, ΔH = -196 kJ mol^-1. State and explain the effect on the equilibrium yield of SO3 of (a) increasing pressure and (b) increasing temperature. [4 marks]
3. Explain why a compromise temperature of about 450 °C is used in the Haber process. [3 marks]
4. What happens to Kc for an exothermic reaction when the temperature is raised? Explain. [2 marks]
5. For Kc = 9.0 for A + B ⇌ C + D (no units), starting with 1.0 mol each of A and B, find the equilibrium amount of C. [2 marks]
6. Which changes alter the value of Kc? Choose every correct option.
   A. a change in temperature
   B. adding a catalyst
   C. increasing the pressure
   D. adding more reactant

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 PCl3 = 0.30 mol, PCl5 = 0.20 mol; M1 concentrations 0.15, 0.15, 0.10 mol dm^-3; A1 Kc = 0.15^2/0.10 = 0.225; B1 mol dm^-3.
2. [4] B1 (a) yield increases; B1 fewer gas molecules on the right (3 → 2), so the equilibrium shifts right to reduce pressure; B1 (b) yield decreases; B1 the reverse reaction is endothermic, so the equilibrium shifts left to absorb heat.
3. [3] B1 the forward reaction is exothermic, so a low temperature gives a higher yield; B1 but at low temperature the rate is too slow; B1 450 °C gives an acceptable yield at an acceptable rate.
4. [2] B1 Kc decreases; B1 the equilibrium shifts in the endothermic (reverse) direction, lowering product concentrations relative to reactants.
5. [2] M1 x^2/(1 - x)^2 = 9, x/(1 - x) = 3; A1 x = 0.75 mol.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
