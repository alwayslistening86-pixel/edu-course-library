# S20_Elizabethan_Society_Education_and_Poverty - Practice: Early Elizabethan England: education, leisure and the problem of poverty

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain one difference between the education of a wealthy boy and a poor girl in Elizabethan England.
2. Explain why theatre became popular and why Puritans opposed it.
3. Give two reasons for the increase in poverty in Elizabeth's reign.
4. Explain the difference between the 'deserving' and 'undeserving' poor.
5. Explain how the 1601 Poor Law changed policy towards the poor.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
