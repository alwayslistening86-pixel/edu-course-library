# S24_Hitlers_Early_Career_and_the_Munich_Putsch - Practice: Weimar and Nazi Germany: Hitler's early career and the Munich Putsch

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain how Hitler rose to leadership of the tiny German Workers' Party.
2. Explain two aims from the Twenty-Five Point Programme and the SA's role.
3. Narrate the key events of the Munich Putsch with dates.
4. Explain two consequences of the Munich Putsch for Hitler and for Nazi strategy.
5. Explain why Nazi support remained limited between 1924 and 1928.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
