# S15_Final_Conflict_Reservations_and_Assimilation - Practice: The American West: the buffalo, Little Big Horn, Wounded Knee and reservation policy, c1876-c1895

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why the destruction of the buffalo was so damaging to Plains Indian life.
2. Give one cause and one consequence of the Battle of the Little Bighorn.
3. Explain why the Wounded Knee Massacre happened.
4. Describe two problems of reservation life.
5. Explain whether the Dawes Act helped or harmed Plains Indians, with a reason.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
