# S13_Conflict_with_Plains_Indians_1862_1876 - Practice: The American West: government policy and conflict with Plains Indians, c1862-c1876

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two ways the cattle industry harmed Plains Indian life.
2. Explain why the discovery of gold in the Black Hills undermined the 1868 Fort Laramie Treaty.
3. Give one cause and one consequence of Little Crow's War.
4. Explain why the Sand Creek Massacre caused national outrage.
5. Explain why Red Cloud's War is often seen as a Sioux victory.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
