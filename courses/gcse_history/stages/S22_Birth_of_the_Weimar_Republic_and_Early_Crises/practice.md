# S22_Birth_of_the_Weimar_Republic_and_Early_Crises - Practice: Weimar and Nazi Germany: the birth of the Republic and the crises of 1919-23

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two reasons the Kaiser abdicated in November 1918.
2. Give one strength and one weakness of the Weimar Constitution.
3. Explain the stab-in-the-back myth and why it damaged the Republic's reputation.
4. Compare the Spartacist uprising and the Kapp Putsch: give one similarity and one difference.
5. Explain two causes and two effects of the 1923 hyperinflation crisis.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
