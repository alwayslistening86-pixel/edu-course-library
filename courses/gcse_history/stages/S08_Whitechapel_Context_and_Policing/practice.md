# S08_Whitechapel_Context_and_Policing - Practice: Whitechapel, c1870-c1900: environment, population and policing

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe two features of housing in Whitechapel that made policing difficult.
2. Explain how migration changed Whitechapel's population between the 1840s and 1888.
3. Give two reasons why H Division found it hard to prevent or solve crime in Whitechapel.
4. Explain how the press and the Vigilance Committee responded to the Ripper murders.
5. Explain why Sir Charles Warren resigned in November 1888.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
