# S05_Early_Modern_Enforcement_Punishment_and_Case_Studies - Practice: Early modern enforcement and punishment: the Gunpowder Plotters and Matthew Hopkins

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a timeline of the Gunpowder Plot from Catesby's plan to the executions in January 1606, with at least six dated points.
2. List four methods used by Hopkins and explain why each was unreliable.
3. Explain two reasons why witchcraft prosecutions became so intense in East Anglia in 1645-47.
4. Give one piece of continuity and one change in punishment between 1300 and 1600.
5. Explain what is meant by the Bloody Code and roughly when it began.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
