# S29_Nazi_Youth_Policy_and_the_Economy - Practice: Weimar and Nazi Germany: youth, education and the economy

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two activities of the Hitler Youth or League of German Maidens and one ideological purpose behind them.
2. Explain two ways the school curriculum changed under the Nazis.
3. Explain two policies used to reduce unemployment and give one limitation of the official figures.
4. Explain what Strength Through Joy offered and why it was popular despite limited wage growth.
5. Explain whether you think most German workers were better off under the Nazis by 1939, with a reason.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
