# S17_Puritan_and_Catholic_Challenges_and_Mary_Queen_of_Scots - Practice: Early Elizabethan England: Puritan and Catholic challenges, and Mary, Queen of Scots

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain what Puritans wanted changed about the religious settlement, with one example.
2. Explain why the Papacy's excommunication of Elizabeth was dangerous.
3. Explain two reasons Mary, Queen of Scots had a strong claim to the English throne.
4. Explain why Mary fled to England in 1568.
5. Explain the dilemma Elizabeth faced over what to do with Mary once she arrived.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
