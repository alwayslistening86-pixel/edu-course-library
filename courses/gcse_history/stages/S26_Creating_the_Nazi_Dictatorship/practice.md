# S26_Creating_the_Nazi_Dictatorship - Practice: Weimar and Nazi Germany: creating and consolidating the dictatorship, 1933-34

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain how the Reichstag Fire was used to weaken Nazi opponents.
2. Explain what the Enabling Act allowed Hitler's government to do.
3. Explain two reasons for the Night of the Long Knives.
4. Explain how Hitler became 'Führer' after Hindenburg's death in 1934.
5. Explain how the Gestapo, SS and concentration camps together suppressed opposition.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
