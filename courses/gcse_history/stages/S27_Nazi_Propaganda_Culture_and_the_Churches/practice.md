# S27_Nazi_Propaganda_Culture_and_the_Churches - Practice: Weimar and Nazi Germany: propaganda, culture and control of the Churches

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two propaganda methods Goebbels used to build support for the Nazis.
2. Explain what the Nazis meant by 'degenerate art' and give one example of cultural control.
3. Explain the Concordat's terms and why the Nazis later broke it.
4. Explain the Reich Church's purpose.
5. Explain why historians disagree about how far Nazi rule rested on consent rather than coercion.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
