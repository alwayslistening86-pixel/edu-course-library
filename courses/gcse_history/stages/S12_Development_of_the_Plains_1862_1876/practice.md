# S12_Development_of_the_Plains_1862_1876 - Practice: The American West: railroads, homesteading and cattle, c1862-c1876

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two ways the transcontinental railroad changed life on the Plains.
2. Give two problems homesteaders faced and one solution to each.
3. Explain the difference between a sheriff, a town marshal and a federal marshal.
4. Explain why Abilene became an important cow town.
5. Explain one reason ranchers and homesteaders came into conflict in this period.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
