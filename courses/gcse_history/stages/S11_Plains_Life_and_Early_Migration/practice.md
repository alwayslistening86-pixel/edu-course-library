# S11_Plains_Life_and_Early_Migration - Practice: The American West: Plains Indian life and early migration, c1835-c1862

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two features of Plains Indian beliefs about land that differed from white settlers' beliefs.
2. Give two 'pull' factors and one 'push' factor that encouraged westward migration.
3. Compare the Donner Party and the Mormon migration: give one reason one succeeded and the other failed.
4. Describe two problems faced by early settlers on the Plains.
5. Explain what the Fort Laramie Treaty of 1851 tried to achieve and why it proved difficult.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
