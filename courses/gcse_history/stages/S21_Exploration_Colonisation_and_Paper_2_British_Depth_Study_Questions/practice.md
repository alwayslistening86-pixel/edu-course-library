# S21_Exploration_Colonisation_and_Paper_2_British_Depth_Study_Questions - Practice: Early Elizabethan England: exploration, Virginia, and Paper 2 British depth study question types

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two reasons why English exploration increased in Elizabeth's reign.
2. Explain the significance of Drake's circumnavigation for England's relationship with Spain.
3. Explain Raleigh's role in the attempted colonisation of Virginia.
4. Give two reasons the Roanoke colony failed.
5. Answer Q1 style: 'Describe two features of Drake's circumnavigation of the globe.'

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
