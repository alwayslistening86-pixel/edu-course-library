# S01_How_the_Qualification_Works - Practice: How the qualification works: three papers, four objectives, SPaG and extended writing

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. From memory, list the three papers with their timings, marks and the option you study in each.
2. Classify these command phrases by assessment objective: 'Describe two features of...', 'How useful are Sources A and B...', 'Explain why...', 'How do Interpretations 1 and 2 differ?'
3. Take the statement 'The Homestead Act made the West safe for settlers.' Write one stimulus point you might be given and two additional points of your own that you could add.
4. Correct the spelling and specialist terms in: 'The Bloddy Cod was a sistem of capitle punnishment.'

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
