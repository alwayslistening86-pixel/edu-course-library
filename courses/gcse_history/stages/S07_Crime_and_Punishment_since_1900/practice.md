# S07_Crime_and_Punishment_since_1900 - Practice: Crime and punishment in modern Britain, c1900-present

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give two examples of crimes that did not exist in 1900 and explain what caused each to be defined as a crime.
2. Explain in two sentences how Neighbourhood Watch echoes medieval community responsibility, and one way it differs.
3. Explain why capital punishment was abolished in Britain, using at least two reasons.
4. Compare the treatment of Conscientious Objectors in the two world wars: give one similarity and one difference.
5. Explain why the Derek Bentley case is significant to the history of capital punishment.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
