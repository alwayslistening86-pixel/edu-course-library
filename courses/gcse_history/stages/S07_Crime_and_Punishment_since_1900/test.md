## Test scenario
Paper 1 style. (a) Describe two features of policing in Britain today (4 marks). (b) Explain two consequences of the abolition of capital punishment in 1965 (4 marks). (c) 'The most important reason for the abolition of capital punishment was doubt about miscarriages of justice.' How far do you agree? Use the Bentley case and at least one other factor (16 marks, plan only).
