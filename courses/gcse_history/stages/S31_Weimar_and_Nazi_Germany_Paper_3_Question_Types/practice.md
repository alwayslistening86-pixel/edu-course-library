# S31_Weimar_and_Nazi_Germany_Paper_3_Question_Types - Practice: Paper 3 question types: sources, causation and interpretations

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Plan a Q2 answer: explain why the Nazis were able to reduce unemployment between 1933 and 1939.
2. Plan a Q3(a) answer: how useful would a Nazi propaganda poster and a British newspaper report be for an enquiry into how the Nazis presented themselves to the German public?
3. Write a Q3(b) style answer comparing two brief hypothetical interpretations of Hitler's rise to power (one emphasising his personal skill, one emphasising Weimar's weaknesses).
4. Suggest two reasons those two interpretations might differ (Q3(c) style).
5. Plan a Q3(d) answer: how far do you agree that the main reason for Hitler's rise to power was the Depression?

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
