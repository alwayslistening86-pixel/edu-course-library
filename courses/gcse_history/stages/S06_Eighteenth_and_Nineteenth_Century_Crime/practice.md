# S06_Eighteenth_and_Nineteenth_Century_Crime - Practice: Crime, law enforcement and punishment, c1700-c1900

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain in two sentences why poaching and smuggling counted as 'social crimes'.
2. Give one reason the Witchcraft Act of 1736 ended prosecutions and one reason the Tolpuddle Martyrs were transported despite unions being legal.
3. Put in order: Bow Street Runners, Metropolitan Police Act, compulsory county and borough forces, CID founded. Add a date to each.
4. Explain why public hanging was abolished in 1868, using one reason connected to attitudes.
5. Name one contribution each of John Howard and Elizabeth Fry to prison reform.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
