# S28_Opposition_and_Nazi_Policy_towards_Women - Practice: Weimar and Nazi Germany: opposition to the regime and Nazi policy towards women

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why Pastor Niemöller and the Confessing Church opposed the regime.
2. Explain one difference between the Swing Youth and the Edelweiss Pirates.
3. Explain the Nazi slogan 'Kinder, Küche, Kirche' and what it meant for women's role.
4. Explain two specific Nazi policies aimed at increasing the birth rate.
5. Explain how Nazi employment policy towards women changed over the 1930s and why.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
