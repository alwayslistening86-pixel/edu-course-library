## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Use one made-up scenario. A local history society claims that 'the arrival of the railway was the most important change in the town between 1840 and 1880.' (a) Name which second-order concept the claim relies on and explain how you would test it (4 marks). (b) Write two sentences of similarity or difference comparing the town in 1840 and 1880 (4 marks). (c) A photograph from 1880 shows the new station and a newspaper report of 1841 complains about the noise of building: write one inference from each and say which is more useful for an enquiry into how people reacted to the railway, with a reason based on provenance (6 marks). (d) Suggest two reasons why two later historians might disagree over the railway's importance (4 marks).
