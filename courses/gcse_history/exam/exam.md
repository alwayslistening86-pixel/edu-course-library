# GCSE History (Pearson Edexcel 1HI0) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.
## Format
This cumulative exam reflects Edexcel's real three-paper structure for 1HI0 (168 marks total, of which 8 are for spelling, punctuation, grammar and specialist terminology). Sit all three papers.

**Paper 1: Crime and punishment in Britain, c1000-present, and Whitechapel, c1870-c1900 (1 hour 20 minutes, 52 marks, includes 4 SPaG marks).**
- Section A (thematic study, Crime and Punishment): Q1 describe two features (4 marks); Q2(a) how useful are Sources A and B for a given enquiry (8 marks); Q2(b) suggest a further question, detail and source type (4 marks); Q3 explain one similarity or difference across two named periods (4 marks); Q4 explain why a named change happened, with stimulus points (12 marks).
- Section B (Whitechapel historic environment): Q5 or Q6 (learner answers the one set), how far do you agree, an extended judgement with stimulus points on an aspect of Whitechapel c1870-c1900 (16 marks plus 4 SPaG marks).
- Present one original scenario per question, drawn from Option 10 (Crime and Punishment) and the Whitechapel historic environment, never a real past-paper question.

**Paper 2: The American West, c1835-c1895 (period study) and Early Elizabethan England, 1558-88 (British depth study) (1 hour 50 minutes, 64 marks).**
- Period study (American West): Q1 explain two consequences of a named event (4 marks); Q2 write a narrative account analysing the key events of a named development or conflict (8 marks); Q3 explain the importance of two things from a choice of three (8 marks each, 16 marks total).
- British depth study (Early Elizabethan England): Q1 describe two features (4 marks); Q2 explain why a named event or development happened, with stimulus points (12 marks); Q3 or Q4 (learner answers the one set), how far do you agree, an extended judgement with stimulus points (16 marks).
- Present one original scenario per question, drawn from the learner's chosen options (American West and Early Elizabethan England), never a real past-paper question.

**Paper 3: Weimar and Nazi Germany, 1918-39 (modern depth study) (1 hour 30 minutes, 52 marks, includes 4 SPaG marks).**
- Q1 what can you infer from Source A about a stated aspect (4 marks). Q2 explain why something happened, choice of two topics, with stimulus points (12 marks). Q3(a) how useful are Sources A and B for a given enquiry (8 marks). Q3(b) how do Interpretations 1 and 2 differ (4 marks). Q3(c) suggest why the interpretations differ (4 marks). Q3(d) how far do you agree with Interpretation 2 (16 marks plus 4 SPaG marks).
- Present one original scenario per question, with original, paraphrased sources and interpretations reflecting well-known, paraphrased historians' arguments (never verbatim quotation of any real published interpretation), never a real past-paper question.
