# S05_Christianity_Marriage_and_Family_Part_2 - Practice: Christianity: contraception, divorce, and equality between men and women

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the Catholic and typical Protestant positions on artificial contraception.
2. Explain one reason some Christians oppose divorce and one reason others accept it pastorally.
3. Explain the difference between complementarian and egalitarian Christian views on gender roles.
4. Explain how Galatians 3:28 is used to oppose gender prejudice, and give one area where Christian practice still divides on gender.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
