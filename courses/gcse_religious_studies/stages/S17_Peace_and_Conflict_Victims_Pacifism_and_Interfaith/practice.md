# S17_Peace_and_Conflict_Victims_Pacifism_and_Interfaith - Practice: Peace and conflict: victims of war, terrorism, pacifism, reconciliation and interfaith relations

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two groups of people that Islamic teaching on war requires to be protected.
2. Explain why the majority of Muslims and Muslim scholars condemn terrorism as un-Islamic.
3. Explain one argument for and one argument against pacifism as a Muslim position.
4. Explain what is meant by 'People of the Book' and why it matters for interfaith relations.
5. Practise a part (a) style answer: 'Which of the following is one of the Five Pillars: Shahadah, Eucharist, baptism, or Trinity?'

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
