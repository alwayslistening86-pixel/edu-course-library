# S14_Living_the_Muslim_Life_Shahadah_Salah_Sawm_Zakah - Practice: Living the Muslim life: Shahadah, Salah, Sawm and the Night of Power, and Zakah

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the two parts of the Shahadah and when it is recited across a Muslim's life.
2. Explain how Salah is performed, including wudu, direction and structure.
3. Explain the purpose of Sawm and the significance of the Night of Power.
4. Explain how Zakah is calculated and its dual practical and spiritual role.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
