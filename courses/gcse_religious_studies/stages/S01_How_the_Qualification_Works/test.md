# S01_How_the_Qualification_Works - Test: How GCSE Religious Studies B works: papers, assessment objectives and technique

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Answer in the board's style. (a) Give one example of a non-religious worldview (1 mark). (b) Explain two things AO2 requires a 'discuss' answer to include (4 marks). (c) Explain what a 5-mark 'explain two contrasting religious beliefs' answer must refer to in order to gain full marks (2 marks).

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S01_How_the_Qualification_Works` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S02_Christian_Beliefs_God_Jesus.
