# S02_Christian_Beliefs_God_Jesus - Practice: Christian beliefs: the Trinity, creation, the incarnation and the last days of Jesus

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain what the Trinity means, using the terms 'Father', 'Son' and 'Holy Spirit' correctly.
2. Explain one literal and one metaphorical way Christians interpret the creation accounts.
3. Explain what the incarnation means and why it matters for Christian belief about salvation.
4. Put the events of Jesus' last days in order, from the Last Supper to the ascension.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
