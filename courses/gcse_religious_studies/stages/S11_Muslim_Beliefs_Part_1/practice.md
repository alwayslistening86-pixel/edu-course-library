# S11_Muslim_Beliefs_Part_1 - Practice: Muslim beliefs: the Six Beliefs, the Five Roots, the nature of Allah, and prophethood

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. List the Six Beliefs of Sunni Islam.
2. Explain the historical origin of the Sunni/Shi'a division in one sentence.
3. List the Five Roots of Shi'a Islam and explain 'Adl and Imamah in your own words.
4. Explain three characteristics of Allah's nature, using the correct Arabic terms.
5. Explain what Risalah means and name the four holy books recognised before the Qur'an.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
