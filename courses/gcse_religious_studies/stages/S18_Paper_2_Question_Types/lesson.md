# S18_Paper_2_Question_Types - Lesson: Paper 2 question types: Islam, Religion, Peace and Conflict

## Goal
The learner can answer every remaining Paper 2 question part correctly: the 2-mark 'give two', the 4-mark 'explain two', the 5-mark 'explain two contrasting beliefs' with a source of authority, and the extended 12(+3 SPaG)-mark discuss/evaluate question.

## Syllabus items taught here
- Q-P2-b - Paper 2 part (b): 2-mark 'give two' question: Answer the 2-mark AO1 'give two' question with two accurate points
- Q-P2-c - Paper 2 part (c): 4-mark 'explain two' question: Answer the 4-mark AO1 'explain two reasons/ways' question
- Q-P2-d5 - Paper 2 part (d)(i): 5-mark 'explain two contrasting religious beliefs' question: Answer the 5-mark AO1 question citing a source of wisdom and authority
- Q-P2-d12 - Paper 2 part (d)(ii): 12(+3 SPaG)-mark 'discuss/evaluate' question: Answer the extended AO2 evaluate question with religious and non-religious arguments and a justified conclusion

## How to teach this
Open with: 'Paper 2 uses exactly the same question structure as Paper 1, just applied to Islam instead of Christianity. Today we apply the same technique you already know to Muslim beliefs, punishment, practice and conflict.'

#### Q-P2-b Paper 2 part (b): 2-mark 'give two' question
Part (b) is a 2-mark AO1 question asking the learner to 'give two' examples, reasons or features about Islam (for example 'Give two of the Five Pillars'), worth one mark per accurate point, following the same technique as Paper 1's part (b): state each point briefly with no explanation required, and make sure both points are accurate and genuinely distinct from one another.

#### Q-P2-c Paper 2 part (c): 4-mark 'explain two' question
Part (c) is a 4-mark AO1 question asking the learner to 'explain two' reasons, ways or beliefs about Islam (for example 'Explain two reasons why Zakah is important to Muslims'), worth up to two marks per point. Technique matches Paper 1's part (c): state each point, then develop it with a linked reason or example, so both points are explained, not just listed; two well-developed points score higher than four or five bare, undeveloped ones.

#### Q-P2-d5 Paper 2 part (d)(i): 5-mark 'explain two contrasting religious beliefs' question
Part (d)(i) is a 5-mark AO1 question asking the learner to 'explain two contrasting religious beliefs about X. In your answer you must refer to a source of wisdom and authority' (for example 'Explain two contrasting Muslim beliefs about holy war'). Technique matches Paper 1's part (d)(i): choose two beliefs that genuinely contrast (for example, between Sunni and Shi'a Islam, or between stricter and more flexible interpretations within a tradition), explain each fully, and support at least one point, ideally both, with a specific, accurately cited source of wisdom and authority (a named Surah reference, a hadith, or a named Islamic teaching or tradition).

#### Q-P2-d12 Paper 2 part (d)(ii): 12(+3 SPaG)-mark 'discuss/evaluate' question
Part (d)(ii) is the extended AO2 question on Paper 2, following the same format as Paper 1: a statement (for example ''Weapons of mass destruction can never be justified in Islam.' Discuss two contrasting views on this statement. In your response you should: refer to religious arguments, which may draw on more than one Muslim viewpoint; refer to non-religious arguments; reach a justified conclusion.'), worth 12 marks (15 on the two questions per paper carrying SPaG). Technique matches Paper 1's part (d)(ii): plan first, present religious arguments on both sides (which may include a Sunni/Shi'a or traditional/more liberal contrast), present at least one non-religious argument, weigh them explicitly against each other, and end with a clear, justified conclusion, written in full sentences with accurate specialist terminology for the SPaG marks where they apply.

## Explicitly not here
The full cumulative exam, which draws on both papers together.
