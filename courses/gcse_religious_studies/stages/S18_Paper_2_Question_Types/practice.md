# S18_Paper_2_Question_Types - Practice: Paper 2 question types: Islam, Religion, Peace and Conflict

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Answer part (b) style: 'Give two of the Six Beliefs of Sunni Islam.'
2. Answer part (c) style: 'Explain two reasons why Hajj is important to Muslims.'
3. Plan a part (d)(i) answer: 'Explain two contrasting Muslim beliefs about capital punishment.'
4. Plan a part (d)(ii) answer: ''The most important purpose of punishment is reform, not deterrence.' Discuss two contrasting views on this statement.'

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
