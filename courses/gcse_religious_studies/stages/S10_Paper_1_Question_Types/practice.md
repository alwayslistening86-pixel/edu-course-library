# S10_Paper_1_Question_Types - Practice: Paper 1 question types: Christianity, Religion and Ethics

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Answer part (a) style: 'Which of the following is a Christian sacrament: Eucharist, Zakah, Hajj, or Sawm?'
2. Answer part (b) style: 'Give two Christian teachings about the sanctity of life.'
3. Answer part (c) style: 'Explain two reasons why Christians believe in life after death.'
4. Plan a part (d)(i) answer: 'Explain two contrasting Christian beliefs about euthanasia.'
5. Plan a part (d)(ii) answer: ''The most important reason for opposing abortion is the sanctity of life.' Discuss two contrasting views on this statement.'

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
