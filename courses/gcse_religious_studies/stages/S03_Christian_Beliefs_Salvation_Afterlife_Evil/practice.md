# S03_Christian_Beliefs_Salvation_Afterlife_Evil - Practice: Christian beliefs: salvation, life after death, and the problem of evil

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the terms 'sin', 'grace' and 'atonement' and how they relate to salvation.
2. Explain one similarity and one difference between Catholic and Protestant teaching on the afterlife.
3. State the problem of evil in one sentence, using the words 'omnipotent' and 'benevolent'.
4. Explain the free will defence and the vale of soul-making as two different types of solution to the problem of evil.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
