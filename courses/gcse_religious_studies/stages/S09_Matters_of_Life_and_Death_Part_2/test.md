# S09_Matters_of_Life_and_Death_Part_2 - Test: Matters of life and death: life after death, euthanasia, and the natural world

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Answer in the board's style. (a) Give one Christian argument for life after death (1 mark). (b) Give two non-religious arguments against belief in an afterlife (2 marks). (c) 'Christians should always support hospice care rather than euthanasia.' Discuss two contrasting views on this statement. In your response you should refer to religious arguments and non-religious arguments and reach a justified conclusion (12 marks, plan only).
