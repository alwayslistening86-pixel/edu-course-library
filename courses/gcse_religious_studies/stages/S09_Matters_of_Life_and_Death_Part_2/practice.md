# S09_Matters_of_Life_and_Death_Part_2 - Practice: Matters of life and death: life after death, euthanasia, and the natural world

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two Christian arguments supporting belief in life after death.
2. Explain two non-religious arguments against an afterlife and how Christians respond to each.
3. Explain Christian teaching on euthanasia and one non-religious argument that differs from it.
4. Explain what 'stewardship' means and give one example of how it applies to an environmental issue.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
