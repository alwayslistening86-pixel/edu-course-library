# S04_Christianity_Marriage_and_Family_Part_1 - Practice: Christianity: marriage, sexual relationships, family and parish support

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain Christian teaching on the purpose of marriage, and one non-religious attitude that differs from it.
2. Explain one way Christian teaching on sexual relationships divides between more traditional and more liberal Christians.
3. Explain the purpose of family according to Christian teaching, and give one modern family type Christians respond to differently.
4. Give two ways the local parish supports families.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
