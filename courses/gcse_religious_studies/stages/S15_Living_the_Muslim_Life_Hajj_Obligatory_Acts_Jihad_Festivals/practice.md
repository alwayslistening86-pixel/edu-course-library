# S15_Living_the_Muslim_Life_Hajj_Obligatory_Acts_Jihad_Festivals - Practice: Living the Muslim life: Hajj, the Ten Obligatory Acts, the meaning of Jihad, and festivals

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain three rites performed during Hajj and their significance.
2. List the Ten Obligatory Acts of Shi'a Islam and explain khums.
3. Explain the difference between greater and lesser Jihad, and one condition for lesser Jihad to be legitimate.
4. Explain the significance of Id-ul-Adha and Ashura, one from each tradition.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
