# GCSE Religious Studies (Pearson Edexcel Specification B, 1RB0) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.
## Format
This cumulative exam reflects Edexcel's real two-paper structure for 1RB0 (204 marks total, of which 5% are for spelling, punctuation, grammar and specialist terminology, added to two of the four questions on each paper). Sit both papers.

**Paper 1: Area of Study 1, Religion and Ethics, studied through Christianity (1 hour 45 minutes, 102 marks).**
Four questions, one on each content section (Christian Beliefs; Marriage and the Family; Living the Christian Life; Matters of Life and Death). Each question has: (a) a 1-mark recognition/identify part; (b) a 2-mark 'give two' part; (c) a 4-mark 'explain two' part; (d)(i) a 5-mark 'explain two contrasting religious beliefs about X, referring to a source of wisdom and authority' part; (d)(ii) a 12-mark 'discuss two contrasting views on the statement... refer to religious arguments... refer to non-religious arguments... reach a justified conclusion' part, with 3 additional SPaG marks (making 15 marks) on two of the four questions.
Present one original scenario per question, drawn from the Christian Beliefs, Marriage and the Family, Living the Christian Life, and Matters of Life and Death content taught in this course, never a real past-paper question, and never copying real mark-scheme wording.

**Paper 2: Area of Study 2, Religion, Peace and Conflict, studied through Islam (1 hour 45 minutes, 102 marks).**
Four questions, one on each content section (Muslim Beliefs; Crime and Punishment; Living the Muslim Life; Peace and Conflict), in the same (a)-(d) format and mark tariffs as Paper 1, with SPaG marks again on two of the four questions.
Present one original scenario per question, drawn from the Muslim Beliefs, Crime and Punishment, Living the Muslim Life, and Peace and Conflict content taught in this course, never a real past-paper question, and never copying real mark-scheme wording.
