# SQE1 (Solicitors Qualifying Examination, Stage 1) — Functioning Legal Knowledge - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.
## Format
SQE1 in real life is sat as two separate examination sessions, each a single-best-answer (SBA) multiple-choice paper: FLK1 (Business Law and Practice, Dispute Resolution, Contract Law, Tort, the Legal System/Constitutional/Administrative/EU Law, and Legal Services) and FLK2 (Property Law and Practice, Wills and the Administration of Estates, Solicitors Accounts, Land Law, Trusts Law, Criminal Liability, and Criminal Law and Practice). Each session is 2 hours 33 minutes and contains 85 questions, and each paper (FLK1, FLK2) is sat across two such sessions on separate occasions, giving 170 questions per paper (340 SBA questions across the whole of SQE1). Every question offers five answer options with only one single best answer; questions are drawn from realistic client scenarios rather than abstract legal statements, mirroring the practice questions in every stage of this course. This course's cumulative exam reproduces that structure at a manageable scale: one mixed FLK1 set and one mixed FLK2 set of original SBA questions, spanning every subject taught, with the same scenario-based single-best-answer style used throughout.
## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly. For each question, credit is given only for selecting the single best answer and correctly explaining why each of the four distractors is wrong (misstates the rule, applies the wrong test, confuses a threshold, or answers a related but different question) - matching how every stage's own test items in this course were graded, since day-one competence in SQE1 is about reliably eliminating plausible-looking wrong answers, not just recognising a right one.
## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete.
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the stage(s) that broke down (identify by subject: Business Law and Practice, Dispute Resolution, Contract Law, Tort, Legal System/Constitutional/Administrative/EU Law, Legal Services, Property Law and Practice, Wills and the Administration of Estates, Solicitors Accounts, Land Law, Trusts Law, Criminal Liability, or Criminal Law and Practice), offer targeted review of that subject's stage, then a fresh retry paper drawing fresh original scenarios.
