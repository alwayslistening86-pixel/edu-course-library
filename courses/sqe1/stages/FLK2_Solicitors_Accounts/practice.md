# FLK2_Solicitors_Accounts - Practice: Solicitors Accounts

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A firm receives a single cheque from a client covering both a disbursement to be paid and the firm's already-billed costs. Explain how the sum should be split and recorded.
2. A firm's ledger shows a particular client's balance in debit by £500. Identify the breach and the required correcting action.
3. A client asks whether they are entitled to interest on £50,000 held in the client account for four months with no separate agreement about interest. Advise on the default position.
4. A firm delivers a bill to a client for costs already held as client money. Explain what must happen to that money and by when.
5. Explain what triggers the requirement for a firm to obtain an accountant's report, and one situation in which it would not be required.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
