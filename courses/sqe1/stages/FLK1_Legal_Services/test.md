# FLK1_Legal_Services - Test: Legal Services

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
"A client instructs a firm to help purchase a property for £800,000 in cash, and is vague and evasive when asked about the source of funds, eventually saying only that it is 'family money from abroad.' Which of the following is the most appropriate next step for the fee earner? [Five options spanning: proceed as normal since the client is entitled to confidentiality; ask no further questions to avoid tipping off; apply enhanced due diligence and consider a report to the firm's Money Laundering Reporting Officer without alerting the client to the specific suspicion; refuse to act and inform the client this is because of suspected money laundering; report directly to the National Crime Agency bypassing the firm's own MLRO.]" The learner selects enhanced due diligence plus an internal MLRO report and explains why the other options each breach either the due diligence obligation, the tipping-off prohibition, or the correct internal-then-external reporting chain.
