# FLK1_Legal_Services - Practice: Legal Services

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A new client is vague about the source of £500,000 cash they want to use to buy a property. Identify the AML obligations engaged and the correct internal reporting route.
2. A firm wants to advise a client on which shares to buy as an investment. Identify the financial services regulation issue this raises.
3. A client cannot afford to pay hourly fees upfront for litigation with reasonable prospects of success. Identify at least two funding options that could suit them and explain the trade-offs.
4. Identify which activities are "reserved legal activities" and explain the consequence of an unauthorised person carrying one out.
5. A client alleges a firm refused to act for them because of their religion. Identify the relevant legislation and the type of discrimination potentially engaged.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
