# FLK1_Tort - Practice: Tort

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A shop visitor slips on a wet floor with no warning sign. Analyse the shop's liability under occupiers' liability and, separately, whether the cleaner's employer is vicariously liable.
2. Apply the Caparo test to decide whether an accountant owes a duty of care to a third party who relied on their negligently prepared report.
3. Distinguish a primary victim from a secondary victim on a road traffic accident fact pattern involving a bystander who witnessed the crash.
4. Compare a claimant's prospects under negligence and under the Consumer Protection Act 1987 for injury caused by a defective kettle.
5. Identify whether a factory's regular loud noise at night amounts to private nuisance, applying the relevant factors.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
