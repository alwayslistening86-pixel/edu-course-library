# FLK1_Business_Law_and_Practice - Test: Business Law and Practice

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Single-best-answer style: "A private limited company wishes to raise new capital by issuing 10,000 new ordinary shares to a new investor who is not an existing shareholder. The company's three existing shareholders have not waived their pre-emption rights. Which of the following correctly states what the company must do before allotting the shares to the new investor? [Five plausible options, only one correct: offer the new shares to existing shareholders first in proportion to their existing holdings, unless pre-emption rights are validly disapplied by special resolution.]" The learner must select the correct option and explain in their own words why each distractor misstates the rule (e.g. confusing ordinary and special resolution thresholds, or confusing pre-emption with director-approval requirements).
