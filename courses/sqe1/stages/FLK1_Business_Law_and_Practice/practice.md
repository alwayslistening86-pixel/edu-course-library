# FLK1_Business_Law_and_Practice - Practice: Business Law and Practice

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Three friends trade together informally for two years. Identify their current legal structure and each friend's personal liability.
2. A company wants to raise £200,000. Compare debt finance with a fixed charge against equity finance with pre-emption rights, from the company's perspective.
3. A director wants to award themselves a bonus without disclosing a personal interest in the underlying contract. Identify the statutory duty engaged and the consequence of breach.
4. A company can no longer pay its debts as they fall due but a director believes it can be rescued. Identify the most appropriate insolvency procedure and explain why.
5. A sole trader sells their business premises at a profit. Identify which tax (or taxes) are engaged and on whom.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
