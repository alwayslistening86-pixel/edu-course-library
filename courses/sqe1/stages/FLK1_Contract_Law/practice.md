# FLK1_Contract_Law - Practice: Contract Law

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Run the full formation analysis (offer, acceptance, consideration, intention) on the café/roaster scenario and reach a conclusion on whether a contract exists.
2. Identify whether an exclusion clause limiting liability for late delivery, printed on the back of an invoice sent after the order was placed, is validly incorporated.
3. A seller makes a false statement about a car's mileage which induces the buyer to purchase it. Classify the type of misrepresentation on given facts and identify the available remedy.
4. Apply Hadley v Baxendale to decide whether a buyer's lost resale profit is recoverable as damages for late delivery.
5. Distinguish an indemnity from a guarantee using a worked example of a parent company backing a subsidiary's debt.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
