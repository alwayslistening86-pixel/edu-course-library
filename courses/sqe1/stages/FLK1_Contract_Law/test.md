# FLK1_Contract_Law - Test: Contract Law

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
"A buyer orders goods described as 'grade A' but receives goods that are, in fact, grade B - a defect discovered only after the buyer has used half the delivery in production. Which of the following best describes the buyer's most likely available remedy? [Five options spanning: termination and full refund; damages only, having affirmed by using the goods; rescission for misrepresentation; specific performance; no remedy as risk passed on delivery.]" The learner selects the correct classification (an implied term as to description breached, but the right to terminate may be lost/affected by the buyer's conduct in using the goods) and explains why each distractor misapplies the facts.

Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.FLK1_Contract_Law` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to FLK1_Tort.
