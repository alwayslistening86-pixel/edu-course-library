# FLK1_Dispute_Resolution - Practice: Dispute Resolution

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A claim for breach of a simple contract arose 5 years and 10 months ago. Advise on limitation and any urgency.
2. A defendant has a hopeless defence with no real prospect of success. Identify and explain the correct interim application.
3. Explain the difference between legal advice privilege and litigation privilege, with an example of each.
4. A claimant beats their own Part 36 offer at trial. Explain the costs consequences for the defendant.
5. A judgment creditor knows the debtor's employer but not their bank. Identify the most appropriate enforcement method.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
