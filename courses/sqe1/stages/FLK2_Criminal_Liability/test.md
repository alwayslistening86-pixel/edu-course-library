# FLK2_Criminal_Liability - Test: Criminal Liability

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Single-best-answer style: "D and E agree that E will distract a shopkeeper while D removes goods from a shelf without paying. D is caught leaving the shop with the goods; E was still distracting the shopkeeper and never touched the goods. Which of the following best describes E's liability? [Five plausible options, only one correct: E is liable as a secondary party to D's theft, having intentionally assisted D's offence with knowledge of the essential matters constituting it, even though E never appropriated any property.]" The learner must select the correct option and explain why each distractor confuses principal and secondary liability, or wrongly requires E to have physically appropriated property.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.FLK2_Criminal_Liability` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to FLK2_Criminal_Law_and_Practice.
