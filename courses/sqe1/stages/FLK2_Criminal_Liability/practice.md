# FLK2_Criminal_Liability - Practice: Criminal Liability

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. During a heated argument, D punches V once, breaking V's jaw. Identify the correct OAPA offence and why, working through harm and mens rea in order.
2. D, voluntarily drunk, breaks a window believing (wrongly, only because of the drink) that it is their own house. Identify whether intoxication assists with a criminal damage charge.
3. D enters a shop during opening hours intending only to browse, then decides to steal an item once inside. Identify whether burglary is made out and why.
4. D stabs V during a sudden loss of temper after V taunted D about an unrelated matter for months, culminating in a specific grave insult that evening. Identify what partial defence might be available and what must be shown.
5. D dishonestly tells an elderly relative a false story to persuade them to transfer money, which the relative refuses to do. Identify whether a Fraud Act offence is complete.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
