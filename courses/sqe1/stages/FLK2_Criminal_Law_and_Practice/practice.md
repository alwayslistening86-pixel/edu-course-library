# FLK2_Criminal_Law_and_Practice - Practice: Criminal Law and Practice

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A suspect is arrested for an either-way offence and has been in custody for 20 hours with no authorisation to extend. Advise on the custody time position.
2. The prosecution opposes bail on the basis the defendant might fail to surrender, citing only the seriousness of the charge. Assess whether this alone is sufficient.
3. A defendant charged with an either-way offence indicates a not-guilty plea. Explain how allocation will be decided and what right the defendant retains regardless of the court's decision.
4. A single eyewitness identifies the defendant after a brief glimpse at night from 30 metres away, with no other supporting evidence. Identify the safeguard that applies and its likely effect on the case.
5. A 15-year-old is charged with an offence that would attract a lengthy sentence for an adult. Identify which court is likely to deal with the case and why.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
