# FLK2_Criminal_Law_and_Practice - Test: Criminal Law and Practice

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Single-best-answer style: "A defendant is interviewed under caution at the police station and, on legal advice, declines to answer questions about their whereabouts at the time of the offence. At trial, the defendant for the first time gives an account of their whereabouts that was not put to the police in interview. Which of the following best states the position on inferences from the defendant's silence? [Five plausible options, only one correct: an adverse inference may be drawn under s.34 CJPOA 1994 from the failure to mention, when questioned, a fact later relied on at trial, provided the caution was properly given, even though the defendant had legal advice, since legal advice alone does not automatically prevent an inference.]" The learner must select the correct option and explain why each distractor wrongly treats legal advice as an automatic bar, or misapplies the "fact later relied on" condition.
