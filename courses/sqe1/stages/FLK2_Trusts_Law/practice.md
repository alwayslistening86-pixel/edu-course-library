# FLK2_Trusts_Law - Practice: Trusts Law

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A settlor tells their solicitor "hold my house on trust for whichever of my children needs it most, in shares my trustees think fit" and signs a deed but never transfers the house. Identify which certainties and which constitution issue this raises.
2. A father transfers shares into his young daughter's name without saying why. Identify the presumption that applies and how it could be rebutted.
3. An unmarried couple buy a house in one partner's sole name; the other partner pays for a substantial extension, relying on assurances they would always have a home there. Identify which doctrine(s) could give them an interest and why.
4. A trustee sells trust land to themselves at market value without beneficiary consent. Identify the rule breached and the consequence.
5. A trustee wrongly pays trust money to a stranger, who spends some and uses the rest to buy a car, aware the money came from a breach of trust. Identify the stranger's potential liability and the beneficiaries' possible remedies.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
