# FLK2_Property_Law_and_Practice - Test: Property Law and Practice

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Single-best-answer style: "A commercial landlord serves a s.25 notice opposing the grant of a new tenancy to its tenant, stating that it intends to demolish and reconstruct the premises. The tenant has occupied and traded from the premises for eight years and wishes to remain. Which of the following best states the tenant's position? [Five plausible options, only one correct: the landlord may oppose on this ground under s.30(1)(f) Landlord and Tenant Act 1954, and if it succeeds in establishing a genuine intention to redevelop, the tenant is not entitled to a new tenancy but may be entitled to statutory compensation.]" The learner must select the correct option and explain why each distractor misstates either the availability of the ground, the effect of success, or the compensation position.

Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.FLK2_Property_Law_and_Practice` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
