# FLK2_Property_Law_and_Practice - Practice: Property Law and Practice

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A buyer's solicitor is also instructed by the buyer's mortgage lender. Explain when a conflict of interest would require the solicitor to stop acting for the lender.
2. Contracts have not yet exchanged. Explain what obligations, if any, bind the seller and buyer at this point.
3. Completion has not happened by the contractual date and no notice to complete has been served. Identify what the innocent party can and cannot yet do.
4. A tenant wants to assign the remainder of their commercial lease. Identify what the lease is likely to require before the assignment can proceed.
5. A business tenant's lease has ended but they remain in occupation and paying rent. Identify whether the tenancy continues and on what basis.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
