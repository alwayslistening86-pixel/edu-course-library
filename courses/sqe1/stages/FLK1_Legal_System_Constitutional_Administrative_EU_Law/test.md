# FLK1_Legal_System_Constitutional_Administrative_EU_Law - Test: Legal System of England and Wales, Constitutional and Administrative Law, and EU Law

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
"A local authority cancels a market trader's long-standing pitch licence without notice or any opportunity to make representations, relying on a general statutory power to 'manage the market as it sees fit.' Which of the following best identifies the ground of judicial review most clearly engaged? [Five options: illegality; Wednesbury irrationality; procedural impropriety; legitimate expectation; no ground, as the power is unfettered.]" The learner selects procedural impropriety (no fair opportunity to be heard) and explains why illegality, irrationality and legitimate expectation are each a weaker fit on these specific facts, and why "no ground" misstates that even a broad statutory power must be exercised lawfully and fairly.
