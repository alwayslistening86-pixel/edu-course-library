# FLK1_Legal_System_Constitutional_Administrative_EU_Law - Practice: Legal System of England and Wales, Constitutional and Administrative Law, and EU Law

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A County Court decision conflicts with an earlier Court of Appeal decision on the same point. Explain which binds and why.
2. Apply the literal, golden, mischief and purposive approaches to a short, deliberately ambiguous statutory provision and show how each could produce a different result.
3. A public body refuses a licence without giving the applicant a chance to respond to adverse information. Identify the ground of judicial review engaged and the likely remedy.
4. Identify which HRA 1998 section is engaged where a public authority itself (not Parliament) acts incompatibly with a Convention right, and what remedy is available.
5. Explain the difference between a constitutional convention and a rule of law in the strict prerogative-power sense, using the example of dissolving Parliament.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
