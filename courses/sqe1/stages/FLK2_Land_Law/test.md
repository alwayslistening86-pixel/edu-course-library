# FLK2_Land_Law - Test: Land Law

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Single-best-answer style: "A owns the freehold of a house and grants B, the owner of the neighbouring plot, a right to cross A's driveway to reach the public road, by deed. B's plot is later sold to C. A's house is later sold to D, who was unaware of the right of way at the time of purchase but the right was correctly protected by a registered notice against A's title. Which of the following best states the position after both sales? [Five plausible options, only one correct: the benefit of the easement passes automatically to C as it runs with B's dominant land, and the burden binds D because it was protected by notice on the registered title, regardless of D's actual knowledge.]" The learner must select the correct option and explain why each distractor confuses benefit/burden passing, notice-based protection with actual knowledge, or the position under registered versus unregistered land.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.FLK2_Land_Law` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to FLK2_Trusts_Law.
