# FLK2_Land_Law - Practice: Land Law

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Two friends buy a house together, contributing unequal amounts, with no express declaration of trust. Identify what type of co-ownership most likely exists in equity and why it matters on one friend's death.
2. A right of way was used openly for 22 years without permission or objection. Identify how an easement might have arisen.
3. A restrictive covenant not to run a business from a house was made between the original neighbours 40 years ago. The house has since changed hands twice. Identify what must be shown for the current owner to be bound.
4. A homeowner's agreement lets a lodger use a room but the homeowner retains a key and can enter at any time. Identify whether this is a lease or a licence and why.
5. A tenant under a lease granted in 2015 lawfully assigns it to a new tenant, who later breaches the repairing covenant. Identify who the landlord can pursue and why.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
