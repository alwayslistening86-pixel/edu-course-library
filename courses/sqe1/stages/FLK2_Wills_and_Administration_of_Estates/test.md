# FLK2_Wills_and_Administration_of_Estates - Test: Wills and the Administration of Estates

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Single-best-answer style: "A testator's will, valid in all other respects, is later followed by the testator's marriage. The testator dies two years afterwards without having made a new will, and the earlier will makes no reference to the forthcoming marriage. Which of the following best describes the position? [Five plausible options, only one correct: the marriage revokes the earlier will (since it was not made in contemplation of that marriage), so the estate is distributed under the intestacy rules unless a later valid will is found.]" The learner must select the correct option and explain why each distractor misapplies the revocation-by-marriage rule, the "in contemplation of marriage" exception, or confuses revocation with mere lapse of a gift.
