# S06_Ratio_and_Proportion - Practice: Ratio and proportion

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Share 84 in the ratio 2 : 3 : 7. Then find the amount of the middle share as a fraction of the total.
2. Simplify 1.2 kg : 800 g, then write it in the form 1 : n.
3. 6 taps fill a tank in 40 minutes. How long do 10 identical taps take? Say which type of proportion it is.
4. Higher: y is directly proportional to x^2 and y = 45 when x = 3. Find the formula, then find y when x = 7 and x when y = 20.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
