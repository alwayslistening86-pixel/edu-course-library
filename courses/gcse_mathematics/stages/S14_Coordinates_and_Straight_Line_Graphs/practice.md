# S14_Coordinates_and_Straight_Line_Graphs - Practice: Coordinates, tables of values and straight-line graphs

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Plot the graph of y = 3x - 2 for x = -2 to 4 from a table of values. Find its gradient and where it crosses the y-axis.
2. Find the equation of the line through (2, 5) and (6, 13), and the midpoint of those two points.
3. Higher: find the equation of the line perpendicular to y = -3x + 4 that passes through (6, 1), and the tangent to x^2 + y^2 = 100 at (6, 8).
4. Using a graph of y = x^2 - 4x + 1, estimate the solutions to x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0 and x^2 - 4x + 1 = 3.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
