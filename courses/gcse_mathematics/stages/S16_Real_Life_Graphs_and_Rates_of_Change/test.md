## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'A car travels 60 km in 45 minutes, waits for 15 minutes, then travels 30 km in 30 minutes. a) Sketch a distance-time graph. b) Find its speed in the first part, in km/h. c) Find its average speed for the whole journey.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A speed-time graph shows a car accelerating uniformly from 0 to 20 m/s in 8 s, staying at 20 m/s for 15 s, then slowing uniformly to rest in 5 s. a) Find the acceleration in the first 8 seconds. b) Work out the total distance travelled. c) Find the average speed for the whole journey.'
