# S16_Real_Life_Graphs_and_Rates_of_Change - Practice: Real-life graphs, rates of change and areas under graphs

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A distance-time graph shows a cyclist leaving home, travelling 12 km in 40 minutes, stopping for 20 minutes, then returning in 30 minutes. Describe each stage and find the speed on the outward journey.
2. Draw a tangent to the curve y = x^2 at x = 2 (on a printed graph) and estimate the gradient. Compare with the exact value 4.
3. A speed-time graph goes from 0 to 8 m/s in 4 s, then stays constant for 6 s. Find the total distance.
4. Higher: estimate the area under y = 20/x from x = 1 to x = 5 using four trapezia of equal width, and state whether it is an over- or under-estimate.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
