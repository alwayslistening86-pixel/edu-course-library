## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'A laptop costs 640. In a sale it is reduced by 15%. a) Work out the sale price. b) The shop also offers 5% simple interest per year on 400 left in a savings scheme: work out the interest after 3 years.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A car depreciates by 12% each year. It was bought for 14 000. Work out its value after 3 years, to the nearest 10. A different car is now worth 8 175 after a 9% increase in its value over the last year. What was it worth before the increase?'
