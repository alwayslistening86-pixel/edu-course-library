# S03_Percentages_and_Growth - Practice: Percentages: change, reverse and repeated change

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Without a calculator, find 15% of 320 and then 17.5% of 320, showing the 10% and 5% building.
2. A train fare rises from 24 to 26.40. What is the percentage increase? A price after a 30% increase is 65; what was it originally?
3. 5000 is invested for 3 years at 4% simple interest, and another 5000 at 4% compound interest. Compare the totals and explain the difference.
4. Higher: a population of 800 000 falls by 2.5% a year. Write a formula for the population after n years and use it for n = 6.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
