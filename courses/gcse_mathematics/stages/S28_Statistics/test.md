# S28_Statistics - Test: Sampling, charts, averages, scatter graphs and data quality

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Find the mean, median, mode and range of 3, 7, 4, 9, 7, 5, 22 and say which average is most affected by the value 22. b) Explain why a survey about gym use carried out only outside a gym is biased. c) A scatter graph shows exam results falling as hours of gaming increase. Describe the correlation and say whether it proves gaming causes lower results.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'a) The grouped table below shows the heights of 40 plants; estimate the mean. b) The median and IQR for class A are 62 and 14, and for class B are 58 and 8. Compare the classes. c) A stratified sample of 40 is to be taken from 300 students where Year 10 has 130 students; how many Year 10 students are sampled?'
