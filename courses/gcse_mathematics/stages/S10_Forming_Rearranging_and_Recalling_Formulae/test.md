# S10_Forming_Rearranging_and_Recalling_Formulae - Test: Forming, rearranging and recalling formulae

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) A gym charges 12 joining fee plus 18 per month. Write a formula for the total cost C after m months. b) Make m the subject of your formula. c) Write down the formula for the area of a circle of radius r.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'a) Make x the subject of y = (2x + 5)/(x - 1). b) A cyclist accelerates from 3 m/s at 0.4 m/s^2 for 10 seconds. Find her final speed and the distance covered. c) State the quadratic formula.'
