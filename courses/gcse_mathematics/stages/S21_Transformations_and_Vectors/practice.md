# S21_Transformations_and_Vectors - Practice: Transformations and vectors

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Reflect the triangle with vertices (1, 1), (3, 1), (3, 4) in the line x = 4 and in the line y = -x. List the image coordinates.
2. Describe fully the rotation that takes (2, 1), (4, 1), (4, 3) to (-1, 2), (-1, 4), (-3, 4).
3. Translate the shape with the vector (-3, 2), then describe the single translation that maps the image back to the original.
4. Higher: OA = a and OB = b. P is the midpoint of OA and Q is the point on AB with AQ : QB = 1 : 2. Write OP, AB and OQ in terms of a and b.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
