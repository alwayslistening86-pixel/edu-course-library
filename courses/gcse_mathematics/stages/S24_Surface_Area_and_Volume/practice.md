# S24_Surface_Area_and_Volume - Practice: Surface area and volume of solids

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Find the volume and total surface area of a cuboid 8 cm by 5 cm by 3 cm, and of a cylinder of radius 4 cm and height 9 cm (answers in terms of pi).
2. A prism has a trapezium cross-section with parallel sides 5 cm and 9 cm, height 4 cm, and length 12 cm. Find its volume.
3. Higher: a cone has radius 6 cm and vertical height 8 cm. Find its slant height, volume and curved surface area.
4. Higher: a solid sphere of radius 3 cm is melted and recast as a cylinder of radius 1.5 cm. Find the height of the cylinder.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
