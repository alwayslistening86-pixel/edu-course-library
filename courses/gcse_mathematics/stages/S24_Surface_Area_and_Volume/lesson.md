# S24_Surface_Area_and_Volume - Lesson: Surface area and volume of solids

## Goal
The learner calculates surface area and volume of prisms, cylinders, spheres, cones, pyramids and composite solids.

## Syllabus items taught here
- 10.04a - Surface area and volume of cuboids, prisms, cylinders: find surface area and volume of prisms
- 10.04b - Surface area and volume of spheres, cones, composite solids: use given formulae for curved solids
- 10.04c - Surface area and volume of pyramids: use given formulae for pyramids

## How to teach this
Ask: 'How many small cubes fit in a box that is 6 by 4 by 3, and how much wrapping paper covers it?' It separates volume from surface area.

#### 10.04a Cuboids, prisms and cylinders
Volume of a prism = cross-sectional area x length. Cuboid: V = lwh, surface area = 2(lw + lh + wh). A triangular prism with a right-angled triangle cross-section 3 by 4 and length 10 has volume 1/2 x 3 x 4 x 10 = 60. Cylinder: V = pi r^2 h; curved surface area = 2 pi r h; total surface area = 2 pi r h + 2 pi r^2. For r = 5 and h = 12: V = 300 pi = 942.5 and total surface area = 120 pi + 50 pi = 170 pi = 534.1. Give units cm^3 or cm^2.

#### 10.04b Spheres, cones and composite solids
The formulae are given in the exam. Sphere: V = 4/3 pi r^3, surface area = 4 pi r^2. Cone: V = 1/3 pi r^2 h, curved surface area = pi r l where l is the slant height. Hemisphere: half a sphere; total surface area 3 pi r^2 including the flat face. For a solid made of a cylinder with a hemisphere on top, add or subtract the parts and take care that joined faces are not counted in the surface area. For a hemisphere of radius 4 on a cylinder of radius 4 and height 10, V = 160 pi + 2/3 pi x 64 = 202.67 pi = 636.7 cm^3.

#### 10.04c Pyramids
V = 1/3 x base area x perpendicular height (the vertical height, not the slant height). A square-based pyramid with base 6 cm and vertical height 8 cm has V = 1/3 x 36 x 8 = 96 cm^3. Its slant height on a face is sqrt(8^2 + 3^2) = sqrt(73) = 8.544 cm, and the surface area is 36 + 4 x 1/2 x 6 x 8.544 = 241.3 cm^2. Use Pythagoras to find heights when needed.
