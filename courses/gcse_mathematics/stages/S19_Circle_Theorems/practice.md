# S19_Circle_Theorems - Practice: Circle terminology and circle theorems

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. O is the centre of a circle. Angle ABC at the circumference is 41 degrees where AC is an arc. Find angle AOC and give a reason.
2. AB is a diameter. C lies on the circle with angle CAB = 27 degrees. Find angle ABC, giving two reasons.
3. A chord of length 24 cm is 5 cm from the centre of a circle. Find the radius. Find the length of a tangent from a point 17 cm from the centre of a circle of radius 8 cm.
4. PQRS is a cyclic quadrilateral with angle PQR = 98 degrees. Find angle PSR. The tangent at P makes 54 degrees with chord PR; find the angle in the alternate segment. Then prove the angle-in-a-semicircle theorem.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
