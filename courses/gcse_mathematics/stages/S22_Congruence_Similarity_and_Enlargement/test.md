PS = 6, SQ = 4, ST = 9. Prove the triangles are similar and find QR. c) A bottle has height 20 cm and volume 500 cm^3. A similar bottle has height 30 cm. Find its volume. d) Enlarge the triangle with vertices (2, 1), (4, 1), (4, 2) by scale factor -1/2 from the point (0, 0).'
