# S22_Congruence_Similarity_and_Enlargement - Practice: Congruence, similarity and enlargement

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Triangle ABC has AB = 5, BC = 7, AC = 8. Triangle DEF has DE = 5, EF = 7, DF = 8. Are they congruent? State the condition.
2. Prove that the diagonals of a rectangle are equal using congruent triangles.
3. Triangle ABC has DE parallel to BC (D on AB, E on AC). AD = 3, DB = 5 and DE = 4.5. Prove the triangles are similar, then find BC.
4. Enlarge the triangle (1, 1), (2, 1), (2, 3) by scale factor -2 from the origin. Two similar solids have surface areas 18 cm^2 and 50 cm^2; the smaller has volume 27 cm^3. Find the larger's volume.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
