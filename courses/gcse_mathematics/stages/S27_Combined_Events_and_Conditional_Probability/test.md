## How to run this

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) A card is chosen at random from cards numbered 1 to 20. Find P(multiple of 4 or 7). b) A spinner lands on red with probability 0.4 and blue with probability 0.6. It is spun twice. Draw a tree diagram and find the probability of two different colours.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A box has 5 chocolates (2 dark, 3 milk). Two are taken without replacement. a) Draw a tree diagram. b) Find P(both milk). c) Given that the first is dark, find P(second is dark). d) In a survey of 80 people, 45 have a dog, 30 have a cat and 12 have both; find P(has a dog | has a cat).'
