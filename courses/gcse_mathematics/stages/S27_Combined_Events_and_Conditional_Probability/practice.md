# S27_Combined_Events_and_Conditional_Probability - Practice: Venn diagrams, tree diagrams and conditional probability

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. In a group of 40 people, 22 like tea, 25 like coffee and 10 like both. Draw a Venn diagram and find P(tea only) and P(neither).
2. A biased coin has P(heads) = 0.6 and is flipped twice. Draw a tree diagram and find P(two heads) and P(at least one tail).
3. A bag has 4 green and 6 yellow counters. Two are taken without replacement. Find P(both green) and P(different colours).
4. Higher: using a Venn diagram for 60 students where 34 take Art, 28 take Music, 12 take both, find P(Music | Art) and P(Art | Music).

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
