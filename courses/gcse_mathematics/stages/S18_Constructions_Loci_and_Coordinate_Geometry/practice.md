# S18_Constructions_Loci_and_Coordinate_Geometry - Practice: Drawing, constructions, loci and coordinates in geometry

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Sketch, then accurately construct, triangle ABC with AB = 6 cm, BC = 5 cm and CA = 7 cm. Measure the largest angle.
2. Construct the perpendicular bisector of a 8 cm line PQ and the bisector of a 70-degree angle, keeping the construction arcs.
3. Find the length and midpoint of the line joining A(-2, 1) and B(4, 9). Show that A(0,0), B(4,2), C(3,5)... use A(0, 0), B(4, 2), C(2, 6) and decide whether triangle ABC has a right angle at B.
4. Higher: draw a rectangle 8 cm by 5 cm. Shade the region inside it that is closer to the long side than to the short side and more than 2 cm from the corner A. Construct the shortest route from a point P to a line and measure it.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
