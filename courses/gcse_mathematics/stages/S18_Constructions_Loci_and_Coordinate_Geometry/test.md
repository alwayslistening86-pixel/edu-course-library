## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Construct triangle XYZ with XY = 8 cm, angle X = 40 degrees and XZ = 5 cm. b) Construct the perpendicular bisector of XY, showing all construction arcs. c) Points A(1, 1) and B(7, 9): find the distance AB and the midpoint of AB.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A garden is a rectangle ABCD with AB = 10 m and BC = 6 m (scale 1 cm = 2 m). A tree is to be planted closer to AB than to AD and more than 3 m from C. Construct the region where it can be planted and shade it. Show the construction of the bisector of angle A.'
