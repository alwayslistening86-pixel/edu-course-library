# S25_Pythagoras_and_Trigonometry - Practice: Pythagoras' theorem and trigonometry

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A 5 m ladder has its foot 1.5 m from a wall. How high does it reach, and what angle does it make with the ground?
2. Find the space diagonal of a box 4 cm by 5 cm by 20 cm. Find sin 30, cos 60 and tan 45 exactly.
3. From a point 40 m from the base of a tower the angle of elevation of the top is 32 degrees. Find the height of the tower and the length of the line of sight.
4. Higher: in triangle PQR, PQ = 8, QR = 11 and angle PQR = 52 degrees. Find PR with the cosine rule, then angle PRQ with the sine rule, and the area of the triangle.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
