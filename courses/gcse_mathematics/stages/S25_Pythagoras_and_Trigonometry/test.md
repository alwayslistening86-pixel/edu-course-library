# S25_Pythagoras_and_Trigonometry - Test: Pythagoras' theorem and trigonometry

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) A right-angled triangle has shorter sides 9 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse. b) A ramp is 4 m long and rises 0.8 m. Find the angle it makes with the horizontal. c) A right-angled triangle has an angle of 40 degrees and hypotenuse 10 cm. Find the side opposite the angle.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'a) Write down the exact value of sin 60 x tan 30. b) In triangle ABC, AB = 12 cm, BC = 9 cm and angle ABC = 68 degrees. Find AC and then angle BAC. c) Find the area of the triangle. d) A cuboid is 8 cm by 6 cm by 5 cm; find the angle between its space diagonal and the base.'
