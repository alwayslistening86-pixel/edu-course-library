# S20_Three_Dimensional_Shapes - Lesson: Three-dimensional shapes, plans and elevations

## Goal
The learner names and describes 3D solids, knows their properties, and draws and reads plans and elevations.

## Syllabus items taught here
- 8.01d - Polyhedra terminology: face, edge, vertex, prism, pyramid etc.: describe 3D solids with correct terms
- 8.06a - Properties of cube, cuboid, prism, cylinder, pyramid, cone, sphere: know the features of standard solids
- 8.06b - Plans and elevations of 3D solids: draw and read plans and elevations

## How to teach this
Hold or sketch a triangular prism and ask: 'How many faces, edges and vertices does it have, and how did you count?' Use the results to introduce the vocabulary.

#### 8.01d Polyhedra terminology
A face is a flat surface, an edge is where two faces meet, and a vertex is a corner. A prism has two identical parallel end faces (the cross-section) joined by rectangles. A pyramid has a base and triangular faces meeting at an apex. A polyhedron has only flat faces. Use the correct term in each description and count accurately.

#### 8.06a Properties of standard solids
Cube: 6 faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices. Cuboid: same counts, with opposite faces identical rectangles. Triangular prism: 5 faces, 9 edges, 6 vertices. Square-based pyramid: 5 faces, 8 edges, 5 vertices. For polyhedra, V - E + F = 2 (a good check). Cylinder: 2 flat faces and 1 curved surface, 2 curved edges, no vertices. Cone: 1 flat face, 1 curved surface, 1 vertex. Sphere: one curved surface with no edges or vertices. A net is the flat pattern that folds into the solid; the same solid can have several nets.

#### 8.06b Plans and elevations
The plan is the view from above; the front elevation is the view from the front; the side elevation is the view from the side. Draw what you see: hidden edges are not shown, and edges between faces of different depth are drawn as lines. A triangular prism lying on a rectangular face has a plan that is a rectangle, a front elevation that is a triangle and a side elevation that is a rectangle. Higher: given three views, build the solid from cubes and check each view.
