# S20_Three_Dimensional_Shapes - Practice: Three-dimensional shapes, plans and elevations

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give the number of faces, edges and vertices of a hexagonal prism and check with V - E + F = 2.
2. Describe the differences between a cone and a cylinder using face, edge and vertex vocabulary.
3. Sketch the plan, front elevation and side elevation of an L-shaped prism made from three unit cubes joined in an L, with a height of one cube.
4. Higher: a solid has a square plan, a triangular front elevation and a triangular side elevation. Name the solid, then sketch it.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
