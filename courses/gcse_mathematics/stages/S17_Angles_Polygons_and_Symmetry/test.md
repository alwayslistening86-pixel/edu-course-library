## Test scenario
Foundation: 'In triangle PQR, PQ = PR and angle QPR = 48 degrees. a) Work out angle PQR, giving a reason for each step. b) Line ST is parallel to QR and passes through P, with S on the same side as Q. Find the angle between PS and PQ, giving the angle fact. c) Give the order of rotational symmetry of a regular hexagon.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A regular polygon has interior angle 156 degrees. Work out the number of sides. Then prove that the opposite angles of a parallelogram ABCD are equal, using angles in parallel lines.'
