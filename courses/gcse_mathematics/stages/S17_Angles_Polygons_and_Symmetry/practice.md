# S17_Angles_Polygons_and_Symmetry - Practice: Geometry vocabulary, angle facts, polygons and symmetry

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Two straight lines cross. One angle is 4x + 10 and its vertically opposite angle is 6x - 20. Find x and all four angles, stating your reasons.
2. AB is parallel to CD. A transversal cuts AB at P and CD at Q. Angle BPQ = 72 degrees. Find angle PQC and angle PQD, naming each angle fact.
3. Find the interior angle of a regular 15-sided polygon, and the number of sides of a regular polygon with each exterior angle 24 degrees.
4. Name the quadrilateral: diagonals bisect each other at 90 degrees but are not equal. State its lines of symmetry and order of rotational symmetry. Higher: prove that the angles of any quadrilateral sum to 360 degrees.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
