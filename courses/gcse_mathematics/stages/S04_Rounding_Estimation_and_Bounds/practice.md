# S04_Rounding_Estimation_and_Bounds - Practice: Rounding, estimation and bounds

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Round 0.070 493 to 2 significant figures, 3 decimal places and 1 significant figure.
2. Estimate 19.8 x 6.1 / 0.52 by rounding to 1 significant figure. Was your estimate too big or too small, and why?
3. A length is 8.4 m to 1 decimal place. Write its error interval, then the greatest possible perimeter of a square with this side.
4. Higher: x = 5.3 and y = 2.1, both to 1 decimal place. Find the lower bound of x - y and the upper bound of x / y.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
