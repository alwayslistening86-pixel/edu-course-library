# S01_Integers_Primes_and_Order - Test: Integers, primes, factors and order of operations

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Write 210 as a product of its prime factors. b) Find the HCF and LCM of 24 and 36. c) Work out 2 + 3 x (7 - 9)^2 - 12 / 4, showing each step.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Given 720 = 2^4 x 3^2 x 5 and 1050 = 2 x 3 x 5^2 x 7, find the HCF and LCM of 720 and 1050, leaving your answers in index form, and explain why 1050 x 720 = HCF x LCM.'

## Grading
