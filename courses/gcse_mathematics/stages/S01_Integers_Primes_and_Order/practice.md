# S01_Integers_Primes_and_Order - Practice: Integers, primes, factors and order of operations

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Work out -8 x (-3) + (-15) / 5, then explain each sign in words.
2. Write 180 as a product of prime factors. Higher: write it using indices and use it to find the HCF and LCM of 180 and 150.
3. Two lighthouses flash every 12 s and 18 s and flash together at 9 pm. When do they next flash together? Which idea is this, HCF or LCM?
4. Evaluate 3 + 4 x 2^3 - (10 - 4) / 2 with no calculator, then predict how a calculator would evaluate 3 + 4 x 2^3 if the learner forgot the order.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
