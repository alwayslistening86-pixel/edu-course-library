# S26_Probability_Basics - Test: Probability basics, relative frequency and counting

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) A fair 8-sided dice is rolled. Find P(a number greater than 5) and P(a square number). b) A coin is flipped 150 times; heads come up 83 times. Estimate P(heads) and comment on whether the coin might be biased. c) List all outcomes for choosing one of three sandwiches and one of two drinks.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A code has three characters: a letter from A to E followed by two digits from 1 to 9. How many codes are possible if digits can repeat, and how many if they cannot? A machine is used 500 times and produces a fault 18 times: estimate the probability of a fault and the expected number of faults in 2500 uses.'
