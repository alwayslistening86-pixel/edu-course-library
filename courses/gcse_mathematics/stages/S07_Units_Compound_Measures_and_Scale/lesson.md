# S07_Units_Compound_Measures_and_Scale - Lesson: Units, compound measures, scales and bearings

## Goal
The learner converts between units, calculates with speed, density and pressure, and uses map scales, scale drawings and three-figure bearings.

## Syllabus items taught here
- 10.01a - Convert standard units: convert length, mass, capacity and area units
- 10.01b - Compound units: speed, density, pressure: calculate with compound measures
- 10.01c - Map scales, bearings, scale drawings: use scales and three-figure bearings

## How to teach this
Ask: 'How many square centimetres are in one square metre?' Most learners say 100; drawing a 1 m square marked in centimetres shows 10 000.

#### 10.01a Converting standard units
Know metric conversions (1 km = 1000 m, 1 m = 100 cm, 1 kg = 1000 g, 1 litre = 1000 ml) and common time and imperial links (1 inch is about 2.54 cm, 1 gallon is about 4.5 litres). For areas and volumes, square or cube the length factor: 1 m^2 = 100 x 100 cm^2 = 10 000 cm^2, and 1 m^3 = 1 000 000 cm^3. Higher problems use these in algebraic contexts and need the units carried through.

#### 10.01b Compound units: speed, density and pressure
Speed = distance / time; density = mass / volume; pressure = force / area. Use the formula triangle or rearrange, and keep units consistent: 90 km/h = 90 000 m / 3600 s = 25 m/s. Average speed is total distance over total time, not the average of two speeds. A metal block of mass 2 700 g and volume 100 cm^3 has density 27 g/cm^3. When a compound unit is requested, state it (m/s, g/cm^3, N/m^2).

#### 10.01c Map scales, scale drawings and bearings
A scale 1 : 25 000 means 1 cm on the map is 25 000 cm = 250 m on the ground. Scale drawings use a stated scale for lengths. A bearing is measured clockwise from north, always with three figures (035 degrees, 270 degrees). To find the back bearing add or subtract 180 degrees. Draw the north line at each point and use a protractor carefully; parallel north lines give alternate/co-interior angle relationships that help with calculation.
