# S07_Units_Compound_Measures_and_Scale - Practice: Units, compound measures, scales and bearings

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Convert 3.5 m^2 to cm^2 and 0.8 litres to cm^3. Explain why the area factor is not 100.
2. A car travels 84 km in 1 h 45 min. Find its average speed in km/h and in m/s.
3. A map has scale 1 : 50 000. Two towns are 6.4 cm apart. Find the real distance in km. B is on a bearing of 072 degrees from A: find the bearing of A from B.
4. A steel cube of side 5 cm has mass 990 g. Find its density in g/cm^3 and in kg/m^3.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
