# S23_Perimeter_and_Area - Test: Perimeter and area

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) A parallelogram has base 14 cm and perpendicular height 6.5 cm; find its area. b) A circle has diameter 18 cm; find the circumference and the area, giving answers to 1 decimal place. c) A shape is a rectangle 12 cm by 8 cm with a semicircle of diameter 8 cm attached to one 8 cm side. Find its area and perimeter.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'a) Find the area of a triangle with sides 9.3 cm and 11.2 cm and included angle 52 degrees. b) A sector of angle 140 degrees has radius 15 cm; find its arc length and area in terms of pi.'
