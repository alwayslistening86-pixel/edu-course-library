# S23_Perimeter_and_Area - Practice: Perimeter and area

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Find the perimeter and area of a rectangle 9.5 cm by 4 cm and of a trapezium with parallel sides 6 cm and 11 cm, height 4 cm.
2. A circle has radius 12 cm. Find its circumference and area. A sector of angle 75 degrees is cut from it: find the arc length and sector area.
3. A shape is a square of side 10 cm with a quarter circle of radius 10 cm removed from one corner (centre at that corner). Find its perimeter and area.
4. Higher: triangle ABC has AB = 8.4 cm, BC = 6.2 cm and angle ABC = 63 degrees. Find its area. A chord subtends 120 degrees at the centre of a circle of radius 6 cm; find the segment area.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
