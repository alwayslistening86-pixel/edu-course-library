# GCSE Mathematics (OCR J560) — Foundation and Higher tier - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.

## Format
A full, timed 1 hour 30 minute, 100-mark paper at the learner's tier, mixing questions from every stage in non-sequential order (as real papers do) rather than grouped by topic. Run one non-calculator and one calculator paper across two sittings for full coverage, matching OCR's real three-paper structure as closely as practical.

## Example structure
A 25-30 question mixed paper covering: number, fractions, percentages, rounding and standard form (S01-S05); ratio, proportion, units and compound measures (S06-S07); algebra: expressions, equations, inequalities, formulae, functions and sequences (S08-S13); graphs (S14-S16); geometry: angles, constructions, circles, solids, transformations and similarity (S17-S22); mensuration and trigonometry (S23-S25); probability (S26-S27); statistics (S28). At Higher tier include the Higher-only extensions from each stage (surds, bounds, algebraic fractions, completing the square, iteration, circle theorems, vectors, sine and cosine rules, conditional probability, cumulative frequency and box plots).

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly. A pass requires consistent performance across all strands under mixed, timed conditions -- not just isolated stage-test passes -- with working shown clearly enough that method marks would genuinely be awarded.

## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete (for that tier).
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name which stage(s) broke down under mixed/timed conditions, offer targeted review or a fresh retry paper.
