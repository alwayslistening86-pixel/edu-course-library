# S20_R035_Delivering_and_Evaluating_a_Campaign - Practice: R035 Topic Area 4: delivering a campaign and evaluating own performance

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a 30-second introduction for a campaign session on healthy snacking for 12 to 13 year olds.
2. List three things that make content delivery 'effective' rather than merely 'adequate'.
3. Choose a feedback method and write how you would record responses honestly, including a hypothetical negative comment.
4. Write one strength and one weakness for a fictional delivery, each with a piece of supporting evidence.
5. Turn the weakness you wrote into a specific, justified suggestion for improvement.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
