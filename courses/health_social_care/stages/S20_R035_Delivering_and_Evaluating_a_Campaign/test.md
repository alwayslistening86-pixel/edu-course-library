## How to run this

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'After delivering a 15-minute campaign session on hand hygiene to a class of Year 7 students, you collected feedback: three students said the hand-washing demonstration was helpful, two said the session felt too long, and one questionnaire response was left blank. (a) Describe how you would have introduced this session (3 marks). (b) Describe two features of effective content delivery you would have used (4 marks). (c) Using the feedback given, write an evaluation covering one strength and one weakness of your planning, communication and engagement (6 marks). (d) Suggest one specific, justified improvement based on this feedback (3 marks).'
