# S01_How_The_Qualification_Works - Practice: How the Cambridge National in Health and Social Care works

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A learner scores raw marks of 55/70 on R032, and 44/60 and 47/60 on the NEA units. Assuming the raw marks convert at the same rate to UMS (use 55 x 80/70 for the exam, rounded), estimate the UMS total and the likely overall grade. Explain why real UMS conversion uses boundaries and not a straight ratio.
2. State in one sentence each which performance objective is being tested by: (a) naming three PPE items, (b) explaining how a homeless man might be affected by poor communication, (c) evaluating a campaign you delivered.
3. Explain the difference between the MB1 word 'limited' and the MB2 word 'partial' with an example sentence about why a person might feel lonely after retirement.
4. Why does the terminal rule mean a January exam sat in Year 10 would not count if the learner certificates in June of Year 11?

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
