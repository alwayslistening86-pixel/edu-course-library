# S01_How_The_Qualification_Works - Test: How the Cambridge National in Health and Social Care works

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'Amira has completed R033 and R035 and scored 48 and 51 UMS. She sits R032 in the final series and gets 66 UMS. (a) Calculate her total and say which overall grade this is. (b) State two things about how R032 is marked that differ from how R033 is marked. (c) The R032 paper has an 8-mark question: name the performance objective it assesses and say what kind of answer is needed. (d) Explain what OCR means by the word comprehensive in a mark band.' Expected: 165 UMS, Distinction at Level 2 (160 to 179); exam is OCR-marked with levels of response, NEA is teacher-marked and moderated with mark bands; PO3, discussion or evaluation with judgement; comprehensive means complete, with depth and breadth, fully fit for purpose.
