# S07_Safeguarding - Practice: Safeguarding service users

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Define safeguarding in your own words and give a reason why older adults in residential care may need it.
2. List three signs of possible abuse that a care worker might notice and say who they would report to.
3. Write one sentence distinguishing a standard DBS check, an enhanced check and the barred list.
4. Explain how a lack of safeguarding might affect a 15-year-old with a learning disability in a residential unit across all four PIES areas.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
