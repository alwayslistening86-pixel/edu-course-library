# S21_R035_NEA_Skills_and_Task1_Evidence - Practice: R035 NEA skills and Task 1 evidence: research, independence rules and the public health challenge

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Find two different types of source (not from this lesson) about a public health challenge of your choice and write one fact each source adds, with a full reference for both.
2. Explain in your own words why 'I chose this topic because it's interesting' would only reach MB1 for the reasons-for-choice criterion.
3. Write what full understanding of importance to a healthy society would look like for a challenge of your choice, linking to at least two of the four reasons for a healthy society.
4. List three things a teacher must not do while a student is planning their R035 campaign, and explain why each rule exists.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
