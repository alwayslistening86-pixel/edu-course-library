# S09_Safety_Procedures_Measures_and_Security - Practice: Safety procedures, safety measures and security

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write one-line definitions of procedure and measure, with two examples of each from a hospital ward.
2. Explain how a fire evacuation procedure protects a residential home for people with limited mobility.
3. Explain why a visitor signing-in book helps in an emergency.
4. Choose three security measures for a children's day centre and explain how each protects children and staff.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
