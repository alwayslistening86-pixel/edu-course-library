## Test scenario
Scenario: 'St Anne's Day Centre for older adults has a new manager. In the last month a visitor walked in unchallenged, a wheelchair brake was reported broken but left in use, and there was no assembly-point roll call during a fire drill. (a) Identify one procedure and one measure the centre could use to reduce slips (2 marks). (b) State the difference between a procedure and a measure (2 marks). (c) Explain how equipment considerations protect service users and staff (6 marks; Level 3 needs two examples, developed points). (d) Discuss how security measures could protect users and staff in the centre (8 marks).'
