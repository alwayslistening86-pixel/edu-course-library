# S12_Factors_Affecting_Growth_and_Development - Practice: R033 factors affecting growth and development

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give one positive and one negative example for each of the six factor types affecting a teenager.
2. Explain in three sentences how family income (economic) could affect a child's physical and intellectual development.
3. For a fictional 70-year-old widow, explain how two social and two emotional factors might affect her wellbeing.
4. Check this sentence for 'explain' versus 'describe': 'Pollution is bad for children.' Rewrite it to be an explanation.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
