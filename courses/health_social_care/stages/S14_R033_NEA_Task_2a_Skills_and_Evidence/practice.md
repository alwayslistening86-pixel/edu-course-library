# S14_R033_NEA_Task_2a_Skills_and_Evidence - Practice: R033 NEA skills for Task 2a: interviewing, authenticity and evidencing life events

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Plan eight open interview questions for a fictional interview about a person's life event, arranged by PIES and financial. Then check none is a yes/no question.
2. Explain in your own words the difference between quoting a source and showing understanding of it.
3. Write the referencing details you would record for a website, a book and an AI tool.
4. Explain why 'Mr Khan, 47 Elm Road, was made redundant' could not be submitted to the moderator, and how you would anonymise it.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
