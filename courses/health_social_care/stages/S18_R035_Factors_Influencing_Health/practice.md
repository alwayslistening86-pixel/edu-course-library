# S18_R035_Factors_Influencing_Health - Practice: R035 Topic Area 2: factors influencing health, leading a healthy lifestyle and barriers

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Sort these into the correct factor group: smoking, low income, poor GP access, stress, exercise, qualifications, rural location, diet.
2. Explain how two lifestyle factors and one socio-economic factor could combine to affect one fictional person's health.
3. List all eight healthy-lifestyle choices and give one PIES benefit for four of them.
4. Explain how advertising and cost could both act as barriers for the same person trying to eat more healthily.
5. Explain the difference between a factor that influences health and a barrier that stops healthy behaviour.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
