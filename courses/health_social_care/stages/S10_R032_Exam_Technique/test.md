# S10_R032_Exam_Technique - Test: R032 exam technique: paper structure, command words and extended answers

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'Lena, 16, has a learning disability and is starting at a college with a health centre on site. The nurse, Ade, wants to make her feel comfortable. (a) State two special methods of communication that could be used (2 marks). (b) Explain how two ways of protecting Lena from harm work in the health centre (6 marks, Level 3 needs two developed examples with terminology). (c) Discuss how Ade's communication skills and use of person-centred values could affect Lena's health and wellbeing (8 marks, PO3, Level 3 needs two or more examples and a developed, balanced discussion). Time: 15 minutes.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S10_R032_Exam_Technique` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
