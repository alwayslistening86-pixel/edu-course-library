# S06_Active_Listening_Special_Methods_and_Why_Communication_Matters - Test: Active listening, special methods of communication and the importance of effective communication

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'Mr Osei, 70, recently had a stroke that affects his speech and right hand. At a residential home, staff are sometimes rushed and talk over him. His daughter thinks he understands more than staff realise. (a) Name two special methods of communication that could help Mr Osei and say how each would help (4 marks). (b) Describe how a carer could use active listening with Mr Osei (4 marks). (c) Discuss the impact of good and poor communication on Mr Osei's health and wellbeing (8 marks; Level 3 needs two examples and a balanced, developed answer).'
