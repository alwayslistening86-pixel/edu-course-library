# S06_Active_Listening_Special_Methods_and_Why_Communication_Matters - Practice: Active listening, special methods of communication and the importance of effective communication

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a short dialogue of a care worker using at least four active listening skills with a resident who is upset about a missed family visit. Label each skill.
2. Match each to a person and explain: advocate, Braille, BSL, interpreter, Makaton, voice-activated software.
3. A receptionist records the wrong allergy on a patient's file. Explain the possible consequences and name the communication skill that failed.
4. Explain why jargon is a barrier and give two examples of jargon replaced by plain words.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
