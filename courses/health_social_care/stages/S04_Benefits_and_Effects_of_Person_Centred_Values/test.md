# S04_Benefits_and_Effects_of_Person_Centred_Values - Test: Benefits of person-centred values and the effects when they are not applied

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'At Willow Day Centre, staff do not ask members what activities they would like. Mr Patel, who loves cooking and is diabetic, is given the same activity every day and sweet snacks. He has stopped coming to the coffee morning and shouts at staff. (a) Identify two person-centred values not applied (2 marks). (b) Explain two benefits to the day centre of applying person-centred values (4 marks). (c) Discuss the effects on Mr Patel's health and wellbeing of the values not being applied, making links between at least three PIES areas (8 marks; Level 3 needs two or more examples considered, a developed, connected discussion and consistent terminology).'
