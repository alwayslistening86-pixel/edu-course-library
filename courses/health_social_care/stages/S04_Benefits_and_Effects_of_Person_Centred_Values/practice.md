# S04_Benefits_and_Effects_of_Person_Centred_Values - Practice: Benefits of person-centred values and the effects when they are not applied

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give three benefits of applying person-centred values to a care home and explain each with an example from care staff.
2. Explain how standardisation of care benefits a service user with dementia who sees different carers each day.
3. A nursing home resident who needs a soft-textured diet is repeatedly given normal meals. Write a chain showing one physical, one emotional and one social effect and how they connect.
4. Write a definition of each PIES term and give one example of a 'not applied' effect for a child in a foster placement.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
