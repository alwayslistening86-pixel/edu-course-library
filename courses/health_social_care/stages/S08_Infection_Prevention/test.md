# S08_Infection_Prevention - Test: Infection prevention: cleanliness, personal hygiene and PPE

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'Priya works as a care assistant in a nursing home. Yesterday she helped two residents with personal care, then made drinks for the lounge, wearing the same gloves and a ring with a stone. Two residents now have a stomach bug. (a) Identify two infection prevention mistakes Priya made (2 marks). (b) Describe two personal hygiene measures the home should expect of Priya (4 marks). (c) Explain how a range of infection prevention measures protect residents and staff in a nursing home (6 marks; Level 3 needs two examples with developed points and consistent use of terms such as cross-infection).'
