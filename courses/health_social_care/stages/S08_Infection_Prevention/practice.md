# S08_Infection_Prevention - Practice: Infection prevention: cleanliness, personal hygiene and PPE

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why a children's day nursery must clean toys regularly and one way this protects staff.
2. Put the steps for a correct hand washing routine in order and explain why jewellery should not be worn.
3. For each of these tasks choose PPE and justify: dressing a leg wound; cleaning a bathroom; serving lunch in a care home; assisting in an operating theatre.
4. Explain how infection prevention in a walk-in centre protects both patients and staff.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
