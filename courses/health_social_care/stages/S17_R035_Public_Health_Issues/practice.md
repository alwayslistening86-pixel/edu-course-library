# S17_R035_Public_Health_Issues - Practice: R035 Topic Area 1: current public health issues and why a healthy society matters

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. State all four reasons OCR gives for why a healthy society matters, in your own words.
2. Choose two public health challenges from the specification list and, for each, state what it is, who is most affected and one impact on society.
3. Name one charity, the NHS, a government agency and the WHO, and give one real example of what each does for public health.
4. Pick a real campaign (not from this lesson) and describe its aim, one benefit it offers and its target audience.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
