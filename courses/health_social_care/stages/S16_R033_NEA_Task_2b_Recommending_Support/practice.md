# S16_R033_NEA_Task_2b_Recommending_Support - Practice: R033 NEA Task 2b: research and recommending personalised support

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Create a research log layout of your own with columns you think you need, and explain why each column matters for authenticity.
2. Read this fictional statement: 'Mrs Doyle needs company; the charity Age UK exists.' Explain whether it describes, justifies or applies a value, and what would be missing for MB3.
3. Explain in your own words the difference between formal, informal and charity support in a recommendation and why coordinated care matters.
4. For a fictional man with anxiety after redundancy, list the person-centred values from R032 and say how each could be shown in a recommendation.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
