# S15_Sources_of_Support - Test: R033 sources of support, practitioners and informal carers

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'Mrs Hughes, 74, is recently bereaved and has had a fall that reduces her mobility. Her son lives 200 miles away. (a) Give one example each of formal, informal and charity support suitable for her and say what each would provide (6 marks). (b) Describe the role of one practitioner who could help her mobility and one who could help her grief (4 marks). (c) Explain how one of the six ways of meeting needs would be applied for her (2 marks).'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S15_Sources_of_Support` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
