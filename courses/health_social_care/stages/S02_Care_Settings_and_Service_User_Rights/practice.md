# S02_Care_Settings_and_Service_User_Rights - Practice: Care settings and the rights of service users

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Sort into health or social care: pharmacy, foodbank, walk-in centre, day centre, opticians, homeless shelter, nursing home, support group. Then justify your placement of the nursing home.
2. For each of the five rights, write one example of how a care worker in a day centre for older adults would meet it.
3. Explain, using a chain of reasoning, how the right to confidentiality benefits a service user's wellbeing.
4. A resident is never asked what time she wants to get up and is hurried through washing. Name two rights not being met and one likely effect on her.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
