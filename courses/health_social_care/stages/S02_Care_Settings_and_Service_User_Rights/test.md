## Test scenario
Scenario: 'Tomas, aged 78, has moved into a residential home after a stroke. Staff always ask him what he would like to eat, they talk to him and his daughter about his care plan, and they keep his notes locked away. (a) Identify two health care settings and two social care settings (4 marks). (b) Explain how any two of Tomas's rights are being met in this scenario (4 marks). (c) Explain how maintaining his rights could benefit Tomas's health and wellbeing (6 marks, Level 3 needs two developed examples using terms such as empowerment and trust).'
