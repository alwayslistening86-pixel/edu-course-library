# S19_R035_Planning_a_Campaign - Practice: R035 Topic Area 3: planning a health promotion campaign

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write one PIES-related aim for a campaign encouraging better sleep habits among 14 to 16 year olds.
2. List the resources, a realistic timescale and one safety consideration for a 20-minute campaign session delivered to a small group.
3. Choose two engagement methods for an audience of primary-school children and justify each choice.
4. Design a simple feedback method suitable for a teenage audience and write two questions it would ask.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
