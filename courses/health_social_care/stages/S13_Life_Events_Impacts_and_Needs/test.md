## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: 'Ravi, 47, has a heart attack (unexpected). He can no longer do his physical job, must take three months off, and his wife works part time. (a) Classify Ravi's event and say why it is unexpected (2 marks). (b) Explain how the event may affect Ravi physically, intellectually, emotionally, socially and financially (10 marks). (c) Identify three needs of Ravi's arising from these impacts (3 marks).'
