# S13_Life_Events_Impacts_and_Needs - Practice: R033 life events, their impacts and the needs they create

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Classify each as physical event, relationship change or life circumstance, and expected or unexpected: retirement; divorce; a broken leg from a skiing fall; changing school at 12; puberty; a company closing.
2. For a fictional man aged 52 who is made redundant, explain one impact in each of the five areas.
3. For a woman who becomes a widow at 68, list three needs and link each to a specific impact.
4. Explain why two people who both experience divorce might have different needs.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
