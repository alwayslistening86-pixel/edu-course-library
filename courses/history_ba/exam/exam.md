# History (BA Hons) - Open University qualification structure (Q01) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Three essays (30 marks each, one from each stage of the degree) plus one source-analysis question (20 marks). Questions are original, in undergraduate-history style; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage tests. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 4 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Stage 1] 'Revolutions are made by ideas, not by economic and social structures.' Assess the validity of this view with reference to at least two revolutions you have studied. [30 marks]
2. [Stage 2] 'Witch persecution in early modern Europe was primarily a mechanism of gender control, not of religious belief.' Assess the validity of this view. [30 marks]
3. [Stage 3] 'The Holocaust was the product of long-planned Nazi ideology, not of wartime circumstance and cumulative radicalisation.' Assess the validity of this view, engaging with the intentionalist/functionalist historiographical debate. [30 marks]
4. [Source analysis] Source: a British parliamentary select committee report of 1834, drawing on interviews with factory workers, recommending restrictions on child labour in textile mills. With reference to its genre, provenance, perspective and your own knowledge, evaluate the value and limitations of this source for an enquiry into the human cost of Britain's Industrial Revolution. [20 marks]

## Grading
Mark essays by this course's undergraduate-history levels (1-5) and the source question by this course's source-analysis levels. A pass needs at least 40% of the 110 marks overall (the standard UK honours pass threshold) and a Level 3 or higher on at least two of the three essays. Report the total, the mark per question and the weakest topic areas.

## Outcome
- **Pass:** record `exam_status: "passed"`. The course is complete.
- **Not yet:** leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the weakest topic areas, offer targeted review, and retry with fresh papers.
