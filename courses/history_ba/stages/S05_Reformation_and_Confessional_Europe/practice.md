# S05_Reformation_and_Confessional_Europe - Practice: The Catholic Reformation; the Thirty Years' War; print culture and the witch trials

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two ways the Council of Trent responded to the Protestant Reformation. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 reaffirmed contested Catholic doctrine, explained (e.g. transubstantiation, faith plus good works) (B1 if stated but not explained); B2 reformed clerical discipline/training, explained (residency requirements, seminaries) (B1 if stated but not explained).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
