## Test items
1. Explain why the Provisional Government lost authority between February and October 1917. [6 marks]
2. 'The Treaty of Versailles was a fair and moderate peace settlement.' Assess the validity of this view, engaging with the historiographical debate. [30 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 continued Russia's participation in the unpopular war, alienating soldiers and civilians; B1 failed to resolve peasant land hunger, a central popular demand; B1 shared uneasy 'dual power' with the Petrograd Soviet, undermining its practical authority; B1 the Kornilov Affair (August 1917) discredited the right and, by contrast, boosted Bolshevik credibility after they helped resist it; B1 Lenin's April Theses gave the Bolsheviks a clear, popular slogan ('Peace, Land, Bread') the Provisional Government could not match; B1 credit any other accurate, developed point (e.g. economic collapse, war-weariness).

Credit: the case against fairness/moderation - Keynes's contemporary critique of the reparations as economically ruinous, the War Guilt clause's punitive symbolism, severe territorial and military restrictions; the case for its relative moderation - Germany survived as a unified state and was not partitioned, comparisons with the even harsher terms Germany itself had imposed on Russia at Brest-Litovsk (1918), and later historians' defence of Versailles as more measured than nationalist myth later claimed; a strong answer names Keynes and engages with the debate rather than asserting one side.
