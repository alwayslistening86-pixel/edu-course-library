## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two features of the Berlin Blockade and Airlift, 1948-49. [4 marks]

[4] B2 the USSR cut Western land access to West Berlin, attempting to force the Allies out, explained (B1 if stated but not explained); B2 the Western Allies responded with a sustained air-supply operation (the Airlift) rather than military confrontation, explained (B1 if stated but not explained); credit also that the crisis hardened the division of Germany into two separate states (1949).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
