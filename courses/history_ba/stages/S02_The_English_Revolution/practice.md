# S02_The_English_Revolution - Practice: Causes of the English Civil War; regicide and republic; Restoration and the Glorious Revolution

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two causes of the English Civil War, one financial/constitutional and one religious. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 financial/constitutional cause named and briefly explained: Ship Money or Personal Rule/ruling without Parliament, or the attempt to arrest the five MPs (B1 if named but not explained); B2 religious cause named and briefly explained: Laud's reforms or the Scottish Prayer Book crisis/Bishops' Wars (B1 if named but not explained).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
