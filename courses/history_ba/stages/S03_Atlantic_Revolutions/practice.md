# S03_Atlantic_Revolutions - Practice: The American, French and Haitian Revolutions

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two Enlightenment or ideological influences on the American and French Revolutions. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)

[4] B2 Locke's natural-rights/consent-of-the-governed theory, explained (American) (B1 if named but not explained); B2 the language of universal rights in the Declaration of the Rights of Man, explained (French) (B1 if named but not explained); credit any two accurate, developed influences, e.g. 'no taxation without representation' as an application of consent theory.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
