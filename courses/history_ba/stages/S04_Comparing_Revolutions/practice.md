# S04_Comparing_Revolutions - Practice: Theories of revolution; the Russian Revolution of 1917 as a comparative case; synthesis

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give one similarity and one difference between Skocpol's structural theory of revolution and a Marxist explanation of the French Revolution. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 similarity, explained: both look to underlying social/structural conditions rather than only individual decisions (B1 if stated but not explained); B2 difference, explained: Skocpol's model is comparative and emphasises international/military pressure on the state plus peasant revolt, while Marxist accounts of France emphasise bourgeois-versus-aristocratic class conflict specifically (B1 if stated but not explained).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
