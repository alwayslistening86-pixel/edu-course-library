## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner predicts or attempts each item first, then checks it (by running code, or against the model answer). Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain, in general terms, why Huffman coding gives frequently occurring characters shorter codes. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 shorter codes are used for the characters that appear most often; B1 so the total number of bits used across the whole (long) piece of text is minimised, even though rarer characters get longer codes.
