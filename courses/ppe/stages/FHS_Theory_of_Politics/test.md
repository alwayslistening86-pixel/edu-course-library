# FHS_Theory_of_Politics - Test: Theory of Politics (Paper 203)

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
"'Liberty and equality are not really in conflict - the appearance of conflict comes from equivocating between different conceptions of each.' Discuss, using the concepts of negative/positive liberty and at least one theory of equality or justice." A strong answer names the specific conceptions in play (as in the opening scenario) rather than asserting "liberty matters" or "equality matters" unqualified.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.FHS_Theory_of_Politics` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
