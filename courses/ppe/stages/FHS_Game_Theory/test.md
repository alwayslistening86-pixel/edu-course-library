## Test scenario
Two-part structure, matching the real paper. Part one: "Define Nash equilibrium and subgame perfect equilibrium, and explain, with an example, why the second is a genuine refinement of the first." Part two: "A government is deciding whether to intervene to prevent a bank run. Using a global-games framework, explain how limited common knowledge about the bank's true solvency can generate a unique prediction where a standard multiple-equilibrium model could not."
