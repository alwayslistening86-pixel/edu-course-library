# FHS_Game_Theory - Practice: Game Theory (Economics optional paper)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Build a payoff matrix for a real strategic situation (e.g. two firms setting prices) and find any dominant strategies and the Nash equilibrium.
2. Solve a simple market-entry game by backward induction and explain why the incumbent's threat to fight entry may not be credible.
3. Explain why an infinitely repeated Prisoner's Dilemma can sustain cooperation while a known, finitely repeated one may not.
4. Set up a simple Bayesian game with two possible types and solve for the Bayesian Nash equilibrium.
5. Compare first-price and second-price sealed-bid auctions and explain the revenue equivalence result in your own words.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
