# FHS_International_Relations - Practice: International Relations (Paper 214)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Using the opening scenario, write one paragraph of realist analysis and one paragraph of liberal-institutionalist analysis, each naming what evidence would distinguish them.
2. Explain a postcolonial critique of how "sovereignty" is applied unevenly to different states in practice.
3. Choose one post-1990 conflict or crisis and explain it using two different theoretical approaches from this stage.
4. Assess whether international law meaningfully constrained a real state's behaviour in a case of your choosing.
5. Explain the difference between a systemic (anarchy-driven) and a domestic-level explanation of a real foreign policy decision.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
