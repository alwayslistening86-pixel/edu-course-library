# Prelims_Politics - Test: Introduction to the Theory and Practice of Politics

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Answer as the paper requires: four questions, at least one from Section A and two from Section B. Q1 (Section A): "'Genuine freedom is obedience to a law we give ourselves.' Discuss with reference to Rousseau." Q2 (Section A): "Does Mill's harm principle give a coherent basis for opposing all paternalistic legislation?" Q3 (Section B): "Explain, with reference to at least one real case, why proportional and majoritarian electoral systems produce systematically different party systems." Q4 (Section B): "'Populism is best understood as a style of politics, not an ideology.' Discuss."

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.Prelims_Politics` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to Prelims_Philosophy.
