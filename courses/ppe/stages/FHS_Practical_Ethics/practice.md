# FHS_Practical_Ethics - Practice: Practical Ethics (Paper 128)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Using the trolley pair, state a principled account of doing/allowing or intention/foresight that explains the difference in intuitions - or argue the difference is not morally significant after all.
2. Apply the non-identity problem to a real climate-policy choice and explain why ordinary harm-based reasoning struggles with it.
3. Test retributivist and consequentialist justifications of punishment against the "punishing an innocent person" objection.
4. Using Thomson's violinist analogy, explain why moral status and a right to use another's body are separable questions in the abortion debate.
5. Assess Singer's drowning-child argument against the demandingness objection.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
