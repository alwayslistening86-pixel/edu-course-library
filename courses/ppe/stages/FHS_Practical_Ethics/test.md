# FHS_Practical_Ethics - Test: Practical Ethics (Paper 128)

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
"'If the outcome is the same, the manner of bringing it about cannot matter morally.' Discuss with reference to at least two of: war and self-defence, euthanasia, and the doctrine of double effect." A strong answer uses P128.10's concepts explicitly and connects at least two applied topics rather than treating them separately.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.FHS_Practical_Ethics` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to FHS_Theory_of_Politics.
