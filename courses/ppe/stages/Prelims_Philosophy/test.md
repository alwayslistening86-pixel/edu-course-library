# Prelims_Philosophy - Test: Introduction to Philosophy - Logic, General Philosophy, Moral Philosophy

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Show adequate knowledge in each of the three required sections, in the paper's own words. Logic: "Symbolise and evaluate the validity of: 'All philosophers are curious. No cynic is curious. Therefore, no cynic is a philosopher.'" General Philosophy: "Can the problem of induction be solved, or only managed?" Moral Philosophy: "'Utilitarianism cannot accommodate the special obligations we owe to family and friends.' Discuss with reference to Mill."

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.Prelims_Philosophy` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to Prelims_Economics.
