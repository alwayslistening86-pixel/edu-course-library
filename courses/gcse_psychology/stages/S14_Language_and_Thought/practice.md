# S14_Language_and_Thought - Practice: Language and thought, and human/animal communication

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State whether Piaget or the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis argues that thinking depends on/is shaped by language. [1 mark]
2. Briefly explain what Von Frisch's research found about honeybee communication. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis (linguistic relativity).
2. [2] B1 the waggle dance (a figure-of-eight dance whose angle and duration communicate direction and distance of a food source) allows bees to tell other bees where food is; B1 correctly attributed to Von Frisch.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
