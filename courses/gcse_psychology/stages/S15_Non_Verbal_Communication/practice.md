# S15_Non_Verbal_Communication - Practice: Non-verbal communication and its explanations

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name and briefly explain one function of eye contact. [2 marks]
2. Briefly describe what Yuki et al.'s emoticon study found. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 a genuine function, e.g. regulating the flow of conversation, signalling attraction, or expressing emotion; B1 correctly explained.
2. [2] B1 Japanese participants focused more on the eyes and American participants focused more on the mouth when interpreting an emoticon's emotion; B1 correctly attributed to Yuki et al.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
