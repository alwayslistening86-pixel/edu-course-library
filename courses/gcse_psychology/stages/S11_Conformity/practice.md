# S11_Conformity - Practice: Social influence: conformity

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain how increasing the difficulty of a task affects conformity, and why. [2 marks]
2. Briefly describe the task used in Asch's line-judgement study. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 conformity tends to increase as task difficulty/ambiguity increases; B1 because people are less confident in their own judgement and rely more on the group for guidance.
2. [2] B1 participants judged, out loud, which of three comparison lines matched a standard line in length; B1 confederates deliberately gave the same wrong answer on certain trials, before the genuine participant answered.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
