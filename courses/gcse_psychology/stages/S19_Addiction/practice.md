# S19_Addiction - Practice: Addiction: characteristics, explanations and treatments

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State two features of the ICD dependence syndrome criteria. [2 marks]
2. Briefly explain what Kaij's twin study found, and what it suggests about addiction. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 B1 any two of: tolerance; withdrawal; compulsion to use; loss of control; neglecting other activities; continuing despite harm (one mark each, up to two).
2. [2] B1 a significantly higher concordance rate for alcoholism was found in identical (monozygotic) twins than in non-identical (dizygotic) twins; B1 suggesting a genetic contribution/vulnerability to addiction.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
