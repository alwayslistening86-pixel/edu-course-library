# S19_Addiction - Test: Addiction: characteristics, explanations and treatments

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: multiple-choice, short-answer, calculation and extended-writing questions, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints, before any marking. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Distinguish between substance misuse and addiction. [2 marks]
2. Explain what tolerance and withdrawal mean as part of the ICD dependence syndrome criteria. [2 marks]
3. Describe Kaij's twin study and explain why comparing identical and non-identical twins is a useful way to investigate a genetic explanation of addiction. [4 marks]
4. Explain how aversion therapy is intended to treat addiction, and describe one alternative treatment. [3 marks]
5. Which ICD dependence syndrome criterion describes needing increasing amounts of a substance to achieve the same effect? Choose every correct option.
   A. tolerance
   B. withdrawal
   C. loss of control
   D. compulsion

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 substance misuse is using a substance in a harmful way/against guidance, without necessarily involving dependence; B1 addiction involves dependence -- compulsion, loss of control and withdrawal when the substance is reduced/stopped.
2. [2] B1 tolerance -- needing increasing amounts of the substance to achieve the same effect; B1 withdrawal -- unpleasant physical/psychological symptoms when the substance is stopped or reduced.
3. [4] B1 concordance rates for alcoholism were compared between identical (monozygotic) and non-identical (dizygotic) twins; B1 a significantly higher concordance rate was found in identical twins; B1 identical twins share all their genes while non-identical twins share on average half, so a higher concordance in identical twins is consistent with a genetic contribution; B1 correct caveat, e.g. this cannot fully rule out shared environment, since identical twins may also be treated more similarly.
4. [3] B1 aversion therapy pairs the substance with an unpleasant response (e.g. a nausea-inducing drug) to create a learned negative association with the substance; B1 correctly explained; B1 an alternative treatment correctly described, e.g. self-help groups/a 12-step programme, offering peer support and a structured route to recovery.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
