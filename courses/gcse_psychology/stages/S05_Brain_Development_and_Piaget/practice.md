# S05_Brain_Development_and_Piaget - Practice: Early brain development and Piaget's stage theory

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Identify the Piagetian stage in which a child typically first develops object permanence, and the stage in which they typically master conservation. [2 marks]
2. Briefly explain what McGarrigle and Donaldson's 'naughty teddy' study found, and why it challenges Piaget. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 object permanence -- sensorimotor stage; B1 conservation -- concrete operational stage.
2. [2] B1 when a 'naughty teddy' puppet appeared to accidentally rearrange the counters, significantly more young children correctly conserved than in Piaget's original task; B1 suggesting Piaget's own procedure/method may have confused children, rather than children genuinely lacking the ability to conserve that young.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
