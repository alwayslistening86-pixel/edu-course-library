# S06_Learning_Mindset_and_Style - Practice: Effects of learning on development: mindset and learning styles

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between a fixed mindset and a growth mindset. [2 marks]
2. Briefly explain Willingham's criticism of matching teaching to a learner's claimed 'learning style'. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 fixed mindset -- the belief that ability/intelligence is a fixed trait that cannot be meaningfully changed; B1 growth mindset -- the belief that ability can be developed through effort and learning from mistakes.
2. [2] B1 well-controlled studies do not show that matching teaching style to a person's claimed learning style actually improves learning outcomes; B1 the evidence instead supports matching the teaching method to the content being taught, regardless of the learner's stated preference.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
