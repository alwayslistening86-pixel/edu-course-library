# S09_Procedures_Reliability_and_Ethics - Practice: Research methods: procedures, reliability, validity and ethics

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain what counterbalancing is, and why it is used in a repeated measures design. [2 marks]
2. Distinguish between reliability and validity. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 half the participants complete the conditions in one order, half in the reverse order; B1 to cancel out order effects (e.g. practice or fatigue) across the group as a whole.
2. [2] B1 reliability is the consistency of a measure/finding, i.e. whether the same result would be obtained if repeated; B1 validity is whether a study/measure genuinely measures what it claims to, and whether its findings can be trusted/generalised.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
