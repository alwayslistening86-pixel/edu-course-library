# S18_Mental_Health_and_Depression - Test: Introduction to mental health and clinical depression

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: multiple-choice, short-answer, calculation and extended-writing questions, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints, before any marking. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Name three symptoms included in the ICD diagnostic criteria for depression. [3 marks]
2. Explain the psychological explanation of depression, referring to negative schemas and attributions. [3 marks]
3. Describe Wiles et al.'s study and explain what it found about the effectiveness of CBT. [4 marks]
4. Explain one way poor mental health can affect the individual, and one way it can affect wider society. [2 marks]
5. Which explanation of depression proposes that low levels of serotonin contribute to low mood? Choose every correct option.
   A. the biological explanation
   B. the psychological explanation
   C. Piaget's stage theory
   D. the Sapir-Whorf hypothesis

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 B1 B1 any three of: persistently low mood; low energy; disturbed sleep; changed appetite; reduced self-confidence/self-worth (one mark each, up to three).
2. [3] B1 negative schemas -- deeply held, overly negative patterns of belief about the self, the world and the future; B1 negative attributions -- habitually explaining negative events as one's own fault, permanent and affecting everything; B1 both together making a person more vulnerable to becoming/remaining depressed.
3. [4] B1 a clinical trial investigated adding CBT to usual antidepressant treatment, for patients whose depression had not responded well to medication alone; B1 correctly described; B1 those who received CBT alongside usual treatment showed significantly better improvement than those who continued usual treatment alone; B1 supporting CBT's effectiveness as a treatment for depression.
4. [2] B1 individual effect, e.g. strained relationships, or effects on physical wellbeing such as disrupted sleep/appetite; B1 societal effect, e.g. increased demand on social care/health services, or economic costs.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
