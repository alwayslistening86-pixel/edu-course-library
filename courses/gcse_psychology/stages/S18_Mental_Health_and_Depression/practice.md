# S18_Mental_Health_and_Depression - Practice: Introduction to mental health and clinical depression

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question (including full working for any calculation) before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between unipolar and bipolar depression. [2 marks]
2. State the neurotransmitter most associated with the biological explanation of depression, and whether depression is linked to high or low levels of it. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 unipolar -- persistently low mood only; B1 bipolar -- episodes of low mood alternating with episodes of elevated mood/mania.
2. [2] B1 serotonin; B1 low levels.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
