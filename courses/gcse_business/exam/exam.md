# GCSE Business (AQA 8132) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Paper 1 covers Topics 3.1-3.4 (S01-S11): Section A (multiple-choice/short-answer), Section B and Section C (case-study/data-response sets). Paper 2 covers Topic 3.1 again (through marketing/finance) plus Topics 3.5-3.6 (S12-S15), with the same Section A/B/C structure. Questions are original; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage tests, keeping each paper to its real topic split and AQA's real question styles (case-study scenarios with data to apply topic content to). The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 10 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1] State two internal sources of finance and one external source of finance a small business could use. [3 marks]
2. [Paper 1] Explain one advantage and one disadvantage to a business of operating as a private limited company (ltd) rather than a sole trader. [4 marks]
3. [Paper 1] A firm delayers, removing a layer of middle management. Explain one likely effect on its chain of command and one likely effect on its wage costs. [4 marks]
4. Which of these is an example of non-financial motivation? Choose every correct option.
   A. giving an employee more responsibility and interesting work
   B. paying an employee commission on sales
   C. paying an employee a performance-related bonus
   D. offering an employee profit sharing
5. [Paper 1] A furniture manufacturer switches from job production (building each piece to order) to flow production on a continuous line. Explain one benefit and one drawback of this change. [4 marks]
6. [Paper 2] A firm's revenue is £250,000 and its cost of sales is £160,000. Calculate its gross profit and gross profit margin, showing your working. [3 marks]
7. [Paper 2] Explain the difference between price skimming and price penetration as pricing strategies, and identify one type of product for which each would be suitable. [4 marks]
8. Which of these is a method of secondary market research? Choose every correct option.
   A. reading a published market report on the internet
   B. running a focus group with potential customers
   C. handing out a questionnaire in the street
   D. interviewing customers face to face
9. [Paper 2] A business invests £60,000 in new machinery, expecting total profit of £27,000 over 3 years. Calculate the average rate of return, showing your working. [3 marks]
10. [Paper 2] Discuss whether expanding into overseas markets is, on balance, a good strategy for a successful UK small business. [12 marks]

## Grading
Mark short answers and multiple-choice against the mark schemes; mark calculations with method shown (a correct final answer with no valid method shown earns only what the mark scheme allows for a bare answer); mark extended-response questions by levels. A pass needs at least 50% of the 180 marks overall and at least 40% on each paper. Report the total, the percentage per paper and the weakest topic areas. AQA's grade boundaries change every series, so the course does not claim a predicted grade.
