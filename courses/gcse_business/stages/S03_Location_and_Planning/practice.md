# S03_Location_and_Planning - Practice: Business location and business planning

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A furniture manufacturer needs large quantities of timber delivered regularly. State, with a reason, one location factor that is likely to matter most to this business. [2 marks]
2. State two purposes of a business plan. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 availability of raw materials (or proximity to them)/transport costs; B1 correct reason, e.g. minimising the cost and time of transporting bulky/heavy timber.
2. [2] B1 helping the entrepreneur clarify/think through their idea before committing money; B1 persuading lenders/investors that the business is viable, to help raise external finance.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
