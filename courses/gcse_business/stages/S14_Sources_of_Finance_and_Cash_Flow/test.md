## Test items
1. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of a business raising finance through a bank loan rather than an overdraft. [4 marks]
2. Explain why a new start-up business is more likely to rely on external sources of finance than an established, profitable business. [3 marks]
3. A business's cash-flow forecast for March has an opening balance of £5,000, forecast inflows of £7,500 and forecast outflows of £9,000. Calculate the net cash flow and closing balance for March, showing your working, and state whether this is a cause for concern. [4 marks]
4. Explain two possible solutions a business could use if its cash-flow forecast shows a future cash shortage. [4 marks]
5. Which of these best explains why a profitable business can still fail due to cash-flow problems? Choose every correct option.
   A. it can run out of cash to pay day-to-day bills even while it is profitable on paper
   B. profit and cash are always exactly the same thing, so this could never happen
   C. cash-flow problems only affect businesses that are already loss-making
   D. cash-flow forecasts always accurately predict the future with no risk of error

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 advantage of a loan, e.g. predictable, fixed regular repayments over an agreed period, often at a lower rate of interest than an overdraft; B1 correctly explained; B1 disadvantage, e.g. security/collateral is often required, and it is less flexible than an overdraft (the full amount is borrowed and repaid on a fixed schedule regardless of need); B1 correctly explained.
2. [3] B1 a new business has no retained profit built up yet, since it has not yet traded profitably; B1 and usually few unwanted assets it could sell; B1 so it must turn to external sources, e.g. loans, family/friends or government grants, whereas an established business can more easily fund itself internally.
3. [4] M1 net cash flow = 7,500-9,000; A1 = -£1,500 (a net outflow); A1 closing balance = 5,000-1,500 = £3,500; B1 not an immediate crisis since the closing balance is still positive, but a cause for concern/worth monitoring since cash is falling and would run out if the trend continued.
4. [4] B1 solution 1, e.g. arranging an overdraft to cover the short-term gap, or rescheduling payments (paying suppliers later/chasing customers to pay sooner); B1 correctly explained; B1 solution 2, e.g. reducing outflows (cutting/delaying costs) or increasing inflows (a short-term sales promotion); B1 correctly explained.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
