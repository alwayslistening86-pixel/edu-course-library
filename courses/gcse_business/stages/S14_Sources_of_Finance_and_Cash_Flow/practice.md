# S14_Sources_of_Finance_and_Cash_Flow - Practice: Sources of finance and cash flow

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State one internal source of finance and one external source of finance available to a small business. [2 marks]
2. A business has an opening balance of £4,000, cash inflows of £10,000 and cash outflows of £9,200 for the month. Calculate its net cash flow and closing balance, showing your working. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 internal source, e.g. retained profit/selling unwanted assets; B1 external source, e.g. a bank loan/overdraft/trade credit/family and friends.
2. [3] M1 net cash flow = 10,000-9,200; A1 = £800; A1 closing balance = 4,000+800 = £4,800.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
