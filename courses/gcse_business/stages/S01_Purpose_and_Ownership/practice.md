# S01_Purpose_and_Ownership - Practice: The purpose of business and business ownership

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State one reason a person might choose to start their own business. [1 mark]
2. A single person owns and runs a plumbing business, employing two staff but taking full responsibility for any debts. Identify this form of ownership, and state one disadvantage of it. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 any valid reason, e.g. to be their own boss/independence, financial reward, a gap in the market spotted, pursuing a passion.
2. [2] B1 sole trader; B1 valid disadvantage, e.g. unlimited liability (personal assets at risk) or harder to raise finance.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
