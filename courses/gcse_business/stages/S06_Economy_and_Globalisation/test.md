## Test items
1. Explain how high employment in the economy can both help and hinder a business. [4 marks]
2. Explain two ways a UK business can compete internationally against lower-cost overseas rivals. [4 marks]
3. Explain the effect of the pound weakening against the dollar on a UK business that imports raw materials from the USA. [3 marks]
4. Explain why a fall in consumer spending during an economic downturn is likely to hit a luxury holiday company harder than a supermarket. [3 marks]
5. If the pound strengthens against the euro, what is the likely effect on a UK exporter selling to Europe? Choose every correct option.
   A. its products become more expensive for European buyers, making exporting harder
   B. its products become cheaper for European buyers, making exporting easier
   C. there is no effect on exporting at all
   D. its raw material import costs rise

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 help: high employment generally means consumers have more disposable income, boosting spending/sales; B1 correctly explained; B1 hinder: a shortage of available workers can make recruitment harder and push up wage costs; B1 correctly explained.
2. [4] B1 way 1, e.g. competing on design -- offering more innovative/distinctive products; B1 correctly explained; B1 way 2, e.g. competing on quality -- higher standards/reliability than cheaper rivals; B1 correctly explained (accept competitive pricing where achievable).
3. [3] B1 the raw materials become more expensive in pounds; B1 because more pounds are now needed to buy the same amount of dollars' worth of materials; B1 this raises the business's costs, which can reduce profit or force a price rise.
4. [3] B1 in a downturn consumers cut back especially on non-essential/luxury spending; B1 holidays are a discretionary/non-essential purchase, easily postponed or cancelled; B1 whereas food from a supermarket is a necessity, so demand falls much less, if at all.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
