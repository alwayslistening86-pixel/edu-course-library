# S06_Economy_and_Globalisation - Practice: The economic climate and globalisation

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the likely effect of a rise in interest rates on a business that has a large bank loan. [2 marks]
2. The pound strengthens sharply against the euro. State, with a reason, the likely effect on a UK business that exports most of its products to Europe. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 the cost of repaying the loan (interest payments) rises; B1 this raises the business's costs, which can reduce profit or discourage further borrowing/investment.
2. [2] B1 its exports become more expensive for European buyers/harder to sell; B1 because more euros are now needed to buy the same amount of pounds' worth of UK goods.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
