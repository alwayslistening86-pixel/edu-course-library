# S11_Motivation_and_Training - Test: Motivating and training employees

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: multiple-choice, short-answer, data-response/case-study and extended-response questions, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints and no calculator unless the question is a genuinely complex calculation. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain two financial methods a business could use to motivate its employees. [4 marks]
2. Explain why well-motivated employees can benefit a business. [3 marks]
3. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of off-the-job training compared with on-the-job training. [4 marks]
4. Explain why induction training is important for a new employee. [2 marks]
5. Which of these is an example of a non-financial method of motivation? Choose every correct option.
   A. giving an employee more responsibility and more interesting work
   B. paying an employee a share of the business's profit
   C. paying an employee commission on every sale made
   D. increasing an employee's basic salary

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 method 1, e.g. commission -- extra pay linked directly to sales made; B1 correctly explained; B1 method 2, e.g. profit sharing -- employees receive a share of profit, giving them a stake in the business doing well; B1 correctly explained (accept salary/wage rises).
2. [3] B1 well-motivated staff tend to be more productive/produce higher-quality work; B1 they are also more likely to stay with the business; B1 reducing the business's recruitment and training costs of replacing staff who leave.
3. [4] B1 advantage, e.g. often higher quality/more specialist instruction, with no disruption to ongoing production while training happens; B1 correctly explained; B1 disadvantage, e.g. more expensive, and the employee is unproductive/absent during the training; B1 correctly explained.
4. [2] B1 it introduces the new employee to the business's policies, procedures, layout and their specific role; B1 helping them settle in and become productive faster than if left to work things out alone.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
