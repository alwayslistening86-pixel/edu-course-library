# S11_Motivation_and_Training - Practice: Motivating and training employees

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State one financial method and one non-financial method a business could use to motivate its sales staff. [2 marks]
2. Distinguish between on-the-job and off-the-job training, giving one example of each. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 financial method, e.g. commission on sales/a bonus/profit sharing; B1 non-financial method, e.g. more responsibility, better management style, fringe benefits, or training.
2. [2] B1 on-the-job training happens while continuing to work, e.g. shadowing a colleague; B1 off-the-job training happens away from the normal workstation, e.g. an external course.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
