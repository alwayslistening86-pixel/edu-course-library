# S13_Market_Research_and_Marketing_Mix - Practice: Market research and the marketing mix (4Ps)

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Identify whether each of these is primary or secondary market research: (a) a business runs its own focus group with potential customers; (b) a business reads a published industry report found online. [2 marks]
2. Identify the pricing strategy being described: 'a new games console launches at a high price, which is gradually lowered over the following two years.' [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 (a) primary; B1 (b) secondary.
2. [1] B1 price skimming.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
