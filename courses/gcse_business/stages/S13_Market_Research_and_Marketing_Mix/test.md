Give the whole test at once, with no hints and no calculator unless the question is a genuinely complex calculation. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of primary research compared with secondary research. [4 marks]
2. Distinguish between qualitative and quantitative market research data, giving an example of each. [4 marks]
3. Explain the difference between price skimming and price penetration, and identify which is more likely to suit a business entering an already crowded, competitive market. [3 marks]
4. A product is in the decline stage of its product life cycle. Suggest and explain one extension strategy the business could use to prolong its life. [3 marks]
5. Explain the difference between a retailer and a wholesaler in the 'place' element of the marketing mix. [2 marks]
6. Which of these is an example of a sales promotion technique? Choose every correct option.
   A. a free sample given out to encourage a trial purchase
   B. a television advertisement
   C. sponsoring a sports team
   D. posting regularly on social media

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 advantage, e.g. data is tailored exactly to the business's specific needs/question; B1 correctly explained; B1 disadvantage, e.g. more time-consuming/expensive than secondary research; B1 correctly explained.
2. [4] B1 quantitative data is numerical, e.g. '70% of respondents preferred product A'; B1 correctly explained (easy to compare/analyse statistically); B1 qualitative data is opinions/attitudes/reasons, e.g. why a customer prefers a brand; B1 correctly explained (richer detail but harder to compare/more subjective).
3. [3] B1 price skimming sets a high initial price, lowered over time; B1 price penetration sets a deliberately low initial price to build market share quickly, often raised later; B1 price penetration better suits a crowded/competitive market, since a low price helps win customers away from established rivals.
4. [3] B1 valid extension strategy, e.g. updated packaging, new features, targeting a new market, fresh advertising, or a price reduction; B1 correctly named; B1 correctly explained, e.g. new features could attract existing customers to buy again/attract new customers, boosting sales before the product would otherwise decline further.
5. [2] B1 a retailer sells products directly to the general public/end consumer; B1 a wholesaler buys in bulk from producers and sells smaller quantities on to retailers, rather than to the public directly.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
