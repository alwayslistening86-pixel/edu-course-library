## Test items
1. Explain why a newly-started business is more likely to set survival as an objective than profit maximisation. [2 marks]
2. Explain how a business's objectives might change as it grows from a small start-up into a large, established firm. [3 marks]
3. A factory owner wants to cut costs by reducing staff pay, but local employees rely on that pay to live comfortably. Explain this conflict of stakeholder objectives. [3 marks]
4. Identify all five stakeholder groups named in this specification, and state one want of the local community as a stakeholder. [6 marks]
5. Which stakeholder group primarily wants a business to pay them promptly and in full, and to keep ordering from them regularly? Choose every correct option.
   A. suppliers
   B. customers
   C. the local community
   D. owners

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 a new business faces high risk of failure/an uncertain, unfamiliar market/limited cash reserves; B1 so simply staying in operation matters more at first than maximising profit, which can come once it is established.
2. [3] B1 a start-up often focuses on survival; B1 an established, growing firm can shift focus to growth/increasing market share/profit maximisation; B1 a large, dominant firm may add wider social/ethical objectives once profit and survival are secure.
3. [3] B1 owners want higher profit, which cutting costs (including pay) can help achieve; B1 employees want fair/higher pay and job security; B1 these two stakeholder objectives directly conflict, and the business must weigh/balance them.
4. [6] B1 owners; B1 employees; B1 customers; B1 local community; B1 suppliers; B1 valid want of the local community, e.g. local jobs, minimal pollution/traffic/noise, or community investment.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
