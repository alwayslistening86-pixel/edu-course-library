# S02_Aims_Objectives_Stakeholders - Practice: Business aims/objectives and stakeholders

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Distinguish between a business aim and a business objective, with an example of each. [2 marks]
2. Identify two stakeholder groups of a supermarket, and state one thing each group wants from it. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 an aim is a long-term overall goal, e.g. to become the market leader; B1 an objective is a more specific, measurable, shorter-term target, e.g. to increase sales by 10% this year.
2. [4] B1 stakeholder 1 named correctly (e.g. employees, customers, suppliers, local community, owners); B1 correct want stated for stakeholder 1; B1 stakeholder 2 named correctly; B1 correct want stated for stakeholder 2.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
