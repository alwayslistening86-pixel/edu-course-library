# S15_Financial_Calculations_and_Performance - Practice: Financial calculations and analysing financial performance

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A business has fixed costs of £1,800, sells its product at £15 and has a variable cost of £9 per unit. Calculate the contribution per unit and the break-even output, showing your working. [3 marks]
2. A business's revenue is £80,000 and its cost of sales is £50,000. Calculate its gross profit and gross profit margin, showing your working. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 contribution per unit = 15-9 = £6; M1 break-even output = 1,800/6; A1 = 300 units.
2. [3] M1 gross profit = 80,000-50,000; A1 = £30,000; A1 gross profit margin = 30,000/80,000 x 100 = 37.5%.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
