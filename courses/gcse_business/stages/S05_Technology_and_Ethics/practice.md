# S05_Technology_and_Ethics - Practice: Technology, ethics and the environment

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State one benefit and one drawback to a small business of selling through e-commerce rather than only from a physical shop. [2 marks]
2. Give one example of an environmental consideration a manufacturing business must think about. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 benefit, e.g. can reach customers nationally/globally, or lower overheads than a shop; B1 drawback, e.g. faces far more competition, or needs investment in a website/secure payments/delivery.
2. [1] B1 any valid example, e.g. waste disposal, recycling, air/noise pollution, or traffic congestion from deliveries.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
