## Test items
1. Distinguish between job production and flow production, giving one advantage of flow production over job production. [3 marks]
2. Explain the difference between just in time (JIT) and just in case (JIC) stock management, and the main risk of each. [4 marks]
3. Explain three factors a business should consider when selecting a supplier. [3 marks]
4. Explain why lean production techniques such as JIT can improve a manufacturing firm's efficiency. [3 marks]
5. Which of these best describes just in case (JIC) stock management? Choose every correct option.
   A. holding a buffer of stock in case of unexpected demand or delivery problems
   B. ordering stock to arrive at the exact moment it is needed, with none held in reserve
   C. never holding any stock at all under any circumstances
   D. only used by businesses using job production

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 job production makes a single, often unique product at a time to a customer's requirements; B1 flow production makes a continuous stream of identical, standardised products; B1 advantage of flow, e.g. much lower unit costs at high volume/allows specialisation and automation.
2. [4] B1 JIT: stock arrives exactly when needed, minimal stock held; B1 risk, e.g. vulnerable to a delayed delivery causing a stock-out/halting production; B1 JIC: the business holds a buffer/reserve of stock; B1 risk, e.g. ties up cash/incurs storage costs, or unsold stock can become obsolete/wasted.
3. [3] B1 price -- lower cost improves the business's own profit margins; B1 quality -- poor supplies can mean poor output/reputational damage; B1 reliability -- late/inconsistent deliveries can disrupt production, especially under JIT.
4. [3] B1 lean production aims to cut waste (of time, materials, effort or stock) throughout the process; B1 JIT specifically avoids the cost of storing/holding large amounts of stock; B1 lower waste/storage costs reduce the firm's average cost of production, improving efficiency/profit margins.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
