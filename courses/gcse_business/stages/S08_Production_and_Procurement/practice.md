# S08_Production_and_Procurement - Practice: Production processes and procurement

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Identify the production method being described: 'a hand-built luxury sports car, made to one customer's exact specification.' [1 mark]
2. State one benefit and one drawback of a business using just in time (JIT) stock management. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 job production.
2. [2] B1 benefit, e.g. lower storage costs/less waste from unsold stock; B1 drawback, e.g. vulnerable to running out of stock if a delivery is delayed or demand spikes.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
