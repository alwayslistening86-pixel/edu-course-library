# S09_Quality_and_Customer_Service - Test: Quality and good customer service

Give the whole test at once, with no hints and no calculator unless the question is a genuinely complex calculation. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain what is meant by total quality management (TQM), and how it differs from checking quality only at the end of production. [3 marks]
2. Explain one cost and one benefit to a business of maintaining high quality. [4 marks]
3. Explain why the growth of social media and online reviews has made good customer service more important for businesses. [3 marks]
4. Explain two reasons why good customer service can improve a business's sales. [4 marks]
5. Which of these best describes total quality management (TQM)? Choose every correct option.
   A. an approach where quality is treated as everyone's responsibility throughout the organisation
   B. checking product quality only once, at the very end of production
   C. a system used only by businesses using job production
   D. a legal requirement for all UK businesses

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 TQM treats quality as everyone's responsibility throughout the whole organisation; B1 aiming to identify/prevent quality problems as early as possible, not just catch them at the end; B1 this differs from end-of-line inspection alone, which only catches problems after they have already occurred, wasting more resources.
2. [4] B1 cost, e.g. inspection/staff training, or the cost of any recalls that still occur; B1 correctly explained; B1 benefit, e.g. additional sales/a stronger reputation, or the ability to charge a higher price; B1 correctly explained.
3. [3] B1 negative reviews/complaints about poor service can now spread very quickly and widely online; B1 far beyond the customers directly affected, reaching many potential future customers; B1 so poor service now carries a much greater reputational risk than before ICT/social media existed.
4. [4] B1 reason 1, e.g. builds customer satisfaction and loyalty, so satisfied customers return/repeat purchase; B1 correctly explained; B1 reason 2, e.g. satisfied customers recommend the business to others (positive word of mouth); B1 correctly explained.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
