# S09_Quality_and_Customer_Service - Practice: Quality and good customer service

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State two consequences to a business of producing goods of poor quality. [2 marks]
2. State one example of good post-sales customer service. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 valid consequence 1, e.g. lost sales/customers; B1 valid consequence 2, e.g. costly product recalls/reputational damage.
2. [1] B1 any valid example, e.g. a help line, servicing/repairs, or training customers to use the product.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
