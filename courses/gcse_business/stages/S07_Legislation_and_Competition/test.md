# S07_Legislation_and_Competition - Test: Legislation and the competitive environment

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: multiple-choice, short-answer, data-response/case-study and extended-response questions, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints and no calculator unless the question is a genuinely complex calculation. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain two costs to a business of complying with health and safety law. [4 marks]
2. Explain the risk to a business of breaking consumer law, e.g. selling goods that are not of satisfactory quality. [3 marks]
3. Explain two ways operating in a highly competitive market can create uncertainty and risk for a business. [4 marks]
4. Explain why complying with employment law, although it raises costs, can also benefit a business. [3 marks]
5. Which type of law requires a business to provide a safe working environment for its employees? Choose every correct option.
   A. health and safety law
   B. consumer law
   C. employment law on minimum wage only
   D. no law covers this area

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 cost 1, e.g. buying/maintaining safe equipment; B1 correctly explained; B1 cost 2, e.g. staff training/time spent on risk assessments; B1 correctly explained (accept the cost of appointing a health and safety officer).
2. [3] B1 the business can face fines/lawsuits/be forced to refund customers; B1 as well as serious reputational damage/loss of customer trust; B1 which can reduce future sales, beyond just the direct legal cost.
3. [4] B1 way 1, e.g. a competitor can launch a better/cheaper product without warning, losing the business customers; B1 correctly explained; B1 way 2, e.g. prices/profit margins can be squeezed by rivals, or demand can be hard to predict; B1 correctly explained.
4. [3] B1 employment law raises costs, e.g. through the National Minimum Wage or administrative compliance; B1 but it protects staff/can improve staff morale and motivation; B1 which can improve retention/productivity and the business's reputation as a fair employer.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
