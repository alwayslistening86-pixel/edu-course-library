# S07_Legislation_and_Competition - Practice: Legislation and the competitive environment

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State one example of employment law and one example of consumer law that a UK business must comply with. [2 marks]
2. Explain one way operating in a highly competitive market can benefit customers. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 employment law example, e.g. National Minimum Wage/protection from unfair dismissal/anti-discrimination law; B1 consumer law example, e.g. right to a refund/repair for goods not of satisfactory quality or not as described.
2. [2] B1 e.g. firms must keep prices lower/quality higher to attract and keep customers; B1 correctly explained, e.g. this gives customers better value/more choice than in a market with little competition.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
