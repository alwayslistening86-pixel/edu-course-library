# S04_Expansion_and_Scale - Test: Expanding a business, and economies/diseconomies of scale

Give the whole test at once, with no hints and no calculator unless the question is a genuinely complex calculation. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of a business growing through a takeover rather than organic growth. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by economies of scale, giving one specific example of a type of economy of scale. [3 marks]
3. Explain why a very large firm might experience diseconomies of scale as it continues to grow. [3 marks]
4. A small bakery chain merges with a larger regional bakery chain to form one combined business. Identify this method of expansion, and state one risk it carries. [2 marks]
5. Which of these is an example of an economy of scale? Choose every correct option.
   A. a large firm negotiating a lower price per tonne for raw materials because it buys in bulk
   B. a firm's average cost rising because communication becomes difficult across a huge organisation
   C. a small firm paying a higher rate of interest on a loan than a large firm
   D. a firm's workers becoming demotivated as the organisation grows very large

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 advantage, e.g. much faster growth/instant access to an established firm's customers, brand or expertise; B1 correctly explained; B1 disadvantage, e.g. expensive/needs significant finance, or integration risk from clashing systems/cultures; B1 correctly explained.
2. [3] B1 economies of scale are falls in a firm's average cost of production as its scale of output grows; B1 example, e.g. purchasing economies -- bulk-buying raw materials at a lower price per unit, or technical/financial economies; B1 correctly explained.
3. [3] B1 communication problems -- harder for information/instructions to flow accurately through a huge organisation; B1 poor motivation/coordination -- workers/managers can feel like a small, unvalued part of a huge organisation, and coordinating many sites/departments becomes harder; B1 these raise average costs, outweighing further economies of scale.
4. [2] B1 external growth by merger; B1 valid risk, e.g. clashing management styles/cultures/systems, or loss of full control for the original owners.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
