## Test items
1. Explain two reasons why a business needs to identify and understand its customers. [4 marks]
2. A car manufacturer sells a cheap, compact city car and, separately, a large luxury saloon. Explain how income segmentation might explain this range of products. [3 marks]
3. Explain why a business selling products to both teenagers and retirees might need two different marketing approaches. [3 marks]
4. Identify all four ways of segmenting a market named in this specification. [4 marks]
5. Which of these is an example of segmenting a market by location? Choose every correct option.
   A. offering different products in urban stores compared with rural stores
   B. offering a budget range and a premium range at different prices
   C. targeting an advert specifically at teenagers
   D. targeting an advert specifically at women

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 reason 1, e.g. to select the correct marketing mix for that customer group; B1 correctly explained; B1 reason 2, e.g. to increase sales/remain competitive against rivals who understand customers well; B1 correctly explained (accept avoiding costly mistakes).
2. [3] B1 income segmentation divides customers by how much disposable income they have; B1 the compact city car targets lower-income customers seeking an affordable, practical option; B1 the luxury saloon targets higher-income customers willing/able to pay a premium price, so each product/price point suits a different income segment.
3. [3] B1 age segmentation recognises these two groups have different needs, tastes and spending patterns; B1 e.g. teenagers may be reached effectively through social media, while retirees may respond better to other media (e.g. print, TV); B1 so a single, identical marketing approach risks failing to reach one or both groups effectively.
4. [4] B1 gender; B1 age; B1 location; B1 income.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
