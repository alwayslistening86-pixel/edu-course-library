# S12_Customers_and_Segmentation - Practice: Understanding customers and market segmentation

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State two ways a market can be segmented. [2 marks]
2. Explain one reason a business needs to understand its customers before launching a new product. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 segmentation way 1, e.g. gender/age/location/income; B1 segmentation way 2, a different one of gender/age/location/income.
2. [2] B1 e.g. to avoid costly mistakes/wasting money on a product nobody wants; B1 correctly explained, e.g. understanding customers first means the product/marketing mix can be tailored to what they will actually buy.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
