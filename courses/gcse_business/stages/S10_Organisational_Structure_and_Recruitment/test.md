## Test items
1. Explain the difference between span of control and chain of command, using an example of each. [4 marks]
2. A business removes a layer of middle management from its structure. Explain the likely effect on the remaining managers' span of control and on communication speed. [3 marks]
3. Distinguish between a job description and a person specification, with an example of what each might contain. [4 marks]
4. Explain one benefit to a business of a zero-hours contract, and one benefit of this contract type to an employee. [2 marks]
5. Which term describes a manager passing authority for a specific task down to a subordinate, while remaining ultimately responsible for the outcome? Choose every correct option.
   A. delegation
   B. delayering
   C. recruitment
   D. job analysis

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 span of control is the number of subordinates a manager directly supervises; B1 correct example; B1 chain of command is the line of authority from the top of the business down to the most junior staff; B1 correct example.
2. [3] B1 the remaining managers' span of control widens, since they now directly manage more staff; B1 communication is likely to become faster/more direct (a shorter chain of command); B1 but the correctly explained trade-off, e.g. each manager now has more staff to closely supervise, which can be harder to manage well.
3. [4] B1 a job description sets out the specific duties/responsibilities of the role; B1 correct example, e.g. 'manage a team of five sales staff'; B1 a person specification sets out the skills/qualifications/experience an ideal candidate should have; B1 correct example, e.g. 'at least two years' retail management experience'.
4. [2] B1 benefit to the business, e.g. flexibility to only pay staff when there is enough demand/work available; B1 benefit to the employee, e.g. flexibility to accept or decline shifts around other commitments.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
