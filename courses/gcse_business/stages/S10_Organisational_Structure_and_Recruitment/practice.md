# S10_Organisational_Structure_and_Recruitment - Practice: Organisational structures and recruitment/selection

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Identify whether each business is likely to have a tall or flat organisational structure: (a) a large multinational bank with many layers of management; (b) a five-person start-up where everyone reports directly to the owner. [2 marks]
2. State one advantage and one disadvantage of recruiting internally rather than externally. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 (a) tall; B1 (b) flat.
2. [2] B1 advantage, e.g. faster/cheaper, or the candidate is already known/trained in the business's ways; B1 disadvantage, e.g. brings no new skills/ideas, or creates a new vacancy elsewhere.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
