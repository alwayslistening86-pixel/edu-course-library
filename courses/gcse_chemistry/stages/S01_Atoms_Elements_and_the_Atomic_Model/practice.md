# S01_Atoms_Elements_and_the_Atomic_Model - Practice: Atoms, elements, compounds, mixtures and the model of the atom

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A sample of an element has atoms with 17 protons, 18 neutrons and 17 electrons. Give its atomic number, mass number and name, and state the charge on the atom.
2. Explain how Rutherford's alpha scattering results disproved the plum pudding model.
3. Write the electronic structures of oxygen, magnesium and argon.
4. Higher: boron has isotopes 10B (20 per cent) and 11B (80 per cent). Calculate its relative atomic mass.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
