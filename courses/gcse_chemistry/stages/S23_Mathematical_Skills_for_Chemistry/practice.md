# S23_Mathematical_Skills_for_Chemistry - Practice: Mathematical requirements for GCSE Chemistry

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A titration uses 25.0 cm3 of 0.100 mol/dm3 acid. Convert the volume to dm3, calculate moles, and state the answer in an appropriate number of significant figures.
2. Rearrange moles = mass / Mr to make mass the subject and calculate the mass of 0.25 mol of CaCO3.
3. Plot the rate graph from given data of gas volume against time, draw a line of best fit and find the gradient of the straight section.
4. Higher: draw a tangent at 20 s to the curve and calculate the rate of reaction at that point, in cm3/s.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
