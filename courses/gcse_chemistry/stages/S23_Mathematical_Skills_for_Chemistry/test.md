# S23_Mathematical_Skills_for_Chemistry - Test: Mathematical requirements for GCSE Chemistry

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Write 0.000045 m in standard form. b) Calculate the mean of 21.3, 21.5 and 21.4 cm3. c) Give 0.03847 to 2 significant figures.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Rate data show 30 cm3 of gas at 10 s and 42 cm3 at 20 s from a tangent. Calculate the rate, then calculate the mass of magnesium (Ar 24) that produces 48 cm3 of hydrogen at RTP (24 dm3/mol).'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S23_Mathematical_Skills_for_Chemistry` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass. This was the last stage: the cumulative exam is now unlocked once every stage test is passed.
