# S14_Crude_Oil_and_Hydrocarbons - Practice: Crude oil, fractional distillation, hydrocarbon properties and cracking

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why the fractions separate at different heights in the column, using boiling point.
2. Write a balanced equation for the complete combustion of propane and one for the incomplete combustion producing carbon monoxide.
3. Explain why kerosene is less viscous than bitumen.
4. Write a balanced cracking equation for C12H26 producing one alkane and two ethene molecules.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
