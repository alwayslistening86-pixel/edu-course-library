# S11_Energy_Changes_and_Cells - Practice: Exothermic and endothermic reactions, reaction profiles, bond energies, cells and fuel cells (required practical 4)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Classify each as exothermic or endothermic: burning methane, dissolving ammonium nitrate, neutralisation, thermal decomposition of calcium carbonate.
2. Sketch reaction profiles for an exothermic and an endothermic reaction, labelling reactants, products, activation energy and overall energy change.
3. Higher: calculate the energy change for CH4 + 2O2 gives CO2 + 2H2O using C-H 413, O=O 498, C=O 805 and O-H 464 kJ/mol.
4. Explain how a cell is made from zinc and copper electrodes and why a zinc-copper cell gives a larger voltage than a zinc-iron cell.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
