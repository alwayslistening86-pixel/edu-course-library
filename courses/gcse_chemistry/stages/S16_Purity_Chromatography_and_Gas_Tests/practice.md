# S16_Purity_Chromatography_and_Gas_Tests - Practice: Pure substances, formulations, chromatography and gas tests (required practical 6)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why a sample with a melting range of 78-84 degrees Celsius is not pure.
2. A dye travels 3.6 cm while the solvent travels 8.0 cm. Calculate the Rf value and say what it tells you.
3. A chromatogram of an ink shows three spots; explain what this means and how a known dye could be matched.
4. Describe the tests for hydrogen, oxygen, carbon dioxide and chlorine, and the observation for each.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
