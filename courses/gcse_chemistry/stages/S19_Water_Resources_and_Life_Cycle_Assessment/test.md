## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S19_Water_Resources_and_Life_Cycle_Assessment` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
