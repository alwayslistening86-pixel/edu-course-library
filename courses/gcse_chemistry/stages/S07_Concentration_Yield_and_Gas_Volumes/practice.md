# S07_Concentration_Yield_and_Gas_Volumes - Practice: Concentration, percentage yield, atom economy and gas volumes

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Calculate the concentration in g/dm3 when 12 g of salt is dissolved to make 300 cm3 of solution.
2. A student expects 8.0 g of a product and obtains 5.2 g. Calculate the percentage yield and give two reasons for the low yield.
3. Calculate the atom economy for making hydrogen from CH4 + H2O gives CO + 3H2 (desired product H2).
4. Higher: 25.0 cm3 of sodium hydroxide neutralises 20.0 cm3 of 0.100 mol/dm3 hydrochloric acid; calculate the concentration of the alkali, and the volume of hydrogen at RTP from 0.05 mol of magnesium and excess acid.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
