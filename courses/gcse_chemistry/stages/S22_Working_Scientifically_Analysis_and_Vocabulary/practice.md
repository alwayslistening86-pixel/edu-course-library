# S22_Working_Scientifically_Analysis_and_Vocabulary - Practice: Working scientifically: analysis, evaluation and scientific language

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Plot a line graph of these volumes of gas collected: 10 s gives 12 cm3, 20 s gives 21, 30 s gives 27, 40 s gives 30, 50 s gives 30. Describe the pattern with figures.
2. Five repeat readings for temperature rise are 8.2, 8.4, 8.3, 11.0, 8.3 degrees Celsius. Identify the anomaly, calculate the mean without it and the range.
3. Write a conclusion for an experiment where the volume of gas collected increases as acid concentration increases.
4. Convert 250 cm3 to dm3, 0.35 g to milligrams, and write 0.000042 m in standard form and in micrometres.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
