# S22_Working_Scientifically_Analysis_and_Vocabulary - Test: Working scientifically: analysis, evaluation and scientific language

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Calculate the mean of 4.2, 4.5, 4.4 and 4.5 g. b) Name the type of graph you would draw to show how many students choose each flavour of crisps. c) State the SI unit of mass and of volume.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Data show the rate of a reaction doubles for every 10 degree rise until 40 degrees Celsius, then falls sharply. Write a conclusion, evaluate the precision of the data, and suggest an improvement.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S22_Working_Scientifically_Analysis_and_Vocabulary` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
