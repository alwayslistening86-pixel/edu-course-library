# S13_Reversible_Reactions_and_Equilibrium - Practice: Reversible reactions, equilibrium and Le Chatelier's principle

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain what is meant by a dynamic equilibrium, and why a closed system is needed.
2. For an exothermic reversible reaction, predict and explain the effect of raising temperature on the yield.
3. Higher: predict the effect of increasing pressure on the equilibrium 2SO2 + O2 (double arrow) 2SO3.
4. Higher: explain why removing ammonia from the mixture increases the yield in the Haber process.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
