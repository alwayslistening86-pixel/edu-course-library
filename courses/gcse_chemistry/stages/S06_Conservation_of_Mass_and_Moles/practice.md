# S06_Conservation_of_Mass_and_Moles - Practice: Conservation of mass, relative formula mass, uncertainty, moles and limiting reactants

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Balance: Al + O2 gives Al2O3; and Fe2O3 + CO gives Fe + CO2.
2. Calculate the Mr of Ca(OH)2 and the percentage by mass of oxygen in it.
3. A student heats 5.0 g of copper carbonate and finds the solid left is 3.2 g. Explain the mass change and identify the gas.
4. Higher: calculate the mass of magnesium oxide from 6.0 g of magnesium, and find the limiting reactant if 6.0 g of magnesium reacts with 2.0 g of oxygen.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
