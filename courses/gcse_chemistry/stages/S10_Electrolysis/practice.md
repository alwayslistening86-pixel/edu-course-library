# S10_Electrolysis - Practice: Electrolysis of molten and aqueous substances (required practical 3)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Predict and explain the products of the electrolysis of molten sodium chloride.
2. Predict the products at each electrode for aqueous copper sulfate and for aqueous sodium chloride.
3. Explain why aluminium oxide is mixed with cryolite and why the anodes need replacing.
4. Higher: write half equations for the electrolysis of molten lead bromide and state which is oxidation and which reduction.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
