# S09_Acids_Salts_and_pH - Practice: Acids, salts, pH and titration (required practicals 1 and 2)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write word and symbol equations for zinc with sulfuric acid, and copper oxide with nitric acid.
2. Describe how to prepare crystals of copper sulfate, explaining why excess copper oxide is used.
3. A student's titre values are 22.30, 22.55, 22.35 and 22.25 cm3; identify the concordant results and calculate the mean.
4. Higher: 25.0 cm3 of sodium hydroxide requires 20.0 cm3 of 0.100 mol/dm3 sulfuric acid (H2SO4 + 2NaOH gives Na2SO4 + 2H2O); calculate the sodium hydroxide concentration.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
