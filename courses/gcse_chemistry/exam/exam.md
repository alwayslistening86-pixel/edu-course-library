# GCSE Chemistry (AQA 8462) — separate science, Foundation and Higher tier - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.

## Format
Two timed papers at the learner's tier, each 1 hour 45 minutes and 100 marks, matching AQA's real structure: Paper 1 covers topics 4.1 to 4.5 (atomic structure and periodic table, bonding, quantitative chemistry, chemical changes, energy changes) and Paper 2 covers 4.6 to 4.10 (rate and extent of change, organic chemistry, chemical analysis, the atmosphere, using resources). Each paper mixes multiple-choice, structured, closed short-answer and open-response questions, with about 20 per cent of marks on mathematical skills and 15 per cent on working scientifically and required practicals.

## Example structure
Paper 1 draws on S01-S11; Paper 2 draws on S12-S20. Include at least one required-practical-based question per paper (S09, S10, S11, S12, S16, S17, S19) and questions using S21-S23 skills. At Higher tier include Higher-only content: moles and limiting reactants, titration calculations, half equations, bond energies, Le Chatelier, condensation polymers and evaluation questions.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly. A pass requires consistent performance across all topics under timed conditions, with calculations set out step by step with units and extended answers that use correct scientific terminology.

## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete (for that tier).
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name which stage(s) broke down under mixed/timed conditions, offer targeted review or a fresh retry paper.
