# S02_A1_Grammar_Numbers_Pronouns - Test: Verbs, pronouns, numbers and simple negative sentences

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Write a short paragraph (4-5 sentences) introducing yourself: your name, job, where you live, what time you start work, and one thing you do not do. Use at least one verb from each conjugation class, one question, one number/time expression, one negative sentence and one possessive adjective.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S02_A1_Grammar_Numbers_Pronouns` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S03_A1_Vocab_People_Food.
