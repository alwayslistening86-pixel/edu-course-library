# S05_A1_CanDo_Skills - Practice: A1 can-do check: understanding and being understood in everyday basics

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Listen to (or read aloud slowly) three short weather/family sentences and answer one gist question on each.
2. Read a mock shop sign, menu and note, and extract the key fact from each.
3. Role-play asking for directions and a price, asking the other person to repeat once.
4. Give a one-minute spoken description of home, family and daily routine.
5. Write a short postcard and fill in a simple personal-details form.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
