# S05_A1_CanDo_Skills - Lesson: A1 can-do check: understanding and being understood in everyday basics

## Goal
The learner meets the CEFR A1 can-do descriptors for listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing in Romanian.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-A1-CD-listen1 - Can-do: listening at A1: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand familiar words and very basic phrases spoken slowly and clearly
- RO-A1-CD-read1 - Can-do: reading at A1: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand familiar names, words and very simple sentences
- RO-A1-CD-spokeninteract1 - Can-do: spoken interaction at A1: CEFR self-assessment grid: interact simply if the other person helps
- RO-A1-CD-spokenprod1 - Can-do: spoken production at A1: CEFR self-assessment grid: use simple phrases to describe where I live and people I know
- RO-A1-CD-write1 - Can-do: writing at A1: CEFR self-assessment grid: write a short simple postcard and fill in forms

## How to teach this
Explain that A1 is not tested by grammar accuracy alone: the CEFR defines it by what the learner can actually DO with the language. Walk through the five skill areas one at a time, using material already taught in S01-S04 as the raw vocabulary and grammar.

#### RO-A1-CD-listen1 Can-do: listening at A1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand familiar words and very basic phrases concerning themselves, their family and immediate concrete surroundings, when people speak slowly and clearly. Practise this by having the tutor read short, slow sentences built from S01-S04 vocabulary (greetings, family, numbers, weather) and having the learner point to a picture, answer with one word, or say true/false. Example listening line: "Astăzi e frig și plouă." -- learner identifies the weather described. Success means recognising the gist and key words, not every grammatical detail.

#### RO-A1-CD-read1 Can-do: reading at A1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand familiar names, words and very simple sentences, for example on notices and posters or in catalogues. Practise with realistic short texts: a shop sign ("Deschis 9-18"), a menu line ("Ciorbă de legume -- 15 lei"), a simple note ("Ne vedem la ora 5 la gară."). The learner should extract the key fact (opening hours, price, meeting time and place) without needing to translate every word.

#### RO-A1-CD-spokeninteract1 Can-do: spoken interaction at A1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can interact in a simple way provided the other person is prepared to repeat or rephrase things more slowly and help them formulate what they are trying to say. Practise short two-turn exchanges: greeting, asking a name, asking a price, asking for directions -- with the tutor playing a patient native speaker who slows down and rephrases on request ("Poți repeta, vă rog?" -- Can you repeat, please?). Judge success by whether communication succeeds, not by grammatical perfection.

#### RO-A1-CD-spokenprod1 Can-do: spoken production at A1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can use simple phrases and sentences to describe where they live and people they know. Have the learner give a short unscripted spoken description (1 minute): where they live, who is in their family, and one daily-routine detail, drawing directly on S02-S04 material. Accept simple, list-like sentences; do not expect connectors or complex tenses at this stage.

#### RO-A1-CD-write1 Can-do: writing at A1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can write a short, simple postcard, for example sending holiday greetings, and can fill in forms with personal details. Set two tasks: (1) write a 3-4 sentence postcard to a friend describing the weather and one activity; (2) fill in a simple form with nume (name), prenume (first name), vârstă (age), adresă (address), naționalitate (nationality). Check basic spelling of diacritics and correct simple present-tense verb forms.

## Explicitly not here
A2 can-do descriptors (short simple texts, routine exchanges, describing past experience) follow once perfect compus and near future are taught.
