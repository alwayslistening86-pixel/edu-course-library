# S14_B1_Vocab_Opinions_World - Lesson: Opinions, media, environment and Romanian culture

## Goal
The learner expresses and responds to opinions, discusses media and technology, talks about environmental issues, and describes Romanian festivals and traditions.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-B1-V3 - Opinions, agreement and disagreement: learn and apply: opinions, agreement and disagreement
- RO-B1-V4 - Media, news and technology: learn and apply: media, news and technology
- RO-B1-V5 - Environment and nature: learn and apply: environment and nature
- RO-B1-V6 - Romanian festivals and traditions: learn and apply: Romanian festivals and traditions

## How to teach this
Raise a mild talking point ('Should schools ban mobile phones?') and ask the learner to react. Use their reaction to teach opinion language, then widen to media, environment and finally Romanian cultural traditions.

#### RO-B1-V3 Opinions, agreement and disagreement
Cred că... (I think that...), Părerea mea este că... (My opinion is that...), Sunt de acord (I agree), Nu sunt de acord (I disagree), Ai dreptate (You're right), Din punctul meu de vedere... (From my point of view...), Pe de o parte... pe de altă parte... (On the one hand... on the other hand...). Example: "Cred că este important să citim, dar pe de altă parte, tehnologia ne ajută și ea." Combine with the subjunctive (G3-G4) and connectors (G8) from earlier stages to build a genuine short argument.

#### RO-B1-V4 Media, news and technology
știri (news), ziar (newspaper), a naviga pe internet (to browse the internet), rețele de socializare (social media), aplicație (app), telefon inteligent (smartphone), a posta (to post). Example: "Citesc știrile pe telefon în fiecare dimineață, dar prefer ziarul pentru articole lungi." (I read the news on my phone every morning, but I prefer the newspaper for long articles.) This vocabulary supports the media-literacy and technology discussions common at B1-B2 speaking tests.

#### RO-B1-V5 Environment and nature
mediu înconjurător (environment), poluare (pollution), reciclare (recycling), schimbări climatice (climate change), energie regenerabilă (renewable energy), a proteja natura (to protect nature), pădure (forest). Example: "Poluarea din orașele mari este o problemă serioasă; ar trebui să folosim mai mult transportul public." (Pollution in big cities is a serious problem; we should use public transport more -- note "ar trebui" conditional from G6.)

#### RO-B1-V6 Romanian festivals and traditions
Crăciun (Christmas) with colinde (carols) sung house to house; Paște (Easter) with the tradition of painting eggs (ouă vopsite) and the greeting "Hristos a înviat!" / "Adevărat a înviat!"; Mărțișor (1 March) -- a small red-and-white charm given to mark the start of spring; Ziua Națională (1 December, National Day, marking the 1918 union). Example: "De Mărțișor, oferim un mic talisman roșu și alb prietenilor și familiei ca semn de primăvară." Keep descriptions general and factual rather than quoting any copyrighted source.

## Explicitly not here
Business correspondence, culture/arts vocabulary and formal debating register build on this at B2.
