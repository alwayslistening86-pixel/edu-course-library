# S01_A1_Grammar_Basics - Practice: Sounds, gender and articles -- the building blocks of Romanian

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Read aloud: "Ești român? Nu, sunt englez, dar am o casă în România." Mark every diacritic sound you produce correctly.
2. Sort these nouns by gender and give plural forms: scaun, masă, tren, carte, băiat, fată.
3. Add the definite article to: student, fată, scaun, casă.
4. Turn "un profesor" and "o studentă" into their definite forms and use each in a short sentence with a fi.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
