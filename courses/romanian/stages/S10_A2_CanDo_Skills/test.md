# S10_A2_CanDo_Skills - Test: A2 can-do check: everyday exchanges and simple connected description

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Combined mini-assessment: (1) listen to/read a short passage about someone's job and family and extract three facts; (2) read a mock advert or timetable and find two specific pieces of information; (3) role-play a routine shopping or travel transaction; (4) give a two-minute connected spoken self-description; (5) write a short note and a simple thank-you letter.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S10_A2_CanDo_Skills` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S11_B1_Grammar_Imperfect_Subjunctive.
