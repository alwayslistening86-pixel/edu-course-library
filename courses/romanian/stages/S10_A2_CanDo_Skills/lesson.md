# S10_A2_CanDo_Skills - Lesson: A2 can-do check: everyday exchanges and simple connected description

## Goal
The learner meets the CEFR A2 can-do descriptors for listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing in Romanian.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-A2-CD-listen1 - Can-do: listening at A2: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand phrases and high-frequency vocabulary on immediate personal relevance
- RO-A2-CD-read1 - Can-do: reading at A2: CEFR self-assessment grid: read short simple texts and find predictable information
- RO-A2-CD-spokeninteract1 - Can-do: spoken interaction at A2: CEFR self-assessment grid: communicate in simple routine tasks on familiar topics
- RO-A2-CD-spokenprod1 - Can-do: spoken production at A2: CEFR self-assessment grid: use a series of phrases to describe family, living conditions and background
- RO-A2-CD-write1 - Can-do: writing at A2: CEFR self-assessment grid: write short simple notes and a very simple personal letter

## How to teach this
Explain the jump from A1 to A2: the learner now handles not just isolated words but short connected exchanges about immediate personal relevance (shopping, local area, work, family). Walk through each skill using S06-S09 material as raw content.

#### RO-A2-CD-listen1 Can-do: listening at A2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand phrases and the highest-frequency vocabulary related to areas of most immediate personal relevance (basic personal and family information, shopping, local area, employment). Practise with slightly longer, natural-speed-but-clear listening passages: a short shop conversation, a simple travel announcement ("Trenul spre Iași are o întârziere de zece minute."), a description of someone's job. The learner should extract two or three specific pieces of information, not just the gist.

#### RO-A2-CD-read1 Can-do: reading at A2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can read very short, simple texts and find specific, predictable information in everyday material such as advertisements, menus and timetables. Practise with a train timetable, a job advert, a simple recipe, or a short email arranging to meet. The learner should locate specific facts (a time, a price, a place, a requirement) reliably.

#### RO-A2-CD-spokeninteract1 Can-do: spoken interaction at A2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can communicate in simple and routine tasks requiring a simple, direct exchange of information on familiar topics and activities. Practise unscripted routine transactions: buying clothes (S08), booking travel (S08), describing a minor ailment at a pharmacy (S09). Judge success on the learner sustaining a multi-turn exchange without needing every sentence rephrased, unlike at A1.

#### RO-A2-CD-spokenprod1 Can-do: spoken production at A2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can use a series of phrases and sentences to describe, in simple terms, their family and other people, living conditions, educational background and present or most recent job. Have the learner give a 2-minute connected spoken description covering all of these, using perfect compus for past events, adjectives for character/appearance (S09) and prepositions for their home (S07-S09).

#### RO-A2-CD-write1 Can-do: writing at A2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can write short, simple notes and messages, and a very simple personal letter, for example thanking someone. Set two tasks: (1) write a short note arranging to meet a friend (time, place, what to bring); (2) write a 5-6 sentence thank-you letter for a gift or hospitality, using perfect compus to describe what happened and adjectives to describe how it was.

## Explicitly not here
B1 can-do descriptors (understanding main points of standard speech, entering unprepared into familiar conversation, giving reasons for opinions) follow once the imperfect and subjunctive are taught.
