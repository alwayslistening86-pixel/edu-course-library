# S04_A1_Vocab_World_Around - Practice: Finding your way, daily life, appearance and weather

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give directions from a school to a bank on a simple map, using at least three direction phrases.
2. Describe your daily routine from waking up to going to bed, with at least six time-anchored actions.
3. Describe what you and a friend are wearing today, using at least four colour-clothing combinations with correct agreement.
4. Describe today's weather and then describe typical weather in each of the four seasons.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
