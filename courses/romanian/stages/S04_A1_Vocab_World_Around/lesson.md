# S04_A1_Vocab_World_Around - Lesson: Finding your way, daily life, appearance and weather

## Goal
The learner asks for and gives directions, describes their daily routine, describes colours and clothing, and talks about the weather and seasons.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-A1-V5 - Directions and places in town: learn and apply: directions and places in town
- RO-A1-V6 - Daily routine and time expressions: learn and apply: daily routine and time expressions
- RO-A1-V7 - Colours, clothing and describing objects: learn and apply: colours, clothing and describing objects
- RO-A1-V8 - Weather and seasons: learn and apply: weather and seasons

## How to teach this
Give the learner a simple hand-drawn town map and ask: 'How would you ask a stranger the way to the station?' Build directions vocabulary from their attempt, then move outward to routine, appearance and weather.

#### RO-A1-V5 Directions and places in town
Places: gara (the station), piața (the square/market), banca (the bank), spitalul (the hospital), școala (the school), biserica (the church), magazinul (the shop). Directions: la stânga (left), la dreapta (right), drept înainte (straight ahead), aproape/departe (near/far). Key phrases: "Scuzați-mă, unde este gara?" (Excuse me, where is the station?) "Mergeți drept înainte, apoi la stânga." (Go straight ahead, then left.) "Este aproape de aici." (It's near here.) Note that these place names appear in their definite (-ul/-a) form, reinforcing the article from RO-A1-G4.

#### RO-A1-V6 Daily routine and time expressions
mă trezesc (I wake up), mă spăl (I wash), mă îmbrac (I get dressed), mănânc micul dejun (I eat breakfast), plec la muncă/școală (I leave for work/school), mă întorc acasă (I return home), cinez (I have dinner), mă culc (I go to bed). Time expressions: dimineața (in the morning), la prânz (at midday), după-amiaza (in the afternoon), seara (in the evening), noaptea (at night), în fiecare zi (every day). Example: "În fiecare dimineață mă trezesc la ora șapte și mănânc micul dejun." These reflexive-looking verbs are previewed here; full reflexive conjugation is taught explicitly in A2 (RO-A2-G3).

#### RO-A1-V7 Colours, clothing and describing objects
Colours (agree with noun gender): roșu/roșie (red), albastru/albastră (blue), verde (green, same for both), galben/galbenă (yellow), negru/neagră (black), alb/albă (white). Clothing: cămașă (shirt), pantaloni (trousers), rochie (dress), pantofi (shoes), haină/jachetă (coat/jacket), pălărie (hat). Example: "Port o rochie albastră și pantofi negri." (I'm wearing a blue dress and black shoes.) Practise adjective agreement with feminine and masculine nouns side by side: "un pantof negru" vs "o rochie neagră".

#### RO-A1-V8 Weather and seasons
Cum e vremea? (What's the weather like?) E soare (It's sunny), plouă (it's raining), ninge (it's snowing), e cald/frig/răcoare (it's hot/cold/cool), e vânt (it's windy), e înnorat (it's cloudy). Seasons: primăvara (spring), vara (summer), toamna (autumn), iarna (winter) -- note these are the same words used adverbially ("in spring" = "primăvara"). Example: "Iarna ninge des în România, dar vara e foarte cald." (In winter it often snows in Romania, but in summer it's very hot.)

## Explicitly not here
Travel-specific vocabulary and more nuanced weather/opinion language come in A2 and B1.
