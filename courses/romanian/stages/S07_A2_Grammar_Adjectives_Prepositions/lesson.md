# S07_A2_Grammar_Adjectives_Prepositions - Lesson: Describing, comparing and locating things

## Goal
The learner places and agrees descriptive adjectives correctly, forms comparatives and superlatives, uses common irregular verbs, and uses prepositions of place and time.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-A2-G5 - Common irregular verbs: learn and apply: common irregular verbs
- RO-A2-G6 - Descriptive adjectives: agreement and position: learn and apply: descriptive adjectives, agreement and position
- RO-A2-G7 - Comparative and superlative of adjectives: learn and apply: comparative and superlative of adjectives
- RO-A2-G8 - Prepositions of place and time: learn and apply: prepositions of place and time

## How to teach this
Show two photos of houses, one small and plain, one large and beautiful, and ask the learner to compare them. Build irregular verbs, adjective agreement, comparison and prepositions from that comparison task.

#### RO-A2-G5 Common irregular verbs
Key irregulars to drill: a merge (to go: merg, mergi, merge, mergem, mergeți, merg), a face (to do/make: fac, faci, face, facem, faceți, fac), a putea (can: pot, poți, poate, putem, puteți, pot), a vrea (to want: vreau, vrei, vrea, vrem, vreți, vor), a ști (to know: știu, știi, știe, știm, știți, știu), a veni (to come: vin, vii, vine, venim, veniți, vin). These verbs appear constantly in daily speech ("Pot să vin mâine?" -- Can I come tomorrow?) and their irregular stems must be memorised individually.

#### RO-A2-G6 Descriptive adjectives: agreement and position
Adjectives normally follow the noun and agree in gender and number: "o casă frumoasă" (a beautiful house, f.), "un băiat frumos" (a handsome boy, m.), "case frumoase" / "băieți frumoși" (plural). A small set of common adjectives (mare - big, mic - small, bun - good, prost - bad/silly) can go before the noun for emphasis: "un mare artist" (a great artist) has a slightly different nuance from "un artist mare" (a physically big artist). For most descriptive adjectives, post-nominal position is the neutral, correct default to teach first.

#### RO-A2-G7 Comparative and superlative of adjectives
Comparative: mai + adjective (+ decât for "than"): "Casa mea e mai mare decât a ta." (My house is bigger than yours.) Equal comparison: la fel de + adjective + ca: "la fel de mare ca..." Superlative: cel/cea/cei/cele mai + adjective: "cea mai mare casă din oraș" (the biggest house in town). Irregular: bun -> mai bun -> cel mai bun (good/better/best); rău -> mai rău -> cel mai rău (bad/worse/worst). Practise with real comparisons between two known people or places.

#### RO-A2-G8 Prepositions of place and time
Place: în (in), pe (on), sub (under), lângă (next to), între (between), la (at/to), din (from). Time: la ora... (at ... o'clock), în (in, for months/years: în mai), de la...până la (from...until), înainte de / după (before/after), de (since, for duration: de trei ani). Example: "Cartea este pe masă, lângă lampă." (The book is on the table, next to the lamp.) "Lucrez aici de trei ani." (I've worked here for three years -- note prezent + de for ongoing duration, unlike English's present perfect.)

## Explicitly not here
Object pronouns (accusative and dative clitics) follow in the next stage.
