# S07_A2_Grammar_Adjectives_Prepositions - Test: Describing, comparing and locating things

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Describe your ideal holiday destination: what it is like (adjectives), how it compares with somewhere you have been (comparative/superlative), what you can and want to do there (irregular verbs), and where things are located (prepositions), plus when you plan to go (a preposition of time).

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S07_A2_Grammar_Adjectives_Prepositions` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S08_A2_Grammar_Pronouns_Travel_Shopping.
