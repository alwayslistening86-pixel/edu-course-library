# S22_C1_CanDo_Skills - Lesson: C1 can-do check: fluent, flexible and precise language use

## Goal
The learner meets the CEFR C1 can-do descriptors for listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing in Romanian.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-C1-CD-listen1 - Can-do: listening at C1: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand extended speech even when not clearly structured, understand films with little effort
- RO-C1-CD-read1 - Can-do: reading at C1: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand long complex factual and literary texts and specialised articles
- RO-C1-CD-spokeninteract1 - Can-do: spoken interaction at C1: CEFR self-assessment grid: express myself fluently and spontaneously, use language flexibly for social and professional purposes
- RO-C1-CD-spokenprod1 - Can-do: spoken production at C1: CEFR self-assessment grid: present clear detailed descriptions of complex subjects integrating sub-themes
- RO-C1-CD-write1 - Can-do: writing at C1: CEFR self-assessment grid: express myself in clear well-structured text at some length on complex subjects

## How to teach this
Explain that C1 is defined by flexibility and implicit understanding, not just accuracy: the learner should now cope with speech that isn't neatly organised, and texts that assume cultural/contextual knowledge. Walk through each skill using S20-S21 material.

#### RO-C1-CD-listen1 Can-do: listening at C1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand extended speech even when it is not clearly structured and when relationships are only implied and not signalled explicitly, and can understand television programmes and films without too much effort. Practise with a deliberately loosely structured spoken passage (someone thinking aloud, changing topic mid-sentence, using implied contrast without "dar/însă") and ask the learner to reconstruct the implicit logical relationships.

#### RO-C1-CD-read1 Can-do: reading at C1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand long and complex factual and literary texts, appreciating distinctions of style, and can understand specialised articles and longer technical instructions even outside their own field. Practise with a longer text mixing register (S21-V2) and ask the learner to identify shifts in style and tone across it, plus a short technical instruction from an unfamiliar field (S21-V1 style).

#### RO-C1-CD-spokeninteract1 Can-do: spoken interaction at C1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can express themselves fluently and spontaneously without much obvious searching for expressions, and can use language flexibly and effectively for social and professional purposes. Practise a mixed-register role-play that shifts mid-conversation from social chat to a professional request (S20-G3 register shifting), judged on how naturally the learner adapts without hesitation.

#### RO-C1-CD-spokenprod1 Can-do: spoken production at C1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can present clear, detailed descriptions of complex subjects, integrating sub-themes, developing particular points and rounding off with an appropriate conclusion. Have the learner give a 4-5 minute presentation on a complex subject (e.g. a social change and its several causes) that explicitly integrates at least two sub-themes and closes with a genuine, non-formulaic conclusion.

#### RO-C1-CD-write1 Can-do: writing at C1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can express themselves in clear, well-structured text, expressing points of view at some length, and can write about complex subjects in a letter, essay or report, underlining what they consider the salient issues. Set the task: write a 300-350 word structured essay on a complex subject, explicitly stating which issues the learner considers most salient and why, using the cohesion devices and hedging from S20.

## Explicitly not here
C2 can-do descriptors (near-effortless comprehension at native speed, effortless fluent conversation with idiom, smoothly-flowing style-appropriate production) follow once rare constructions and full register range are taught.
