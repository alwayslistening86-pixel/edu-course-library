# S24_C2_CanDo_Skills - Lesson: C2 can-do check: near-native mastery across every skill

## Goal
The learner meets the CEFR C2 can-do descriptors for listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing in Romanian.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-C2-CD-listen1 - Can-do: listening at C2: CEFR self-assessment grid: no difficulty understanding any spoken language at fast native speed
- RO-C2-CD-read1 - Can-do: reading at C2: CEFR self-assessment grid: read with ease virtually all forms of the written language
- RO-C2-CD-spokeninteract1 - Can-do: spoken interaction at C2: CEFR self-assessment grid: take part effortlessly in any conversation, convey finer shades of meaning precisely
- RO-C2-CD-spokenprod1 - Can-do: spoken production at C2: CEFR self-assessment grid: present a clear smoothly-flowing description or argument in an appropriate style
- RO-C2-CD-write1 - Can-do: writing at C2: CEFR self-assessment grid: write clear smoothly-flowing text in an appropriate style, complex letters, reports or articles

## How to teach this
Explain that C2 is not "perfection" but effortless, near-native command across registers and contexts -- the CEFR's own descriptors emphasise ease and precision, not flawlessness. Walk through each skill using the full range of the course, especially S23's register control.

#### RO-C2-CD-listen1 Can-do: listening at C2
CEFR descriptor: the learner has no difficulty understanding any kind of spoken language, whether live or broadcast, even delivered at fast native speed, provided they have some time to get familiar with the accent. Practise with fast, natural, unscripted-style speech including regional accent features and colloquial reductions (previewed in C2-V2), followed by comprehension questions that require catching nuance and implication, not just main points.

#### RO-C2-CD-read1 Can-do: reading at C2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can read with ease virtually all forms of the written language, including abstract, structurally or linguistically complex texts such as manuals, specialised articles and literary works. Practise with a dense, abstract paraphrased text (e.g. a summary of a philosophical or legal argument) and a literary-register paraphrase (S23-G2 style), checking that the learner reads fluently without needing to slow down for structure or vocabulary.

#### RO-C2-CD-spokeninteract1 Can-do: spoken interaction at C2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can take part effortlessly in any conversation or discussion and has good familiarity with idiomatic expressions and colloquialisms, expressing themselves fluently and conveying finer shades of meaning precisely. Practise a fast-paced, multi-participant-style discussion (tutor plays devil's advocate, shifts topic abruptly, uses idiom from C2-G4) and judge whether the learner keeps pace and responds with precise, nuanced meaning rather than approximation.

#### RO-C2-CD-spokenprod1 Can-do: spoken production at C2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can present a clear, smoothly-flowing description or argument in a style appropriate to the context, with an effective logical structure that helps the listener notice and remember significant points. Have the learner give a 5-6 minute presentation on a complex, chosen topic, explicitly adapting style to a stated audience (e.g. "explain this to a general public audience" vs "to fellow specialists"), judged on flow, structure and audience-appropriate register.

#### RO-C2-CD-write1 Can-do: writing at C2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can write clear, smoothly-flowing text in an appropriate style, including complex letters, reports or articles which present a case with an effective logical structure. Set the task: write a 350-400 word piece (report or article) on a complex subject of the learner's choice, in a register the learner specifies in advance (e.g. academic, journalistic, literary), demonstrating the register-control skills built across S20-S23.

## Explicitly not here
This is the top of the ladder: the cumulative exam follows, drawing on every stage from A1 to C2.
