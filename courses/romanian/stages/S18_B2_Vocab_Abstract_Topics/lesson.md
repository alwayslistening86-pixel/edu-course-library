# S18_B2_Vocab_Abstract_Topics - Lesson: Society, business, culture and debate

## Goal
The learner discusses education, society and politics, writes formal correspondence, discusses culture and the arts, and debates with nuanced language.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-B2-V1 - Education, society and politics: learn and apply: education, society and politics
- RO-B2-V2 - Business and formal correspondence: learn and apply: business and formal correspondence
- RO-B2-V3 - Culture, arts and literature: learn and apply: culture, arts and literature
- RO-B2-V4 - Debating and nuanced opinion: learn and apply: debating and nuanced opinion

## How to teach this
Pick a mildly complex topical issue (school reform, a new law) and ask the learner to discuss it from several angles: personal, cultural, and in a formal letter. Build all four vocabulary sets from that single discussion.

#### RO-B2-V1 Education, society and politics
sistem de învățământ (education system), reformă (reform), inegalitate (inequality), societate (society), guvern (government), alegeri (elections), politician, cetățean (citizen), drepturi (rights), lege (law). Example: "Reforma sistemului de învățământ ar trebui să reducă inegalitatea dintre orașe și sate." (The education reform should reduce inequality between towns and villages.) This vocabulary supports the abstract-topic discussion expected at B2.

#### RO-B2-V2 Business and formal correspondence
Stimate Domn / Stimată Doamnă (Dear Sir/Madam, formal letter opening, using the vocative from G6), Cu stimă / Cu respect (Yours sincerely/respectfully, closing), a solicita (to request formally), a anexa (to attach), factură (invoice), ofertă (offer/quote), termen limită (deadline). Example: "Stimată Doamnă, Vă scriu pentru a solicita informații suplimentare despre oferta dumneavoastră. Anexez documentele necesare. Cu stimă," -- a full miniature formal-letter template.

#### RO-B2-V3 Culture, arts and literature
literatură (literature), roman (novel), poezie (poetry), pictură (painting), muzeu (museum), spectacol (performance/show), regizor (director), scriitor (writer). Example: "Romanul descrie viața la sat în perioada interbelică." (The novel describes village life in the interwar period.) Discuss well-known Romanian cultural figures generally by name and period only, without quoting their texts (e.g. Mihai Eminescu, poet; George Enescu, composer), respecting copyright by paraphrase and context rather than quotation.

#### RO-B2-V4 Debating and nuanced opinion
Pe de o parte..., pe de altă parte... (previewed in B1, now used for full debate structure), avantaje și dezavantaje (advantages and disadvantages), a argumenta (to argue a case), a contrazice (to contradict), a fi de acord parțial (to partly agree), în concluzie (in conclusion), cu toate acestea (nevertheless). Example structure: "Pe de o parte, tehnologia ne ajută să comunicăm mai ușor. Pe de altă parte, poate izola oamenii. Cu toate acestea, cred că avantajele sunt mai mari. În concluzie, tehnologia este utilă dacă este folosită cu măsură."

## Explicitly not here
Specialised professional register and literary/journalistic style deepen at C1.
