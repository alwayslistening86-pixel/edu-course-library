# S03_A1_Vocab_People_Food - Practice: Meeting people, family and eating out

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Act out meeting a Romanian family for the first time, using formal then informal greetings.
2. Draw a small family tree and describe it in Romanian using possessive adjectives.
3. Write a short restaurant dialogue ordering a starter, a main course and a drink, then asking for the bill.
4. Roleplay buying three items at a market, asking the price of each and the total.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
