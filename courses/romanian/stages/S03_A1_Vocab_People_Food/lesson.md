# S03_A1_Vocab_People_Food - Lesson: Meeting people, family and eating out

## Goal
The learner greets people appropriately, introduces themselves and their family, and orders food and drink in a café or restaurant.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-A1-V1 - Greetings and introductions: learn and apply: greetings and introductions
- RO-A1-V2 - Family members: learn and apply: family members
- RO-A1-V3 - Food, drink and the restaurant: learn and apply: food, drink and the restaurant
- RO-A1-V4 - Shopping, numbers and prices: learn and apply: shopping, numbers and prices

## How to teach this
Role-play a first meeting: 'You are at a friend's house in Bucharest. Greet the family, introduce yourself, and then everyone sits down to eat.' Build the scene vocabulary item by item.

#### RO-A1-V1 Greetings and introductions
Bună ziua (good day, formal/all-purpose), Bună (hi, informal), Bună dimineața (good morning), Bună seara (good evening), La revedere (goodbye), Noapte bună (goodnight). Introductions: "Mă numesc Ana." / "Numele meu este Ana." (My name is Ana.) "Cum te cheamă?" (informal) / "Cum vă numiți?" (formal) (What's your name?) "Îmi pare bine." (Pleased to meet you.) "Ce mai faci?" / "Ce mai faceți?" (How are you? informal/formal) -- "Bine, mulțumesc, și tu/dumneavoastră?" (Fine, thanks, and you?). Romanian marks formality through the tu/dumneavoastră (you) distinction from the first lesson.

#### RO-A1-V2 Family members
mamă/mama (mother), tată/tata (father), părinți (parents), frate (brother), soră (sister), fiu (son), fiică (daughter), soț (husband), soție (wife), bunic/bunică (grandfather/grandmother), unchi/mătușă (uncle/aunt), văr/verișoară (male/female cousin), copil/copii (child/children). Example dialogue: "Ai frați sau surori?" -- "Da, am un frate și o soră. Fratele meu se numește Radu." Practise possessive + family noun combinations from the grammar stage: "sora mea", "fratele meu".

#### RO-A1-V3 Food, drink and the restaurant
Core foods: pâine (bread), brânză (cheese), carne (meat), pește (fish), legume (vegetables), fructe (fruit), supă (soup), salată (salad). Drinks: apă (water), suc (juice), cafea (coffee), ceai (tea), vin (wine), bere (beer). Restaurant phrases: "O masă pentru doi, vă rog." (A table for two, please.) "Ce recomandați?" (What do you recommend?) "Aș vrea o ciorbă și un mici." (I'd like a soup and a mici -- a classic grilled dish.) "Nota de plată, vă rog." (The bill, please.) "Poftă bună!" (Enjoy your meal! -- said before eating.)

#### RO-A1-V4 Shopping, numbers and prices
la piață (at the market), la magazin (at the shop), Cât costă? (How much does it cost?), Cât fac în total? (How much does it come to?), lei (the Romanian currency, leu/lei), un leu, doi lei, cinci lei; bani (money), a plăti (to pay), a cumpăra (to buy). Example: "Cât costă un kilogram de mere?" -- "Cinci lei." Practise combining V4 with the numbers from RO-A1-G8: "Aș vrea trei mere și două portocale, vă rog."

## Explicitly not here
Travel, shopping for clothes and describing character come in A2.
