# S09_A2_Vocab_Life_Topics - Lesson: Health, hobbies, home, people and work

## Goal
The learner talks about their health, free time, home, describes people's character and appearance, and gives basic information about school or work.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-A2-V3 - Health and the body: learn and apply: health and the body
- RO-A2-V4 - Free time and hobbies: learn and apply: free time and hobbies
- RO-A2-V5 - House and home: learn and apply: house and home
- RO-A2-V6 - Describing people: learn and apply: describing people
- RO-A2-V7 - School and work basics: learn and apply: school and work basics

## How to teach this
Ask: 'Tell me about a typical Saturday: how do you feel, what do you do for fun, where are you, who are you with, and do you talk about work or school?' Build all five vocabulary sets from that one narrative.

#### RO-A2-V3 Health and the body
Body parts: cap (head), mână (hand), picior (leg/foot), spate (back), stomac (stomach), gât (throat). Ailments: Mă doare capul (My head hurts, literally "the head hurts me" -- dative construction), Am febră (I have a fever), Sunt răcit (I have a cold), Mă simt rău/bine (I feel bad/good). At the doctor: "Ce vă supără?" (What's troubling you?) "De când aveți aceste simptome?" (Since when have you had these symptoms?) la farmacie (at the pharmacy), un medicament (a medicine).

#### RO-A2-V4 Free time and hobbies
a citi (to read), a face sport (to do sport), a asculta muzică (to listen to music), a merge la film (to go to the cinema), a găti (to cook), a picta (to paint), a juca fotbal/tenis (to play football/tennis -- note "a juca" for sports vs "a cânta" for instruments). Example: "În timpul liber, îmi place să citesc și să fac sport." (In my free time, I like reading and doing sport -- "îmi place să" + infinitive-like subjunctive is the standard structure, previewed here before formal subjunctive teaching in B1.)

#### RO-A2-V5 House and home
apartament/casă (flat/house), cameră (room), bucătărie (kitchen), baie (bathroom), dormitor (bedroom), sufragerie (living room), balcon (balcony), curte (yard/garden). Furniture: pat (bed), masă (table), scaun (chair, from A1), canapea (sofa), dulap (wardrobe/cupboard). Example: "Apartamentul meu are două dormitoare, o bucătărie și un balcon mic." Practise combining with prepositions of place from RO-A2-G8: "Patul este lângă fereastră."

#### RO-A2-V6 Describing people
Character: amabil (kind), vesel (cheerful), timid (shy), harnic (hard-working), leneș (lazy), inteligent (intelligent). Appearance: înalt/scund (tall/short), slab (thin), are părul lung/scurt (has long/short hair), are ochi albaștri (has blue eyes). Example: "Sora mea este înaltă, are părul lung și este foarte harnică." Note the "a avea" construction for physical features, parallel to English "has".

#### RO-A2-V7 School and work basics
școală (school), liceu (secondary school), facultate (university/faculty), a învăța (to study/learn), notă (grade/mark), examen (exam). Work: serviciu/muncă (job/work), birou (office), coleg (colleague), șef (boss), salariu (salary). Example: "Lucrez într-un birou și am un șef amabil." "Sunt încă la facultate, învăț economie." These set up the fuller professions vocabulary taught at B1 (RO-B1-V1).
