# S20_C1_Grammar_Discourse_Idiom - Lesson: Nuanced connectors, idiom, register control and complex argumentation

## Goal
The learner shifts register fluidly, uses idiomatic expressions naturally, builds complex cohesive argumentation, uses advanced hedged subjunctive/conditional forms, and analyses Romanian word formation.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-C1-G1 - Nuanced discourse connectors and register shifting: learn and apply: nuanced discourse connectors and register shifting
- RO-C1-G2 - Idiomatic expressions and fixed phrases: learn and apply: idiomatic expressions and fixed phrases
- RO-C1-G3 - Stylistic variation: formal versus colloquial: learn and apply: stylistic variation, formal versus colloquial
- RO-C1-G4 - Complex argumentation and cohesion devices: learn and apply: complex argumentation and cohesion devices
- RO-C1-G5 - Advanced subjunctive and conditional in hedged statements: learn and apply: advanced subjunctive and conditional in hedged statements
- RO-C1-G6 - Word formation: prefixes and suffixes: learn and apply: word formation, prefixes and suffixes

## How to teach this
Give the learner the same piece of news and ask them to explain it three ways: to a close friend, in a formal report, and as a nuanced hedged opinion ("it seems that... one might say that..."). This single exercise draws out all six grammar points.

#### RO-C1-G1 Nuanced discourse connectors and register shifting
Beyond B1-B2 connectors, C1 requires more precise discourse markers: mai mult decât atât (moreover), în ciuda faptului că (despite the fact that), nu numai că... dar și... (not only... but also...), or altfel spus (in other words), de fapt (in fact/actually), în esență (essentially). Register shifting means choosing "e" vs "este" (colloquial vs neutral copula), or "o grămadă de" (colloquial, "a ton of") vs "o cantitate mare de" (formal, "a large quantity of") depending on audience -- a skill, not just a vocabulary list, requiring conscious practice switching between the same content in two registers.

#### RO-C1-G2 Idiomatic expressions and fixed phrases
Common idioms: a bate câmpii (to talk nonsense, literally "to beat the fields"), a tăia frunze la câini (to idle, literally "to cut leaves for dogs"), a-i părea rău (to be sorry), a-și face griji (to worry), a trage o sperietură (to get a fright), a fi cu capul în nori (to be absent-minded, literally "to have one's head in the clouds"). Example: "Nu-ți face griji, o să iasă bine până la urmă." (Don't worry, it'll turn out fine in the end.) At C1, idioms should be understood receptively and a working set used productively in natural contexts, not translated word-for-word.

#### RO-C1-G3 Stylistic variation: formal versus colloquial
C1 learners must control register consciously: colloquial contractions (n-am for nu am, s-a for s-a, ce faci? shortened casually to ce faci, mă?) versus formal written Romanian, which avoids contractions and favours the passive/impersonal se and genitive-dative case forms (B2-G8) over paraphrase. Practise rewriting the same three sentences in both a text-message-casual style and an essay-formal style, noting vocabulary, contraction and construction choices that shift.

#### RO-C1-G4 Complex argumentation and cohesion devices
At C1, argumentation spans multiple paragraphs with signposted structure: în primul rând... în al doilea rând... nu în ultimul rând (firstly... secondly... last but not least), pornind de la premisa că (starting from the premise that), a concluziona (to conclude, more formal than "în concluzie"). Cohesion also relies on anaphoric reference (aceasta, acest fapt, ceea ce presupune) to avoid repeating nouns. Practise building a 3-paragraph argument with clear signposting and no repeated nouns where a pronoun/reference would do.

#### RO-C1-G5 Advanced subjunctive and conditional in hedged statements
Hedging softens claims: s-ar putea să (it might be that), se pare că (it seems that), aș zice că (I'd say that, colloquial hedge), nu aș exclude posibilitatea ca... să (I wouldn't rule out the possibility that...). Example: "S-ar putea ca proiectul să fie amânat, deși nu aș exclude posibilitatea să se termine la timp." (The project might be delayed, though I wouldn't rule out it finishing on time.) These constructions layer subjunctive within hedging frames, a hallmark of C1 nuance.

#### RO-C1-G6 Word formation: prefixes and suffixes
Productive prefixes: ne- (negation: fericit -> nefericit, unhappy), re- (repetition: a face -> a reface, to redo), în-/îm- (a-> verb-forming: bogat -> a îmbogăți, to enrich). Productive suffixes: -tor/-toare (agent noun: a cânta -> cântător, singer), -ime (collective: tânăr -> tinerime, youth as a collective), -bil (capability: a lucra -> lucrabil is rare, but a crede -> credibil, credible, from Latin roots), -ie (abstract noun: bucuros -> bucurie, joy). Practise deriving 5 new words from known roots using these patterns, and recognising unfamiliar words by breaking down their morphology.
