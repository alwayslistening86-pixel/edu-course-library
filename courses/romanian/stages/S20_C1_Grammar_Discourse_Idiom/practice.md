# S20_C1_Grammar_Discourse_Idiom - Practice: Nuanced connectors, idiom, register control and complex argumentation

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the same short piece of news in three registers: casual, neutral and formal.
2. Use five idioms from the lesson correctly in original sentences.
3. Rewrite five sentences from colloquial to formal Romanian and vice versa.
4. Write a three-paragraph argument on a topic of your choice with clear signposting and no repeated nouns.
5. Write five hedged statements using different hedging constructions.
6. Derive five new words from given roots using the prefixes/suffixes taught, and explain their meaning.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
