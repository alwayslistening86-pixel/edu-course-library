# S13_B1_Grammar_Case_Work_Travel - Lesson: Ownership, impersonal statements, work and travel

## Goal
The learner uses possessive pronouns and the genitive-dative marker correctly, forms impersonal statements with se, and talks about work/professions and travel arrangements.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-B1-G9 - Possessive pronouns and the genitive-dative marker al/a/ai/ale: learn and apply: possessive pronouns and the genitive-dative marker al/a/ai/ale
- RO-B1-G10 - Impersonal and reflexive-passive se: learn and apply: impersonal and reflexive-passive se
- RO-B1-V1 - Work and professions: learn and apply: work and professions
- RO-B1-V2 - Travel arrangements and accommodation: learn and apply: travel arrangements and accommodation

## How to teach this
Ask: 'Whose car is that -- and in general, how do people usually get to work in your country?' The first question needs the genitive-dative possessive marker; the second needs the impersonal se; both lead naturally into work and travel vocabulary.

#### RO-B1-G9 Possessive pronouns and the genitive-dative marker al/a/ai/ale
Possessive pronouns (mine, yours...) use al/a/ai/ale + the possessive adjective, agreeing with the thing possessed: "Cartea este a mea." (The book is mine, feminine singular thing possessed -> "a mea".) "Scaunul este al meu." (The chair is mine, masculine -> "al meu".) "Cărțile sunt ale mele." (The books are mine, feminine plural -> "ale mele".) The same al/a/ai/ale marker introduces genitive possession with nouns: "mașina al cărui..." is avoided in favour of "mașina lui Ion" (Ion's car, using the genitive-marked name) or "cartea profesorului" (the teacher's book, noun in genitive form).

#### RO-B1-G10 Impersonal and reflexive-passive se
"Se" is used for impersonal statements (equivalent to "one/people/you" in general statements) and for a passive-like meaning: "Se spune că va ploua." (It is said/People say that it will rain.) "Aici se vorbește română." (Romanian is spoken here.) "Cum se scrie cuvântul acesta?" (How is this word written/spelled?) This construction is extremely common in signs, instructions and general statements, and is often more natural than the true passive voice (taught at B2).

#### RO-B1-V1 Work and professions
Professions: medic (doctor), profesor/profesoară (teacher), inginer (engineer), avocat (lawyer), contabil (accountant), programator (programmer), vânzător (salesperson). Workplace: a candida (to apply), interviu (interview), contract de muncă (employment contract), concediu (holiday/leave), a demisiona (to resign). Example: "Sunt inginer și lucrez la o companie de construcții de cinci ani." Practise describing a profession's typical duties: "Un medic examinează pacienții și prescrie tratamente."

#### RO-B1-V2 Travel arrangements and accommodation
a rezerva o cameră (to book a room), hotel cu mic dejun inclus (hotel with breakfast included), recepție (reception), cazare (accommodation), a anula o rezervare (to cancel a booking), stațiune (resort), pensiune (guesthouse). Example: "Aș vrea să rezerv o cameră dublă pentru trei nopți, cu mic dejun inclus." (I'd like to book a double room for three nights, with breakfast included.) Combine with the conditional-optative from RO-B1-G6 for polite booking requests.
