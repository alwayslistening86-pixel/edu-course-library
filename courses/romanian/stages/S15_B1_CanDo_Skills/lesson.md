# S15_B1_CanDo_Skills - Lesson: B1 can-do check: independent use in familiar situations

## Goal
The learner meets the CEFR B1 can-do descriptors for listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing in Romanian.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-B1-CD-listen1 - Can-do: listening at B1: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand the main points of clear standard speech on familiar matters
- RO-B1-CD-read1 - Can-do: reading at B1: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand texts of everyday or job-related language and personal letters
- RO-B1-CD-spokeninteract1 - Can-do: spoken interaction at B1: CEFR self-assessment grid: deal with most travel situations and enter unprepared into familiar conversation
- RO-B1-CD-spokenprod1 - Can-do: spoken production at B1: CEFR self-assessment grid: connect phrases to describe experiences, dreams and give reasons for opinions
- RO-B1-CD-write1 - Can-do: writing at B1: CEFR self-assessment grid: write simple connected text on familiar topics and personal letters

## How to teach this
Explain that B1 marks "independent user" status: the learner is no longer limited to rehearsed exchanges but can improvise within familiar territory, and can explain reasons, not just facts. Walk through each skill using S11-S14 material.

#### RO-B1-CD-listen1 Can-do: listening at B1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand the main points of clear standard speech on familiar matters regularly encountered in work, school, leisure, etc. Practise with a slightly longer monologue or dialogue at natural speed on a familiar topic (a colleague describing their job, a friend recounting a holiday using imperfect/perfect compus contrast). The learner should identify the main points and overall structure, even if some details are missed.

#### RO-B1-CD-read1 Can-do: reading at B1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand texts that consist mainly of high-frequency everyday or job-related language, and can understand the description of events, feelings and wishes in personal letters. Practise with a personal email or letter describing a recent trip or a work situation, including some emotional language ("m-am simțit foarte fericit", "mi-aș fi dorit să..."). The learner should follow the sequence of events and identify feelings expressed.

#### RO-B1-CD-spokeninteract1 Can-do: spoken interaction at B1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can deal with most situations likely to arise while travelling in an area where the language is spoken, and can enter unprepared into conversation on familiar topics of personal interest. Practise unscripted role-plays: a travel problem (delayed train, wrong hotel booking) requiring negotiation, and a spontaneous conversation about hobbies, work or opinions (drawing on S14) with no pre-agreed script.

#### RO-B1-CD-spokenprod1 Can-do: spoken production at B1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can connect phrases in a simple way to describe experiences and events, dreams, hopes and ambitions, and can briefly give reasons and explanations for opinions and plans. Have the learner give a 2-3 minute talk about a real or invented life goal or dream trip, narrating past experience (perfect compus/imperfect) that led to it and giving reasons (pentru că, deoarece) for their plans.

#### RO-B1-CD-write1 Can-do: writing at B1
CEFR descriptor: the learner can write simple connected text on topics which are familiar or of personal interest, and can write personal letters describing experiences and impressions. Set the task: write a 10-12 sentence letter to a friend describing a recent trip or life event, using imperfect for background, perfect compus for events, at least one relative clause, and at least one opinion/reason construction.

## Explicitly not here
B2 can-do descriptors (understanding extended speech/lectures, active discussion participation, writing essays with arguments) follow once the full subjunctive/conditional system and passive voice are taught.
