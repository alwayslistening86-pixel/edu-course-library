# S19_B2_CanDo_Skills - Lesson: B2 can-do check: fluent, spontaneous engagement with complex topics

## Goal
The learner meets the CEFR B2 can-do descriptors for listening, reading, spoken interaction, spoken production and writing in Romanian.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-B2-CD-listen1 - Can-do: listening at B2: CEFR self-assessment grid: understand extended speech and lectures, follow complex argument, understand most TV news
- RO-B2-CD-read1 - Can-do: reading at B2: CEFR self-assessment grid: read articles and reports on contemporary problems and understand literary prose
- RO-B2-CD-spokeninteract1 - Can-do: spoken interaction at B2: CEFR self-assessment grid: interact with fluency and spontaneity, take an active part in discussion
- RO-B2-CD-spokenprod1 - Can-do: spoken production at B2: CEFR self-assessment grid: present clear detailed descriptions and explain a viewpoint with advantages and disadvantages
- RO-B2-CD-write1 - Can-do: writing at B2: CEFR self-assessment grid: write clear detailed text, an essay or report giving reasons for or against

## How to teach this
Explain that B2 is the level at which the learner becomes a genuinely independent user on abstract, not just personal, topics, and can hold their own in discussion with native speakers. Walk through each skill using S16-S18 material.

#### RO-B2-CD-listen1 Can-do: listening at B2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can understand extended speech and lectures and follow even complex lines of argument, provided the topic is reasonably familiar, and can understand most TV news and current affairs programmes. Practise with a short recorded-style monologue presenting an argument (e.g. for/against a policy), using B2 connectors (deși, cu toate că, pe de altă parte). The learner should trace the line of argument, not just individual facts.

#### RO-B2-CD-read1 Can-do: reading at B2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can read articles and reports concerned with contemporary problems in which writers adopt particular attitudes or viewpoints, and can understand contemporary literary prose. Practise with a short opinion article on a social issue (S18-V1) and a short literary extract (paraphrased, not directly quoted, per copyright rules), identifying the writer's stance and the tone.

#### RO-B2-CD-spokeninteract1 Can-do: spoken interaction at B2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity that makes regular interaction with native speakers quite possible, and can take an active part in discussion in familiar contexts. Practise an unscripted debate (S18-V4 structure) on a topical issue, requiring the learner to respond to counterarguments in real time, not just present a prepared opinion.

#### RO-B2-CD-spokenprod1 Can-do: spoken production at B2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can present clear, detailed descriptions on a wide range of subjects related to their field of interest, and can explain a viewpoint on a topical issue giving advantages and disadvantages of various options. Have the learner give a 3-4 minute structured presentation on a topic of their choice, following the advantages/disadvantages/conclusion structure from S18-V4.

#### RO-B2-CD-write1 Can-do: writing at B2
CEFR descriptor: the learner can write clear, detailed text on a wide range of subjects, and can write an essay or report passing on information or giving reasons in support of or against a particular point of view. Set the task: write a 200-250 word essay on a social or cultural issue, with a clear thesis, supporting arguments (using concessive/purpose clauses from S17), and a conclusion.

## Explicitly not here
C1 can-do descriptors (understanding implicit meaning, flexible language use for social/professional purposes, integrating sub-themes) follow once nuanced connectors and idiom are taught.
