# S08_A2_Grammar_Pronouns_Travel_Shopping - Lesson: Object pronouns, travel and shopping

## Goal
The learner uses direct and indirect object pronouns correctly, and can travel and shop for clothes using appropriate vocabulary.

## Syllabus items taught here
- RO-A2-G9 - Direct object pronouns (accusative clitics): learn and apply: direct object pronouns, accusative clitics
- RO-A2-G10 - Indirect object pronouns (dative clitics): learn and apply: indirect object pronouns, dative clitics
- RO-A2-V1 - Travel and transport: learn and apply: travel and transport
- RO-A2-V2 - Shopping and clothes sizes: learn and apply: shopping and clothes sizes

## How to teach this
Ask: 'You see a jacket you like in a shop. What do you say to the shop assistant, and how do you tell a friend about it afterwards ("I bought it, I liked it")?' Use the story to introduce object pronouns and shopping/travel vocabulary together.

#### RO-A2-G9 Direct object pronouns (accusative clitics)
Accusative clitic pronouns: mă (me), te (you), îl (him/it, m.), o (her/it, f.), ne (us), vă (you pl.), îi (them, m.), le (them, f.). They usually precede the verb: "Îl văd." (I see him.) "O cunosc." (I know her.) In perfect compus, îl/o etc. combine with the auxiliary: "Am văzut-o ieri." (I saw her yesterday -- note "o" moves after the participle and attaches with a hyphen.) Practise with everyday objects: "Unde e cheia? Nu o găsesc." (Where's the key? I can't find it.)

#### RO-A2-G10 Indirect object pronouns (dative clitics)
Dative clitic pronouns: îmi (to me), îți (to you), îi (to him/her), ne (to us), vă (to you pl.), le (to them). Essential with verbs like a plăcea (to like/please): "Îmi place cafeaua." (I like coffee, literally "coffee is pleasing to me".) "Îți place filmul?" (Do you like the film?) Also with a da (to give), a spune (to tell): "Îi dau cartea." (I give him/her the book.) Note îi is both accusative (them, m.) and dative (to him/her) -- context disambiguates; this ambiguity is a known common error point.

#### RO-A2-V1 Travel and transport
tren (train), autobuz (bus), avion (plane), mașină (car), bilet (ticket), gară (station, previewed in A1), aeroport (airport), a rezerva (to book), plecare/sosire (departure/arrival), întârziere (delay). Phrases: "Aș vrea un bilet dus-întors la Iași." (I'd like a return ticket to Iași.) "La ce oră pleacă trenul?" (What time does the train leave?) "Trenul are întârziere." (The train is delayed.)

#### RO-A2-V2 Shopping and clothes sizes
mărime (size), a proba (to try on), cabină de probă (fitting room), prea mare/mic (too big/small), Aveți mărimea...? (Do you have size...?) Clothing extends A1-V7: fustă (skirt), tricou (t-shirt), pulover (jumper), geacă (jacket). Phrases: "Pot să probez această rochie?" (Can I try on this dress?) "Aveți o mărime mai mare?" (Do you have a bigger size?) "Îmi place, o cumpăr." (I like it, I'll buy it -- combining dative "îmi place" from G10.)
