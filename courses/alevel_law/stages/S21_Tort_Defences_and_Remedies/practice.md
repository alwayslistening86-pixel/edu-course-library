# S21_Tort_Defences_and_Remedies - Practice: Contributory negligence and consent; damages and injunctions

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the difference between contributory negligence and consent (volenti) as tort defences. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 contributory negligence is a partial defence that apportions/reduces damages by the claimant's share of responsibility (Law Reform (Contributory Negligence) Act 1945); B1 consent/volenti is a complete defence, barring the claim entirely, where the claimant freely and fully agreed to run the specific risk.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
