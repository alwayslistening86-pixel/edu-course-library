## Test items
1. Explain the exceptions recognised in Young v Bristol Aeroplane Co Ltd [1944] KB 718 to the Court of Appeal being bound by its own past decisions. [6 marks]
2. Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of the Law Commission's role in law reform, compared with judicial development of the law through precedent. [15 marks]

Content credit: Law Commission strengths (independent, expert-led, thorough consultation, can codify/consolidate a whole area coherently) against its weakness (no power to compel Parliament to legislate, so much work is never implemented); precedent's strengths (certainty, flexibility via distinguishing/the 1966 Practice Statement, responsive to real disputes) against its weaknesses (reactive/haphazard, depends on the right case reaching a high enough court, can leave a bad rule in place for years); a substantiated conclusion on which mechanism better serves timely, coherent reform.
