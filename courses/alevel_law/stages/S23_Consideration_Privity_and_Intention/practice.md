# S23_Consideration_Privity_and_Intention - Practice: Consideration and promissory estoppel, privity; intention to create legal relations

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the general rule on past consideration, and its exception. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B2 general rule: an act done before a promise was made is past consideration, and cannot support that later promise (Re McArdle [1951] Ch 669); B1 exception: where the earlier act was done at the promisor's request with both parties understanding payment would follow, a later promise can validly fix the amount (Pao On v Lau Yiu Long [1980] AC 614).

When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
