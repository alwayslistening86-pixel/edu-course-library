# S19_Occupiers_Liability_Trespassers_and_Private_Nuisance - Practice: Occupiers' liability to trespassers; private nuisance

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the three s1(3) conditions before any duty at all arises to a trespasser under the Occupiers' Liability Act 1984. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 the occupier is aware of the danger, or has reasonable grounds to believe it exists; B1 the occupier knows or has reasonable grounds to believe a person is or may come into the vicinity of the danger; B1 the risk is one the occupier may reasonably be expected to offer some protection against in all the circumstances.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
