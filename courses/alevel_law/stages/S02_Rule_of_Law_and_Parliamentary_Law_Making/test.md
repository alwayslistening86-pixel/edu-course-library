## Test items
1. Explain two requirements of the rule of law. [4 marks]
2. Suggest two advantages and two disadvantages of Parliament's law-making process and the influences on it. [10 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M2 no person may be punished except for a clear breach of an established (not retrospective) law, and law must be reasonably certain; M2 (or) equality before the law -- government and officials are bound by the same law as ordinary citizens, enforced by the same courts; credit any two, each with a short explanation of why it matters.
2. [10] M2 advantage: democratic legitimacy through elected MPs debating and voting; M2 advantage: thorough scrutiny across multiple stages in two Houses, including expert Committee-stage input; M2 disadvantage: the process can be slow and a crowded legislative timetable can crowd out non-Government Bills, delaying needed reform; M2 disadvantage: influences such as well-resourced pressure groups or the media can distort priorities toward vocal or well-funded interests rather than the wider public interest; M2 credit any other correctly explained, distinct advantage or disadvantage (e.g. Green/White Paper consultation improving policy quality, versus a government's large Commons majority reducing genuine scrutiny).
