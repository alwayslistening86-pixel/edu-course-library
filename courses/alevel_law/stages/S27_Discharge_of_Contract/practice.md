# S27_Discharge_of_Contract - Practice: Performance and breach; frustration

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the two options available to an innocent party facing an anticipatory breach. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 accept the repudiation immediately and sue at once for damages, without waiting for the performance date (Hochster v De La Tour (1853) 2 E & B 678); B1 affirm the contract, keep it alive, and wait for/press for performance (White and Carter (Councils) Ltd v McGregor [1962] AC 413).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
