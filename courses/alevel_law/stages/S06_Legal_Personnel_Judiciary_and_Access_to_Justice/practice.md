# S06_Legal_Personnel_Judiciary_and_Access_to_Justice - Practice: Legal personnel and the judiciary; access to justice and funding

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Name two mechanisms that protect judicial independence. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 security of tenure (senior judges removable only by a demanding process, e.g. an address of both Houses); B1 immunity from suit for acts done in a judicial capacity -- credit either, or separation from the executive since the Constitutional Reform Act 2005.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
