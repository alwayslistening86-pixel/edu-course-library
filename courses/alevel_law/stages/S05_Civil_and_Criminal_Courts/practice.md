# S05_Civil_and_Criminal_Courts - Practice: The civil legal system and dispute resolution; the criminal legal system and lay people

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A dispute is worth £8,000. Which civil track will it normally be allocated to, and what does that mean for costs recovery? [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 the small claims track (claims broadly up to £10,000); B1 costs recovery is very limited on the small claims track, designed for litigants in person to bring or defend a claim without the risk of a large costs order.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
