# A-level Accounting (AQA 7127) - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Paper 1 (Financial Accounting) covers S01-S07 and S14-S21 (sections 1-8, 14-18 plus the shared 3.17/3.18/annex content). Paper 2 (Accounting for Analysis and Decision-Making) covers S01-S03, S08a-S13 and S17-S21 (sections 1-3, 8-13, 17-18 plus the annex). Each paper has Section A (10 MC questions, 1 mark each, plus short-answer questions to 30 marks total), Section B (40 marks of structured, mostly calculation-based questions), and Section C (two 25-mark extended-response evaluation questions). Questions are original; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage-test items. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest to reach full-length papers.

## 11 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1, Section A] Which accounting concept means a business is accounted for separately from its owner(s)? Choose every correct option.
   A. Going concern
   B. Business entity
   C. Prudence
   D. Materiality
2. [Paper 1, Section A] Which of these is capital expenditure? Choose every correct option.
   A. Repainting an office wall
   B. Buying a new delivery van
   C. Paying the month's wages
   D. Buying inventory for resale
3. [Paper 1, Section A] A business pays £2,400 insurance on 1 September, covering the 12 months to 31 August next year. Its year end is 31 December. Calculate the prepayment carried forward. [3 marks]
4. [Paper 1, Section B] A machine costs £30,000, has a residual value of £3,000, and a 6-year useful life. Calculate the annual straight-line depreciation charge and the carrying amount after 2 years. [4 marks]
5. [Paper 1, Section B] The trial balance fails to balance by £460 (debits exceed credits). A suspense account is opened. It is found that a purchase invoice of £460 was correctly entered in the purchases account but never posted to the supplier's (payables) account. Prepare the correcting journal entry. [3 marks]
6. [Paper 1, Section C] Evaluate whether a sole trader should convert her business to a private limited company, given her main concerns are personal financial risk and access to further capital for expansion. [25 marks]
7. [Paper 2, Section A] Which is a fixed cost? Choose every correct option.
   A. Direct materials
   B. Sales commission
   C. Factory rent
   D. Piece-rate direct labour
8. [Paper 2, Section A] Selling price is £45/unit, variable cost is £27/unit, and fixed costs are £54,000. Calculate the break-even point in units. [3 marks]
9. [Paper 2, Section B] Standard material cost is 4kg at £5/kg = £20/unit. Actual output of 600 units used 2,500kg costing £12,250. Calculate the material price variance and the material usage variance, stating favourable or adverse for each. [6 marks]
10. [Paper 2, Section B] A project requires an initial investment of £70,000 and generates a net cash inflow of £25,000/year for 4 years. Using discount factors of 0.909, 0.826, 0.751 and 0.683 for years 1-4, calculate the NPV. [6 marks]
11. [Paper 2, Section C] A company is deciding between full absorption costing and activity based costing for pricing a new, low-volume, highly customised product line alongside its existing high-volume standard product. Evaluate which costing method the company should use for this decision. [25 marks]

## Grading
Mark short and structured answers against the mark schemes (method marks need the method shown; a multiple-choice item is worth 1 mark and needs the exactly correct option(s)). Mark each 25-mark Section C answer against its five-level response scheme (Level 1, 1-5: an asserted but unsupported judgement; Level 2, 6-10: a basic judgement with limited evaluation; Level 3, 11-15: an underdeveloped judgement supported by evaluation of a range of evidence; Level 4, 16-20: a reasoned but in places unbalanced judgement supported by evaluation of a wide range of evidence; Level 5, 21-25: a clear and balanced response supported by an astute consideration of a wide range of evidence), within which up to 5 marks reward the AO2 calculation/application and up to 20 marks the AO3 evaluation. A pass needs at least 50% of the 240 marks overall and at least 40% on each paper (this course's own standard; AQA's real grade boundaries change every series). Report the total, the percentage per paper, and the weakest topic areas.
