## Test items
1. Inventory is £21,000 and cost of sales is £168,000. Calculate the inventory holding period in days. [2 marks]
2. A rising receivables collection period most likely signals: Choose every correct option.
   A. Faster collection of cash from customers
   B. Weaker credit control or customers taking longer to pay
   C. Higher gross profit margin
   D. Lower cost of sales
3. State two limitations of using ratio analysis to compare two different companies' performance. [4 marks]
4. Explain why a ratio calculated for a single business, on its own, is of limited use without a benchmark. [2 marks]
5. Which ratio would best help assess whether a business is overstocked? Choose every correct option.
   A. Current ratio
   B. Receivables collection period
   C. Inventory holding period
   D. ROCE

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] M1 21,000/168,000 x 365; A1 = 46 days.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] B2 the two companies may use different accounting policies (e.g. different depreciation methods or inventory valuation), making the ratios not directly comparable; B2 ratios are based on historical, often single year-end figures and say nothing about qualitative factors such as management quality, staff morale or brand strength.
4. [2] B1 a ratio only becomes meaningful when compared against something - the same business's prior year, a budget, or a competitor; B1 without a benchmark there is no way to judge whether the figure represents good or poor performance.
5. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
