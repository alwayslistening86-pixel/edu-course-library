# S08a_Ratio_Analysis_Profitability_and_Liquidity - Test: Ratio analysis I: profitability and liquidity

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Revenue is £180,000, profit from operations is £36,000, and capital employed is £240,000. Calculate the operating profit margin and ROCE. [4 marks]
2. Which ratio excludes inventory from current assets? Choose every correct option.
   A. Current ratio
   B. Quick (acid test) ratio
   C. Gross profit margin
   D. ROCE
3. Current assets are £72,000, of which inventory is £30,000, and current liabilities are £48,000. Calculate the current ratio and the quick ratio. [4 marks]
4. A business reports a healthy profit for the year of £40,000 but its bank balance has fallen by £15,000 over the same period. Give two possible reasons this could happen without any error in the accounts. [4 marks]
5. Which best describes what ROCE measures? Choose every correct option.
   A. How much of every £1 of sales is gross profit
   B. How efficiently the capital invested in the business generates operating profit
   C. Whether current assets can cover current liabilities
   D. How quickly inventory is sold

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 operating margin = 36,000/180,000 x 100; A1 = 20%; M1 ROCE = 36,000/240,000 x 100; A1 = 15%.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] M1 current ratio = 72,000/48,000; A1 = 1.50:1; M1 quick ratio = (72,000-30,000)/48,000; A1 = 0.88:1.
4. [4] B2 the business may have spent cash on capital expenditure (e.g. buying new equipment) or on repaying loan capital - neither is an expense in the statement of profit or loss, so neither reduces reported profit, but both use cash; B2 profit may be tied up in rising inventory or receivables (goods bought/sold on credit but not yet paid for), or drawings/dividends were paid out in cash beyond the reported profit.
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
