## Test items
1. Which UK legislation specifically requires accountants to report knowledge or suspicion of money laundering, overriding normal client confidentiality? Choose every correct option.
   A. The Companies Act alone
   B. Anti-money-laundering legislation
   C. The Partnership Act 1890
   D. IFRS Accounting Standards
2. Explain the role of a professional accountancy body's disciplinary process in maintaining public trust in the profession. [3 marks]
3. An accountant suspects, but cannot yet prove, that a colleague has been inflating expense claims. Describe the appropriate first course of action, and explain why waiting until absolute proof is obtained would not be appropriate. [4 marks]
4. Which best describes when an accountant should escalate a concern beyond the internal channel? Choose every correct option.
   A. Never - internal channels are always sufficient
   B. If the internal response is inadequate, the matter is very serious, or suspicion falls on senior management
   C. Only if a client specifically asks them to
   D. Immediately, before ever raising it internally, in every case

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
2. [3] B3 it gives a mechanism to sanction members (up to exclusion from membership) who breach the code of ethics, which both deters unethical conduct and demonstrates to the public that the profession takes its own standards seriously, supporting confidence in accountants' work generally, not just for the individual client concerned.
3. [4] B2 raise the concern through the appropriate internal channel (e.g. a manager, an ethics/compliance function, or a formal whistleblowing procedure), having first gathered and documented the relevant facts objectively; B2 waiting for absolute proof is not appropriate because the fundamental principles require a professionally sceptical, timely response to a genuine, reasonable suspicion - delay could allow further loss or wrongdoing, and 'certainty' is rarely achievable or required before raising a concern through proper channels.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
