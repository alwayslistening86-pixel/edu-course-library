## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full workings (for calculations) or a full written answer before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt, with workings shown, before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer/mark scheme and have them redo any step they got wrong.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
