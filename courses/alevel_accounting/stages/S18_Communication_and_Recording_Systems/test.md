# S18_Communication_and_Recording_Systems - Test: Stakeholders, communication and recording systems

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A company's gross profit margin has fallen from 42% to 36% over the year. Write a brief comment, suitable for a non-specialist company director, explaining what this means and one possible reason. [3 marks]
2. Which stakeholder is most likely to be primarily interested in whether a business can pay them on time for goods already delivered on credit? Choose every correct option.
   A. Shareholders
   B. Suppliers
   C. The general public
   D. Government (as a regulator, not as HMRC)
3. Give one advantage and one disadvantage of a computerised accounting system compared with a manual system. [2 marks]
4. Why might a technical table of ratios alone be an ineffective way to communicate findings to a non-specialist business owner? Choose every correct option.
   A. Ratios are always wrong
   B. It does not explain what the figures mean or what action, if any, should follow
   C. Non-specialists cannot read numbers
   D. Ratios should never be shown to owners

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B3 the business is now keeping less of every £1 of sales as gross profit after the direct cost of the goods sold than it did last year, which could be because selling prices have been cut (e.g. to compete), the cost of buying/making the goods has risen without prices being raised to match, or the product mix has shifted towards lower-margin items (any one plausible reason, expressed clearly for a non-specialist, for the full marks).
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [2] B1 advantage: it can automate posting, enforce double entry and produce reports far faster than manual bookkeeping (or: reduces the risk of arithmetic/posting error); B1 disadvantage: it requires upfront investment and staff training, and carries risks around data security or system failure that a paper-based system does not (any valid, contrasted advantage and disadvantage).
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
