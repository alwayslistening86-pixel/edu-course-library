# S06_Sole_Trader_Financial_Statements - Test: Sole trader financial statements

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Revenue is £110,000, opening inventory £7,000, purchases £68,000, closing inventory £9,000, and operating expenses £22,000. Prepare a summary statement of profit or loss showing cost of sales, gross profit and profit for the year. [5 marks]
2. In the cost of sales calculation, closing inventory is: Choose every correct option.
   A. Added, because it increases the cost of goods available
   B. Deducted, because goods still unsold were not part of what was actually sold
   C. Ignored - only opening inventory matters
   D. Treated as a current liability
3. A sole trader's opening capital was £60,000. Profit for the year is £24,000 and drawings were £18,000. Calculate closing capital. [3 marks]
4. Which of these appears in the statement of financial position rather than the statement of profit or loss? Choose every correct option.
   A. Cost of sales
   B. Operating expenses
   C. Trade receivables
   D. Revenue
5. A trial balance shows non-current assets at carrying amount £75,000, inventory £9,000, receivables £14,000, cash £2,000, payables £11,000 and a bank loan (repayable in 4 years) of £20,000. Calculate capital employed (net assets). [4 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 cost of sales = 7,000 + 68,000 - 9,000 = £66,000; A1 gross profit = 110,000 - 66,000 = £44,000; M1 profit for the year = 44,000 - 22,000; A1 = £22,000; B1 correct structure/labelling (cost of sales, gross profit, profit for the year, each correctly derived in that order).
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] M1 60,000 + 24,000; M1 - 18,000; A1 closing capital = £66,000.
4. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
5. [4] M1 total assets = 75,000 + 9,000 + 14,000 + 2,000 = £100,000; M1 total liabilities = 11,000 + 20,000 = £31,000; A1 capital employed = 100,000 - 31,000 = £69,000; B1 this must equal opening capital + profit for the year - drawings for the same period.
