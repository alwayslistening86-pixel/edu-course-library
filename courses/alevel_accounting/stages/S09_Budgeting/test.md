# S09_Budgeting - Test: Budgeting

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Opening cash balance is £8,000, forecast receipts are £54,000 and forecast payments are £59,000. Calculate the closing cash balance and state one action management might consider if this were repeated for several months. [4 marks]
2. Which best describes zero-based budgeting? Choose every correct option.
   A. Starting from last year's budget and adjusting for inflation
   B. Every item of spending must be justified afresh each period, regardless of past spending
   C. Only used by public sector organisations
   D. A budget that is never revised once set
3. Budgeted materials cost for the month is £22,000; actual materials cost is £24,500. Calculate the variance and state whether it is favourable or adverse, with a reason. [3 marks]
4. A key disadvantage of incremental budgeting compared with zero-based budgeting is: Choose every correct option.
   A. It takes far longer to prepare each period
   B. It can carry forward past inefficiencies without them being questioned
   C. It cannot be used for cash budgets
   D. It always increases spending
5. Explain two purposes of budgeting other than control. [4 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 8,000 + 54,000 - 59,000; A1 closing balance = £3,000; B2 management might arrange overdraft facilities, delay non-essential payments, chase receivables faster, or review whether spending/sales forecasts are realistic (any one sensible, credited action, up to 2 marks).
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] M1 variance = 22,000 - 24,500 = £-2,500; A1 £2,500 adverse; B1 actual cost exceeded the budgeted cost, which reduces profit compared with the plan, hence adverse.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [4] B2 planning - deciding in advance what resources are needed and how activities will be funded, before the period begins; B2 coordination - ensuring different departments' plans are consistent with one another (e.g. the production budget matches the sales budget) (any two valid purposes from planning, coordination, motivation, responsibility - 2 marks each).
