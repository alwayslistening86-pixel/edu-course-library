# S15_Partnership_Accounts - Test: Partnership accounts

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Profit for the year is £45,000. Partner A has capital of £30,000 and Partner B has capital of £30,000, both earning interest on capital at 4%. Partner A also receives a salary of £6,000. Remaining profit is shared equally. Prepare the appropriation account showing the total amount due to each partner. [6 marks]
2. A partner's salary in a partnership appropriation account is: Choose every correct option.
   A. An expense deducted before arriving at profit for the year
   B. An appropriation of profit, not a statement of profit or loss expense
   C. Only paid if the partnership makes a loss
   D. The same as interest on capital
3. Which best distinguishes a partner's capital account from their current account? Choose every correct option.
   A. The capital account holds the permanent capital invested; the current account accumulates profit share less drawings and fluctuates year to year
   B. The current account is only used by limited companies
   C. The capital account changes every year with the profit share
   D. There is no meaningful difference between them
4. A new partner is admitted. Goodwill is valued at £30,000. The two existing partners previously shared profit 2:1 and will now share profit with the new partner 2:1:1. Calculate the goodwill adjustment credited to each existing partner in the old ratio. [3 marks]
5. Explain why goodwill is credited to the existing partners in the old profit-sharing ratio but then debited to all partners (including any new one) in the new ratio. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] M1 interest on capital, each = 30,000 x 0.04 = £1,200; M1 remaining profit = 45,000 - 1,200 - 1,200 - 6,000 = £36,600; A1 each partner's equal share of the remainder = 18,300; A1 total to A = 1,200 + 6,000 + 18,300 = £25,500; A1 total to B = 1,200 + 18,300 = £19,500; B1 the two totals sum back to £45,000.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
4. [3] M1 total goodwill = £30,000, old ratio 2:1 (total 3 parts); A1 partner 1 (2 parts) = 30,000 x 2/3 = £20,000; A1 partner 2 (1 part) = 30,000 x 1/3 = £10,000.
5. [2] B1 the existing partners built up the goodwill before the change, so they are credited with its value in the ratio they previously shared profit in; B1 the debit in the new ratio then removes goodwill from the accounts again (netting to zero) while leaving each partner's capital account correctly adjusted so the new/continuing partner has not gained an unearned share of past value.
