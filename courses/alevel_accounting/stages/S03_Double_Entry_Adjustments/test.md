# S03_Double_Entry_Adjustments - Test: Period-end adjustments: accruals, irrecoverable debts, depreciation

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A business pays £4,800 rent quarterly in advance on 1 November, covering November, December and January. Its year end is 31 December. Calculate the amount to charge to this year's statement of profit or loss and the prepayment carried forward. [4 marks]
2. Writing off an irrecoverable debt of £600 is recorded as: Choose every correct option.
   A. Debit Receivables £600, Credit Irrecoverable Debts Expense £600
   B. Debit Irrecoverable Debts Expense £600, Credit Receivables £600
   C. Debit Cash £600, Credit Receivables £600
   D. Debit Sales £600, Credit Receivables £600
3. A van costs £24,000 and is depreciated at 20% reducing balance. Calculate the depreciation charge for Year 1 and Year 2, and the carrying amount at the end of Year 2. [4 marks]
4. Receivables before adjustment are £30,000. A specific irrecoverable debt of £2,000 is written off, and an allowance for doubtful debts of 3% of the remaining receivables is then created. Calculate the receivables figure to show in the statement of financial position. [3 marks]
5. Which asset is best suited to the reducing balance method rather than straight line? Choose every correct option.
   A. An office building expected to be used evenly for 40 years
   B. A delivery vehicle that loses most of its value early and needs more repairs as it ages
   C. Freehold land
   D. A 5-year office lease depreciated evenly
6. An asset with a carrying amount of £3,500 is sold for £4,200. Calculate the profit or loss on disposal and state where it is reported. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 months used by year end = 2 (Nov-Dec); A1 expense = 4,800 x 2/3 = £3,200; M1 months prepaid = 1 (January); A1 prepayment = 4,800 x 1/3 = £1,600.
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [4] M1 Year 1 charge = 24,000 x 0.20 = £4,800; A1 carrying amount end Year 1 = £19,200; M1 Year 2 charge = 19,200 x 0.20 = £3,840; A1 carrying amount end Year 2 = £15,360.
4. [3] M1 receivables after write-off = 30,000 - 2,000 = £28,000; M1 allowance = 28,000 x 0.03 = £840; A1 receivables shown = 28,000 - 840 = £27,160.
5. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
6. [2] B1 profit on disposal = 4,200 - 3,500 = £700; B1 reported as income (a gain) in the statement of profit or loss.
