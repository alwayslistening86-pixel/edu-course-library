# S07_Limited_Company_Accounts - Test: Limited company accounts

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Profit from operations is £84,000, finance costs are £6,000, and corporation tax is charged at 19% of profit before tax. Calculate profit for the year before tax and profit for the year after tax. [4 marks]
2. Where is a dividend paid to shareholders accounted for? Choose every correct option.
   A. As an expense in the statement of profit or loss, before profit from operations
   B. As an expense in the statement of profit or loss, after tax
   C. Deducted from retained earnings in the statement of changes in equity
   D. Added to share capital
3. Retained earnings brought forward are £55,000. Profit for the year after tax is £32,000 and a dividend of £12,000 is paid during the year. Calculate retained earnings carried forward. [3 marks]
4. Which is a current liability in a company's statement of financial position? Choose every correct option.
   A. A bank loan repayable in 5 years
   B. Corporation tax payable
   C. Share capital
   D. A non-current asset held for sale in 3 years
5. A company issues 20,000 new £1 ordinary shares at £1.60 each, all received in cash. State the amount credited to share capital and the amount credited to share premium. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 profit before tax = 84,000 - 6,000 = £78,000; M1 tax = 78,000 x 0.19 = £14,820; A1 profit after tax = 78,000 - 14,820 = £63,180; A1 correct order of deduction (finance costs before tax, tax after).
2. Correct: C (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] M1 55,000 + 32,000; M1 - 12,000; A1 retained earnings carried forward = £75,000.
4. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
5. [3] M1 share capital credited = 20,000 x £1 (nominal value) = £20,000; A1 correct; B1 share premium credited = 20,000 x (1.60 - 1) = £12,000 (the excess of proceeds over nominal value).
