# S12_Absorption_and_ABC_Costing - Test: Absorption costing and activity based costing

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in AQA's style: short calculations with mark schemes (M = method, A = accuracy, B = independent/bookwork mark), multiple-choice items, and short written/evaluative questions. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Budgeted overheads are £150,000 and budgeted machine hours are 25,000. A product needs 4 machine hours per unit, direct materials cost £18/unit and direct labour costs £10/unit. Calculate the overhead absorbed per unit and the total absorption cost per unit. [5 marks]
2. Under ABC, a cost driver is: Choose every correct option.
   A. Any fixed cost in the business
   B. The factor that causes a particular overhead cost to be incurred
   C. The total number of units produced
   D. Another name for direct labour hours
3. A quality-inspection cost pool totals £45,000 for 900 inspections in the period. A batch of 50 units requires 6 inspections. Calculate the inspection cost per unit for this batch under ABC. [3 marks]
4. State one benefit and one limitation of activity based costing compared with traditional absorption costing. [2 marks]
5. A product's total absorption cost is £64/unit. The business requires a 25% markup on cost. Calculate the selling price. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 OAR = 150,000/25,000 = £6/machine hour; A1 overhead absorbed = 4 x 6 = £24/unit; M1 total cost = 18 + 10 + 24; A1 = £52/unit; B1 correct method (OAR calculated before it is applied to the product).
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] M1 cost per inspection = 45,000/900 = £50; M1 inspection cost for the batch = 6 x 50 = £300; A1 cost per unit = 300/50 = £6.00.
4. [2] B1 benefit: ABC gives more accurate product costs where overheads are driven by activity/complexity rather than simple volume, which can improve pricing and profitability decisions; B1 limitation: ABC requires far more data collection and analysis to identify cost pools and drivers, making it more costly and time-consuming to operate.
5. [2] M1 64 x 1.25; A1 selling price = £80.00.
