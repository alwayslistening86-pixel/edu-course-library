## Test items
1. A production manager sees an adverse material usage variance for the month. Give two possible causes other than careless or wasteful use of material by the production team. [4 marks]
2. Which is a genuine limitation of external evaluation compared with internal evaluation? Choose every correct option.
   A. External evaluation can see detailed management information the internal manager cannot
   B. External evaluation relies only on what is published, which is less detailed than internal management information
   C. External evaluation is not affected by differing accounting policies between businesses
   D. External evaluation always uses more up-to-date information
3. Explain why comparing a business's current-year ROCE with last year's ROCE (internal, trend-based evaluation) might give a fuller picture than comparing it with a single competitor's ROCE for the same year (external evaluation). [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B2 the material purchased may have been of lower quality than standard (perhaps due to a favourable price variance from buying cheaper material), causing more wastage; or the standard itself may be out of date/unrealistic, e.g. it does not reflect a recent, unavoidable change in the production process; B2 machinery may be faulty or poorly maintained, causing more scrap than expected, or an unusually large one-off spoilage event occurred (any two plausible, distinct causes, 2 marks each).
2. Correct: B (exactly these options, no others)
3. [3] B3 the business's own accounting policies are consistent year to year (assuming no policy change), so a trend comparison avoids the comparability problem that arises when comparing against a competitor who may use different depreciation methods, inventory valuation or other policies; a trend also shows direction of travel (improving or worsening) which a single competitor snapshot does not.
