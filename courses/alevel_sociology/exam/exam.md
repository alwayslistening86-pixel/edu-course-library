# A-level Sociology (AQA 7192): Education, Families and Households, Beliefs in Society, and Crime and Deviance, with Theory and Methods - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Paper 1 covers Education (S01-S05), Methods in Context (S06) and Theory and Methods (S07-S11, examined here at the 10-mark, no-Item tariff). Paper 2 covers Families and Households (S12-S14) and Beliefs in Society (S15-S17), each examined as a self-contained 40-mark topic (10+10+20). Paper 3 covers Crime and Deviance (S18-S23) and Theory and Methods again (S07-S11's content, examined here at the 30-mark, 10+20 tariff). Questions are original, in AQA's style, at real tariffs; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage-test items, and always write a genuine, specific Item (a short descriptive scenario or research summary, 2-4 sentences) for every question that requires 'applying material from the Item'. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest to fill each paper to its real 80-mark structure.
