# S11_Modernity_Postmodernity_Value_Freedom_and_Policy - Practice: Modernity/postmodernity, value freedom, and theory/methods and social policy

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two features of postmodernist views of society. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by value freedom in sociological research. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 the rejection of 'grand narratives'/single totalising theories (e.g. Marxism, functionalism) in favour of fragmented, competing perspectives, developed; B2 fluid, media/consumption-constructed identity rather than identity fixed by class or occupation, developed.
2. [3] B1 the idea that a sociologist's research and conclusions should be objective, separate from their personal political/moral values; B1 associated with positivism and (for choice of topic only) Weber; B1 critics (e.g. Gouldner) argue it is impossible since values inevitably shape topic choice, theory, method and interpretation.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
