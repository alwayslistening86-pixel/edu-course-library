# S12_Family_Perspectives_and_Changing_Patterns - Practice: Family perspectives, and changing patterns of marriage/cohabitation/divorce/family diversity

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two functions of the family according to Murdock. [4 marks]
2. Explain the Marxist feminist view of the role of the family under capitalism. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 sexual function: regulating sexual relationships between adults, developed; B2 (or) reproductive function (producing the next generation), economic function (providing materially for members), or educational function (primary socialisation into society's norms/values) -- credit any two, each developed.
2. [3] B1 women's unpaid domestic labour reproduces and maintains the workforce at no cost to employers; B1 the family also absorbs/socialises the next generation of workers, again at no cost to capital; B1 women's oppression in the family is therefore explained as serving capitalism's interests specifically, not men's interests alone (contrasted with radical feminism).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
