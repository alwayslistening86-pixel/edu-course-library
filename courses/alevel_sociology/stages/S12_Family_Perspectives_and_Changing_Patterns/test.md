## Test items
1. Outline three reasons for the rise in cohabitation in the UK since the mid-20th century. [6 marks]
2. [Paper 2, Section A style] Applying material from the Item below and your knowledge, analyse two ways in which the family may benefit capitalism, according to Marxists.

Item: Some sociologists argue the family absorbs and conceals the frustrations produced by alienating, low-control work, while also raising and maintaining the next generation of workers at no direct cost to employers. [10 marks]
