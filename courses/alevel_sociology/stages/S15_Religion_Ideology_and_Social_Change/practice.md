# S15_Religion_Ideology_and_Social_Change - Practice: Ideology, science and religion; religion and social change/stability

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two functions of religion according to functionalists. [4 marks]
2. Explain Weber's argument in The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 promoting social integration/a shared sense of belonging through collective ritual and symbols (Durkheim), developed; B2 reinforcing value consensus/the collective conscience by representing society itself through the sacred (Durkheim's totemism study), developed.
2. [3] B1 Calvinist beliefs (predestination, worldly success as a possible sign of being one of the elect) combined with an ascetic, frugal lifestyle; B1 created a psychological drive to work hard and reinvest profit rather than spend it; B1 Weber argued this contributed causally to the rise of modern rational capitalism, showing religion can drive social change, not just reflect existing stability.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
