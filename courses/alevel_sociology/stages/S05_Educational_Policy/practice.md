# S05_Educational_Policy - Practice: Selection, marketisation and privatisation; policy for equality and globalisation

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two features of marketisation in education. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by the Pupil Premium. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 parental choice of school/open enrolment, developed; B2 league tables of exam results and Ofsted inspection driving competition between schools, developed.
2. [3] B1 extra government funding attached to pupils eligible for free school meals (or otherwise identified as disadvantaged); B1 intended to be spent by schools on closing the achievement gap for those pupils; B1 an example of a compensatory-education policy responding to material/cultural deprivation explanations (S02).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
