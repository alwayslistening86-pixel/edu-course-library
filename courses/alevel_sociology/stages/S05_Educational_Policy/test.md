1. Outline three criticisms of marketisation policies in education. [6 marks]

Item: Since 1988, English state schools have competed for pupils partly on the basis of published exam results and Ofsted judgements, with successful schools often attracting applications from families able to move house, arrange transport or otherwise navigate the admissions process. [30 marks]
