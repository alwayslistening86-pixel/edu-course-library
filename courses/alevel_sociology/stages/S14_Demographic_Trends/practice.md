# S14_Demographic_Trends - Practice: UK demographic trends since 1900 and their effects on family life

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two reasons for the long-term fall in the UK's birth rate since 1900. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by an ageing population. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 greater availability/effectiveness of contraception giving women reproductive choice, developed; B2 women's rising educational/career opportunities raising the cost of childbearing and delaying/reducing family size, developed.
2. [3] B1 a rising proportion of the population aged 65 and over relative to younger age groups; B1 caused by the combination of a falling birth rate and rising life expectancy; B1 raises policy/social concerns such as pension costs, health and social care demand, and a shrinking working-age population relative to those it supports.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
