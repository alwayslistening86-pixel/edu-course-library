# S01_Perspectives_on_the_Role_of_Education - Practice: Functionalist, Marxist and New Right perspectives on education

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two functions of education according to functionalists. [4 marks]
2. Outline two ways in which Marxists argue education serves the interests of capitalism. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 social solidarity/value consensus: education creates a shared sense of belonging and common values (Durkheim), developed with an example (e.g. shared subjects/rituals); B2 role allocation/skills for the economy: education sorts pupils by ability into appropriate roles and teaches the specialist skills the division of labour needs (Durkheim/Davis and Moore), developed with an example.
2. [4] B2 the correspondence principle (Bowles and Gintis): the social relationships of school mirror those of the capitalist workplace (hierarchy, external control, alienation), preparing pupils to accept exploitation, developed; B2 the myth of meritocracy: education legitimates class inequality by presenting unequal outcomes as fairly earned through ability and effort, developed.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
