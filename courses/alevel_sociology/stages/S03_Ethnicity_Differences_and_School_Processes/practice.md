# S03_Ethnicity_Differences_and_School_Processes - Practice: Ethnicity differences in achievement, and teacher/pupil relationships and labelling

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two internal (in-school) factors that may explain ethnic differences in educational achievement. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by a self-fulfilling prophecy in education. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 teacher labelling/stereotyping and disproportionate discipline or exclusion (e.g. Gillborn and Youdell), developed; B2 an ethnocentric curriculum failing to reflect some minority ethnic pupils' culture/heritage, or institutional practices like the 'A-to-C economy' (Gillborn), developed.
2. [3] B1 a teacher labels a pupil (e.g. as bright or as a troublemaker) based on an initial judgement; B1 the label shapes the teacher's behaviour toward the pupil (e.g. more encouragement, or lower expectations/harsher discipline); B1 the pupil internalises this treatment, changing their own self-image/effort so the original label becomes true in practice.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
