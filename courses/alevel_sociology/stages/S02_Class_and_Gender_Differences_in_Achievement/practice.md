# S02_Class_and_Gender_Differences_in_Achievement - Practice: Class and gender differences in educational achievement

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two ways in which material deprivation might affect a pupil's educational achievement. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by Bourdieu's concept of cultural capital. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 poverty limiting resources for study (e.g. no computer/internet access, no quiet study space, cannot afford trips or equipment), developed; B2 financial pressure leading to part-time work or early exit from education/reduced attendance, developed.
2. [3] B1 the knowledge, tastes, ways of speaking and attitudes that middle-class families pass on to their children; B1 schools value and reward this cultural capital (it matches the dominant, middle-class culture of education); B1 pupils without it are disadvantaged even with equal ability, converting cultural into educational (and later economic) capital/inequality.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
