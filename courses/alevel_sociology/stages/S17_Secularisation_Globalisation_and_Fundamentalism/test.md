## Test items
1. Outline two criticisms of the secularisation thesis. [4 marks]
2. [Paper 2, Section B style] Applying material from the Item below and your knowledge, evaluate the view that religious fundamentalism is best understood as a modern reaction to secularisation and globalisation, rather than a simple survival of tradition.

Item: Fundamentalist movements often make active use of modern media and communications technology to spread their message and organise support, while explicitly rejecting secular-liberal social changes they see as morally corrosive. [20 marks]

1. [4] B2 'believing without belonging' (Davie): declining attendance does not necessarily mean declining belief, developed; B2 the continuing high religiosity of the USA despite being a highly modern, industrialised society challenges the equation of modernity with secularisation, developed.
