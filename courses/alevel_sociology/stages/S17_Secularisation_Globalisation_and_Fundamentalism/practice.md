# S17_Secularisation_Globalisation_and_Fundamentalism - Practice: The secularisation debate, globalisation and religion, and fundamentalism

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two pieces of evidence often used to support the secularisation thesis. [4 marks]
2. Explain what Davie meant by 'believing without belonging'. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 declining church attendance/membership over the long term in the UK and much of Western Europe, developed; B2 religion's declining influence over law, politics, education and everyday life as these become separate, secular spheres (differentiation), developed.
2. [3] B1 formal religious institutional participation (e.g. regular church attendance/membership) has declined; B1 while private religious/spiritual belief has remained comparatively widespread; B1 suggests secularisation may reflect a change in the form religiosity takes, not a straightforward overall decline in religious belief.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
