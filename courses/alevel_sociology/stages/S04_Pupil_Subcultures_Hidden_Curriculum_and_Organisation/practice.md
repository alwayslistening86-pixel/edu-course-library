# S04_Pupil_Subcultures_Hidden_Curriculum_and_Organisation - Practice: Pupil subcultures, the hidden curriculum, and streaming/setting

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two features of an anti-school pupil subculture. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by the hidden curriculum. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 rejecting the school's definition of success/academic values (e.g. mocking conformist pupils, as in Willis's 'ear'oles'), developed; B2 gaining status among peers through behaviour the school punishes (e.g. truanting, messing about, defiance of teachers), developed.
2. [3] B1 the implicit values/behaviours school teaches through its structure and routines, not through explicit lesson content; B1 example: hierarchy/deference to authority, or competitiveness, or gendered assumptions, correctly identified; B1 Marxists link it to preparing pupils (especially working-class pupils) for the discipline of the capitalist workplace (correspondence principle).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
