## Test items
1. Outline two reasons why women may participate in religion at higher rates than men. [4 marks]
2. [Paper 2, Section B style] Applying material from the Item below and your knowledge, analyse two reasons why minority ethnic groups in the UK may show higher rates of religious participation than the White British population on average.

Item: Sociologists studying religious practice among first-generation migrant communities in the UK have highlighted both the cultural/community role religion can play after migration, and demographic differences between minority ethnic and White British populations. [10 marks]
