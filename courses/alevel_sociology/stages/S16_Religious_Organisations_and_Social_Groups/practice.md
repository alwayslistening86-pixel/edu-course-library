# S16_Religious_Organisations_and_Social_Groups - Practice: Religious organisations, and religion by class/gender/ethnicity/age

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two differences between a church and a sect, using Troeltsch's typology. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by a world-affirming new religious movement, using Wallis's typology. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 a church has large, inclusive membership open to all of society and a close relationship with the state, whereas a sect has smaller, exclusive membership demanding genuine commitment, developed; B2 a church is typically conservative/legitimates the existing social order, whereas a sect is typically in tension with or rejects wider society, developed.
2. [3] B1 a movement that accepts mainstream society rather than rejecting or merely tolerating it; B1 typically offers techniques/practices for personal success, fulfilment or self-improvement within ordinary life, functioning almost like a service; B1 tends to attract more affluent members seeking personal benefit rather than compensation for disadvantage, contrasted with world-rejecting movements.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
