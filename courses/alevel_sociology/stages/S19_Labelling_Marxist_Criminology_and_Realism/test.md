## Test items
1. Outline two ways in which media/social reaction can amplify deviance. [4 marks]
2. [Paper 3, Section B style, with item] Applying material from the Item below and your knowledge, evaluate the Marxist view that the law is enforced in the interests of the ruling class.

Item: Critics of the criminal justice system point out that street crimes committed disproportionately by poorer offenders receive heavy police and media attention, while corporate and white-collar offences, which can cause far greater financial and physical harm, are comparatively under-policed and under-reported. [20 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B2 exaggerated, sensationalised reporting constructs a group as 'folk devils' (moral panic, Cohen), driving demands for tougher control, developed; B2 the resulting stronger social reaction/labelling pushes the group further from mainstream society, e.g. into a more organised subculture, ironically increasing the deviant behaviour reacted against (deviancy amplification spiral), developed.
