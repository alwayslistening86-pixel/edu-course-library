# S06_Methods_in_Context - Practice: Applying research methods specifically to studying education

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full, developed answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Outline two practical issues a sociologist might face researching pupils in a school. [4 marks]
2. Explain why the power difference between an adult researcher and child pupils can affect the validity of interview data in educational research. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 gaining access via gatekeepers (head teacher/parents), who may restrict topics or refuse access, developed; B2 the constraints of the school timetable/exam schedule limiting when and how research can be carried out, developed.
2. [3] B1 pupils may see the researcher as similar to a teacher/authority figure; B1 this can lead pupils to give answers they think the researcher wants to hear rather than honest views (reduced validity); B1 example: a pupil under-reporting misbehaviour or true feelings about a teacher in a formal interview.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed, especially explicit use of a given Item where the question requires it.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
