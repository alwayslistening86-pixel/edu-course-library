## Test items
1. Outline two ethical issues a sociologist should consider when researching bullying among pupils. [4 marks]
2. [Paper 1, Section B style] Applying material from the Item below and your knowledge, evaluate the strengths and limitations of using participant observation to investigate pupils' experience of streaming.

Item: A sociologist wanting to understand how pupils in low streams experience school day to day might consider spending time in lessons as an observer, getting to know pupils informally over several weeks, rather than relying only on interviews or questionnaires. [20 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B2 informed consent is complicated by pupils' status as minors (needing parental/gatekeeper consent as well as, ideally, pupil assent), developed; B2 protection from harm/confidentiality -- researching bullying risks distressing victims or exposing pupils named as bullies to consequences, developed.
2. [20] Mark out of 20 using AQA's levels of response for a 20-mark 'Applying material from the Item and your knowledge, evaluate ...' question (AO1 knowledge + AO2 application of the item + AO3 analysis and evaluation): Level 4 (16-20) sustained, explicit application of the item's material throughout; accurate, conceptually secure and reasonably wide-ranging knowledge; sustained analysis and evaluation (e.g. weighing strengths against weaknesses, comparing perspectives/studies) reaching a supported conclusion; clear, coherent, well-organised answer using sociological terminology accurately. Level 3 (11-15) mostly effective use of the item; generally accurate knowledge with reasonable range; evaluation is present and mostly effective but not fully sustained or balanced; reasonably organised answer. Level 2 (6-10) limited or generic use of the item (not tied to its specific detail); knowledge is evident but narrow, with some inaccuracy, and answers tend toward description rather than evaluation; evaluation, where present, is superficial, asserted rather than argued, or one-sided. Level 1 (1-5) little or no effective use of the item; knowledge is very limited, muddled or largely irrelevant; little or no evaluation. 0 marks: no relevant content. Credit method-in-context issues: gatekeeper access/permission to be present in lessons; the school setting potentially reducing or the classroom's normal surveillance culture potentially increasing the observer effect; ethical issues of covert versus overt observation with children; the time/practical cost against the validity gained from seeing pupils' real behaviour and interactions in low-stream lessons rather than relying on what they say in a formal interview; balanced judgement referencing the item's issue (streaming) specifically.
