# S11_Power_and_Conflict_Poetry_I_Overview_and_Four_Founding_Poems - Practice: The Power and Conflict cluster overview, and four poems on power, control and nature's force

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain what 'power' means in each of these four poems, in one sentence per poem.
2. Compare Ozymandias and London on how each presents power as ultimately fragile or oppressive.
3. Explain how the dramatic monologue form in My Last Duchess lets Browning expose the Duke without the Duke realising it.
4. Explain what the mountain in the Prelude extract might represent beyond a literal geographical feature.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
