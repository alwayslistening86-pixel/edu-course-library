# S07_A_Christmas_Carol_Staves_4_to_5_Structure_and_Language - Practice: A Christmas Carol: Staves 4-5, the novella's structure, and Dickens's narrative voice and language techniques

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why Dickens withholds speech from the Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come and what effect this has.
2. Explain why Tiny Tim's mourned death is placed alongside Scrooge's unmourned one within the same stave.
3. List three concrete actions Scrooge takes in Stave 5 that prove his transformation, rather than just stating he changed.
4. Identify one moment of narrative intrusion or irony and explain its effect on the reader's judgement of a character.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
