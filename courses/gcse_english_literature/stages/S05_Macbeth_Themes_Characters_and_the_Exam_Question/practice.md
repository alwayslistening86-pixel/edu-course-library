# S05_Macbeth_Themes_Characters_and_the_Exam_Question - Practice: Macbeth: themes of ambition, kingship and guilt, the play's key characters, and the Paper 1 extract question

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write one sentence tracing Macbeth's change from 'brave' soldier to feared tyrant, with a brief supporting reference.
2. Explain how Banquo's choice not to act on the witches' prophecy comments on Macbeth's choice to act on his.
3. Explain the significance of Macduff being 'not born' in the natural way to the play's ideas about fate versus choice.
4. Write the opening two sentences of an essay on 'How does Shakespeare present guilt in Macbeth?', analysing a short invented extract line of your choice.
5. List three pieces of evidence (brief quotation or close paraphrase) you would use for an essay on ambition, one from each act-group studied (1-3, 4, 5).

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
