# S04_Macbeth_Acts_4_to_5_Structure_and_Language - Practice: Macbeth: the plot of Acts 4-5, the play's dramatic structure, and its patterns of imagery

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Narrate Act 4's central atrocity and explain why it is a turning point in how the audience sees Macbeth.
2. Explain how each of the three final prophecies is fulfilled in a way Macbeth did not expect.
3. Sketch the five-act tragic arc as a simple diagram, labelling each act's function.
4. Trace one image pattern (blood, darkness or sleep) through three different moments in the play and explain how its meaning changes.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
