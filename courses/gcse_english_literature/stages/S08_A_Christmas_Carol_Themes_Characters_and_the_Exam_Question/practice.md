# S08_A_Christmas_Carol_Themes_Characters_and_the_Exam_Question - Practice: A Christmas Carol: themes of greed, poverty and family, the novella's key characters, and the Paper 1 extract question

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write one sentence explaining why Scrooge's greed being learned (not innate) matters to the novella's argument about redemption.
2. Explain how the Cratchits function both realistically and symbolically within the novella's social message.
3. Explain why the three spirits are structured to escalate from gentle to frightening.
4. Write the opening two sentences of an essay on 'How does Dickens present poverty in A Christmas Carol?', analysing a short invented extract line of your choice.
5. List three pieces of evidence (brief quotation or close paraphrase) you would use for an essay on redemption, one from each stave-group studied (1-3, 4, 5).

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
