# S06_A_Christmas_Carol_Overview_Context_and_Staves_1_to_3 - Practice: A Christmas Carol: whole-novella overview, Victorian context, and Staves 1-3

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why Dickens structured the novella in 'staves' rather than chapters, and what that word choice suggests about the book's purpose.
2. Explain how the New Poor Law and workhouse system connect to Scrooge's comment early in Stave 1.
3. Explain why the Ghost of Christmas Past shows Scrooge scenes from his own youth rather than other people's lives.
4. Explain the significance of Ignorance and Want as a warning within Stave 3.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
