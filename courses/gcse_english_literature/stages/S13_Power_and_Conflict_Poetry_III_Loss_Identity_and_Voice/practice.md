# S13_Power_and_Conflict_Poetry_III_Loss_Identity_and_Voice - Practice: Six poems on loss, identity and voice: Poppies, War Photographer, Tissue, The Emigree, Checking Out Me History and Kamikaze

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Compare Poppies and Kamikaze on how each presents a family member's experience of someone else going to, or returning from, conflict.
2. Explain what the paper/tissue metaphor suggests about power in Dharker's poem.
3. Explain why The Emigree's speaker's memory might be described as 'possibly imagined' and why that matters to the poem's meaning.
4. Explain how the mixed dialect and standard English in Checking Out Me History supports its argument.
5. Explain why War Photographer contrasts the calm darkroom with the horror of the photographs.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
