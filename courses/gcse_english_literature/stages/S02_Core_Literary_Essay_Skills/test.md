# S02_Core_Literary_Essay_Skills - Test: The transferable skills every literature essay needs: quotation, language analysis, structure analysis, context, comparison, planning and accuracy

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Given the invented short extract: 'The gates hung open, rusted and useless, and no one came.' Write one full analytical paragraph (60-100 words) that names at least one device, explains its effect, and is planned rather than written straight off.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S02_Core_Literary_Essay_Skills` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S03_Macbeth_Overview_Context_and_Acts_1_to_3.
