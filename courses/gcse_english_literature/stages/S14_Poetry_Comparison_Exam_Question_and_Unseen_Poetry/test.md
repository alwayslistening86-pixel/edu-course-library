# S14_Poetry_Comparison_Exam_Question_and_Unseen_Poetry - Test: Writing the Paper 2 poetry comparison question, and approaching unseen poetry alone and in comparison

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
The examiner prints 'Ozymandias' and asks you to compare it with one other poem from the Power and Conflict anthology of your choice, on how each poem presents the power of nature or time over human ambition. Write a full response (200-300 words), respecting each poem's copyright status in how much you quote.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S14_Poetry_Comparison_Exam_Question_and_Unseen_Poetry` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass. This was the last stage: the cumulative exam is now unlocked once every stage test is passed.
