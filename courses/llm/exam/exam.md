# LLM (Master of Laws) — University of London - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.
## Format
UoL's real assessment format examines each of the sixteen sub-modules (four per chosen module) separately by a 45-minute unseen closed-book exam, with no coursework component. This cumulative paper adapts that format to one sitting: four questions, one drawn from each of the four chosen modules, each requiring the learner to answer on two of that module's four sub-modules (the examiner's/learner's choice), always including at least one explicit critical-evaluation element (not pure description) per question. All scenarios are original; no real UoL past-paper question is used.
## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly.
## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete.
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the module/sub-module(s) that broke down, offer targeted review of the specific sub-module(s) and a fresh retry paper with new original scenarios.
