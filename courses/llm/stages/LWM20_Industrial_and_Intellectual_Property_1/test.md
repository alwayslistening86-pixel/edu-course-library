# LWM20_Industrial_and_Intellectual_Property_1 - Test: Industrial and Intellectual Property A-B — Patents, trade secrets and copyright

## How to run this

## Test scenario
A UK software company develops a machine-learning model that optimises delivery routes, implemented as a specific sequence of claimed method steps run on standard hardware, and seeks patent protection while also keeping its training methodology as a closely-guarded trade secret. Separately, a journalist quotes six short passages (each under 30 words) from the company's internal technical white paper, without permission, in a critical exposé of the company's practices. Advise on (a) the patentability of the routing method under the Aerotel/Macrossan test, (b) the strategic patent-versus-trade-secret choice for the training methodology, and (c) whether the white paper quotations infringe copyright or fall within a fair dealing defence.
