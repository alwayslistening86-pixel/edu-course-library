# LWM11_European_Union_Competition_Law_1 - Practice: EU Competition Law A-B — Anti-competitive agreements and abuse of dominance

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply the object/effect distinction to a horizontal agreement between competitors to exchange future pricing intentions, without any explicit price-fixing agreement.
2. Apply the Vertical Block Exemption Regulation logic to a manufacturer-distributor agreement restricting the distributor from selling outside an assigned territory.
3. Apply the Hoffmann-La Roche dominance factors to a firm with 55% market share facing low barriers to entry and a fast-growing new competitor.
4. Apply the as-efficient-competitor test to a dominant firm's loyalty rebate scheme, using Intel's evidential approach.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
