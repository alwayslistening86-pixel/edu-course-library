## Test scenario
A dominant supplier of specialist industrial components, holding 62% of the relevant market, offers large customers a rebate scheme where the rebate rate increases sharply once a customer's purchases exceed 80% of its total requirements from that supplier, with no comparable scheme available to smaller customers. A smaller rival, capable of matching the supplier's costs, complains it cannot compete for large customers once the rebate threshold effect takes hold. Separately, three mid-sized competitors in an unrelated market are found to have exchanged detailed, non-public future pricing plans in a series of meetings, though no explicit agreement to fix prices was reached. Advise on (a) whether the rebate scheme is likely to infringe Article 102, applying the as-efficient-competitor test, and (b) whether the pricing information exchange infringes Article 101, applying the object/effect distinction and Cartes Bancaires.
