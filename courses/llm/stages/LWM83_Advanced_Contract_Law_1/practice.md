# LWM83_Advanced_Contract_Law_1 - Practice: Advanced Contract Law A — Contested doctrines in contract performance and breach

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Evaluate whether a long-term IT outsourcing agreement is a "relational contract" capable of an implied good faith term, applying Yam Seng and Candey.
2. Apply the Cavendish legitimate-interest test to a shareholders' agreement clause requiring a departing employee-shareholder to forfeit unvested shares at no value.
3. Compare how Hadley v Baxendale and The Achilleas would each treat a claim for lost sub-charter profit following late redelivery of a chartered vessel.
4. Evaluate whether a supplier facing a genuine, unforeseen raw-material shortage is better advised to argue frustration or to rely on a force majeure clause.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
