## Test scenario
A specialist components manufacturer supplies a long-term distributor under a 10-year agreement with no express good faith clause. The manufacturer begins favouring a new, larger distributor in a way that starves the original distributor of stock, without breaching any express term. The agreement also contains a clause requiring the distributor to pay a fixed £250,000 "exit fee" if it terminates early, which the manufacturer says reflects its investment in the relationship. Advise the distributor on (a) whether an implied duty of good faith might be argued, applying Yam Seng, Bates v Post Office and Candey, and (b) whether the exit fee is a valid liquidated damages clause or a penalty, applying Cavendish/ParkingEye.
