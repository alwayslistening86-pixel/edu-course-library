# LWM20_Industrial_and_Intellectual_Property_2 - Practice: Industrial and Intellectual Property C-D — Trade marks, passing off and integrated theory

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply the absolute and relative grounds for refusal to an application to register the word "CRISP" for potato crisps, and a separate mark identical to an existing registered competitor mark.
2. Apply the average consumer test to decide whether a discount supermarket's own-brand packaging, visually similar but differently named, infringes a well-known snack brand's registered mark.
3. Apply the Jif Lemon trinity to a case where a well-known chef's name and image are used without permission on an unrelated food product's packaging.
4. For any two doctrines studied across this module, identify which theoretical justification for IP (utilitarian, natural rights, personality, or social planning) best explains the rule, and where the justifications would pull in different directions.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
