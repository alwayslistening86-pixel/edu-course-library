## Test scenario
A small UK bakery registers the word mark "GOLDEN CRUST" for bread, and later discovers a much larger regional chain has begun using near-identical packaging, colours and a phonetically similar name "GOLDENE CRUST" for its own bread range, without registering any mark. Separately, the bakery's founder, well known locally, discovers a rival business using her name and photograph, without permission, to imply her endorsement of their (unrelated) product. Advise the bakery on (a) trade mark infringement against the chain, (b) any passing off claim, and (c) the founder's personal claim regarding her name and image, and identify which theoretical justification for IP best supports extending protection to each claim.
