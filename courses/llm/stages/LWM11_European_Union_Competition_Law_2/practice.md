# LWM11_European_Union_Competition_Law_2 - Practice: EU Competition Law C-D — Merger control and enforcement procedure

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply the SIEC test to a proposed merger between the second and third largest firms in an oligopolistic market, where no single firm would reach a 40% market share post-merger.
2. Explain why a merger falling just below the EUMR's turnover thresholds might still require separate notifications in three different Member States plus the UK CMA.
3. Explain the practical compliance consequence for a business of the shift from prior notification to self-assessment under Regulation 1/2003.
4. Evaluate whether a cartel victim is better served bringing a follow-on damages claim in a national court that already has a binding Commission infringement decision to rely on, compared with a stand-alone claim.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
