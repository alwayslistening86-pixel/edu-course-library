## Test scenario
Two mid-sized European manufacturers, both above the EUMR's notification thresholds, propose a merger that would give the combined entity 38% of the relevant EU market, with the next largest competitor holding 22%. A smaller rival argues the merger would allow unilateral price increases even without any collusion. Separately, following a Commission decision finding a cartel among unrelated suppliers in the same broader industry, several customers who purchased from the cartel members (but were not parties to the Commission proceedings) wish to bring follow-on damages claims in the English courts. Advise on (a) how the merger would be assessed under the SIEC test and the one-stop-shop procedure, and (b) how the Antitrust Damages Directive framework assists the follow-on claimants, including the evidential effect of the Commission's decision.
