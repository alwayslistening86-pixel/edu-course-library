## Test scenario
A commercial agent sells a manufacturer's goods across Northern Europe under an oral agreement with no fixed term. After 6 years, the manufacturer terminates by email with one week's notice, saying market conditions have changed. Separately, the same manufacturer had earlier sold a batch of goods to a buyer who, unknown to the manufacturer, only had a voidable title in raw materials used to make them; a mercantile agent later sold the goods on to an innocent purchaser. Finally, the manufacturer's business insurance policy is subject to a dispute after a warehouse fire, where the manufacturer failed to mention a prior, unrelated minor fire at a different site when taking out the policy. Advise on (a) the agent's termination rights under the 1993 Regulations, (b) the innocent purchaser's title, and (c) the effect of the non-disclosure under the Insurance Act 2015.
