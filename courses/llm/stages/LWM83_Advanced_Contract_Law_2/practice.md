# LWM83_Advanced_Contract_Law_2 - Practice: Advanced Contract Law B-D — Agency, sale of goods and insurance

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply Freeman & Lockyer to a company employee who signs a supply contract outside their actual authority but consistent with a role the company allowed them to hold out publicly.
2. Apply the Commercial Agents Regulations 1993 to a manufacturer that terminates a commercial agent without notice or compensation after 8 years.
3. Apply s.15A SGA 1979 to a buyer seeking to reject a large commercial shipment for a minor labelling defect affecting 2% of units.
4. Evaluate whether the Insurance Act 2015's proportionate-remedies reform strikes a better balance than the old all-or-nothing avoidance rule, using a worked non-disclosure example.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
