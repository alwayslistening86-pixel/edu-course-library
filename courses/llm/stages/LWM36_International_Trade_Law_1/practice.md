# LWM36_International_Trade_Law_1 - Practice: International Trade Law A-B — Export sale terms and carriage of goods by sea

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Compare how a total loss of goods at sea would be resolved under a CIF contract versus a contract silent on trade terms governed only by default SGA rules.
2. Apply the Hague-Visby package limitation to a claim for a damaged container where the actual loss significantly exceeds the calculated limitation figure.
3. Explain each of the bill of lading's three functions using a single worked shipment from a UK exporter to an overseas buyer who resells the goods in transit.
4. Evaluate whether container shipping and electronic bills of lading have undermined the practical justification for the CIF documents-not-goods rule.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
