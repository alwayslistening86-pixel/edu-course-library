## Test scenario
A UK exporter agrees to sell 1,000 tonnes of machinery parts CIF Rotterdam to a Dutch buyer. The exporter ships the goods, obtains a conforming bill of lading and insurance policy, and tenders the documents; the buyer pays against the documents. During the voyage, the vessel encounters a storm and a portion of the cargo is damaged due to inadequate stowage by the carrier. The buyer, having taken up the documents, later discovers the damage and seeks to recover the full commercial value of the damaged goods from the carrier. Advise on (a) whether the exporter has performed its CIF obligations despite the damage, (b) the buyer's rights against the carrier under the Hague-Visby Rules, including any limitation on recovery, and (c) how the buyer acquired the right to sue the carrier given it was not the original shipper.
