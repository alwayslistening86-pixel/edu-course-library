# S16_Lagos_Challenges_and_Solutions - Practice: Challenges of rapid growth in Lagos, and urban planning solutions

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why Makoko developed as an informal settlement rather than through planned housing.
2. Explain one challenge Lagos faces in providing clean water and sanitation, and one consequence of that challenge.
3. Explain the link between unemployment, the informal economy and crime in Lagos.
4. Evaluate whether the Lagos BRT scheme is likely to improve quality of life for the urban poor.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
