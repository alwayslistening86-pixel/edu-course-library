# S19_Development_and_the_Development_Gap - Practice: Classifying development, measuring it, the Demographic Transition Model and closing the development gap

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give two economic and two social measures of development.
2. Explain one limitation of using GNI per head alone to measure development.
3. Explain what happens to birth rate, death rate and population at DTM Stage 2 and why.
4. Choose two strategies to reduce the development gap and explain how each could help a specific LIC.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
