# S06_Climate_Change - Practice: Evidence, causes and management of climate change

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain how ice cores provide evidence of past climate.
2. Sort these causes of climate change into natural or human: burning coal, volcanic eruption, deforestation, changes in the Earth's orbit.
3. Give one mitigation and one adaptation strategy, and explain the difference between them.
4. Explain one effect of climate change on people and one on the environment.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
