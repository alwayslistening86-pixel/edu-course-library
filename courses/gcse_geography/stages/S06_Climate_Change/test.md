# S06_Climate_Change - Test: Evidence, causes and management of climate change

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Name one type of evidence for past climate. b) State one natural and one human cause of climate change. c) Give one example of adaptation.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain the difference between mitigation and adaptation, and evaluate why a country might need to use both approaches rather than relying on just one.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S06_Climate_Change` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S07_Ecosystems_and_Rainforest_Characteristics.
