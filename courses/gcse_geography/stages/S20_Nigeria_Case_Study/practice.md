# S20_Nigeria_Case_Study - Practice: Jamaica's tourism and Nigeria: an NEE case study of changing industry, trade, aid and environment

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of tourism for Jamaica's development.
2. Locate Nigeria and give two reasons for its regional importance.
3. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of TNCs operating in Nigeria, using Shell as an example.
4. Evaluate whether Nigeria's oil wealth has improved quality of life for most Nigerians.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
