# S15_Urbanisation_and_Lagos_Growth - Practice: Global urbanisation trends and the growth of Lagos

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain, using push and pull factors, why someone might migrate from rural Nigeria to Lagos.
2. Explain the difference between rural-urban migration and natural increase as causes of urban growth.
3. Explain why megacities are increasingly located in LICs and NEEs rather than HICs.
4. Give one social and one economic opportunity that urban growth has brought to Lagos.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
