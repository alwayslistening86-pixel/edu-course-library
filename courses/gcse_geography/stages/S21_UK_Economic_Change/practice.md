# S21_UK_Economic_Change - Practice: UK economic change: deindustrialisation, the post-industrial economy, rural change, infrastructure and regional divides

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two causes of UK deindustrialisation.
2. Give one named example each of a UK rural growth area and a UK rural decline area, and explain the difference between them.
3. Explain how the Northern Powerhouse initiative aims to address the north-south divide.
4. Explain two ways the UK remains connected to the wider world.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
