# S18_Urban_Sprawl_Regeneration_and_Sustainability - Test: Urban sprawl, the Salford Quays regeneration project, and sustainable urban living

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Define urban sprawl. b) Name the media organisations that relocated to MediaCityUK. c) Give one feature of sustainable urban living.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain why Salford Quays needed regeneration and evaluate whether MediaCityUK represents a genuinely sustainable model of urban redevelopment.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S18_Urban_Sprawl_Regeneration_and_Sustainability` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
