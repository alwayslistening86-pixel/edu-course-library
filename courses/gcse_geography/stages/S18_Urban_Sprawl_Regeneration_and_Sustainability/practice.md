# S18_Urban_Sprawl_Regeneration_and_Sustainability - Practice: Urban sprawl, the Salford Quays regeneration project, and sustainable urban living

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain what urban sprawl is and one problem it causes for the rural-urban fringe.
2. Explain why Salford Quays fell derelict, and what regeneration changed there.
3. Give three features of sustainable urban living and explain how each helps.
4. Evaluate whether public transport improvements alone are likely to solve urban congestion.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
