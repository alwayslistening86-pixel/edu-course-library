# S07_Ecosystems_and_Rainforest_Characteristics - Practice: Ecosystems, global biome distribution, and the physical characteristics of tropical rainforests

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Draw a simple food web for a UK woodland with at least four species, including one producer, one herbivore and one decomposer.
2. Explain what might happen to a food web if one predator species was removed.
3. Label the four layers of a tropical rainforest and give one plant or animal adaptation for each.
4. Explain why rainforest soils are nutrient-poor despite the lush vegetation above them.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
