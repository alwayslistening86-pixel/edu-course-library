# S22_Resource_Management_and_UK_Water - Test: Resource significance, global inequality, and managing UK water supply

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Give one reason water is essential to wellbeing. b) State which part of the UK has a water surplus and which has a deficit. c) Name one UK reservoir that transfers water to a city.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain why the geographical mismatch between the UK’s water supply and demand makes transfer schemes necessary, and evaluate one advantage and one disadvantage of relying on transfer rather than reducing demand.'
