# S22_Resource_Management_and_UK_Water - Practice: Resource significance, global inequality, and managing UK water supply

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why water security matters for a country's development, using one example of what happens when it fails.
2. Explain why UK water demand has risen even though the UK's population growth has been relatively modest.
3. Explain, using rainfall and population, why the UK has a water deficit in the south and east.
4. Explain why water needs to be transferred from the Lake District to Manchester, naming the reservoirs involved.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
