# S23_Global_Water_Resource_Option - Practice: Global water resource management: patterns, insecurity and strategies, including Lesotho and Kenya

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two factors that make a region more likely to experience water deficit.
2. Explain two impacts of water insecurity, using specific reasoning for each.
3. Compare the Lesotho Highlands Water Project and Kenyan sand dams, explaining one advantage each has that the other lacks.
4. Evaluate whether large-scale transfer schemes like Lesotho's are a sustainable long-term solution to water insecurity.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
