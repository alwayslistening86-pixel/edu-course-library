# S23_Global_Water_Resource_Option - Test: Global water resource management: patterns, insecurity and strategies, including Lesotho and Kenya

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Name one factor affecting water availability. b) State one impact of water insecurity. c) Name the country the Lesotho Highlands Water Project supplies water to.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Evaluate the Lesotho Highlands Water Project and Kenyan sand dams as two contrasting strategies for managing water insecurity, reaching a supported judgement on which is more sustainable and why.'
