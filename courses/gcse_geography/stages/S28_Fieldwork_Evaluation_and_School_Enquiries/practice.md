# S28_Fieldwork_Evaluation_and_School_Enquiries - Practice: Evaluating fieldwork, plus the Cleveleys and Salford Quays enquiries in full

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Suggest one further piece of data that would strengthen the Cleveleys enquiry's conclusion, and explain why.
2. Judge how reliable a conclusion based on a single day's fieldwork at one site is likely to be, giving reasons.
3. Outline the question, one data collection method and the conclusion for the Cleveleys enquiry.
4. Outline the question, one data collection method and the conclusion for the Salford Quays enquiry.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
