# S28_Fieldwork_Evaluation_and_School_Enquiries - Test: Evaluating fieldwork, plus the Cleveleys and Salford Quays enquiries in full

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Suggest one piece of further data that could improve a fieldwork enquiry. b) Name the two fieldwork enquiries you have studied. c) State one limitation of fieldwork data collected on a single day.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'For an unfamiliar fieldwork context describing a one-day environmental quality survey with a small sample, evaluate the reliability of its conclusion and suggest further data that would improve it, drawing on what you learned evaluating the Cleveleys and Salford Quays enquiries.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S28_Fieldwork_Evaluation_and_School_Enquiries` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
