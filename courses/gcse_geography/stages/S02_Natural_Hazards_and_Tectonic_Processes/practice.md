# S02_Natural_Hazards_and_Tectonic_Processes - Practice: What a natural hazard is, and how plate tectonics causes earthquakes and volcanoes

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a one-sentence definition of a natural hazard that includes both 'event' and 'potential to cause harm'.
2. Sort these into hazard types: earthquake, tropical storm, volcanic eruption, drought.
3. Explain, using two factors, why an earthquake of the same magnitude could kill many more people in one country than another.
4. Draw and label a simple cross-section of a destructive margin, showing subduction and where magma rises.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
