# S03_Tectonic_Hazards_Effects_Responses_and_Case_Studies - Practice: Effects of, and responses to, tectonic hazards, comparing Chile 2010 and Nepal 2015

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Sort these into primary or secondary effects: collapsed bridge, disease outbreak, landslide, building destroyed by shaking.
2. Give one immediate and one long-term response to a major earthquake.
3. State the magnitude and approximate death toll for Chile 2010 and for Nepal 2015, and explain the difference using wealth.
4. Explain why volcanoes can be predicted with more confidence than earthquakes.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
