# S33_Numerical_and_Statistical_Skills - Test: Geographical skills: numerical calculation, fieldwork data reliability, and statistical skills

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Calculate the mean of: 4, 6, 8, 8, 9. b) What does a control site help you check in fieldwork? c) Name one way a graph can be presented misleadingly.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Given a described bivariate data set, describe the relationship shown on a scatter graph, calculate the interquartile range of one variable, and explain why extrapolating beyond the plotted data would be less reliable than interpolating within it.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S33_Numerical_and_Statistical_Skills` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S34_Data_Sources_Enquiry_Writing_and_SPaG.
