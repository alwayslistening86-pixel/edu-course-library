# S05_Tropical_Storms_Case_Study_and_UK_Weather - Practice: Typhoon Haiyan, monitoring tropical storms, and UK weather hazards including Storm Desmond

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. State two figures (deaths, wind speed or displaced people) from Typhoon Haiyan.
2. List three types of UK weather hazard.
3. Explain the two atmospheric causes of Storm Desmond's record rainfall.
4. Give one piece of evidence used to argue that UK weather is becoming more extreme, and one reason to be cautious about that evidence.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
