# S09_Cold_Environments - Practice: Cold environments: characteristics, interdependence, adaptations and the Alaska case study

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why permafrost makes construction difficult in cold environments.
2. Give two adaptations of cold-environment animals and explain how each helps survival.
3. List two development opportunities and two challenges for Alaska.
4. Evaluate one strategy that could allow Alaska's resources to be developed while limiting damage to the environment.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
