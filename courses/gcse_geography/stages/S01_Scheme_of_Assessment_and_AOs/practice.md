# S01_Scheme_of_Assessment_and_AOs - Practice: How AQA GCSE Geography is assessed: three papers and four assessment objectives

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write out the three papers with their correct title, length and mark total, without looking.
2. A question begins 'Explain how...'. Which AO does it mainly reward, and what should the answer include that a 'describe' answer would not?
3. Explain why AO4 appears on all three papers rather than in one separate 'skills' paper.
4. A learner writes only facts with no connecting reasoning in a 6-mark 'explain' question. Which AO have they under-used, and what one change would fix it?

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
