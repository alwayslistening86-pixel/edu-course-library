# S17_UK_Cities_and_Manchester - Test: UK population distribution and Manchester's migration, opportunities and challenges

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Locate Manchester in the UK. b) Give one social opportunity and one social challenge in Manchester. c) Name one type of migration that has shaped Manchester.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain how migration has shaped Manchester’s growth and character, and explain why economic opportunities in the city have not removed inequality between its neighbourhoods.'
