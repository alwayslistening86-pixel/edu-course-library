# S17_UK_Cities_and_Manchester - Practice: UK population distribution and Manchester's migration, opportunities and challenges

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why Manchester's population is culturally diverse, referring to specific groups of migrants.
2. Give two economic opportunities and two challenges facing Manchester today.
3. Explain the link between deindustrialisation and the brownfield land challenge in Manchester.
4. Explain one way Manchester has created environmental opportunities through regeneration.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
