# S02_Specifications_Ideas_and_the_Make_and_Evaluate_Stage - Test: Writing specifications, generating ideas and the make-and-evaluate stage

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Practice scenario (continuing the camping stove from S01): a) Write four specification points for the stove, at least two quantitative and two qualitative, each traceable to research or ACCESS FM (8). b) Sketch or describe two genuinely different design ideas addressing the specification (6). c) Explain how the maker would use modelling in the make-and-evaluate stage to test one idea before full manufacture (4). d) Classify two of your specification points as need/want (2). This tests understanding, not a finished specification document.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S02_Specifications_Ideas_and_the_Make_and_Evaluate_Stage` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S03_Manufacturing_Considerations_and_Communicating_Outcomes.
