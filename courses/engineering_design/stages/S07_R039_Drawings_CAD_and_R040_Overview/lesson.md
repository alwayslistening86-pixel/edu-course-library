# S07_R039_Drawings_CAD_and_R040_Overview - Lesson: Orthographic and assembly drawings, CAD presentation drawings, and the R040 overview

## Goal
The learner produces orthographic and assembly drawings to convention, produces CAD formal presentation drawings with evidence of 3D modelling, and knows how R040 is assessed.

## Syllabus items taught here
- R039-T3 - Task 3: orthographic and assembly drawings: produce a third-angle orthographic drawing and an assembly drawing using BS 8888 conventions
- R039-T4 - Task 4: CAD formal presentation drawings: use CAD software to produce formal presentation drawings with photographic evidence of 3D modelling
- R040-A - R040 assessment overview: six tasks and mark bands: know the six integrated tasks, the 60 marks, and how the three OCR mark bands are applied per task

## How to teach this
Ask the learner: 'Task 1 and 2 showed your idea to a client - who reads Task 3's drawings instead, and what do they need from them that a rendered sketch doesn't give?' Use the answer (a manufacturer, needing accurate, measurable information) to shift from presentation drawing to production-accurate drawing, then introduce R040 as the unit where the product actually gets made.

#### R039-T3 Task 3: orthographic and assembly drawings
Task 3 asks for a third-angle orthographic projection drawing and an assembly drawing of the developed design proposal, using BS 8888 conventions taught in S03 (title block, correct units, stated scale, tolerances, consistent dimensioning, correct line types). These must be accurate enough that someone else could manufacture and assemble the product from them, which is a different standard from the presentation-focused drawings in Tasks 1-2. MB3 evidence is fully conventioned and accurate; MB1 evidence is missing conventions or has significant inaccuracies. Activity: list which BS 8888 conventions the learner's own developed desk-organiser proposal would need to show in its orthographic drawing.

#### R039-T4 Task 4: CAD formal presentation drawings
Task 4 asks the learner to use CAD software to produce formal presentation design proposals, including photographic evidence of 3D CAD modelling (screenshots or renders showing the model from different angles, or evidence of a simulated/animated view). This tests the CAD skills and the advantages of CAD (speed, accuracy, 3D visualisation) taught in S04, applied to produce polished, professional-looking output rather than hand-drawn work. MB3 evidence shows confident, accurate CAD use with clear photographic/visual evidence; MB1 evidence shows minimal or unconvincing CAD use. Activity: describe what photographic evidence of a 3D CAD model of the desk organiser should show to count as strong Task 4 evidence.

#### R040-A R040 assessment overview: six tasks and mark bands
R040 is a 36 GLH, OCR-set and OCR-moderated practical assignment worth 60 marks (60 UMS), completed over roughly 10-12 hours of supervised, independent centre-assessed time, using the same three mark bands as R039 (MB1/MB2/MB3). Its six tasks move from analysing an existing product through to making and evaluating a real prototype: Task 1 product analysis (ACCESS FM and a comparison matrix), Task 2 product disassembly, Task 3 virtual 3D CAD modelling, Task 4 production planning, Task 5 prototype production, Task 6 prototype evaluation. Where R039 focuses on communicating a design, R040 focuses on analysing, modelling and physically realising one. Activity: name R040's six tasks in order and state which R038 Topic Areas each one draws most directly on.
