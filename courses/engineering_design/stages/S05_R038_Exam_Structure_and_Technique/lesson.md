# S05_R038_Exam_Structure_and_Technique - Lesson: R038 exam structure, command words and level-of-response technique

## Goal
The learner knows R038's exam structure and terminal assessment rule, responds correctly to command words, and structures extended responses to reach Level 3.

## Syllabus items taught here
- R038-EX1 - R038 exam structure and the terminal assessment rule: know the 1 hour 15 minute, 70-mark paper, its question types and that it must be sat in the final assessment series
- R038-EX2 - Command and descriptor words (Appendix B): respond correctly to command words used in multiple choice, short answer and extended response questions
- R038-EX3 - Level-of-response technique for extended questions: structure an extended response to reach Level 3: two or more developed points with genuine discussion and correct terminology

## How to teach this
Give the learner two answers to the same question - one a single unexplained sentence, one with two developed, justified points - and ask which would score more marks and why. Use this to introduce that R038 rewards development and terminology, not just a correct-sounding statement, and that command words tell you exactly how much development is expected.

#### R038-EX1 R038 exam structure and the terminal assessment rule
R038 is a 1 hour 15 minute written exam worth 70 marks (80 UMS), the qualification's only externally examined component, drawing on all four Topic Areas taught in S01-S04. It mixes multiple choice questions (one correct response), short answer questions (from single-mark list items to two-or-more-mark developed responses, marked left to right and line by line for list-style answers) and extended response questions marked using the three-level-of-response scheme (S below). OCR's terminal assessment rule requires R038 to be sat in the learner's final assessment series, after R039 and R040 are complete, so the exam reflects the most current standard of understanding. Activity: state, in the learner's own words, why sitting R038 last makes sense given what R039 and R040 assess.

#### R038-EX2 Command and descriptor words (Appendix B)
Appendix B defines the command words used across R038's questions, and understanding them precisely is required to answer to the mark available: for example "state"/"identify" wants a brief, undeveloped fact; "describe" wants an accurate account of what something is or does; "explain" wants a reason or mechanism (why or how); "discuss"/"evaluate" wants weighing up more than one side or factor, which is what extended, level-of-response questions specifically require. Answering an "explain" question with only a "state"-level answer caps the marks available regardless of how correct the fact is. Activity: for a practice question using "explain", identify what a "state"-level answer to it would be missing.

#### R038-EX3 Level-of-response technique for extended questions
Extended questions are marked on three levels, each with a mark range that scales to the question's total tariff: Level 3 (thorough discussion, consistent correct terminology, two or more points genuinely developed with discussion, debate or weighing of pros and cons) scores highest; Level 2 (adequate discussion, some terminology, one or two points with some justification) scores in the middle; Level 1 (brief, little or no terminology, undeveloped points) scores lowest. To reach Level 3, a learner should plan at least two distinct points before writing, develop each with a reason or example, and use the topic's correct technical terms throughout rather than everyday language. Activity: take a Level 1-style one-sentence answer to a practice question and rewrite it as a Level 3 answer with two developed points.
