# S08_R040_Product_Analysis_Disassembly_and_CAD_Modelling - Practice: Product analysis with ACCESS FM, product disassembly and virtual 3D CAD modelling

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Build a comparison matrix scoring two versions of a product against three ACCESS FM criteria.
2. Identify the material and manufacturing process for three components of a product you could disassemble.
3. State one hazard and one safety control relevant to disassembling a product with a spring or a sharp edge.
4. List the views and simulation evidence a strong 3D CAD model should include.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
