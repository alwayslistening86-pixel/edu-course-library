# S10_Prototype_Evaluation_Grading_NEA_Rules_and_Synoptic_Links - Lesson: Prototype evaluation, grading, NEA rules and synoptic links across the qualification

## Goal
The learner evaluates a manufactured prototype against its specification with justified improvements, calculates a plausible overall grade from unit UMS, states the NEA rules, and links R038 content to the practical NEA tasks.

## Syllabus items taught here
- R040-T6 - Task 6: prototype evaluation and improvement: evaluate the finished prototype against the specification and propose justified improvements
- J822-GRADE - Grading: UMS, unit grades and overall grades Level 1 Pass to Level 2 Distinction*: calculate unit and overall boundaries and read the performance descriptors
- J822-NEA - NEA rules: OCR-set and OCR-moderated assignments, independence and authenticity: follow the rules for supervised, independent, authenticated evidence in R039 and R040
- J822-SYN - Synoptic links between R038 and the NEA units: link R038's designing-process, requirements, communication and evaluation content to the practical tasks in R039 and R040

## How to teach this
Ask the learner: 'You've now planned, drawn, modelled and made a prototype across this whole course - how does OCR actually turn all of that into one final grade, and what stops someone else from making it for you?' Use this to join up everything taught in S01-S09 into the bigger picture the exam board certificates.

#### R040-T6 Task 6: prototype evaluation and improvement
Task 6 asks the learner to evaluate the manufactured prototype against every point in the original specification, using the outcome-evaluation methods from S04 (measuring dimensions/functionality, quantitative comparison, user testing) with specific, evidenced findings rather than general impressions, then propose and justify potential design improvements addressing the gaps found. This is the final step of the design-make-evaluate cycle (S01) applied to the real prototype, and it is where the whole unit's earlier tasks are judged against their original purpose. MB3 evidence evaluates every specification point with specific evidence and proposes well-justified, targeted improvements; MB1 evidence is vague or evaluates only some points. Activity: write one specific, evidenced evaluation finding and one justified improvement for the ergonomic pen grip prototype.

#### J822-GRADE Grading: UMS, unit grades and overall grades Level 1 Pass to Level 2 Distinction*
Each unit is marked out of its raw marks (R038: 70; R039: 60; R040: 60) and converted to a Uniform Mark Scale score out of a fixed unit maximum (R038: 80 UMS; R039: 60 UMS; R040: 60 UMS; 200 UMS maximum overall), which lets results from different assessment series be compared fairly. The three unit UMS scores are summed, and the total is read against grade boundaries: Distinction* at Level 2 needs at least 180 total UMS, Distinction 160, Merit 140, Pass 120; Level 1 grades are Distinction 100, Merit 80, Pass 60; below 60 is unclassified. Activity: given example unit UMS scores of 55 (R038), 45 (R039) and 50 (R040), add them and state the resulting overall grade.

#### J822-NEA NEA rules: OCR-set and OCR-moderated assignments, independence and authenticity
R039 and R040 are OCR-set assignments: OCR provides the brief and marking criteria, the centre (school) marks the work, and OCR moderates a sample to check standards nationally. Learners must complete the practical tasks independently once the assignment window opens; teachers may give general guidance (clarifying the brief or marking criteria, answering safety questions) but must not tell a learner what to draw, model or make, correct their work in detail, or supply ready-made solutions. Work must be the learner's own, authentic evidence, and centres formally confirm this before submitting for moderation. Activity: give an example of acceptable general guidance and an example of unacceptable, too-specific help for the same R040 task.

#### J822-SYN Synoptic links between R038 and the NEA units
J822's NEA units are deliberately synoptic with R038: the design strategies, research methods, ACCESS FM, specification writing, manufacturing considerations, drawing conventions and evaluation methods taught for R038's exam (Topic Areas 1-4, taught in S01-S04) underpin the decisions learners must make and justify throughout R039 and R040 - for example R040 Task 1 uses ACCESS FM directly (R038-1.2c), R039 Task 3 uses BS 8888 conventions directly (R038-3.2), and R040 Task 6 uses the outcome-evaluation methods directly (R038-4.3). Learners who treat R038 as separate exam-only knowledge miss the practical grounding it gives every NEA task. Activity: name one R038 Topic Area and explain how it directly supports a specific task in R039 or R040.
