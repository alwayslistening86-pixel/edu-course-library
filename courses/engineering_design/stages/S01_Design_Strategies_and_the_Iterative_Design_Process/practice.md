# S01_Design_Strategies_and_the_Iterative_Design_Process - Practice: Design strategies, the iterative design process and research methods

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Match each of the six design strategies to a one-line scenario that suits it best.
2. Draw and label the design-make-evaluate cycle from memory.
3. For a chosen everyday product, name one primary and one secondary research method that could have informed its design.
4. Analyse a mobile phone case against all eight ACCESS FM factors.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
