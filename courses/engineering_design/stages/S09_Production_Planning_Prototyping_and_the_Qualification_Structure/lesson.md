# S09_Production_Planning_Prototyping_and_the_Qualification_Structure - Lesson: Production planning, prototype manufacture, and how J822 is structured

## Goal
The learner plans prototype production with a risk assessment, manufactures a prototype with a photographic diary, and knows J822's overall unit structure, GLH, weighting and performance objectives.

## Syllabus items taught here
- R040-T4 - Task 4: production planning and risk assessment: plan prototype production stages, tools, quality checks, timings and a risk assessment
- R040-T5 - Task 5: prototype production and photographic diary: manufacture a prototype following the production plan and record a photographic diary of key stages, tools and processes
- J822-STRUCT - Qualification structure, units, GLH and the terminal assessment rule: know the three mandatory units, their GLH and weighting, 120 GLH overall and the terminal assessment rule for R038
- J822-PO - Performance objectives PO1 to PO4 and their weightings: know what recall, application, analysis/evaluation and skills demonstration mean and where each is assessed

## How to teach this
Ask the learner: 'If you handed your CAD model to someone else and said "just make it", what would go wrong without a plan?' Use this to introduce production planning as the bridge between a virtual model and an actual, safely made prototype, then zoom out to how this whole qualification is put together and marked.

#### R040-T4 Task 4: production planning and risk assessment
Task 4 asks for a written production plan covering the manufacturing stages needed to make the prototype, the tools and equipment required, quality checks at key points, realistic timings, and a risk assessment identifying hazards and the control measures that will be used. A strong plan is specific to the actual prototype being made (not a generic list of "use tools safely") and sequenced logically so it could be followed by someone else. This plan is what Task 5's manufacture should then follow. MB3 evidence is complete and specific across task, tool, quality check, timing and safety measure; MB1 evidence is vague or missing several of these. Activity: write three rows of a production plan (stage, tool, quality check) for making a simple prototype part, plus one risk and its control.

#### R040-T5 Task 5: prototype production and photographic diary
Task 5 asks the learner to manufacture the prototype following the Task 4 production plan, recording a photographic diary that explains the key stages, tools and processes actually used as the build happens, not written up afterwards from memory. The diary should show genuine progress (not just a finished item) and connect back to the plan, noting where reality matched or departed from it. MB3 evidence shows a well-documented build with clear stage-by-stage photographic evidence and explanation; MB1 evidence shows minimal documentation or only a final photograph. Activity: describe three photographs (what each shows and what caption/explanation would go with it) that would evidence a well-documented prototype build.

#### J822-STRUCT Qualification structure, units, GLH and the terminal assessment rule
J822 has three mandatory units and no optional units: R038 Principles of engineering design (48 GLH, externally examined, 70 marks), R039 Communicating designs (36 GLH, OCR-set/moderated NEA, 60 marks) and R040 Design, evaluation and modelling (36 GLH, OCR-set/moderated NEA, 60 marks), totalling 120 GLH and 190 raw marks. OCR weights the units by GLH share: R038 40%, R039 30%, R040 30%. The terminal assessment rule (taught in S05) requires R038 to be sat in the learner's final assessment series, after both NEA units are complete. Activity: state, without looking, each unit's GLH and whether it is examined or centre-assessed.

#### J822-PO Performance objectives PO1 to PO4 and their weightings
J822 assesses four performance objectives: PO1 recall knowledge and show understanding (about 17.5-21% of the qualification), PO2 apply knowledge and understanding (about 30.5-34%), PO3 analyse and evaluate knowledge, understanding and performance (about 20.5-22.5%), and PO4 demonstrate and apply skills and processes relevant to the subject area (about 27%). R038's short answer and multiple choice questions mainly assess PO1, its extended questions add PO3, while R039 and R040's practical tasks mainly assess PO2 and PO4, with PO3 also present in R040's evaluation task. Activity: for each performance objective, name one stage or task from this course that directly assesses it.

## Explicitly not here
Prototype evaluation (S10), grading, NEA rules and synoptic links (S10).
