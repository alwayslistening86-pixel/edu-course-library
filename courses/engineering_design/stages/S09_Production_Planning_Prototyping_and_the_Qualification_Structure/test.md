## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Practice scenario (continuing the ergonomic pen grip redesign): a) Write a production plan for manufacturing the new grip prototype, including at least three stages, tools, quality checks, a timing estimate and one risk with its control (10). b) Describe the photographic diary evidence that would be produced while manufacturing it, including what two specific photographs would show (6). c) State J822's unit structure, GLH and the terminal assessment rule (4). d) Explain which performance objective is mainly assessed by writing the production plan, and which by carrying out the manufacture (4). This tests understanding, not a finished physical prototype.
