## Test scenario
Practice scenario (continuing the camping stove): a) Explain one manufacturing consideration that would affect how the stove's body is made and why (5). b) Explain one influence (from market pull/technology push, standards, legislation, obsolescence or the 6Rs) most relevant to a camping product and its effect on the design (5). c) Recommend a drawing type for presenting the stove concept to a client and a different one for sending to the manufacturer, with reasons (4). d) List the BS 8888 conventions the manufacturing drawing must include (6). This tests understanding, not a finished drawing.
