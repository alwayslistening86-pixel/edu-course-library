# L5_Tort_Law - Test: Tort Law — Negligence, occupiers' liability, nuisance and vicarious liability

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
A gym leaves a broken step unrepaired for three weeks despite two member complaints, marking it only with a small handwritten sign. A member, reading a text message, trips on the step and breaks an ankle; she had been told about the step by a friend an hour earlier but forgot. Separately, a gym instructor, annoyed with a difficult client, deliberately shoves him during a induction session, causing a shoulder injury. Advise on the gym's liability to the injured member (working through duty, breach, causation, remoteness and contributory negligence) and on the gym's vicarious liability for the instructor's conduct.
