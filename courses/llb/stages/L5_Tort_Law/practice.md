# L5_Tort_Law - Practice: Tort Law — Negligence, occupiers' liability, nuisance and vicarious liability

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply Caparo/Robinson to decide whether a personal trainer owes a duty of care to a client who is injured following the trainer's instructions.
2. Apply the Bolton v Stone risk factors to a fireworks display that injures a spectator standing at the recommended safe distance.
3. A hospital negligently delays treatment, but the claimant's condition would have caused the same outcome regardless. Apply factual causation.
4. A trespassing child is injured by a concealed, unfenced quarry the occupier knew children regularly accessed. Apply the Occupiers' Liability Act 1984.
5. A delivery driver deviates significantly from their route to visit a friend and causes an accident. Analyse for vicarious liability.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
