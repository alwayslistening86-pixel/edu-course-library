# L5_Property_Law - Test: Property Law — Estates, registration, leases, easements, covenants and mortgages

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Leo buys a registered freehold house. The register's charges section shows a restrictive covenant "no commercial use" but nothing about access. After moving in, Leo discovers his neighbour Priya has walked a well-worn path across the rear of Leo's garden to reach a footpath for the last 22 years, openly and without ever asking permission, and that Leo's predecessor let a local charity store a shed on part of the garden under a casual, unwritten arrangement, revocable "any time." Advise Leo on whether he is bound by (a) the restrictive covenant, (b) Priya's path use, and (c) the charity's shed arrangement.
