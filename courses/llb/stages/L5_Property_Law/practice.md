# L5_Property_Law - Practice: Property Law — Estates, registration, leases, easements, covenants and mortgages

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why Anna's neighbour's long-standing path use is a stronger candidate for an easement than the delivery company's parking arrangement, applying Re Ellenborough Park and prescription.
2. Apply Street v Mountford to decide whether a "licence agreement" granting a lodger exclusive use of a self-contained room for a fixed rent and term is really a lease.
3. A seller's restrictive covenant not to build above two storeys was registered as a notice against the servient title. Explain whether it binds a later purchaser of that title.
4. Two unmarried partners buy a house together, with one contributing 70% of the deposit and no express declaration of trust made. Apply co-ownership principles to identify their likely beneficial shares.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
