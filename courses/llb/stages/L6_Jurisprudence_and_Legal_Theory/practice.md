# L6_Jurisprudence_and_Legal_Theory - Practice: Jurisprudence and Legal Theory

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Argue the reporting-law scenario from a strict Hart positivist position, then from a Fuller inner-morality-of-law position, and identify precisely where the two arguments diverge.
2. Explain how Dworkin's Hercules would approach a hard case where a clear rule produces a result that seems to conflict with an underlying legal principle.
3. Apply a CLS critique to the "reasonable person" standard in negligence (L5-Tort-2).
4. Apply the Hart-Devlin debate to a hypothetical law criminalising conduct that harms no one but that a majority finds morally offensive.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
