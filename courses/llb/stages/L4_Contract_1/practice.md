# L4_Contract_1 - Practice: Contract Law I — Formation: offer, acceptance, consideration and intention

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Identify whether each of the following is an offer or an invitation to treat, with authority: a shop window display; an auction lot put up without reserve; a job advertisement.
2. Tom telephones Maya to accept but her voicemail is full and the message never gets through. Apply the communication rule to decide whether a contract formed.
3. A builder promises to reduce a £10,000 invoice to £8,000 if the client pays immediately, then tries to sue for the full amount. Apply Foakes v Beer and High Trees.
4. A father promises his adult daughter a flat if she gives up her job to care for him; she does so, and he later refuses. Apply intention to create legal relations and estoppel.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
