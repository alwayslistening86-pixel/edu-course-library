# L6_Alternative_Dispute_Resolution - Practice: Alternative Dispute Resolution

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply interest-based negotiation to a dispute where both parties have publicly stated an inflexible position ("full refund" vs "no refund").
2. Explain why a mediated settlement agreement is not automatically enforceable in the same way as a court judgment, and what step converts it into one.
3. Apply ss.67-69 Arbitration Act 1996 to decide whether a party unhappy with an arbitrator's factual findings (as opposed to legal ruling) can challenge the award.
4. Apply the Halsey factors to a party who refused mediation because they believed, reasonably and in good faith, that they had an overwhelming case.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
