# L6_Alternative_Dispute_Resolution - Test: Alternative Dispute Resolution

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Two former business partners are in dispute over £60,000 owed under a dissolved partnership agreement that contains no arbitration or ADR clause. One partner's solicitor writes proposing mediation; the other partner refuses outright, saying only "see you in court," without giving any reason, and the case eventually goes to trial where that partner wins on a technical limitation point unrelated to the merits. Advise on the costs consequences of the refusal to mediate, applying the Halsey framework, and separately advise what would have changed had the partnership agreement contained a valid arbitration clause.
