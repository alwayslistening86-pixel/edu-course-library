# L5_Commercial_Law - Test: Commercial Law — Sale of goods, agency and international sale essentials

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
A distributor orders 500 units of packaging machinery "suitable for high-humidity environments" from a manufacturer's catalogue. On arrival, the machinery rusts within two months. Separately, the manufacturer's regional sales agent, who has no express authority to agree bespoke payment terms but has done so for other clients without correction, agrees a 90-day payment term with the distributor that conflicts with the manufacturer's standard 30-day terms. Advise the distributor on its rights against the manufacturer for the defective machinery, and advise whether the manufacturer is bound by the 90-day payment term.
