# L4_Legal_System_and_Method - Test: The English Legal System and Legal Method

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
A new Act provides: "No person shall keep a dangerous animal within 100 metres of a school." A resident keeps a large, well-trained guard dog in a securely fenced garden 80 metres from a school, and is prosecuted. Using the literal, golden, mischief and purposive approaches in turn, advise on the strongest and weakest arguments for the prosecution and the defence, and explain which court would first hear the case and where any appeal would go.
