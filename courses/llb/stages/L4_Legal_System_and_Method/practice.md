# L4_Legal_System_and_Method - Practice: The English Legal System and Legal Method

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply the literal, golden and mischief rules separately to whether an electric scooter is a "vehicle" for the park byelaw, reaching a different plausible answer under each.
2. Explain whether a Court of Appeal panel is bound by its own earlier decision that was reached without considering a relevant, binding House of Lords authority.
3. Trace the route of appeal for a shoplifting charge tried in the Magistrates' Court where the defendant wishes to challenge a point of law.
4. Cite a fictitious case, "Green v Black," decided by the Court of Appeal in 2019 and reported at [2019] EWCA Civ 100, in correct OSCOLA form.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
