# L4_Public_Law - Test: Public Law — Constitutional principles and judicial review

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
A university's own published disciplinary code promises a student "the right to an oral hearing before any exclusion decision." A student is excluded by letter, with no hearing offered, for an alleged examination offence, and the letter cites a "power to exclude in serious cases" that does not appear anywhere in the university's governing documents. Advise the student on the grounds of judicial review available, the appropriate remedy, and any procedural obstacles (standing, time limit) to bringing the claim.
