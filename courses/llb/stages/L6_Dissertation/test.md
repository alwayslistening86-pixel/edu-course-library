# L6_Dissertation - Test: Dissertation — Independent legal research and sustained argument

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
The learner has one week of independent time before a supervision meeting. Produce: (a) a single, narrow, contestable dissertation research question on a topic of the learner's choosing from anywhere in this LLB; (b) a one-paragraph justification of the methodology chosen to answer it; (c) a short outline (introduction, legal-position section, critical section, counter-argument engagement, conclusion) showing how the argument will be structured; and (d) three real, correctly OSCOLA-cited sources (a mix of primary and secondary) that will anchor the research.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.L6_Dissertation` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass. This was the last stage: the cumulative exam is now unlocked once every stage test is passed.
