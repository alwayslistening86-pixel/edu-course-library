# L5_Family_Law - Practice: Family Law — Formation and breakdown of relationships, finances and children

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Classify each of the following as making a marriage void, voidable, or valid: the parties are first cousins; one party did not validly consent because of duress; the marriage was never consummated.
2. Apply the no-fault divorce timeline to calculate the earliest possible date for a final order for an application submitted today.
3. Apply White v White and Miller/McFarlane to a 25-year marriage where one spouse was the sole earner and the other raised four children and never worked outside the home.
4. Apply the welfare checklist to a dispute where a 14-year-old expresses a clear wish to live with one parent, but that parent has a documented history of substance misuse.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
