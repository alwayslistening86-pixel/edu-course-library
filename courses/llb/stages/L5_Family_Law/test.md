# L5_Family_Law - Test: Family Law — Formation and breakdown of relationships, finances and children

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Priya and Sam divorce after 15 years of marriage. Priya, a solicitor, earned significantly more throughout; Sam gave up a teaching career after their first child was born and has not worked since. They have two children, aged 9 and 13, currently living with Sam. Sam alleges Priya has been coercively controlling throughout the relationship and wants a non-molestation order and to remain in the family home. Advise on the likely approach to financial remedies (using the s.25 factors and White/Miller-McFarlane principles), the children's arrangements (using the welfare checklist), and the protective orders Sam might seek.
