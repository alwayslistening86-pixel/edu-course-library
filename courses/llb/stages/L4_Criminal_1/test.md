# L4_Criminal_1 - Test: Criminal Law I — General principles: actus reus, mens rea and homicide

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Kai discovers his business partner has been embezzling funds for years and, in a sudden confrontation, strikes him once with a heavy object; the partner dies two days later in hospital, partly because he refused a routine blood transfusion for religious reasons. Advise on Kai's liability for murder or manslaughter, addressing actus reus, mens rea, causation (including the effect of the refused transfusion), and any partial defence realistically available on these facts.
