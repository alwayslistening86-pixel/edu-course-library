# L4_Criminal_2 - Test: Criminal Law II — Non-fatal and property offences, defences and inchoate liability

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Ravi is threatened by a gang leader who says Ravi's sister will be seriously hurt unless Ravi drives the getaway car for a burglary. Ravi, terrified, drives the car; the two others he drives away with had entered a warehouse and taken stock before Ravi arrived. Separately, one of the two, Sam, strikes the warehouse's night guard with a torch when confronted, causing a fractured cheekbone. Advise on the liability of Ravi and Sam, addressing the relevant Theft Act offences, the non-fatal offence against the guard, and any defence realistically available to Ravi.
