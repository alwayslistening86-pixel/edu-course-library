# L4_Contract_2 - Practice: Contract Law II — Terms, vitiating factors, discharge and remedies

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Classify a clause requiring delivery "by 1 March" and a clause requiring goods to be "of first-class quality" as condition, warranty or innominate term, with reasons.
2. Apply UCTA 1977 to decide whether the corrosion-exclusion clause in the opening scenario is effective against a business buyer.
3. A seller tells a buyer a car has "one careful owner" believing it to be true, when in fact it had three. Classify the misrepresentation and identify the buyer's remedies.
4. A company director signs a personal guarantee after the bank's lawyer implies the loan will be withdrawn from the business within the hour if he does not sign immediately. Analyse for economic duress.
5. A contractor cannot complete a build because the site is compulsorily purchased by the local authority mid-project. Analyse for frustration and its financial consequences.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
