# L6_Equity_and_Trusts - Practice: Equity and the Law of Trusts

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Apply the three certainties to a will clause leaving money "to my trustees, hoping they will look after my sister" — is a trust created?
2. Apply Milroy v Lord to a case where a father signs a share transfer form for his son but dies before the company registers the transfer.
3. Apply Jones v Kernott to an unmarried couple who bought a house in joint names with no express declaration of trust, where one later paid off the mortgage alone after separation.
4. A trustee invests the entire trust fund in a single speculative asset without considering diversification. Apply the Trustee Act 2000 duty of care.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
