## How to run this

## Test scenario
Grace transfers her flat into her nephew Tom's sole name, telling him privately "this is just so my ex-husband can't get at it in our divorce — you know it's really still mine, and you'll transfer it back once things settle." Grace continues to live in the flat, pays the mortgage, and calls it "my home" to friends. Two years later, having remarried, Tom refuses to transfer the flat back, asserting it is legally and beneficially his. Advise Grace on whether any trust exists in her favour, addressing the three certainties for an express trust, the resulting and constructive trust doctrines, and any illegality difficulty her own stated purpose might create.
