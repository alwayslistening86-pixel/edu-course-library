# LLB (Bachelor of Laws) — Open University module-framework structure, QAA Law Benchmark graded - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.
## Format
A cumulative, closed-book paper of four original problem questions, one drawn from each of Level 4, Level 5 doctrinal, Level 5 optional and Level 6, reflecting the way the Open University's own LLB examines each of its six bundled modules (W101/W102, W201/W202, W300/W301) separately but testing retained knowledge across the whole degree in one sitting here:
1. A Level 4 problem question combining Contract Law and Criminal Law issues in one factual scenario.
2. A Level 4 Legal System and Method / Public Law problem requiring statutory interpretation and at least one judicial review ground.
3. A Level 5 problem drawn from Tort Law and one of the learner's selected Level 5 optional modules (Family, Property or Commercial Law).
4. A Level 6 problem drawn from Equity and Trusts, with a short reflective section on the learner's Dissertation research question and methodology.
Each question is answered in full IRAC form. No real past-paper question is used; all scenarios are original.
## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly.
## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete.
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the stage(s) that broke down, offer targeted review of the specific module(s) and a fresh retry paper with new original scenarios.
