# GCSE Combined Science: Trilogy (AQA 8464) — Foundation and Higher tier - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.

## Format
Six timed papers at the learner's tier, each 1 hour 15 minutes and 70 marks, matching AQA's real structure: Biology Paper 1 covers topics 4.1 to 4.4 (cell biology, organisation, infection and response, bioenergetics); Biology Paper 2 covers 4.5 to 4.7 (homeostasis and response, inheritance, variation and evolution, ecology); Chemistry Paper 1 covers 5.1 to 5.5 (atomic structure and the periodic table, bonding, quantitative chemistry, chemical changes, energy changes); Chemistry Paper 2 covers 5.6 to 5.10 (rate and extent of change, organic chemistry, chemical analysis, the atmosphere, using resources); Physics Paper 1 covers 6.1 to 6.4 (energy, electricity, particle model, atomic structure); Physics Paper 2 covers 6.5 to 6.8 (forces, waves, magnetism and electromagnetism, key ideas). Each paper mixes multiple-choice, structured, closed short-answer and open-response questions, with mathematical skills and working scientifically assessed throughout.

## Example structure
Biology papers draw on B01-B11, Chemistry papers on C01-C15 and Physics papers on P01-P10, mixing questions across topics rather than grouping them. Include at least one required-practical-based question in each paper and questions using the W1-W2 and M1 skills. At Higher tier include Higher-only content such as the inverse square law, negative feedback, moles, equilibrium, momentum and the motor effect, and evaluation questions.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly. A pass requires consistent performance across all six papers under timed conditions with working shown and extended answers that use correct scientific terminology.

## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete (for that tier).
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name which stage(s) broke down under mixed or timed conditions, offer targeted review or a fresh retry paper.
