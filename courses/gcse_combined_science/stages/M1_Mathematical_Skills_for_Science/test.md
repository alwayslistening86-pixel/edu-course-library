# M1_Mathematical_Skills_for_Science - Test: Mathematical requirements for GCSE Combined Science

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Write 4500 in standard form. b) Calculate the mean of 6.2, 6.4, 6.3 and 6.5. c) A graph of distance against time is a straight line through the origin; state what its gradient represents.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A sample of 12 quadrats has a mean of 3.5 plants per square metre in a field of 4000 square metres. Estimate the population, then rearrange density = mass / volume to find the volume of 240 g of a metal of density 8 g/cm3, giving each answer to two significant figures.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.M1_Mathematical_Skills_for_Science` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass. This was the last stage: the cumulative exam is now unlocked once every stage test is passed.
