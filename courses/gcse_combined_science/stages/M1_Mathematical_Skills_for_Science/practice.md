# M1_Mathematical_Skills_for_Science - Practice: Mathematical requirements for GCSE Combined Science

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write 0.000035 m in standard form and in micrometres, and find 15 per cent of 240 g. Estimate 4.1 x 19.8 to one significant figure.
2. Five readings are 12.1, 12.4, 12.2, 15.9 and 12.3 s. Identify the anomaly, then find the mean of the rest and the range. Find the mean, mode and median of 3, 4, 4, 5, 14.
3. Rearrange V = IR to make R the subject and calculate R for 6 V and 0.5 A. Substitute into F = ma for a 2 kg mass with a resultant force of 5 N.
4. Plot the points (0, 0), (2, 6), (4, 12), (6, 18), calculate the gradient and write the equation y = mx + c. Higher: estimate the gradient at t = 3 s on a curved distance-time graph by drawing a tangent, and the area under a velocity-time graph by counting squares.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
