# C06_Reactivity_of_Metals - Lesson: The reactivity series, extraction and oxidation

## Goal
The learner uses the reactivity series to predict reactions and methods of extraction, and describes oxidation and reduction.

## Syllabus items taught here
- 5.4.1.1 - Metal oxides: learn and apply: metal oxides
- 5.4.1.2 - The reactivity series: learn and apply: the reactivity series
- 5.4.1.3 - Extraction of metals and reduction: learn and apply: extraction of metals and reduction
- 5.4.1.4 - Oxidation and reduction in terms of electrons (Higher tier): learn and apply: oxidation and reduction in terms of electrons (higher tier)

## How to teach this
Start with: 'Why did people use copper and gold thousands of years before aluminium?' Build the reactivity idea.

#### 5.4.1.1 Metal oxides
Metals react with oxygen to produce metal oxides; the reactions are oxidation because the metal gains oxygen. For example 2Mg + O2 gives 2MgO. Reduction is the loss of oxygen, so a metal oxide can be reduced to the metal by a more reactive element such as carbon.

#### 5.4.1.2 The reactivity series
From most to least reactive: potassium, sodium, lithium, calcium, magnesium, aluminium, (carbon), zinc, iron, (hydrogen), copper, silver, gold. The order is found from reactions with water, dilute acids and oxygen: reactive metals such as potassium react violently with water, magnesium reacts slowly with cold water but vigorously with acid, copper does not react with dilute acid. A more reactive metal displaces a less reactive one from its compound: zinc displaces copper from copper sulfate solution. Higher tier: displacement is a transfer of electrons: Zn + Cu2+ gives Zn2+ + Cu.

#### 5.4.1.3 Extraction of metals and reduction
Ores are rocks containing enough metal to make it economical to extract. Unreactive metals such as gold are found as the metal itself. Metals less reactive than carbon, such as iron, are extracted by reduction with carbon: iron(III) oxide + carbon gives iron + carbon dioxide (2Fe2O3 + 3C gives 4Fe + 3CO2). Metals more reactive than carbon, such as aluminium, must be extracted using electrolysis.

#### 5.4.1.4 Oxidation and reduction in terms of electrons (Higher tier)
Oxidation is loss of electrons and reduction is gain of electrons (OIL RIG). In Mg + Cu2+ gives Mg2+ + Cu, magnesium is oxidised (loses 2 electrons) and copper ions are reduced (gain 2 electrons). Write half equations: Mg gives Mg2+ + 2e-; Cu2+ + 2e- gives Cu. A reaction with both oxidation and reduction is a redox reaction.
