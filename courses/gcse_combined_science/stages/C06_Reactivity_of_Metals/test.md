## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) State which is more reactive, zinc or copper, and how a displacement reaction shows it. b) Explain why aluminium is not extracted by heating with carbon. c) Write the word equation for magnesium reacting with oxygen.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Write half equations for the reaction of magnesium with copper ions and explain which is oxidation and which is reduction.'
