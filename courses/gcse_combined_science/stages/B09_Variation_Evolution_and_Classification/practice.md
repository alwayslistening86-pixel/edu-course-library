# B09_Variation_Evolution_and_Classification - Practice: Variation, evolution, genetic technology and classification

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the causes of variation and how a mutation can be beneficial, neutral or harmful.
2. Explain how natural selection led to a change in the peppered moth population, and compare Darwin's and Lamarck's ideas.
3. Describe selective breeding steps and one example, and the steps of genetic engineering with one benefit and one concern.
4. Explain how fossils form and why the fossil record is incomplete, give causes of extinction, and explain how resistant bacteria evolve. Describe the Linnaean system and binomial names.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
