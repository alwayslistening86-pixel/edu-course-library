# C12_Hydrocarbons_and_Crude_Oil - Practice: Crude oil, hydrocarbons and cracking

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe how crude oil formed and why it is a mixture of hydrocarbons, and write the formulae of methane, ethane, propane and butane.
2. Describe fractional distillation and explain how boiling point, viscosity and flammability change with chain length.
3. Write balanced equations for complete combustion of propane and explain how incomplete combustion produces carbon monoxide and soot.
4. Explain why cracking is needed, its conditions, and write an equation for decane cracking to octane and ethene. State the test for an alkene.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
