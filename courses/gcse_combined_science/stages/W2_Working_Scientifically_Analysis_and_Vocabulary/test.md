# W2_Working_Scientifically_Analysis_and_Vocabulary - Test: Working scientifically: analysis, evaluation and scientific language

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Calculate the mean of 4.2, 4.5, 4.4 and 4.5. b) Name the type of graph you would draw to show the number of students who have each eye colour. c) State the SI unit of mass and of time.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Data show the rate of a reaction doubles for every 10 degree rise until 40 degrees Celsius, then falls. Write a conclusion, evaluate the precision of the data and suggest an improvement.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.W2_Working_Scientifically_Analysis_and_Vocabulary` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to M1_Mathematical_Skills_for_Science.
