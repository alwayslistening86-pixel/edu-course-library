# C01_Atomic_Structure - Practice: Atoms, mixtures and the model of the atom

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Distinguish an element, a compound and a mixture, and name a method to separate each mixture: sand and water, salt and water, and inks.
2. Describe how the plum pudding model was replaced by the nuclear model, and how Bohr and Chadwick refined it.
3. Give the relative charge and relative mass of a proton, neutron and electron, and the number of each in 27Al with atomic number 13.
4. Write the electronic structure of magnesium and chlorine. Higher: chlorine is 75% Cl-35 and 25% Cl-37; calculate its relative atomic mass.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
