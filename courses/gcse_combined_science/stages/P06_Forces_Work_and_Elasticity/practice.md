# P06_Forces_Work_and_Elasticity - Practice: Forces, work done and elasticity

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give two scalar and two vector quantities and describe contact and non-contact forces with examples.
2. Calculate the weight of a 60 kg person (g = 9.8 N/kg) and the resultant of 20 N to the right and 12 N to the left.
3. Calculate the work done by a 50 N force over 6 m and explain the relationship between work done and energy transferred.
4. A 12 N force stretches a spring by 0.06 m: calculate the spring constant. Describe the practical to test Hooke's law. Higher: calculate the energy stored at that extension.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
