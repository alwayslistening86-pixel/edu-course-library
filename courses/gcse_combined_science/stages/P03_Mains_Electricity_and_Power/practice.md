# P03_Mains_Electricity_and_Power - Practice: Mains electricity, power and the National Grid

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Compare a.c. and d.c., and state the frequency and potential difference of UK mains.
2. Name the three wires in a plug with their colours and functions, and explain how a fuse and the earth wire protect a user.
3. A kettle has power 2.3 kW at 230 V: calculate the current. Calculate the energy transferred by a 2 kW heater in 5 minutes.
4. Explain why electricity is transmitted at high voltage and how step-up and step-down transformers are used in the National Grid.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
