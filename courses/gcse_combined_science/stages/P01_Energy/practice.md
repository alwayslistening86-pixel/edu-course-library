# P01_Energy - Practice: Energy stores, transfers, power and resources

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Name the energy stores and describe the energy changes in a car braking and a kettle boiling.
2. Calculate the kinetic energy of a 1200 kg car at 15 m/s and the gravitational potential energy gained by a 5 kg mass lifted 3 m (g = 9.8 N/kg).
3. A motor does 4800 J of work in 8 s: calculate the power. A lamp transfers 20 J usefully from 80 J: calculate efficiency.
4. Compare fossil fuels, nuclear, wind, solar, hydroelectric and biofuel for reliability, cost and environmental effect.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
