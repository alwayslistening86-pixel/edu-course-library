# C03_Bonding_and_States_of_Matter - Practice: Chemical bonds and states of matter

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Draw dot-and-cross diagrams for sodium chloride and magnesium oxide, showing the electron transfer and the charges.
2. Draw dot-and-cross diagrams for hydrogen, water and methane and explain covalent bonding.
3. Describe metallic bonding as a sea of delocalised electrons around positive ions.
4. Describe the arrangement and movement of particles in a solid, liquid and gas, and write state symbols in the equation for sodium reacting with water.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
