# P05_Atomic_Structure_and_Radiation - Practice: Atoms, isotopes and radioactive decay

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe the structure of an atom, its size, and the isotopes carbon-12 and carbon-14.
2. Describe the plum pudding model, the alpha scattering experiment and how the nuclear model replaced it.
3. Compare alpha, beta and gamma radiation in charge, range, penetration and ionisation, and complete: U-238 alpha decay to thorium.
4. A sample has 800 Bq. Find its activity after 3 half-lives and explain the difference between irradiation and contamination.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
