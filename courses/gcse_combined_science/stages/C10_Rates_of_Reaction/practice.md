# C10_Rates_of_Reaction - Practice: Rates of reaction and catalysts

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A reaction produces 40 cm3 of gas in 20 s. Calculate the mean rate and the rate at 10 s from a tangent on a graph.
2. List the factors affecting the rate of reaction and explain each using collision theory.
3. Describe the sodium thiosulfate and hydrochloric acid investigation, identifying variables and how the rate is measured.
4. Explain how a catalyst lowers the activation energy and draw its effect on a reaction profile.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
