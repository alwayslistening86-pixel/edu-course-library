# P07_Motion - Practice: Distance, speed, velocity and acceleration

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A person walks 3 m east then 4 m west. State their distance and displacement.
2. A jogger covers 1500 m in 250 s: calculate the speed and compare with typical speeds of walking, cycling and driving.
3. Describe the motion shown by a distance-time graph with a steep slope, a horizontal section and a curve, and calculate speed from the gradient.
4. A car accelerates from 5 to 25 m/s in 8 s: calculate the acceleration. Higher: a car accelerates uniformly at 4 m/s2 from rest over 18 m; use v squared - u squared = 2as to find its speed.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
