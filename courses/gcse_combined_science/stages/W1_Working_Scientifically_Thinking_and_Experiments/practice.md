# W1_Working_Scientifically_Thinking_and_Experiments - Practice: Working scientifically: scientific thinking and experimental skills

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a hypothesis and list independent, dependent and control variables for an investigation into the effect of temperature on the rate of reaction between marble chips and acid.
2. Give two ethical questions science cannot answer alone and how evidence can still inform each.
3. Write a risk assessment line for using a Bunsen burner and boiling water.
4. A student's repeat readings are 12.1, 12.4 and 15.9 s. Identify the anomaly, calculate the mean of the good results and suggest two improvements.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
