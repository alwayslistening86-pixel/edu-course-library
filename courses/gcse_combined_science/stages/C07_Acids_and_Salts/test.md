# C07_Acids_and_Salts - Test: Acids, neutralisation and making salts

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Name the salt made from hydrochloric acid and sodium hydroxide. b) State the pH of a neutral solution and the ion that makes a solution acidic. c) Describe two steps in making pure dry crystals of a soluble salt from an insoluble base.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain why ethanoic acid has a higher pH than hydrochloric acid of the same concentration.'
