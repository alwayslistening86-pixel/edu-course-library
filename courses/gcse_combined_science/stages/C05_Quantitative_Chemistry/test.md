# C05_Quantitative_Chemistry - Test: Conservation of mass, moles and concentration

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Calculate the relative formula mass of sodium carbonate, Na2CO3. b) Explain why the mass of a solid product appears to increase when magnesium burns in air. c) Calculate the concentration in g/dm3 of a solution containing 12 g of solute in 400 cm3.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Calculate the mass of aluminium oxide formed from 5.4 g of aluminium in 4Al + 3O2 -> 2Al2O3.'
