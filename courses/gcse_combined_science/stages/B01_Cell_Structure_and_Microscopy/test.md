# B01_Cell_Structure_and_Microscopy - Test: Cell structure and microscopy

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Name three structures found in plant cells but not animal cells and give one function of each. b) An image of a cell is 8 mm wide and the magnification is x400; calculate the real width in micrometres. c) Explain why a root hair cell has no chloroplasts.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'An electron microscope image of a cluster of ribosomes is 12 mm long at a magnification of x100 000. Calculate the real length in micrometres, in standard form, and explain why an electron microscope was needed.'
