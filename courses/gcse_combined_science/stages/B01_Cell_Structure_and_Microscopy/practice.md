# B01_Cell_Structure_and_Microscopy - Practice: Cell structure and microscopy

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Make a table comparing a bacterial cell, an animal cell and a plant cell for: nucleus, cell wall, chloroplasts, mitochondria, plasmids.
2. A cell appears 45 mm long in a drawing. Its true length is 0.03 mm. Calculate the magnification. A different cell is 0.05 mm across; how wide is its drawing at a magnification of x200?
3. Explain two adaptations each of a sperm cell and a root hair cell and how each helps its function.
4. Describe how to set up a light microscope to view onion epidermis stained with iodine, and how to draw what you see with a title and a magnification.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
