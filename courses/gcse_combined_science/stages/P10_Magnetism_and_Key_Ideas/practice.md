# P10_Magnetism_and_Key_Ideas - Practice: Magnetism, electromagnetism and key ideas

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe the field pattern around a bar magnet and how to plot it with a compass, and distinguish permanent and induced magnets.
2. Describe the field of a straight wire and a solenoid and give ways to increase the strength of an electromagnet.
3. Higher: describe the motor effect and use Fleming's left-hand rule to give the direction of force on a wire.
4. Higher: calculate the force on a 0.3 m wire carrying 5 A in a field of 0.2 T, and describe how an electric motor turns.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
