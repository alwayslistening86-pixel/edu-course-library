# P10_Magnetism_and_Key_Ideas - Test: Magnetism, electromagnetism and key ideas

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Describe the magnetic field pattern around a bar magnet and how to plot it. b) State two ways to increase the strength of an electromagnet. c) Explain what happens when two north poles are brought together.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A 0.15 m wire carries 4 A perpendicular to a 0.25 T field. Calculate the force and state the direction rule.'
