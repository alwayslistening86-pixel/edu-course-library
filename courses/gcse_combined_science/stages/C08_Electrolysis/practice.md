# C08_Electrolysis - Practice: Electrolysis

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain what happens in the electrolysis of molten lead bromide, naming the electrodes, electrolyte and products.
2. Describe how aluminium is extracted from aluminium oxide dissolved in molten cryolite and why the carbon anodes are replaced.
3. Predict the products at each electrode for sodium chloride solution and copper sulfate solution using the rules for aqueous electrolysis.
4. Higher: write half equations for the production of chlorine and hydrogen and identify oxidation and reduction.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
