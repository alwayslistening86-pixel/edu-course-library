## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) State why Group 0 elements are unreactive. b) Describe what you would see when sodium is added to water. c) Give two physical properties of the Group 7 elements.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain why potassium is more reactive than lithium and why chlorine is more reactive than bromine, in terms of electron structure. Write an equation for chlorine reacting with potassium bromide solution.'
