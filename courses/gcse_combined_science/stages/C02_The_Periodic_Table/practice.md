# C02_The_Periodic_Table - Practice: The periodic table and groups

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe how Mendeleev arranged the elements and why he left gaps, and how the modern table is arranged.
2. Compare metals and non-metals in position, properties and the ions they form.
3. Describe the reactions of lithium, sodium and potassium with water, give the equation for lithium and explain the trend in reactivity.
4. Describe the trends in Group 7 and Group 0, and write an equation for chlorine displacing bromine from potassium bromide.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
