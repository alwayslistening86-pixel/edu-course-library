# B04_Circulation_Health_and_Plant_Organisation - Practice: The circulatory system, health and plant organisation

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe the pathway of blood through the heart, lungs and body, naming the four chambers, and explain why the left ventricle wall is thicker.
2. Compare arteries, veins and capillaries, and give the function of red cells, white cells, platelets and plasma.
3. Explain coronary heart disease and evaluate two treatments (statins and stents) with an advantage and a disadvantage of each.
4. Explain the difference between benign and malignant tumours and why a link between a lifestyle factor and a disease does not prove it causes it.
5. Name the plant tissues (epidermis, palisade and spongy mesophyll, xylem, phloem, meristem) and explain how guard cells and stomata control transpiration.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
