# C09_Energy_Changes - Practice: Exothermic and endothermic reactions

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Give two examples each of exothermic and endothermic reactions and everyday uses of each.
2. Describe how to measure the temperature change when an acid neutralises an alkali in a polystyrene cup, and the variables to control.
3. Sketch reaction profiles for an exothermic and an endothermic reaction and label the activation energy and overall energy change.
4. Higher: calculate the energy change for H2 + Cl2 -> 2HCl using H-H 436, Cl-Cl 243 and H-Cl 432 kJ/mol.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
