# P04_Particle_Model_and_Thermal_Physics - Practice: Particle model, density and specific heat capacity

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A block of mass 240 g has volume 30 cm3: calculate density. Describe how to find the density of an irregular solid using a displacement can.
2. Describe the arrangement of particles in solids, liquids and gases and explain why mass is conserved in a change of state.
3. Calculate the energy needed to heat 0.5 kg of water by 20 degrees Celsius (specific heat capacity 4200 J/kg degree C) and describe how to measure specific heat capacity of a metal block.
4. Calculate the energy to melt 0.2 kg of ice (latent heat 334 000 J/kg) and explain how gas particle motion causes pressure.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
