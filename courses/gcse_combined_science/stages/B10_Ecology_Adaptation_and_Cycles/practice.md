# B10_Ecology_Adaptation_and_Cycles - Practice: Communities, adaptation, cycles and sampling

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Define population, community and ecosystem, give abiotic and biotic factors that affect a community, and explain interdependence and competition.
2. Explain structural, behavioural and functional adaptations for a camel, a polar bear and a cactus. Higher: explain adaptations of extremophiles.
3. A field is 2500 square metres. The mean number of daisies in 1 square metre quadrats is 6. Estimate the population and explain how to place the quadrats randomly.
4. Describe the carbon cycle and the water cycle, naming the processes and the role of microorganisms in decay.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
