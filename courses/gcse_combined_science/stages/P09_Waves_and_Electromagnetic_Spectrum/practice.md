# P09_Waves_and_Electromagnetic_Spectrum - Practice: Waves and the electromagnetic spectrum

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Compare transverse and longitudinal waves with examples and define amplitude, wavelength, frequency and period.
2. A wave has frequency 50 Hz and wavelength 6.8 m: calculate its speed. Describe how to measure the speed of waves on a ripple tank or string.
3. Put the electromagnetic waves in order of frequency and give a use and a hazard of each.
4. Describe how to compare infrared radiation from different surfaces. Higher: explain how radio waves are produced and how refraction changes a wave.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
