# C04_Structure_and_Properties - Test: Structure, bonding and properties of substances

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Explain why simple molecules have low melting points. b) Give two properties of graphite that make it useful as an electrode. c) State what is meant by an alloy.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain why sodium chloride conducts when molten but not when solid, and why graphite conducts but diamond does not.'
