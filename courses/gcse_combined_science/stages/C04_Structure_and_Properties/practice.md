# C04_Structure_and_Properties - Practice: Structure, bonding and properties of substances

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the properties of ionic compounds: high melting point and conduction when molten or dissolved but not when solid.
2. Explain why simple molecular substances have low melting points and do not conduct, and describe polymers.
3. Compare diamond, graphite, graphene and fullerenes in structure, properties and uses.
4. Explain why metals conduct and are malleable, and why an alloy is harder than a pure metal.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
