# B08_Reproduction_DNA_and_Inheritance - Practice: Reproduction, DNA and inheritance

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Make a table comparing sexual and asexual reproduction: parents, gametes, variation, examples, advantages and disadvantages.
2. Describe meiosis and fertilisation, giving the chromosome number in a body cell and a gamete.
3. Explain what a gene, an allele and the genome are, and define dominant, recessive, homozygous, heterozygous, genotype and phenotype.
4. Draw Punnett squares for Bb x Bb, for two carriers of cystic fibrosis, and for the inheritance of sex, and give the probabilities.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
