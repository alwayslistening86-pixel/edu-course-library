# P08_Newtons_Laws_Braking_and_Momentum - Practice: Newton's laws, stopping distances and momentum

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain Newton's first law using terminal velocity, and Newton's third law using an example of a pair of forces.
2. A 1200 kg car has a resultant force of 3000 N: calculate acceleration. Describe the practical that investigates force, mass and acceleration for a trolley.
3. Explain thinking distance, braking distance and stopping distance, and list factors that affect each. State typical reaction times.
4. Higher: calculate the momentum of a 1500 kg car at 20 m/s and the common velocity when a 2 kg trolley at 3 m/s sticks to a stationary 1 kg trolley.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
