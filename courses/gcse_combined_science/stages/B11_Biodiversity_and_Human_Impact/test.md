# B11_Biodiversity_and_Human_Impact - Test: Biodiversity, land use and global warming

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) State two human activities that reduce biodiversity. b) Name two gases that contribute to global warming. c) Give one way biodiversity can be maintained.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Evaluate the impact of clearing tropical forest for palm oil on local biodiversity and on global carbon dioxide levels.'
