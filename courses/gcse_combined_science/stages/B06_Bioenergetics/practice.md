# B06_Bioenergetics - Practice: Photosynthesis, respiration and metabolism

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write the word equation and the balanced symbol equation for photosynthesis and for aerobic respiration.
2. Pondweed produces 30, 14, 8 and 5 bubbles per minute with the lamp at 10, 20, 30 and 40 cm. Describe the pattern and say what is happening. Higher: compare the light intensity at 10 cm and 20 cm using the inverse square law.
3. Explain three limiting factors of photosynthesis, interpret a rate graph, and list uses of the glucose a plant makes.
4. Compare aerobic respiration, anaerobic respiration in muscle and in yeast, and explain heart rate, breathing rate and oxygen debt in exercise. Define metabolism with examples.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
