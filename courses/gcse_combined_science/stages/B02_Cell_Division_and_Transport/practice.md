# B02_Cell_Division_and_Transport - Practice: Cell division, stem cells and transport in cells

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Describe what happens to the chromosomes during the cell cycle and mitosis, ending with two identical daughter cells.
2. Give two sources of stem cells in humans and plants, and one benefit and one risk of using stem cells in medicine.
3. A potato cylinder of mass 5.0 g gains 0.4 g in pure water and loses 0.3 g in a strong sugar solution. Calculate the percentage change in mass in each and explain both in terms of osmosis.
4. Compare diffusion, osmosis and active transport in a table. Higher: calculate the surface area to volume ratio of a 2 cm cube and a 4 cm cube and explain why large organisms need exchange surfaces.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
