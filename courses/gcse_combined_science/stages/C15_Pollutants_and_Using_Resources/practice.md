# C15_Pollutants_and_Using_Resources - Practice: Atmospheric pollutants, water and sustainable resources

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Name carbon monoxide, sulfur dioxide, oxides of nitrogen and particulates, and explain how each forms and its effect.
2. Describe the steps that make fresh water potable, and how sewage and waste water are treated.
3. Describe how to analyse and purify a water sample by testing pH, distilling and testing for dissolved solids.
4. Describe the stages of a life cycle assessment and ways to reduce resource use. Higher: evaluate bioleaching and phytomining.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
