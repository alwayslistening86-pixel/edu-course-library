## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) State the approximate percentage of oxygen and nitrogen in air today. b) Name two greenhouse gases and one human activity that produces each. c) Describe one way the carbon footprint of a product could be reduced.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain how carbon dioxide in the atmosphere decreased over billions of years and evaluate evidence that human activity causes global warming.'
