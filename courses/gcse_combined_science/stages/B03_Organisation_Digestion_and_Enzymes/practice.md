# B03_Organisation_Digestion_and_Enzymes - Practice: Organisation, the digestive system and enzymes

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Put cell, tissue, organ, organ system and organism in order and give a digestive-system example of each.
2. Name the enzyme, substrate and products for amylase, protease and lipase, and where each is made.
3. Describe the food tests for starch, reducing sugars, protein and lipids and what a positive result looks like.
4. Iodine stops turning blue-black after 300, 90, 60 and 150 seconds in amylase at pH 4, 6, 7 and 8. State the conclusion and optimum pH. Higher: explain the shape of the results using the active site, and explain the roles of bile in fat digestion.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
