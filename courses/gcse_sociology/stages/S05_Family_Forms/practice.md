# S05_Family_Forms - Practice: Family forms in the UK and globally: the Rapoports' family diversity, and nuclear, extended, reconstituted, lone-parent and same-sex families

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two of the Rapoports' types of family diversity, with an example of each.
2. Explain the difference between a vertically and a horizontally extended family.
3. Explain what a reconstituted family is and give a reason such families have become more common.
4. Explain why the Rapoports argued no single family type should be treated as 'the norm'.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
