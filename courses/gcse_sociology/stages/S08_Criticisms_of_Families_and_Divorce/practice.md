# S08_Criticisms_of_Families_and_Divorce - Practice: Criticisms of the family, and divorce: pattern, reasons for the rise, and consequences

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain two criticisms of the family as an institution, naming the perspective or theorist behind each.
2. Explain Delphy and Leonard's argument in your own words.
3. Explain three separate reasons for the rise in divorce since 1945.
4. Explain one consequence of divorce for children and one for spouses.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
