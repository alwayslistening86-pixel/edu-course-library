# S16_Life_Chances - Practice: Life chances: class, gender, race and ethnicity, sexuality/age/disability/religion, and Devine's revisit of the affluent worker

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain one way class shapes life chances, with supporting evidence.
2. Explain one example each of how gender and how ethnicity can shape life chances.
3. Choose one of sexuality, age, disability or religion, and explain how it can affect life chances.
4. Summarise Devine's finding about affluent workers and explain why it challenges the embourgeoisement thesis.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
