# S19_Research_Design_Sampling_and_Data_Collection_Methods - Practice: Research methods: research design, pilot studies, sampling, questionnaires, interviews, observations and mixed methods

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a research aim and a matching testable hypothesis on a sociological topic of your choice.
2. Explain the purpose of a pilot study, with an example of a problem it might catch.
3. Explain the difference between stratified and quota sampling.
4. Evaluate questionnaires versus unstructured interviews for researching a stated sociological topic, giving a clear recommendation.
5. Explain one strength and one ethical concern of covert participant observation.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
