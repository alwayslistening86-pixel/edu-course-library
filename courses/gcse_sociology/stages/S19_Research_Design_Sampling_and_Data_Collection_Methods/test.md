'a) Define a hypothesis. b) Name two sampling methods other than random sampling. c) State one strength of interviews over questionnaires.' Extended: 'A sociologist wants to investigate how teenagers' friendship groups influence their attitudes to school. Recommend and justify a suitable research method, referring to its strengths and weaknesses for this specific topic.'
