# S10_Education_Capitalism_and_Achievement - Practice: Education and capitalism: the correspondence principle, and educational achievement by class, gender, ethnicity, and parental choice

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain the correspondence principle in your own words, with one specific example of a school practice it applies to.
2. Compare the Marxist and functionalist views of education in a short paragraph, stating which offers a more convincing account and why.
3. Explain one piece of evidence for class-based inequality in educational achievement.
4. Explain one factor sociologists link to gender or ethnicity differences in achievement.
5. Explain Ball's concept of parentocracy and give one criticism of the policy of increased parental choice.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
