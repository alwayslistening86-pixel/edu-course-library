# S13_Criminal_and_Deviant_Behaviour - Practice: Criminal and deviant behaviour: class, gender, ethnicity and age, Albert Cohen, Carlen, and public debates

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain one reason official statistics might overstate working-class crime relative to white-collar crime.
2. Explain one theory for why recorded crime is higher among men than women.
3. Summarise Albert Cohen's explanation of delinquent subcultures.
4. Summarise Carlen's explanation of some women's offending.
5. Discuss one public debate about crime and punishment from the list above, giving more than one viewpoint.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
