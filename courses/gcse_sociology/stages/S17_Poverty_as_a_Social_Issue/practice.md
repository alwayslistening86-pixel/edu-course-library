# S17_Poverty_as_a_Social_Issue - Practice: Poverty as a social issue: absolute and relative poverty, the culture of poverty, Murray's underclass theory, and alleviating poverty

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Define absolute poverty and relative poverty, giving an example of each.
2. Explain Townsend's argument for measuring poverty relative to normal living standards.
3. Compare the culture-of-poverty explanation with Murray's underclass theory, noting one similarity and one difference.
4. Evaluate one government attempt to alleviate poverty, giving a strength and a limitation.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
