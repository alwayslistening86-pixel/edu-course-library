# S18_Power_Authority_and_Power_Relationships - Practice: Power and authority: Weber's three types of authority and sources of power, and power relationships by class, gender, sexuality, race, age, disability and religion, including Walby on patriarchy

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain Weber's three types of authority, giving a distinct example of each.
2. Explain the difference between a formal and an informal source of power, with an example of each.
3. Explain Walby's argument that patriarchy operates across several structures, naming at least two of them.
4. Choose two factors (from class, gender, sexuality, race, age, disability, religion) and explain how each can shape power relationships.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
