# S09_Roles_and_Functions_of_Education - Practice: Roles and functions of education: Durkheim and Parsons, meeting economic needs/social mobility/social cohesion, school types and alternatives to schooling

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain Durkheim's view of education's core role in your own words.
2. Explain Parsons' idea of education as a 'bridge' between ascribed and achieved status.
3. Give an example of how education might meet the economy's needs, and an example of how it might enable social mobility.
4. Explain the difference between home schooling and deschooling.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
