# S02_The_Sociological_Approach_Concepts_and_Classical_Theorists - Practice: The sociological approach: defining society and key concepts, the consensus/conflict debate, and classical theorists Durkheim, Marx and Weber

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Define socialisation, norms, values and roles, giving one example of each.
2. Explain the difference between a consensus and a conflict view of society.
3. Explain, in one sentence each, what Durkheim, Marx and Weber are each best known for contributing to sociology.
4. Explain why sociology developed as a discipline particularly in the 19th century.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
