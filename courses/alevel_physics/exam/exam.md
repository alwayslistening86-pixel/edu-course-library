# A-level Physics (AQA 7408): Astrophysics option - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Three papers under timed conditions: Paper 1 covers S01-S12's content up to 3.6.1 (60 marks of structured questions and 25 multiple-choice); Paper 2 covers S13-S18 with assumed earlier content (same format); Paper 3 has 45 marks on practical skills and data analysis (S22 and the required practicals) and 35 marks on Astrophysics (S19-S21). Questions are original; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage tests. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 17 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1] A photon of wavelength 250 nm strikes a metal of work function 3.2 eV. Calculate the maximum kinetic energy of the emitted photoelectrons in eV and the stopping potential. [4 marks]
2. [Paper 1] A stationary wave on a string of length 0.80 m has three loops at 150 Hz. Find the wave speed and the frequency of the first harmonic. [3 marks]
3. [Paper 1] A ball is kicked at 22 m/s at 40° above the horizontal from level ground. Find its range, ignoring air resistance. [4 marks]
4. [Paper 1] A wire of length 2.0 m and diameter 0.50 mm has resistance 5.6 Ω. Calculate its resistivity. [3 marks]
5. [Paper 1] A 0.25 kg mass on a spring (k = 40 N/m) oscillates with amplitude 6.0 cm. Calculate its period and maximum kinetic energy. [3 marks]
6. [Paper 1] Which are hadrons? Choose every correct option.
   A. proton
   B. positive pion
   C. muon
   D. neutron
7. [Paper 2] A 25 μF capacitor charged to 400 V discharges through a 20 kΩ resistor. Calculate the initial current, the time constant and the time for the pd to fall to 50 V. [4 marks]
8. [Paper 2] Calculate the height above the Earth's surface of a satellite with an orbital period of 6.0 hours (M_E = 5.97 x 10^24 kg, R_E = 6.37 x 10^6 m). [3 marks]
9. [Paper 2] 0.20 mol of an ideal gas at 290 K is compressed at constant temperature from 5.0 x 10^-3 m^3 to 2.0 x 10^-3 m^3. Find the initial and final pressures, and explain the change in molecular terms. [4 marks]
10. [Paper 2] Radon-222 has a half-life of 3.8 days. What fraction of a sample remains after 10 days, and what is the decay constant in s^-1? [3 marks]
11. [Paper 2] An electron is accelerated from rest through 1500 V and enters a uniform magnetic field of 3.0 mT at right angles. Calculate its speed and the radius of its path. [4 marks]
12. [Paper 2] Which quantities are equal to zero at the midpoint between two equal positive point charges? Choose every correct option.
   A. electric field strength
   B. electric potential
   C. force on a test charge
   D. potential gradient
13. [Paper 3] A student measures the period T of a pendulum for several lengths l and plots T^2 against l. The best-fit gradient is 4.10 s^2 m^-1, with worst-fit gradients of 4.22 and 3.96. Calculate g and its absolute uncertainty, and comment on agreement with 9.81 m s^-2. [5 marks]
14. [Paper 3] Describe how to verify the inverse-square law for gamma radiation, including safety and how to deal with the uncertain position of the source inside its holder. [6 marks]
15. [Paper 3] A star has apparent magnitude 4.5 and absolute magnitude -1.5. Calculate its distance in parsecs and light years. [3 marks]
16. [Paper 3] Compare a Cassegrain reflector with a refractor of the same aperture, referring to chromatic and spherical aberration. [4 marks]
17. [Paper 3] A galaxy's spectral line of rest wavelength 656.3 nm is observed at 675.0 nm. Using H0 = 65 km s^-1 Mpc^-1, find its distance. [3 marks]
