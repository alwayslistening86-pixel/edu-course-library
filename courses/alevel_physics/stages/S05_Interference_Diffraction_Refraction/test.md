# S05_Interference_Diffraction_Refraction - Test: Interference, diffraction and refraction

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator and the data booklet. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A grating with 300 lines per mm is illuminated by light of wavelength 540 nm. Calculate the angle of the second-order maximum and the total number of maxima seen. [4 marks]
2. In a double-slit experiment, 12 fringe spacings measure 43.2 mm on a screen 2.40 m from slits 0.40 mm apart. Calculate the wavelength. [3 marks]
3. Explain why the cladding of an optical fibre must have a lower refractive index than the core, and state what is meant by modal dispersion. [3 marks]
4. Light enters glass (n = 1.52) from air at an angle of incidence of 40°. Calculate the angle of refraction. [2 marks]
5. State two conditions needed for two sources to be coherent. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 d = 1/(3.00 x 10^5) m; A1 θ2 = 18.9°; M1 n_max = d/λ = 6.17, so 6; A1 total maxima 2 x 6 + 1 = 13.
2. [3] M1 w = 3.60 mm; M1 λ = ws/D; A1 6.00 x 10^-7 m.
3. [3] B1 total internal reflection needs light to travel into a lower-index medium; B1 so the ray at the core-cladding boundary can reflect totally; B1 modal dispersion: rays taking different paths take different times, spreading the pulse.
4. [2] M1 sin 40° = 1.52 sin θ; A1 25.0°.
5. [2] B1 same frequency (wavelength); B1 constant phase difference.
