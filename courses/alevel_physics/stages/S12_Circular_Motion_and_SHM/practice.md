# S12_Circular_Motion_and_SHM - Practice: Circular motion and simple harmonic motion

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A 0.20 kg ball on a 0.80 m string is whirled in a horizontal circle at 3.0 revolutions per second. Find the tension (ignore gravity). [3 marks]
2. A mass on a spring oscillates with amplitude 4.0 cm and period 0.50 s. Find its speed when the displacement is 2.0 cm. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] M1 ω = 6π = 18.8 rad/s; M1 F = mω^2 r; A1 57 N.
2. [3] M1 ω = 4π; M1 v = ω root(A^2 - x^2); A1 0.435 m/s.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
