# S07_Motion_and_Projectiles - Test: Motion along a straight line and projectiles

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: structured questions with marks shown (and some multiple choice). Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows working and may use a calculator and the data booklet. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A stone is thrown horizontally at 12 m s^-1 from a cliff 30 m high. Calculate the time to reach the sea, the horizontal distance travelled and the speed on impact. [5 marks]
2. Explain how air resistance affects the path of a projectile. [3 marks]
3. In required practical 3, a student plots h against t^2 and obtains a straight line of gradient 4.95 m s^-2 which does not pass through the origin. Calculate g and suggest a reason for the intercept. [3 marks]
4. A velocity-time graph is a curve. Explain how to find the instantaneous acceleration at t = 3 s and the displacement over the first 3 s. [2 marks]
5. A car accelerates uniformly from rest to 20 m s^-1 in 8.0 s. Calculate its acceleration and the distance covered. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 30 = (1/2)(9.81)t^2; A1 t = 2.47 s; A1 distance 29.7 m; M1 vertical velocity 9.81t = 24.3 m s^-1; A1 speed 27.1 m s^-1.
2. [3] B1 drag opposes the velocity and increases with speed; B1 horizontal speed decreases, so the range is reduced; B1 maximum height lower and the descent steeper than the ascent (asymmetric path).
3. [3] M1 g = 2 x gradient; A1 9.90 m s^-2; B1 a systematic error, e.g. a delay between switching off the electromagnet and the ball's release, or h measured to the wrong point on the ball.
4. [2] B1 draw a tangent at t = 3 s and find its gradient; B1 find the area under the curve from 0 to 3 s (e.g. by counting squares).
5. [2] B1 2.5 m s^-2; B1 80 m.
