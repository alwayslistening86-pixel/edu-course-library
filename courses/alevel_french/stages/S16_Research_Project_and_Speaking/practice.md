# S16_Research_Project_and_Speaking - Practice: The individual research project and the speaking exam

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Proposez un titre de projet de recherche sous forme de question, lié à un pays francophone autre que la France, et expliquez pourquoi il vous intéresse (en français, 3-4 phrases). [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B1 relevant title as a question; B1 linked to a French-speaking country other than France; B2 justification in accurate French (B1 for a partial answer).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
