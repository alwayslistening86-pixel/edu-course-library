# S16_Research_Project_and_Speaking - Test: The individual research project and the speaking exam

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: reading comprehension in French, translation, grammar in context and a short piece of writing or speaking, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no dictionary and no hints. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Carte de conversation (5 minutes de préparation, puis discussion avec le tuteur) : sous-thème « Le rôle du bénévolat ». Document : un graphique montrant que les retraités sont les plus nombreux parmi les bénévoles, et une citation : « Le bénévolat, c'est donner sans compter, mais aussi recevoir. » Questions : (1) Que montre ce document ? (2) Pourquoi, à votre avis, les jeunes s'engagent-ils moins ? (3) Le bénévolat devrait-il être récompensé ? [25 marks]
2. Présentation (2 minutes) de votre projet de recherche, puis discussion (environ 9 minutes) avec le tuteur, qui vous pose des questions sur vos sources, vos conclusions et vos opinions. [35 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [25] Mark out of 25 using this course's approximation of AQA's speaking criteria: knowledge of the sub-theme and response to the card (up to 5), discussion: developed, justified opinions and interaction with the examiner (up to 10), range and accuracy of language (up to 5), pronunciation and intonation, fluency (up to 5). The tutor plays the examiner, asking follow-up questions and challenging opinions.
2. [35] Mark out of 35 using this course's approximation of AQA's criteria: presentation of findings and knowledge of the subject (up to 10), analysis, conclusions and justified opinions in discussion (up to 10), spontaneous interaction (up to 5), range and accuracy of language (up to 5), pronunciation and fluency (up to 5).

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S16_Research_Project_and_Speaking` exactly. Mark each answer against its mark scheme (method marks need the method shown; a multiple-choice item is worth 1 mark and needs every correct option and nothing else). 60 marks in all; a pass needs at least 36 (60%, rounded up). Record `pass` or `fail` in `syllabus_status`, with the mark and a short honest note on what was missed.

## If not passed
Name the specific misconceptions the wrong answers show, run remediation on those, then re-test with fresh items of the same types (never this exact set).

## On pass
Record the pass. Every stage is now passed, so the cumulative exam becomes available.
