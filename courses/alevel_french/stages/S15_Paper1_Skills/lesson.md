# S15_Paper1_Skills - Lesson: Listening, reading and translation; word order and other constructions

## Goal
The learner applies strategies for listening and reading comprehension (gist, detail, inference, answering in French), translates accurately into English and into French, and uses word order after direct speech and other constructions (depuis and il y a, comparatives, indirect speech).

## Syllabus items taught here
- 3.3.1.14 - Word order
- 3.3.1.15 - Other constructions

## How to teach this
Give the learner a French headline with a false friend (e.g. « une librairie ») and ask for a translation. Teach in French as much as the learner can manage, switching to English to explain grammar. Each theme lesson gives key vocabulary, facts and arguments with at least one French-speaking country's context, then a grammar point used in context. Get the learner producing French every session: short spoken or written answers, then longer arguments. Statistics about French-speaking societies change; check recent sources (INSEE, Le Monde, France 24) before quoting them.

#### 3.3.1.14 Word order
**Word order: inversion after speech.** After direct speech, the verb of saying comes before its subject: « Je suis d'accord », dit-il. « Pourquoi ? » a demandé la journaliste. « C'est fini », répondit Julien. With a pronoun subject add -t- where needed: « Oui », répondit-elle ; « Non », a-t-il ajouté.

#### 3.3.1.15 Other constructions
**Other constructions.** Depuis + present for actions still going on (J'apprends le français depuis six ans = I have been learning); il y a + time = ago (il y a deux ans); depuis + imperfect for had been (Il attendait depuis une heure). Comparative constructions: plus … que, moins … que, aussi … que, autant de … que, de plus en plus, de moins en moins, plus … plus (Plus on lit, plus on apprend). Indirect speech: change tenses and pronouns after a past verb of saying: « Je viendrai » → Il a dit qu'il viendrait ; « Je suis fatigué » → Il a dit qu'il était fatigué ; « J'ai fini » → Il a dit qu'il avait fini ; questions: Elle a demandé si nous voulions partir.

**Paper 1 strategies.** Listening (30 marks): read the questions first, predict vocabulary, listen for gist then detail, watch for negatives, numbers and distractors; answer in French briefly; manipulate language rather than copying long chunks. Reading (50 marks): skim, then scan; answer the question asked; for inference questions, justify from the text; for summary tasks, cover the points required in your own words. Translation into English (10 marks): meaning first, then natural English; watch tenses, false friends (actuellement = currently, une librairie = a bookshop, sensible = sensitive), and depuis. Translation into French (10 marks): check genders, agreements, tenses and prepositions; don't leave gaps.
