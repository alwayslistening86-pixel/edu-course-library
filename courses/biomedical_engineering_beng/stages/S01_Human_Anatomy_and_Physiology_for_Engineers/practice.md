# S01_Human_Anatomy_and_Physiology_for_Engineers - Practice: Human Anatomy and Physiology for Engineers

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Classify the hip, knee and skull sutures by joint type, and state the practical consequence for someone designing a hip implant versus a knee implant. [3 marks]
2. A resting patient has HR = 72 bpm and SV = 65 mL. Calculate cardiac output in L/min. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 hip = synovial ball-and-socket, knee = synovial modified hinge, sutures = fibrous (immovable); B1 the hip's 3 rotational DOF are relatively straightforward to reproduce with a ball-and-socket implant; B1 the knee's combined rotation/translation is harder to reproduce, so knee prostheses need more complex articulating geometry.
2. [2] M1 CO = HR x SV = 72 x 65 = 4680 mL/min; A1 4.68 L/min.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
