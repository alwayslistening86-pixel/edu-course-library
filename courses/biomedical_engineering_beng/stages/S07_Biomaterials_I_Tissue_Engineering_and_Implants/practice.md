# S07_Biomaterials_I_Tissue_Engineering_and_Implants - Practice: Biomaterials I: Tissue Engineering and Implants

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Classify hydroxyapatite coating on a hip stem as bioinert, bioactive or bioresorbable, and state what that classification means for how it interacts with surrounding bone. [2 marks]
2. Give one advantage and one disadvantage of using a ceramic (e.g. alumina) rather than a metal for a hip replacement bearing surface. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 bioactive; B1 it forms a direct chemical bond with living bone rather than simply being encapsulated (bioinert) or gradually broken down and replaced (bioresorbable).
2. [2] B1 advantage: extremely low wear rate; B1 disadvantage: brittle, risk of catastrophic fracture under impact/malpositioning.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
