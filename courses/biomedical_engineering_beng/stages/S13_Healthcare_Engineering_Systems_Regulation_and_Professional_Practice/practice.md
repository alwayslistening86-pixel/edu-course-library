# S13_Healthcare_Engineering_Systems_Regulation_and_Professional_Practice - Practice: Healthcare Engineering Systems, Regulation and Professional Practice

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the risk-based classification (Class I, IIa, IIb or III) most appropriate for an implantable pacemaker, and give one reason. [2 marks]
2. Name the international standard framework for medical device risk management referenced in this stage. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 Class III; B1 it is implantable and life-sustaining, so carries the highest risk to the patient if it fails, requiring the most rigorous conformity assessment.
2. [1] B1 ISO 14971.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
