# S03_Introductory_Materials_Science_for_Engineers - Practice: Introductory Materials Science for Engineers

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A test specimen of cross-sectional area 25 mm^2 is loaded to 5.0 kN without exceeding yield. Calculate the engineering stress in MPa. [2 marks]
2. State whether bone in tension is closer in behaviour to a ductile metal or a brittle ceramic, and give one reason. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 sigma = F/A = 5000/(25e-6); A1 = 200 MPa.
2. [1] B1 closer to brittle (fractures with relatively little plastic deformation), because it is a stiff mineral-dominated composite, though it does show some toughening from its collagen phase.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
