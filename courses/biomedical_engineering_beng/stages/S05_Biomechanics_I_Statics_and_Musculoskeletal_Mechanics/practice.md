# S05_Biomechanics_I_Statics_and_Musculoskeletal_Mechanics - Practice: Biomechanics I: Statics and Musculoskeletal Mechanics

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Classify the ankle joint during a calf raise (standing on tiptoe) as a first-, second- or third-class lever, naming the fulcrum, effort and load. [2 marks]
2. A biceps tendon inserts 4 cm from the elbow joint. A 50 N weight is held in the hand, 32 cm from the elbow. Calculate the biceps force needed for equilibrium (ignore forearm weight). [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 second-class lever; B1 fulcrum = ball of the foot (ground contact), load = body weight (acting at the ankle), effort = calf muscle via the Achilles tendon (behind the ankle).
2. [2] M1 F x 0.04 = 50 x 0.32; A1 F = 400 N.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
