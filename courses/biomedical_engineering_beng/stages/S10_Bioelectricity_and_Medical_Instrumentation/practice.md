# S10_Bioelectricity_and_Medical_Instrumentation - Practice: Bioelectricity and Medical Instrumentation

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the approximate frequency range of clinically relevant ECG content, and the minimum sampling rate (Nyquist rate) needed to avoid aliasing it. [2 marks]
2. An instrumentation amplifier has a differential gain of 1000 and a common-mode gain of 0.1. Calculate its CMRR in dB. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 approximately 0.05-150 Hz; B1 minimum sampling rate > 2 x 150 = 300 Hz (Nyquist rate).
2. [2] M1 CMRR = 20 log10(1000/0.1); A1 = 80 dB.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
