# S09_Biochemistry_and_Cell_Biology_for_Engineers - Practice: Biochemistry and Cell Biology for Engineers

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State which membrane transport mechanism moves a molecule against its concentration gradient, and what it requires to do so. [1 mark]
2. An enzyme has Km = 2.0 mM. At a substrate concentration of 0.1 mM (well below Km), state (without calculating) roughly what order of kinetics the reaction rate follows with respect to substrate concentration. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [1] B1 active transport, requiring energy (ATP).
2. [1] B1 approximately first-order (rate roughly proportional to [S]).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
