# S06_Biomechanics_II_Mechanics_of_Biological_Tissue - Practice: Biomechanics II: Mechanics of Biological Tissue

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the solution. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State whether cortical bone is stronger in tension or in compression, and give one typical value for the one you say is stronger. [2 marks]
2. Explain in one sentence why a hip implant that is much stiffer than the surrounding bone can cause the bone to weaken over time. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 compression; B1 ultimate compressive strength ~170-200 MPa (any value in this range, or the tensile figure correctly identified as lower, ~130-150 MPa).
2. [2] B2 the stiffer implant carries a disproportionate share of the load for the same strain (sigma = E x epsilon), so the bone is under-loaded relative to its normal physiological loading and remodels to lower density/strength (Wolff's law) -- accept any answer capturing load-sharing + bone remodelling response.
