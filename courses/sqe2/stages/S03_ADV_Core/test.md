# S03_ADV_Core - Test: Advocacy: submission structure, examination-in-chief and cross-examination basics, and court etiquette

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Reading time (treat as your 45 minutes), then present: "You act for the applicant seeking an extension of 14 days to file a defence, due to your client's serious illness (medical letter in the bundle). Opposing counsel objects, saying the delay prejudices their client's cash flow." Prepare and deliver a structured 3-4 minute submission, then answer two questions the "bench" (your assessor) puts to you, remaining courteous and on-topic.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S03_ADV_Core` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S04_ADV_Worked_Scenarios.
