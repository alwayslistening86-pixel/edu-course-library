# S03_ADV_Core - Practice: Advocacy: submission structure, examination-in-chief and cross-examination basics, and court etiquette

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a one-sentence proposition (your opening line) for a submission asking the court to adjourn a hearing for two weeks.
2. Draft three open, non-leading questions for examination-in-chief of a witness who saw a fall on a wet floor.
3. Rewrite those three questions as closed, leading cross-examination questions designed to suggest the floor was clearly signed as wet.
4. List the three things you must confirm about your bundle before you stand up to make a submission.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
