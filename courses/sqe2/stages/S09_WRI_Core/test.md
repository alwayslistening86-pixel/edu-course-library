# S09_WRI_Core - Test: Legal writing: conventions, attendance notes, letters of advice and other conventional documents

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Reading time, then: "Facts: the client's flight was cancelled; the airline's terms state compensation is payable for cancellations within 14 days of departure unless caused by 'extraordinary circumstances'; the airline says the cancellation was due to a safety-related fault, which the client doubts." Write a 250-300 word letter of advice to the client stating your conclusion, reasoning, and next steps.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S09_WRI_Core` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S10_WRI_Worked_Scenarios.
