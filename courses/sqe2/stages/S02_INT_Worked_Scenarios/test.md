Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

Plan and simulate the interview (structure, questioning, objective-identification for this Wills and Probate context) and produce the attendance note, including any next steps flowing from the dispute.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S02_INT_Worked_Scenarios` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S03_ADV_Core.
