# S02_INT_Worked_Scenarios - Practice: Client interviewing across the five SQE2 practice-area contexts

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. For the Criminal Practice scenario, write three open questions you would ask before mentioning the police evidence.
2. For the Property Practice scenario, list the four pieces of information you must confirm before the interview ends.
3. For the Wills scenario, explain in two sentences why capacity should be addressed sensitively but directly during the interview, not left to guesswork afterwards.
4. Write a 120-word attendance note for the Business Organisations scenario, recording the founders' objectives and at least one unresolved point.
5. Compare the real objective you identified in the Dispute Resolution scenario with the client's opening request, in one sentence each.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
