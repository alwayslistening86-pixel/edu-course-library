# S05_CMA_Core - Test: Case and matter analysis: extracting issues and facts from a bundle, spotting gaps, and producing a client-focused analysis

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Reading time, then: "Bundle: (1) an email from the client's supplier confirming delivery of goods 'by 1 June'; (2) a delivery note signed and dated 10 June; (3) the client's attendance note stating the goods were needed 'urgently for a 3 June deadline' and the client wants to know if they can refuse to pay." Produce a structured analysis (issues, facts/conflicts, gaps, options, recommendation) within a self-imposed 45-minute limit.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S05_CMA_Core` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S06_CMA_Worked_Scenarios.
