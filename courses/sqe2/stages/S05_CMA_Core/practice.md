# S05_CMA_Core - Practice: Case and matter analysis: extracting issues and facts from a bundle, spotting gaps, and producing a client-focused analysis

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. From a short bundle of your own devising (an email and a letter that disagree on one date), write a two-line chronology flagging the conflict.
2. List three legal issues, phrased as questions, that could arise from a dispute over a defective used car sold privately.
3. Write one sentence identifying a decisive gap in a bundle that contains no signed copy of a contract both parties refer to.
4. Draft the "recommendation" paragraph only (60-80 words) for a client who could either accept a low settlement now or litigate with uncertain prospects.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
