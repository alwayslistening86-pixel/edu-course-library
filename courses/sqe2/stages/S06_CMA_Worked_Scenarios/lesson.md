# S06_CMA_Worked_Scenarios - Lesson: Case and matter analysis across the five SQE2 practice-area contexts

## Goal
The learner applies issue-spotting, fact-extraction and client-focused analysis to an unfamiliar bundle in each of the five SQE2 practice-area contexts.

## Syllabus items taught here
- SQE2-CMA-CRI-1 - Worked scenario: prosecution evidence bundle: apply case analysis skill to a Criminal Practice bundle
- SQE2-CMA-DR-1 - Worked scenario: breach of contract bundle: apply case analysis skill to a Dispute Resolution bundle
- SQE2-CMA-PROP-1 - Worked scenario: title and search bundle: apply case analysis skill to a Property Practice bundle
- SQE2-CMA-WILLS-1 - Worked scenario: contested will bundle: apply case analysis skill to a Wills and Probate bundle
- SQE2-CMA-BUS-1 - Worked scenario: shareholder dispute bundle: apply case analysis skill to a Business Organisations bundle

## How to teach this
Ask: "The METHOD from the last stage never changes - what changes is what counts as a 'relevant fact' in each area of law. What is relevant in a bail bundle is different from what is relevant in a title bundle." Use this to show that strong case analysis candidates carry a mental checklist of what typically matters in each of the five contexts, so they read faster and more accurately under time pressure.

#### SQE2-CMA-CRI-1 Worked scenario: prosecution evidence bundle
Scenario: a bundle of a witness statement, custody record, and interview summary in an alleged assault. Issues to spot: does the evidence establish each element of the offence, is there a defence disclosed (self-defence, identification issue), are there procedural points (was interview conducted properly, was disclosure timely)? Relevant facts: timing, any inconsistency between the witness statement and interview account, injuries described. Gaps commonly include missing CCTV or medical evidence. The client-focused recommendation should weigh realistic prospects of a defence against the practical costs (time in custody, trial risk) of contesting versus early resolution.

#### SQE2-CMA-DR-1 Worked scenario: breach of contract bundle
Scenario: correspondence, an unsigned draft agreement, and invoices in a dispute over late delivery. Issues: was a binding contract formed, what were its terms, was there a breach, what loss flows from it. Relevant facts: dates of correspondence showing offer/counter-offer, payment history. Common gap: no signed final contract, so terms must be inferred from conduct and correspondence - flag this prominently as it affects the strength of any claim. Recommendation should address realistic recoverable loss (not the client's largest possible figure) and the cost/benefit of litigating versus negotiating.

#### SQE2-CMA-PROP-1 Worked scenario: title and search bundle
Scenario: official copies of title, a local search result showing a planning condition, and a buyer's query about access. Issues: does title show good and marketable title, does the search reveal anything requiring further enquiry or a restrictive condition affecting use, is there a right of way properly recorded. Relevant facts: any restrictive covenants or charges on the title register, discrepancies between the plan and physical boundaries. Gaps often include missing consent for the planning condition. Recommendation should be practical: what enquiries to raise, and whether the issue is a deal-breaker or a manageable risk.

#### SQE2-CMA-WILLS-1 Worked scenario: contested will bundle
Scenario: the will itself, a medical note near the date of execution, and a letter from a disappointed beneficiary alleging undue influence. Issues: was the will validly executed, was the testator's capacity sufficient at the relevant time, is there real evidence of undue influence or merely suspicion. Relevant facts: date of the medical note relative to execution, who was present at signing, any change from a previous will and who benefited from the change. Gaps often include no attendance note from the solicitor who supervised execution. Recommendation should distinguish a genuinely arguable challenge from an emotional but weak one.

#### SQE2-CMA-BUS-1 Worked scenario: shareholder dispute bundle
Scenario: the articles of association, an email chain about a disputed board decision, and minutes of a meeting one director says was improperly convened. Issues: was the meeting validly convened and quorate, did the decision comply with the articles, is there a minority-shareholder remedy available (e.g. unfair prejudice). Relevant facts: notice given for the meeting, voting percentages, any relevant shareholders' agreement terms. Gaps often include no evidence of notice being served. Recommendation should weigh the cost and relationship damage of a formal remedy against a negotiated resolution between the shareholders.
