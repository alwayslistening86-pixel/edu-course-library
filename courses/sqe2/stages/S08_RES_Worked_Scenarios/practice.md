# S08_RES_Worked_Scenarios - Practice: Legal research across the five SQE2 practice-area contexts

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. For the Criminal scenario, list the two factors (culpability and harm) you would use first to categorise the offence.
2. For the Dispute Resolution scenario, state in one sentence what would happen to your conclusion if the claim involved deliberate concealment of the breach.
3. For the Property scenario, give one practical alternative to litigation you could recommend if the covenant is breached.
4. For the Wills scenario, apply the statutory intestacy formula (invented for practice: spouse takes a fixed statutory legacy then splits the remainder equally with children) to an estate of $500,000 with one child.
5. For the Business scenario, list the two duties most obviously engaged by an undisclosed-interest contract.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
