# S01_INT_Core - Practice: Client interviewing: structure, questioning technique and identifying objectives, and the attendance note

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Role-play a two-minute opening of an interview for a new client who has just said "I need help, my boss won't pay me what I'm owed" - do not ask a single closed question.
2. List three open questions and three closed questions you would use to establish the facts of a road traffic accident, in the order you would ask them.
3. A client says "I want them taken to court." Write the two follow-up questions you would ask to find their real objective.
4. Draft a 150-word attendance note from this exchange: "Client rang to say the other side has offered $5,000 to settle; she wants to know if she should accept; told her we would review the offer against her losses and call back by Friday."

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
