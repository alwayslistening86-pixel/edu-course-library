# S07_RES_Core - Lesson: Legal research method: identify the issue, find the sources, apply the law and reference it

## Goal
The learner frames a precise research question from an instruction, uses appropriate legal databases and materials to find sources, applies the law found to the client's facts, and presents a referenced research answer.

## Syllabus items taught here
- SQE2-RES-1 - Identifying the research issue: learn and apply: framing a precise, answerable legal research question from an instruction
- SQE2-RES-2 - Finding the sources: learn and apply: using primary and secondary sources, legal databases and materials
- SQE2-RES-3 - Applying the law to the facts: learn and apply: reasoning from the sources found to the client's actual facts
- SQE2-RES-4 - Presenting and referencing research: learn and apply: a structured, accurately referenced research answer

## How to teach this
Give the learner a vague instruction ("client wants to know about their rights") and ask them to turn it into one precise, answerable question before touching any source. Show that most marks are lost not from failing to find law, but from researching the wrong question - the real SQE2 task provides source materials rather than requiring live database searching, so framing the question precisely is what determines whether the provided materials get used well.

#### SQE2-RES-1 Identifying the research issue
Turn a client instruction or partner's request into a precise legal question ("Can the client terminate the contract for the supplier's late delivery?" not "tell me about contract law"). Identify what area(s) of law are engaged and what specific facts make the question answerable (the exact clause, the exact date, the exact status of the parties). A vague research question produces a vague, unusable answer even if every source found is technically correct, so spend real time on this step before reading anything provided.

#### SQE2-RES-2 Finding the sources
Know the standard hierarchy: primary sources (legislation on legislation.gov.uk, case law) take priority over secondary sources (textbooks, Practical Law, Halsbury's, professional guidance), which are used to locate and explain the primary sources, not as the final authority. In the SQE2 assessment, source materials are provided rather than searched for live, so the skill tested is selecting and using the RIGHT provided sources efficiently, not database search technique - skim headnotes/summaries first to identify which provided documents actually answer your framed question, then read those in full.

#### SQE2-RES-3 Applying the law to the facts
Do not stop at stating what the law says - apply it to the client's specific facts, reasoning through each element ("section 12 requires written notice; the client's notice was sent by email, so the question is whether email satisfies 'in writing' under this contract/statute"). Address counter-arguments briefly where the point is genuinely arguable both ways, and reach a reasoned conclusion rather than leaving the question open. This is the step markers weight most heavily: finding the right source with no application to the facts scores poorly.

#### SQE2-RES-4 Presenting and referencing research
Present the answer in a structured, skimmable format: the question, a short direct answer, then the supporting reasoning with accurate references to the specific source (section number, case name and citation, or paragraph of guidance) so a reader could check it themselves. Do not copy out large chunks of legislation or judgments - paraphrase and cite precisely. End with a clear conclusion restating the answer to the original question, since a partner reading only the first and last lines should still get the right answer.
