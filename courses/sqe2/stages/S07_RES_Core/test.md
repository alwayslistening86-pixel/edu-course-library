## Test scenario
Reading time, then: "Instruction: 'Client, a tenant, has been locked out by her landlord following a rent dispute - can she get back in?' You are provided with an extract stating that certain the eviction procedures are required by law before a landlord can regain possession of a dwelling, and that failure to follow them may give rise to a claim." Frame the precise research question, apply the provided material to the facts, and write a referenced answer with a clear conclusion.
