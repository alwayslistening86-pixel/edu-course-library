# S29_Forces_and_Newtons_Laws - Test: Forces and Newton's laws

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Particles A (3 kg) and B (5 kg) are connected by a light inextensible string over a smooth pulley and released from rest. Find the acceleration, the tension, and the force on the pulley. [5 marks]
2. A particle of weight 20 N hangs in equilibrium from two strings making angles 40° and 60° with the horizontal. Find the tensions. [5 marks]
3. A car of mass 1000 kg tows a caravan of mass 800 kg. Resistances are 400 N and 300 N respectively, and the driving force is 2500 N. Find the acceleration and the tension in the tow bar. [4 marks]
4. Forces (2i + 5j) N, (-3i + j) N and (pi + qj) N act on a 0.5 kg particle, which accelerates at (4i - 2j) m s^-2. Find p and q. [3 marks]
5. State Newton's third law and identify the partner force of the weight of a book resting on a table. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 5g - T = 5a; M1 T - 3g = 3a; A1 a = 2.45 m s^-2; A1 T = 36.75 N; A1 force on pulley 2T = 73.5 N.
2. [5] M1 resolves horizontally: T1 cos 40° = T2 cos 60°; M1 vertically: T1 sin 40° + T2 sin 60° = 20; M1 solves; A1 T1 = 10.15 N; A1 T2 = 15.56 N.
3. [4] M1 whole system 2500 - 700 = 1800a; A1 a = 1 m s^-2; M1 caravan T - 300 = 800; A1 T = 1100 N.
4. [3] M1 sum = 0.5(4i - 2j) = 2i - j; A1 p = 3; A1 q = -7.
5. [2] B1 statement; B1 the gravitational pull of the book on the Earth (not the table's reaction).
