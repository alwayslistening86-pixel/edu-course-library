# S31_Moments - Test: Moments

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A uniform beam AB, 5 m long and of mass 40 kg, is supported at C and D, 1 m from A and 1 m from B. A 30 kg child stands on the beam at E, x m from A, with x > 4. Find the value of x at which the beam would be on the point of tilting about D, and hence show that the child can stand anywhere on the beam without it tilting about D. [5 marks]
2. A uniform ladder of length 6 m and weight 200 N rests against a smooth vertical wall with its foot on rough horizontal ground, at 60° to the ground. Find the least coefficient of friction for equilibrium. [6 marks]
3. A light rod AB of length 2 m is hinged at A. A force of 30 N acts at B at 40° to the rod. Find the moment about A. [2 marks]
4. A non-uniform rod of mass 8 kg and length 3 m balances on a pivot 1.2 m from end A. Where is its centre of mass? [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 on the point of tilting, R_C = 0; M1 moments about D: 30g × (x - 4) = 40g × 1.5; A1 x - 4 = 2; A1 x = 6, which is beyond B, so the beam never tilts about D; B1 states the child can stand anywhere on the beam.
2. [6] M1 resolving vertically: R = 200; M1 moments about the foot: S × 6 sin 60° = 200 × 3cos 60°; A1 S = 57.74 N; M1 friction F = S; M1 F ≤ μR; A1 μ ≥ 0.289.
3. [2] M1 30 sin 40° x 2; A1 38.6 N m.
4. [2] M1 the centre of mass is directly above the pivot; A1 1.2 m from A.
