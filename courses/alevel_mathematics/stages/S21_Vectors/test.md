# S21_Vectors - Test: Vectors

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. OA = 2i + 5j and OB = 6i - 3j. Find (a) AB, (b) |AB|, (c) the position vector of the point P on AB with AP : PB = 1 : 3. [5 marks]
2. Show that the points A(1, 2, 3), B(3, 5, 4) and C(7, 11, 6) are collinear. [3 marks]
3. Forces (3i + pj) N, (qi - 5j) N and (-7i + 2j) N act on a particle in equilibrium. Find p and q. [3 marks]
4. In triangle OAB, OA = a, OB = b. M is the midpoint of OA and N the midpoint of OB. Prove that MN is parallel to AB and half its length. [4 marks]
5. A boat starts at 3i + j km and moves with velocity 2i + 4j km/h. A buoy is at 11i + 17j. Show that the boat passes through the buoy's position and find when. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] B1 AB = 4i - 8j; M1 root(16 + 64); A1 4root5; M1 a + (1/4)AB; A1 3i + 3j.
2. [3] M1 AB = 2i + 3j + k; M1 BC = 4i + 6j + 2k; A1 BC = 2AB, parallel with common point B, so collinear.
3. [3] M1 sum of components zero; A1 q = 4; A1 p = 3.
4. [4] M1 MN = (1/2)b - (1/2)a; M1 AB = b - a; A1 MN = (1/2)AB; A1 conclusion: parallel and half the length.
5. [3] M1 3 + 2t = 11 gives t = 4; M1 1 + 4t = 17 gives t = 4; A1 both give t = 4, so yes, after 4 hours.
