# S11_Trigonometry_Foundations - Test: Trigonometry: definitions, rules, graphs and radians

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. In triangle ABC, AB = 9 cm, AC = 7 cm and angle BAC = 72°. Find BC and the area of the triangle, each to 3 significant figures. [5 marks]
2. In triangle PQR, PQ = 10 cm, QR = 7 cm and angle QPR = 38°. Find the two possible values of angle PRQ, to 1 decimal place. [4 marks]
3. A sector OAB of a circle, radius 8 cm, has angle 0.9 rad. Find the area of the segment cut off by the chord AB, to 3 significant figures. [3 marks]
4. Sketch y = cos 2x for 0 ≤ x ≤ 2π, showing where it meets the axes. [3 marks]
5. Which are equal to sin 40°? Choose every correct option.
   A. sin 140°
   B. cos 50°
   C. -sin 220°
   D. `sin(-40°)`

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 cosine rule; A1 BC^2 = 81 + 49 - 126 cos 72°; A1 BC = 9.54 cm; M1 (1/2)(9)(7) sin 72°; A1 30.0 cm^2 (3 s.f.).
2. [4] M1 sine rule sin R / 10 = sin 38° / 7; A1 sin R = 0.8795; A1 R = 61.6°; A1 or 118.4°.
3. [3] M1 sector 32 × 0.9 = 28.8; M1 triangle 32 sin 0.9; A1 3.73 cm^2.
4. [3] B1 period π (two full waves); B1 starts at (0, 1); B1 crosses the x-axis at π/4, 3π/4, 5π/4, 7π/4.
5. Correct: A, B, C (exactly these options, no others)
