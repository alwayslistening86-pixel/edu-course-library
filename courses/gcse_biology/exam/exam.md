# GCSE Biology (AQA 8461) — separate science, Foundation and Higher tier - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` shows a passed test in the micro-profile.

## Format
Two timed papers at the learner's tier, each 1 hour 45 minutes and 100 marks, matching AQA's real structure: Paper 1 covers topics 4.1 to 4.4 (cell biology, organisation, infection and response, bioenergetics) and Paper 2 covers 4.5 to 4.7 (homeostasis and response, inheritance, variation and evolution, ecology). Each paper contains a mix of multiple-choice, structured, closed short-answer and open-response questions, with about 15 per cent of marks on mathematical skills and 15 per cent on working scientifically and required practicals.

## Example structure
Paper 1 draws on S01-S06 and S07-S08, mixing questions across topics rather than grouping them; Paper 2 draws on S09-S17. Include at least one required-practical-based question in each paper (S01-S03, S05, S09, S11, S15, S16) and questions using S18-S20 skills. At Higher tier include Higher-only content such as the inverse square law, ADH and dialysis in detail, and evaluation questions.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `exam_rubric` exactly. A pass requires consistent performance across all topics under timed conditions with working shown and extended answers that use correct scientific terminology.

## Outcome
- **Pass** -- record `exam_status: "passed"` in the micro-profile. Course complete (for that tier).
- **Not yet** -- leave `exam_status: "available"`, name which stage(s) broke down under mixed/timed conditions, offer targeted review or a fresh retry paper.
