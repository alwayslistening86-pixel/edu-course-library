# S14_Evolution_Evidence_and_Classification - Practice: Theory of evolution, speciation, fossils, extinction, resistance and classification

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Compare Darwin's and Lamarck's explanations for the long neck of the giraffe and say why Darwin's is accepted.
2. Explain how two populations of one species on separate islands could become two species, and what would show it had happened.
3. Explain why the fossil record is incomplete and why that limits conclusions about early life.
4. Write the full classification of the human using the seven levels, and give the binomial name.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
