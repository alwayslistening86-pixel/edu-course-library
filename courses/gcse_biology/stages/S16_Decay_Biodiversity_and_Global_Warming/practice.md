# S16_Decay_Biodiversity_and_Global_Warming - Practice: Decomposition, environmental change, biodiversity and human impact

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain why compost heaps are turned and kept moist, using the three conditions for decay.
2. Explain two ways in which deforestation increases atmospheric carbon dioxide.
3. Give two consequences of global warming for biodiversity and suggest one action to reduce them.
4. Plan an experiment to test the effect of temperature on decay of milk and name one hazard and one control.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
