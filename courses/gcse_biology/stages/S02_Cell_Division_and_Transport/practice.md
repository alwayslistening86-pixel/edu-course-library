# S02_Cell_Division_and_Transport - Practice: Cell division, stem cells and transport across membranes

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Put in order and describe the stages of the cell cycle for a skin cell, and say how many chromosomes each daughter cell has if the parent has 46.
2. Give two ways in which adult stem cells and embryonic stem cells differ, and one ethical objection to each of two uses of stem cells.
3. A potato cylinder starts at 3.4 g and ends at 3.9 g. Calculate the percentage change in mass and explain what happened in terms of osmosis.
4. Compare diffusion, osmosis and active transport in a table covering: direction, energy needed, and one example each.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
