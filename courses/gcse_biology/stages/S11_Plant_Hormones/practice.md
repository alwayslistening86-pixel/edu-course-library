# S11_Plant_Hormones - Practice: Plant hormones, tropisms and their uses

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Draw a shoot leaning towards a window and mark where auxin is more concentrated and why the shoot bends.
2. Explain why a root grows downwards while a shoot grows upwards when a seedling is placed on its side.
3. A grower spreads ethene near green bananas before selling. Explain why.
4. Higher: design an investigation to test whether removing the shoot tip stops phototropism, stating the control and expected results.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
