# S05_Plant_Organisation_and_Photosynthesis - Test: Plant tissues, transport and photosynthesis

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Write the word equation for photosynthesis. b) Name the tissue that carries sugar around the plant. c) Give three uses of glucose in a plant.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain how transpiration transports water in a plant and describe how humidity, temperature and air movement affect its rate.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S05_Plant_Organisation_and_Photosynthesis` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S06_Respiration_and_Metabolism.
