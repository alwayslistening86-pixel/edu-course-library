# S05_Plant_Organisation_and_Photosynthesis - Practice: Plant tissues, transport and photosynthesis

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Draw a leaf cross-section and label the tissues, saying which tissue is best suited to photosynthesis and why.
2. Explain why a plant wilts and its stomata close on a hot, dry, windy day.
3. A graph of rate against light intensity flattens at high intensity. Give two factors that could now be limiting and how a grower could test which one.
4. Higher: a lamp at 10 cm gives a rate of 24 bubbles per minute. Predict the light intensity ratio at 20 cm and the likely bubble rate if light is the only limiting factor, and explain your reasoning.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
