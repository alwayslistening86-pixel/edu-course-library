# S10_Hormones_and_Reproduction_Control - Practice: Hormones, blood glucose, kidneys, the menstrual cycle, contraception and feedback

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Draw a flow diagram of the response to high blood glucose after a meal and to low blood glucose after fasting.
2. Explain why type 1 and type 2 diabetes need different management.
3. Explain the changes in FSH, LH, oestrogen and progesterone across one menstrual cycle.
4. Evaluate two methods of contraception, one hormonal and one barrier, stating one advantage and one disadvantage of each.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
