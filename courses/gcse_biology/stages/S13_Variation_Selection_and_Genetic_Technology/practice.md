# S13_Variation_Selection_and_Genetic_Technology - Practice: Variation, natural selection, selective breeding, genetic engineering and cloning

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. List two examples of variation in a human population caused by genes, two by environment and one by both.
2. Explain in four steps how antibiotic resistance could arise in a population of bacteria through natural selection.
3. A farmer breeds cattle for high milk yield over many generations. Explain how it works and give one risk.
4. Describe how a gene for human insulin can be inserted into bacteria and why plasmids are used.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
