## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Name the four chambers of the heart. b) Give two functions of blood plasma. c) Describe the difference between a benign and a malignant tumour.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'A stent and a statin are both used to treat coronary heart disease. Evaluate each treatment, giving one advantage and one disadvantage, and explain how lifestyle changes could reduce the need for either.'
