# S04_Heart_Blood_and_Disease - Practice: The heart, blood vessels, blood and non-communicable disease

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Trace blood from the vena cava to the aorta, naming each chamber, valve and vessel type on the route.
2. Compare arteries, veins and capillaries in a table on wall, lumen, valves and function.
3. A patient has blocked coronary arteries. Describe two treatments and one risk of each.
4. A study finds that people who eat more processed meat have more bowel cancer. Explain why this alone does not prove causation, and what additional evidence would help.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
