# S08_Defence_Vaccines_and_Drugs - Practice: Defence systems, vaccines, drugs, monoclonal antibodies and plant defence

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain in stages what happens when a vaccinated person meets the live pathogen for the first time.
2. Why can antibiotics not be used to treat a cold? Give two ways to slow the spread of resistance.
3. A new drug goes through a trial with 200 patients: 100 get the drug, 100 a placebo, and neither doctors nor patients know who is who. Explain the purpose of each feature.
4. Describe how monoclonal antibodies are made and how one could be used to deliver a drug to a tumour.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
