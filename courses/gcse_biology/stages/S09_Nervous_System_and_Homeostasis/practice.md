# S09_Nervous_System_and_Homeostasis - Practice: Homeostasis, the nervous system, the brain, the eye and temperature control

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Draw and label a reflex arc for withdrawing a hand from a hot object and say why the response is rapid.
2. Explain what happens to the lens, ciliary muscles and suspensory ligaments when you look from your phone to a distant tree.
3. Describe and explain the body's response when you are exercising in a hot room.
4. Higher: suggest how MRI scanning and studying brain-damaged patients each give different types of evidence about the function of a brain region.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
