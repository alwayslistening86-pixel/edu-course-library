# S15_Ecosystems_and_Adaptation - Test: Communities, abiotic and biotic factors, adaptations, food webs, sampling and material cycles

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: 'a) Name three abiotic factors. b) Describe one structural and one behavioural adaptation of a desert animal. c) Explain what is meant by a stable community.' Higher (once Foundation is secure): 'Explain how competition and predation could affect the population of a species in an ecosystem and show the effect on a graph.'

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S15_Ecosystems_and_Adaptation` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S16_Decay_Biodiversity_and_Global_Warming.
