# S15_Ecosystems_and_Adaptation - Practice: Communities, abiotic and biotic factors, adaptations, food webs, sampling and material cycles

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Construct a food web with grass, rabbits, mice, foxes and owls, and predict what happens to mice if owls are removed.
2. Sketch a predator-prey graph, label it, and explain the lag.
3. A field of 5000 m2 has quadrat counts of 3, 5, 2, 4, 6 in 1 m2 quadrats. Estimate the population of the species.
4. Describe how you would investigate whether daisies are more abundant in a shady area than a sunny one, naming the equipment and how to avoid bias.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
