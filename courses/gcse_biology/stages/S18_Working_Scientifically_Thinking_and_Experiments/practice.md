# S18_Working_Scientifically_Thinking_and_Experiments - Practice: Working scientifically: scientific thinking and experimental skills

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a hypothesis and list independent, dependent and control variables for an investigation into the effect of temperature on the activity of catalase in liver.
2. Give two examples of ethical questions science cannot answer alone and one way evidence can inform each.
3. Write a risk assessment line for using a Bunsen burner and boiling water when doing Benedict's test.
4. A pupil's results for the same reading vary from 8.1 to 9.4 s. Identify the type of error and suggest two improvements.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
