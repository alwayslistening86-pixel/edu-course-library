# S17_Trophic_Levels_and_Food_Security - Practice: Trophic levels, biomass transfer, food security and farming

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A food chain: grass (1000 kg), locusts (150 kg), lizards (12 kg). Draw the pyramid of biomass and calculate the percentage transfer at each stage.
2. Give three ways in which biomass is lost between trophic levels.
3. Explain why raising cattle in indoor, warm pens can increase meat yield, and give one ethical objection.
4. Describe how fishing quotas and larger net sizes help sustain fish populations.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
