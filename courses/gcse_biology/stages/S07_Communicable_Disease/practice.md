# S07_Communicable_Disease - Practice: Communicable diseases and their causes

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Make a table of measles, HIV, salmonella, gonorrhoea, rose black spot and malaria with pathogen type, symptom, spread and control.
2. Explain why washing hands and cooking chicken thoroughly reduces salmonella infection.
3. Suggest why TMV lowers a crop's yield even though the virus does not kill the plant quickly.
4. Explain why a vector such as a mosquito makes malaria harder to control than a disease spread only person to person.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
