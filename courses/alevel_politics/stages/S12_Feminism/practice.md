# S12_Feminism - Practice: Ideas and principles, types, and thinkers of feminism

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the distinction feminists draw between sex and gender. [3 marks]
2. Give two types of feminism. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 sex: biological differences between men and women; B1 gender: the social roles society ascribes to men and women; B1 feminists argue this shows gender roles are socially constructed and so can be changed, unlike biological sex.
2. [4] B2 liberal feminism; B2 socialist feminism; B2 radical feminism; B2 post-modern feminism, each with a brief, accurate one-line description of its distinguishing claim -- any 2 for full marks.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
