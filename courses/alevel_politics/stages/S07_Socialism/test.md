## Test items
1. Explain how Rosa Luxemburg's ideas differ from Beatrice Webb's on how socialism should be achieved. [6 marks]
2. [Paper 1, Section B style] Evaluate the extent to which the Third Way represents a betrayal of core socialist principles. [24 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 Webb: 'the inevitability of gradualness', a gradualist/parliamentary route to socialism; B1 Webb: the expanding state (not its overthrow) delivers socialism; B1 Luxemburg: rejects evolutionary socialism/revisionism as impossible, since capitalism rests on exploitation reform cannot remove; B1 Luxemburg: proletarian struggle for reform/democracy builds the class consciousness needed for revolution; B1 reflects the wider revolutionary-vs-evolutionary tension within socialism; B1 both nonetheless share core socialist commitments to collectivism/equality/ending exploitation, differing only on method.
2. [24] Mark out of 24 using Edexcel's generic levels for extended political writing: Level 1 (1-5) basic, undeveloped; Level 2 (6-10) some accurate knowledge, limited analysis, mostly descriptive; Level 3 (11-15) accurate knowledge, developed analysis of similarities/differences/tensions, some use of named thinkers, but uneven depth; Level 4 (16-20) accurate, wide-ranging knowledge, sustained analysis using thinkers effectively, evaluation reaching a judgement; Level 5 (21-24) excellent, precise knowledge, sophisticated analysis integrating thinkers throughout, and a fully substantiated, well-weighed judgement. Credit: betrayal - Giddens/Third Way accepts the free market and rejects state ownership/class conflict as central, prioritising equality of opportunity over equality of outcome, arguably closer to modern liberalism; defence - retains core commitments to community, social investment and a role for the state in enabling opportunity, seen by its advocates as a necessary modernisation to remain electorally viable and effective (New Labour's electoral success); balanced judgement using named thinkers (Marx/Engels, Webb, Luxemburg, Crosland, Giddens).
