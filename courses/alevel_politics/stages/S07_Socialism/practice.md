# S07_Socialism - Practice: Core ideas, tensions and thinkers of socialism

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two of socialism's core ideas and principles. [4 marks]
2. Explain the difference between social democracy and the Third Way. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 collectivism; B2 common humanity; B2 equality; B2 social class; B2 workers' control, each briefly and accurately explained -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [3] B1 social democracy seeks to humanise capitalism through redistribution, welfare and regulation while broadly retaining a large state role; B1 the Third Way (Giddens) goes further in accepting the free market, emphasising equality of opportunity and community over state ownership/class conflict; B1 both are gradualist/reformist, unlike revolutionary socialism.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
