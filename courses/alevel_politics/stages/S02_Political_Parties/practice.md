# S02_Political_Parties - Practice: Party functions and funding; the established parties; minor parties; parties in context

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two functions of political parties in UK democracy. [4 marks]
2. Explain one debate about how UK political parties are funded. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 providing a choice of programmes/manifestos giving a mandate; B2 recruiting and training leaders and candidates; B2 representing/aggregating interests; B2 organising government and opposition; B2 educating the electorate -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [3] B1 identifies a funding source (large individual/corporate donations, trade union funding, or state funding); B1 explains the concern (cash for influence/access, or taxpayer objection to funding parties they oppose); B1 links to a specific example or reform proposal.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
