# S03_Electoral_Systems - Practice: FPTP, AMS, STV and CPLS; referendums; electoral system analysis

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give one advantage and one disadvantage of first-past-the-post. [4 marks]
2. Explain one argument for and one argument against the greater use of referendums. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 advantage: e.g. simple/clear constituency link/tends to produce strong single-party government (developed); B2 disadvantage: e.g. disproportional outcomes/wasted votes in safe seats/disadvantages smaller, spread-out parties (developed).
2. [4] B2 for: e.g. enhances legitimacy/direct consent for major, irreversible change (developed); B2 against: e.g. reduces complex issues to a simplistic binary/campaigns vulnerable to misinformation (developed).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
