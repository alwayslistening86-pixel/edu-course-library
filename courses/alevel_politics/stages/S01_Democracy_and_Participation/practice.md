# S01_Democracy_and_Participation - Practice: Direct and representative democracy; the widening franchise; pressure groups and other influences; rights in context

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two differences between direct and representative democracy. [4 marks]
2. Explain the difference between the suffragists and the suffragettes. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 direct democracy is citizens deciding directly (e.g. referendum), representative democracy is citizens electing representatives to decide; B2 direct democracy gives a clear single-issue mandate, representative democracy allows trust/expertise/compromise between elections.
2. [3] B1 suffragists (NUWSS, Fawcett) used constitutional/peaceful methods (lobbying, petitions); B1 suffragettes (WSPU, Pankhurst) used direct action/civil disobedience, sometimes violent; B1 historians debate their relative contribution to the 1918 Act versus WWI service.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
