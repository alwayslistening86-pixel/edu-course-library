# S10_PM_and_Executive - Practice: Structure and powers of the Executive; ministerial responsibility; the Prime Minister and Cabinet

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two royal prerogative powers exercised by the Prime Minister/government. [4 marks]
2. Explain the difference between individual and collective ministerial responsibility. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 appointing/dismissing ministers; B2 making treaties; B2 deploying the armed forces; B2 granting honours, each briefly and accurately explained -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [3] B1 individual: a minister is personally accountable for their department/own conduct, may need to resign for serious failure/misconduct; B1 collective: Cabinet decisions bind all ministers publicly, who must support them or resign; B1 collective responsibility can be suspended by the PM in specific circumstances.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
