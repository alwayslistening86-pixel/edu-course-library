## Test items
1. Explain why class-based voting has declined in the UK since the 1970s. [9 marks]
2. [Paper 1, Section A style, non-source] Evaluate the extent to which the media determines the outcome of UK general elections. [30 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [9] Level 3 (7-9): explains partisan/class dealignment (weaker automatic party loyalty); explains changing occupational structure (decline of traditional heavy industry/union membership, growth of the service sector); explains rise of valence politics (competence, leadership) and issue-based voting (e.g. Brexit in 2019) cutting across class; cites specific elections as evidence; balanced range. Level 2 (4-6): partial. Level 1 (1-3): basic.
2. [30] Mark out of 30 using Edexcel's generic levels for extended political writing: Level 1 (1-6) basic, undeveloped; Level 2 (7-12) some accurate knowledge, limited analysis, largely descriptive; Level 3 (13-18) accurate, relevant knowledge with developed analysis of similarities, differences and connections, some evaluation; Level 4 (19-24) accurate, wide-ranging knowledge, sustained analysis and evaluation with a supported judgement, good use of named examples; Level 5 (25-30) excellent, precise, wide-ranging knowledge, sophisticated sustained analysis and evaluation throughout, and a fully substantiated, well-weighed judgement using precise up-to-date examples.
