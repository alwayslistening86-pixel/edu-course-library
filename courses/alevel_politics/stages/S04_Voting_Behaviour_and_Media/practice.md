# S04_Voting_Behaviour_and_Media - Practice: Three key general elections; the influence of the media

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two factors, other than class, that can influence voting behaviour. [4 marks]
2. Explain what is meant by partisan dealignment. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 age (e.g. younger voters more likely Labour/Green, older voters more likely Conservative); B2 gender; B2 ethnicity; B2 region; B2 education/graduate status -- any 2 with a brief accurate detail for full marks.
2. [3] B1 the weakening of voters' automatic, long-term loyalty to one party; B1 contrasted with earlier decades' strong class-based/family voting habits; B1 leads to more voters switching between elections based on short-term factors (leadership, issues, campaign).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
