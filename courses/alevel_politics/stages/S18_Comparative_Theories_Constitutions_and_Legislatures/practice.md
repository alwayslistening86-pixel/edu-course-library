# S18_Comparative_Theories_Constitutions_and_Legislatures - Practice: Rational, cultural and structural approaches; comparing UK/US constitutions and legislatures

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give the three theoretical approaches to comparative politics named in the specification. [3 marks]
2. Explain one structural difference between the UK and US constitutions. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 rational; B1 cultural; B1 structural, named correctly.
2. [3] B1 identifies a structural difference (e.g. codified/entrenched/federal US vs uncodified/unentrenched/unitary UK, or fusion vs separation of powers); B2 explains its consequence for how each system works.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
