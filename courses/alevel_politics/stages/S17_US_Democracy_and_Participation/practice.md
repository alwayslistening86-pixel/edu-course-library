# S17_US_Democracy_and_Participation - Practice: US elections; campaign finance; party ideas; party coalitions; interest groups; interpretations and debates

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference between a PAC and a Super PAC. [3 marks]
2. Give two typical differences between Democratic and Republican positions. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 a PAC (Political Action Committee) can donate directly to candidates but faces legal contribution limits; B1 a Super PAC can raise and spend unlimited money on independent political advertising, provided it does not formally coordinate with a campaign; B1 Super PACs were enabled by the Citizens United v FEC (2010) ruling.
2. [4] B2 Democrats more progressive on social/moral issues vs Republicans more conservative; B2 Democrats favour greater economic intervention/social welfare vs Republicans favour lower taxes/less intervention; B2 Democrats more supportive of expanded immigration pathways vs Republicans favour stricter enforcement, each with brief accurate detail -- any 2 for full marks.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
