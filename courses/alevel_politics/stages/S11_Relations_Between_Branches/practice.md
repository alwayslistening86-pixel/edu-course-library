# S11_Relations_Between_Branches - Practice: The Supreme Court; Executive-Parliament relations; the EU's impact; the location of sovereignty

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference between legal sovereignty and political sovereignty. [3 marks]
2. Give one example of the Supreme Court influencing the Executive. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 legal sovereignty: the ultimate, supreme legal authority to make/unmake any law, resting with Parliament in UK theory; B1 political sovereignty: where power actually lies in practice given real-world constraints; B1 example showing they can diverge (e.g. the electorate, devolved bodies, or the EU while a member holding practical/political influence despite Parliament's formal legal supremacy).
2. [2] B2 e.g. the 2019 Miller II/Cherry ruling that the prorogation of Parliament was unlawful, constraining executive action via judicial review/ultra vires (or another accurate, developed example).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
