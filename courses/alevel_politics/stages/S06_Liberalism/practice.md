# S06_Liberalism - Practice: Core ideas, tensions and thinkers of liberalism

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference between negative and positive freedom. [3 marks]
2. Give two of John Rawls's key ideas. [4 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [3] B1 negative freedom (classical liberalism): freedom from external interference/being left alone; B1 positive freedom (modern liberalism): the capacity/resources to fulfil one's potential, which may require state support; B1 links to the classical/modern liberal split on the proper size of the state.
2. [4] B2 theory of justice - society must guarantee each citizen a life worth living/fair distribution of resources; B2 the veil of ignorance - designing society's rules without knowing your own position in it, leading to fairer, more redistributive choices.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
