# S09_Parliament - Practice: Commons and Lords: structure, comparative powers, the legislative process, and Parliament-Executive interaction

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two types of member of the House of Lords. [4 marks]
2. Explain what the Salisbury Convention means for the House of Lords. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 life peers (appointed); B2 hereditary peers (a reduced number retained since 1999); B2 Lords Spiritual (senior C of E bishops), briefly explained -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [3] B1 the Lords should not block/wreck at Second Reading a bill implementing a manifesto commitment of the elected government; B1 respects the elected Commons' democratic mandate; B1 the Lords may still amend, and the convention can be contested when there is no clear single-party manifesto.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
