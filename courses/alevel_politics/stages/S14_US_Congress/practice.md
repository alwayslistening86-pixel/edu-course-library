# S14_US_Congress - Practice: Structure and powers; representation; the legislative function; oversight; interpretations and debates

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two differences between the House of Representatives and the Senate. [4 marks]
2. Explain why incumbents in Congress are so often re-elected. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 House 435 members apportioned by population vs Senate 100 (2 per state); B2 House 2-year terms (all up together) vs Senate 6-year staggered terms; B2 House can initiate revenue bills/impeachment vs Senate confirms nominees/ratifies treaties/tries impeachment, each briefly explained -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [3] B1 name recognition built up in office; B1 fundraising advantages over challengers; B1 constituency service and, in many states, favourably drawn ('safe') districts.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
