# S08_The_Constitution - Practice: Nature and sources of the UK constitution; change since 1997; devolution; debates on further reform

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two of the five sources of the UK constitution. [4 marks]
2. Explain why the UK constitution is described as 'unentrenched'. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 statute law; B2 common law; B2 conventions; B2 authoritative works; B2 treaties, each briefly explained -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [3] B1 it can be changed by an ordinary Act of Parliament; B1 no special majority or separate ratification process is needed (unlike, e.g., the US Constitution); B1 links to parliamentary sovereignty - Parliament can make or unmake any law.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
