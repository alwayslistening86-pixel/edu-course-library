# S05_Conservatism - Practice: Core ideas, tensions and thinkers of conservatism

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with a full answer before seeing the model. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Give two of conservatism's core ideas and principles. [4 marks]
2. Explain the key difference between traditional and one-nation conservatism. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B2 pragmatism; B2 tradition; B2 human imperfection; B2 organic society/state; B2 paternalism; B2 libertarianism/neo-liberalism, each briefly and accurately explained -- any 2 for full marks.
2. [3] B1 traditional conservatism is committed to hierarchic/paternalistic values and resistance to change; B1 one-nation conservatism updates this, accepting social reform/welfare to bind rich and poor together in response to industrial-era inequality; B1 both remain distinct from the New Right's neo-liberal individualism.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
