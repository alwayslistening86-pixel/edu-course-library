# A-level Politics (Edexcel 9PL0): UK Politics and Government, Feminism, and the USA - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Paper 1: one 30-mark source question (choice of two) plus one 30-mark non-source question (choice of two) on UK Politics, then a 24-mark essay (choice of two) on Core Political Ideas. Paper 2: the same structure for UK Government and Feminism. Paper 3A: one 12-mark question (choice of two) on any USA content area, one compulsory 12-mark comparative-theories question, and two 30-mark essays (choice of three) synoptically drawing on UK content from Components 1 and 2 where relevant. Questions are original, in Edexcel's style; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage tests. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 10 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1, Section A - source] Source (a newspaper report describing a local council using a citizens' assembly, a form of direct democracy, to decide how to allocate a climate-change budget, provoking criticism from some councillors that it undermines elected representatives). Using the source and your own knowledge, evaluate the case for greater use of direct democracy in the UK. [30 marks]
2. [Paper 1, Section A - non-source] Evaluate the extent to which the UK's electoral systems produce a fair outcome. [30 marks]
3. [Paper 1, Section B] Evaluate the extent to which liberals are divided over the role of the state. [24 marks]
4. [Paper 2, Section A - source] Source (an extract describing a Backbench Business Committee debate that led to a non-binding vote embarrassing to the government, which nonetheless changed no policy). Using the source and your own knowledge, evaluate the effectiveness of Parliament in holding the Executive to account. [30 marks]
5. [Paper 2, Section A - non-source] Evaluate the extent to which the UK constitution has been fundamentally transformed since 1997. [30 marks]
6. [Paper 2, Section B] Evaluate the extent to which feminists agree on the causes of gender inequality. [24 marks]
7. [Paper 3A, Section A] Evaluate the extent to which the Electoral College distorts US presidential elections. [12 marks]
8. [Paper 3A, Section B - compulsory] Using a rational, cultural or structural approach, evaluate why the UK and USA have such different types of executive. [12 marks]
9. [Paper 3A, Section C] Evaluate the extent to which the US Supreme Court has become a political rather than a judicial institution. [30 marks]
10. [Paper 3A, Section C] Evaluate the extent to which Congress is an effective check on the presidency. [30 marks]

Mark each answer by the matching generic-levels scheme (12/24/30 marks as shown). A pass needs at least 50% of the 252 marks overall and at least 40% on each paper.
