# S09_Summary_and_Synthesis_P2Q2 - Practice: Summary and synthesis: main ideas and Paper 2 Question 2

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Summarise this in one sentence: 'The library used to be quiet, with long tables and green lamps; now it has beanbags, vending machines and a café, and students chat over laptops.'
2. Source A: 'The market stalls were crammed together and the traders shouted prices across the aisles.' Source B: 'The supermarket hummed quietly, its aisles wide and tidy.' Write two comparative sentences on the difference between the two shopping experiences, each with an inference.
3. Explain why 'Source A has a pool' alone would not reach Level 3.
4. Write a full 6-8 sentence answer to: 'Use details from both sources to summarise the differences between the two schools.' after reading two short passages of your own choosing.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
