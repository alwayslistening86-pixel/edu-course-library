## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Prompt (as in Paper 1 Question 5 style): 'Look at the image of a deserted, overgrown playground at the end of summer.' EITHER write a description suggested by this image, OR write the opening part of a story about a place that has been abandoned. (24 + 16 marks) Plan for 5 minutes, then write your full response (350-450 words) within 40 minutes.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S18_Descriptive_and_Narrative_Craft_Paper_1_Section_B` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S19_Writing_for_Impact_Argument_and_Rhetorical_Devices.
