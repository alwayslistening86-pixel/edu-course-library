# S18_Descriptive_and_Narrative_Craft_Paper_1_Section_B - Practice: Descriptive and narrative writing craft; the Paper 1 Section B task and Question 5

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Given the title 'The Last Bus', write a five-point plan (opening line, development, turn, ending) in under 5 minutes.
2. Write one paragraph of pure description (no plot movement) of a place at a specific time of day, using exactly one carefully chosen image, no more.
3. Write the opening and closing line only of a short story so that the closing line echoes or answers the opening.
4. Time yourself writing 200 words of description or narrative from an image of your choosing in 20 minutes, then note where you slowed down.
5. Identify one cliche in a piece of your own writing and replace it with a fresh, scene-specific image.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
