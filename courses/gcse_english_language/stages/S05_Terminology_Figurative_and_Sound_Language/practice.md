# S05_Terminology_Figurative_and_Sound_Language - Practice: Terminology III: imagery, sound devices and figurative language

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Identify the device and explain its effect: (a) 'The wind whispered secrets through the reeds.' (b) 'The silence was deafening.' (c) 'Her smile was a locked gate.'
2. Write one simile, one metaphor and one personification about a train station at dawn, each to create a different mood.
3. Explain the difference between alliteration and sibilance with an original example each.
4. Pick two devices from a novel opening you are reading and write a full analytical sentence for each: device, quote, effect.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
