# S05_Terminology_Figurative_and_Sound_Language - Test: Terminology III: imagery, sound devices and figurative language

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Read: 'The fog rolled in like a slow tide, swallowing the harbour lights one by one. Boats groaned at their moorings. Somewhere a bell clanged, hollow and lonely, and the whole town seemed to hold its breath.' (a) Identify five language devices with a short quotation each. (b) For three of them write a full analytical sentence explaining the effect on the reader. (c) Explain why 'clanged' is a more effective choice than 'rang' in this context. (d) Identify what mood the passage creates and how the devices together support it.
