# S03_Terminology_Word_Classes_Phrases_Clauses - Practice: Terminology I: word classes, phrases, clauses and verb forms

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Label the word class of every word in: 'The old man quietly closed the heavy door because he feared the wind.'
2. Underline the subordinate clause and name its subordinator: 'She smiled, although her hands were shaking.'
3. Rewrite 'The window was smashed' in the active voice with a subject of your choice. What is lost when the passive is used?
4. Compare 'You might want to donate' with 'You must donate'. Explain the effect of each modal.
5. Write a two-clause sentence, then add one relative clause and one adverbial phrase, and label the additions.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
