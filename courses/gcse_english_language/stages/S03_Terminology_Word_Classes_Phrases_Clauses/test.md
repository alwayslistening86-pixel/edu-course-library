# S03_Terminology_Word_Classes_Phrases_Clauses - Test: Terminology I: word classes, phrases, clauses and verb forms

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Read: 'The children waited patiently by the gate. When the bus finally arrived, which was late again, they ran towards it and pushed inside. The driver, exhausted, said nothing.' (a) Identify a noun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition and conjunction from the passage. (b) Identify one subordinate clause and one relative clause. (c) State the tense and explain what a change to present tense ('wait', 'arrives') would do to the effect. (d) Rewrite the last sentence in the passive and explain what changes. (e) Explain why 'must' is stronger than 'could' by using each in a sentence of advice.
