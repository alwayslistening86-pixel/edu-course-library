## Test scenario
Original 19th-century-style extract: 'It has become the fashion among the more prosperous families to spend the summer months at the coast, whither they repair in numbers so considerable that the fishing villages, formerly the abode of quiet industry, are now overrun with idle bathers. One cannot but regret it. The fisherman, who has hitherto lived by the labour of his hands, discovers that he may live better by the leisure of others, and the old trade withers accordingly.' (a) Identify the likely form and time period with evidence. (b) Summarise the writer's argument in two sentences. (c) State the writer's attitude and quote two words that show it. (d) Identify three features of nineteenth-century prose (vocabulary, syntax or attitude) in the extract. (e) Explain how a modern writer might treat the same topic differently.
