# S20_Paper_2_Section_B_Viewpoint_Task_and_Technical_Accuracy_I - Practice: The Paper 2 viewpoint writing task, Question 5, and the technical-accuracy assessment objective

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Given the task instruction 'Write a letter to a newspaper explaining your point of view on whether homework should be banned', write the form/audience/purpose in three words each.
2. Combine three short simple sentences into one sentence using a semi-colon or a subordinate clause, without losing meaning.
3. Write four sentences on any topic that each open differently (subject-first, adverb, subordinate clause, minor sentence).
4. Find and fix one comma splice in a paragraph of your own writing.
5. Plan the four-part shape (hook/position, points, counter-view, close) for a viewpoint task of your choosing in bullet form only.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
