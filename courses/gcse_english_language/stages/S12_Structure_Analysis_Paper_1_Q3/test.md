## Test scenario
Original extract: 'The market was already loud when Ibrahim reached it, stalls unfolding like paper flowers, fishmongers shouting numbers into the heat. He weaved between crates and carts, counting his coins. Two weeks ago he would have run here, laughing. Two weeks ago, his brother had been beside him. Now he moved slowly, and the noise slid past him like water. At the far end stood the blue awning where his brother had worked. Ibrahim stopped. The awning was down, the stall bare, a rope hanging where the sign should be. A woman touched his shoulder and said his name, and he turned, and everything went quiet.' Question (2026 format): This extract is from the opening of a story. How has the writer used structure to build a growing sense of loss as the extract develops? You could write about: what the writer focuses your attention on at the beginning; how and why the writer changes this focus as the extract develops; any other structural features. (8 marks)
