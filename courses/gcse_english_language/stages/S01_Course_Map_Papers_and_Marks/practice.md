# S01_Course_Map_Papers_and_Marks - Practice: The course map: two papers, the marks and how a mark is decided

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. From memory, draw a table for Paper 1 and Paper 2 showing each question number, its marks and the assessment objective it tests. Check it against the lesson.
2. Explain to someone who has never seen the exam why Question 4 is treated differently from Question 1 on each paper.
3. Work out what percentage of the whole GCSE each of these is worth: Paper 1 Q4; Paper 2 Q3; the two technical accuracy marks (16 + 16); the Spoken Language endorsement.
4. Here are three one-sentence comments on the phrase 'a wall of grey cloud': (a) 'The writer says grey cloud.' (b) 'The metaphor of a wall suggests the cloud is solid and blocks any escape.' (c) 'The wall metaphor, contrasting with the soft idea of cloud, traps the reader in a claustrophobic sky, foreshadowing the characters' confinement.' Put them in ascending level order and say what quality moves each up.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
