# S01_Course_Map_Papers_and_Marks - Test: The course map: two papers, the marks and how a mark is decided

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: your friend says, 'I will just do well on the reading papers and hope my writing gets me through.' Answer these in a short written response. (a) State the length, total marks and weighting of each paper and how the marks are divided between reading and writing. (b) Explain why this plan is risky, quoting the AO5 and AO6 weightings. (c) List the four Paper 1 Section A questions with marks and AOs. (d) Describe in two or three sentences how an examiner decides between Level 2 and Level 3 on a reading answer. (e) State what the Spoken Language endorsement is worth in marks and how it is reported.
