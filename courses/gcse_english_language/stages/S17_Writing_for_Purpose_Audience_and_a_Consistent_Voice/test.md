# S17_Writing_for_Purpose_Audience_and_a_Consistent_Voice - Test: Adapting writing to purpose, audience and form; keeping one voice throughout

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Task: 'Write a speech for your school assembly persuading younger students that reading for pleasure matters.' Write your opening two paragraphs only (about 150 words), then write two sentences explaining which purpose-and-audience choices you made and why.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S17_Writing_for_Purpose_Audience_and_a_Consistent_Voice` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S18_Descriptive_and_Narrative_Craft_Paper_1_Section_B.
