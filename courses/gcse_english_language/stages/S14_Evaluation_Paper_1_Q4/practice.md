# S14_Evaluation_Paper_1_Q4 - Practice: Evaluating a text: Paper 1 Question 4 (20 marks) and different responses

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. For the statement 'The writer makes the house seem welcoming', write one sentence that agrees and one that disagrees, each with a short quotation from a text of your choice.
2. Turn this weak sentence into an evaluative one: 'It says the room was cold.'
3. Write a 3-sentence conclusion for an answer that partly agrees with a statement.
4. Write a full five-paragraph answer to the test question below under 20 minutes.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
