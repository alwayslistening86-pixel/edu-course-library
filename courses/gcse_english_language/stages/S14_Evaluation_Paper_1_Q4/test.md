## Test scenario
Original extract (second part, lines 15-30): 'The last of the guests had gone, and Marta was alone with the silence of the restaurant. The chairs were stacked upside down on the tables like a forest in winter. She wiped the same counter twice. Outside, snow was starting to fall, thick and slow, and for a moment she felt she might cry, or laugh, or simply sit down. Instead she counted the till. It was short again. She held the coins in her palm, cold as pebbles, and thought of her father, who had run this place for forty years and never once complained. She switched off the light, and the sign in the window went on humming, red and lonely, to the empty street.' Statement: 'Marta is shown to be crushed by the failure of the restaurant.' To what extent do you agree? In your response you could: consider your own impressions of Marta; evaluate how the writer describes Marta's feelings; support your response with references to the text. (20 marks)
