## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Read: 'Everyone knows that homework is a waste of time. My cousin never did it and got a job at eighteen. Teachers say it builds discipline, but they only say that because it is their job. A survey of 30 pupils at my school found that 27 dislike homework, which proves it should be banned.' (a) Identify two opinions, one anecdote used as evidence, and one generalisation. (b) Explain why the survey does not prove the conclusion. (c) State whether the writer is biased and show two signs of bias. (d) For the purpose of deciding whether homework helps GCSE results, say what kind of text would be more useful than this one.
