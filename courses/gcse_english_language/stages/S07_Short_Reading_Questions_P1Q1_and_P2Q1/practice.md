# S07_Short_Reading_Questions_P1Q1_and_P2Q1 - Practice: The short questions: Paper 1 Q1 and Paper 2 Q1

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Read: 'Mr Ade unlocked the shop at six each morning, swept the step, and turned the radio on low. He had run the shop for thirty years and had never once taken a day off sick.' Which four of these statements about Mr Ade are true? A: He opens the shop at six each morning. B: He sweeps the step in the evening. C: He plays the radio loudly. D: He turns the radio on low. E: He has run the shop for thirty years. F: He has taken occasional sick days. G: He has never taken a day off sick. H: He dislikes the radio. (Answer: A, D, E, G.) For each, say which line supports it.
2. Explain why a statement such as 'He seems like an unhappy man' would not be safe to shade, even though nothing in the passage directly contradicts it.
3. Write eight statements (four true, four false) about a paragraph of your own choosing, in the shade-the-boxes format both Paper 1 Q1 and Paper 2 Q1 now use. For each false one, label the trick (number, person, exaggeration, unmentioned idea).
4. The text says 'she carried two heavy bags'. Which of these is safe to tick: (a) she was tired; (b) she had luggage; (c) she was leaving for good?

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
