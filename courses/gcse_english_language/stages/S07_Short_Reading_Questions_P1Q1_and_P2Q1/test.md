# S07_Short_Reading_Questions_P1Q1_and_P2Q1 - Test: The short questions: Paper 1 Q1 and Paper 2 Q1

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Read: 'Every August the Okoye family drove to Whitby, four hours in a car that smelled of oranges and warm plastic. Uncle Tunde sang out of tune, and Ngozi, who was fourteen, pretended not to hear. At the top of the hill they always stopped, and her father, who had grown up in the town, pointed at the sea as if he had arranged it himself.' Part 1 (2026 shade-the-boxes format): Choose the four true statements about Ngozi's father. A: He grew up in the town the family were travelling to. B: He sang out of tune for the whole four-hour journey. C: He pointed at the sea as if he had arranged it himself. D: He was fourteen years old. E: He travelled to Whitby with his family every August. F: He disliked the yearly trip to Whitby. G: He always stopped at the top of the hill with the rest of the family. H: He was visiting Whitby for the first time. (Correct: A, C, E, G.) Part 2 (Paper 2 Q1 format, unchanged): Choose the four true statements about the family's journey as a whole. A: The family travel to Whitby in March. B: The car smells of oranges and warm plastic. C: Ngozi is fourteen. D: Uncle Tunde sings well. E: The father grew up in Whitby. F: They stop at the bottom of the hill. G: The journey takes two hours. H: The father points at the sea. (Correct: B, C, E, H.) Then write one sentence saying which statement in either part is the most tempting wrong answer and why.
