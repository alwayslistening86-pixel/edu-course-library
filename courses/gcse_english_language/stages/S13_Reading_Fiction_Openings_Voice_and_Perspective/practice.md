# S13_Reading_Fiction_Openings_Voice_and_Perspective - Practice: Reading modern fiction: openings, endings, narrative perspective, character and atmosphere

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Identify the perspective and one effect: (a) 'I never told anyone what I saw in the cellar.' (b) 'She had not slept, and the night seemed to know it.' (c) 'The town slept, unaware that by morning nothing would be the same.'
2. Describe the tone of this line and give two words that prove it: 'Well, that was a triumph, obviously.'
3. Read an opening page of a novel of your choice. Write what the writer focuses on first, and where the focus changes.
4. Rewrite two sentences from third person to first person and say what is gained and lost.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
