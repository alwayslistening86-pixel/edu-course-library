# S13_Reading_Fiction_Openings_Voice_and_Perspective - Test: Reading modern fiction: openings, endings, narrative perspective, character and atmosphere

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Original extract: 'When the removal van pulled away, Priya stood on the pavement and refused to go in. The house was tall and narrow and slightly too clean, like something that had been tidied for a photograph. She counted the windows (nine) and imagined a face behind each. Her mother called from the doorstep, brightly, and Priya answered "In a minute," in the voice she used when she meant never. Behind her the street was silent except for the flap of a loose flag.' (a) State the narrative perspective and tense, and one effect of each. (b) Describe the tone of the narration in two words with evidence. (c) State what the writer focuses your attention on at the beginning and how the focus changes by the end. (d) Write one paragraph on how the writer creates unease.
