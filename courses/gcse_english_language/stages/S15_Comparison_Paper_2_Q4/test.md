## Test scenario
Source A (original, 19th-century style): 'I confess I looked upon the railway with dread. The engine, a black and shrieking beast, tore the peaceful fields in two, and the passengers, herded like cattle, seemed to me less travellers than parcels.' Source B (original, modern): 'Honestly? I love a train. There is nothing better than a window seat, a flask of tea and two hours where nobody can expect anything of you. Fields blur, worries blur, and by the time I arrive I am nearly a different person.' Question: Compare how the writers convey their different thoughts and feelings about train travel. In your answer you could compare their different thoughts and feelings; compare the methods they use to convey their thoughts and feelings; support your response with references to both texts.
