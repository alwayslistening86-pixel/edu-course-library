# S23_Spoken_Language_Endorsement_Criteria_and_Reporting - Test: The Spoken Language endorsement's Pass, Merit and Distinction criteria and how it is reported

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Explain, in your own words (100-150 words): what must every candidate demonstrate to achieve at least a Pass in the Spoken Language endorsement, what distinguishes a Merit from a Pass, and how the final result appears on the certificate in relation to the GCSE grade.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S23_Spoken_Language_Endorsement_Criteria_and_Reporting` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass. This was the last stage: the cumulative exam is now unlocked once every stage test is passed.
