# S23_Spoken_Language_Endorsement_Criteria_and_Reporting - Practice: The Spoken Language endorsement's Pass, Merit and Distinction criteria and how it is reported

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. List the two general criteria that must be met at every grade before any grade-specific criterion is considered.
2. Compare the Pass and Merit wording on 'listening and responding to questions' and identify the one word that marks the difference in depth expected.
3. Watch or imagine a talk and decide, feature by feature, whether it is showing Pass, Merit or Distinction level engagement with the audience.
4. Explain in two sentences why the endorsement not affecting the GCSE grade does not make it unimportant.
5. Draft one question you could ask a partner after their talk that would let them show 'perceptive' (Merit/Distinction level) response rather than just 'relevant' (Pass level) response.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
