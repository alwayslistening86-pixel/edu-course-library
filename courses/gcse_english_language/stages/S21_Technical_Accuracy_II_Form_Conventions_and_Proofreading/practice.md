# S21_Technical_Accuracy_II_Form_Conventions_and_Proofreading - Practice: Standard English, spelling and vocabulary range; conventions of Paper 2 forms; proofreading; managing the exam room

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Find and correct three subject-verb agreement errors in a short passage your tutor gives you.
2. Build a personal list of eight ambitious words you can spell confidently, each in one original sentence.
3. Circle every repeated 'said'/'nice'/'good' in a piece of your own writing and replace at least three instances.
4. Write the opening two lines of a formal letter and a speech, showing the correct convention for each.
5. Write out your own minute-by-minute timing plan for a full Paper 1 sitting and a full Paper 2 sitting.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
