# S06_Reading_Explicit_and_Implicit_Meaning - Test: Reading closely: explicit and implicit information, themes and inference

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Read this original passage. 'Nadia arrived at the bus stop twenty minutes early, as she did every morning now. She liked it that way: no one to see her wait, no one to ask why she had stopped wearing her usual bright scarf. When the bus came, she took the seat by the window and turned her face away from the aisle. Her phone buzzed twice in her bag and she did not look.' (a) List four explicit things about Nadia. (b) Write three inferences about her state of mind, each supported by a short quotation and a because-clause. (c) State the main theme in a phrase. (d) A student says 'Nadia is angry with her friends.' Evaluate whether the text supports this, using evidence.

Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S06_Reading_Explicit_and_Implicit_Meaning` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.
