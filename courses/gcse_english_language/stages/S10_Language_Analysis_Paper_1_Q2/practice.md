# S10_Language_Analysis_Paper_1_Q2 - Practice: Analysing language: Paper 1 Question 2 (8 marks)

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Take the phrase 'the shop window blinked'. Identify the technique, quote a word, and write a two-sentence effect.
2. Replace 'walked' in 'She walked into the room' with three different verbs and describe how each changes what we think of her.
3. Write a full paragraph (five sentences) analysing this original extract for the effect of the setting: 'The pier stretched into the fog like a finger pointing nowhere. Gulls screamed. The sea slapped the legs of the boards, again and again, as if it wanted an answer.'
4. Take the paragraph above and score it against the Level 3 and Level 4 descriptors. What would lift it?

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
