# GCSE Spanish (AQA 8692), Higher tier - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Paper 1: a new passage the tutor reads aloud (or the learner reads once, at speed, if no audio is possible), with questions in English and Spanish, plus a dictation of at least 30 words. Paper 2: a role-play, a reading-aloud text with four follow-up questions and unprepared conversation, and a photo-card discussion with unprepared conversation on the theme (spoken or typed in Spanish). Paper 3: a new reading passage with comprehension questions and a translation into English of at least 50 words. Paper 4: a translation into Spanish of at least 50 words, a structured task of about 90 words, and an open-ended task of about 150 words. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 7 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1 -- Sección A, en inglés] (El tutor lee dos veces): «Mi hermano lleva tres años trabajando como profesor en Argentina. Le encanta el país, aunque al principio le costó acostumbrarse a la comida y al clima. El año que viene va a volver a España para estar más cerca de la familia.» Answer in English: (a) What has the brother been doing, and for how long? (b) What did he find hard at first? (c) What is he going to do next year, and why? [5 marks]
2. [Paper 1 -- dictado] (El tutor lee tres veces): «Es importante que reciclemos más para proteger el medio ambiente.» [10 marks]
3. [Paper 3 -- traducción al inglés, mínimo 50 palabras] Traduce al inglés: «Desde que tengo doce años, sueño con hacerme veterinaria porque me encantan los animales. Mis padres siempre me han apoyado, aunque saben que tendré que estudiar muchos años en la universidad. El verano pasado hice unas prácticas en una clínica veterinaria y confirmé que es exactamente lo que quiero hacer.» [10 marks]
4. [Paper 4 -- traducción al español, mínimo 50 palabras] Traduce al español: 'Every weekend I help my grandparents in the garden, and afterwards we usually have lunch together. Last year they taught me how to grow vegetables, and now I would like to have my own garden one day.' [10 marks]
5. [Paper 4 -- Question 3 style, unas 150 palabras] Escribe sobre la importancia de la tecnología y las redes sociales en tu vida, tus experiencias personales, y cómo crees que cambiará la tecnología en el futuro. [25 marks]
6. [Paper 2 -- Juego de rol] Estás hablando con un amigo español sobre planes para el fin de semana. (1) Saluda y sugiere una actividad. (2) Pregunta si le gusta esa actividad. (3) Explica por qué te gusta esa actividad a ti. (4) Responde a la pregunta del examinador. (5) Propón una hora para quedar. [10 marks]
7. [Paper 2 -- Tarjeta con fotos, nivel superior] Tema: el medio ambiente. Foto A: un grupo de voluntarios recogiendo basura en una playa. Foto B: una calle con muchos coches y humo. Describe ambas fotos y luego responde: (1) ¿Qué problemas medioambientales te preocupan más? (2) ¿Qué haces tú personalmente para proteger el medio ambiente? (3) ¿Crees que los jóvenes se toman en serio el cambio climático? (4) ¿Qué deberían hacer los gobiernos en el futuro? [25 marks]

## Answer key for the ready-made items (tutor only)
1. [5] B2 (a) working as a teacher in Argentina; for three years; B1 (b) getting used to the food and the climate; B2 (c) return to Spain; to be closer to the family.
2. [10] Sentido (up to 5): it is important that we recycle more to protect the environment, fully conveyed. Ortografía (up to 5): every word and accent correct (importante, reciclemos -- subjunctive spelling, medio ambiente as two words).
3. [10] Award 1 mark per correctly conveyed section: Since I was twelve; I have dreamed of becoming a vet; because I love animals; My parents have always supported me; although they know I will have to study for many years at university; Last summer I did work experience at a veterinary clinic; and I confirmed that it is exactly what I want to do.
4. [10] B1 Cada fin de semana ayudo a mis abuelos en el jardín; B1 y después normalmente comemos juntos; B1 El año pasado me enseñaron a cultivar verduras; B1 y ahora me gustaría tener mi propio jardín algún día; B1 correct present tense; B1 correct preterite (enseñaron); B1 correct conditional (me gustaría); B1 natural, accurate Spanish overall; B1 correct use of a personal (ayudo a mis abuelos); B1 correct word order.
5. [25] AQA Higher Q3 style, marked on AQA's real grid: content (up to 15) -- coverage and development of both bullets with a clear, developed and justified personal opinion; range of language (up to 5) -- variety of vocabulary and complex structures, more than one time frame, attempted subjunctive/conditional/passive; accuracy (up to 5) -- grammatical control.
6. [10] Mark out of 10 using the S13 approximation of AQA's role-play criteria: communication of the required information for each of the five bullets, including a genuine question, and appropriateness of the response to the unprepared bullet.
7. [25] Mark out of 25 using AQA's real photo card grid: description of both photos (up to 5); unprepared conversation -- answers to the card's questions and follow-on discussion, judged on task achievement, development and justification of opinions, and spontaneous interaction (up to 15, by far the largest component); accuracy of language, including pronunciation and fluency (up to 5).

## Grading
Mark reading and listening against answer keys; mark the dictation on sense and exact spelling; mark translations section by section against the scheme; mark each writing task and the speaking tasks against AQA-style content/range and accuracy grids. A pass needs at least 50% of the 200 marks overall and at least 40% on each paper. AQA's grade boundaries change every series, so the course does not claim a predicted grade.

## Outcome
- **Pass:** record `exam_status: "passed"`. The course is complete.
- **Not yet:** leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the weakest skills, offer targeted review, and retry with fresh materials.
