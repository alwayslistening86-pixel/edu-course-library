# S16_Escritura_Paper4 - Test: Paper 4 Writing: translation into Spanish and extended writing

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: reading comprehension in Spanish, translation both ways, a grammar exercise and a piece of extended writing or speaking, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no dictionary and no hints. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. [Paper 4 -- traducción al español, mínimo 50 palabras] Traduce al español: 'Last year I started a new hobby: I joined a football team, and now I train twice a week. It has helped me make new friends and feel much happier.' [10 marks]
2. [Paper 4 -- Question 2 style, unas 90 palabras] Escribe sobre tu instituto. Menciona: (i) cómo es tu instituto y qué asignaturas estudias; (ii) una experiencia reciente relacionada con los estudios; (iii) tus planes para el año que viene. [15 marks]
3. [Paper 4 -- Question 3 style, unas 150 palabras] «Muchos jóvenes pasan demasiado tiempo delante de una pantalla.» Escribe sobre el papel de la tecnología en tu vida, tus opiniones sobre las redes sociales, y qué te gustaría que cambiara en el futuro. [25 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [10] B1 El año pasado empecé una nueva afición; B1 me uní a un equipo de fútbol; B1 y ahora entreno dos veces por semana; B1 Me ha ayudado a hacer nuevos amigos; B1 y a sentirme mucho más feliz; B1 correct preterite (empecé, me uní); B1 correct present (entreno); B1 correct present perfect (ha ayudado); B1 correct infinitive constructions after ayudar a; B1 natural, accurate Spanish overall.
2. [15] AQA Higher Q2 style, marked on AQA's real grid: content/coverage of all three bullets (up to 10) and range and accuracy of language, including more than one time frame -- present, past and future/conditional (up to 5).
3. [25] AQA Higher Q3 style, marked on AQA's real grid: content (up to 15) -- coverage and development of both bullets with a clear, developed and justified personal opinion; range of language (up to 5) -- variety of vocabulary and complex structures, more than one time frame, attempted subjunctive/conditional; accuracy (up to 5) -- grammatical control.
