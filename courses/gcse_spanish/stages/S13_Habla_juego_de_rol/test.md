# S13_Habla_juego_de_rol - Test: Paper 2 Speaking: role-play and reading aloud

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: reading comprehension in Spanish, translation both ways, a grammar exercise and a piece of extended writing or speaking, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no dictionary and no hints. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. [Paper 2 -- Juego de rol] Estás en una oficina de turismo en España. (1) Saluda y di qué información necesitas sobre la ciudad. (2) Pregunta a qué hora abre el museo. (3) Describe un lugar que te gustaría visitar. (4) Responde a la pregunta del examinador. (5) Pregunta si hay descuento para estudiantes. [10 marks]
2. [Paper 2 -- Lectura en voz alta, nivel superior] Lee en voz alta (mínimo 50 palabras, preparado con antelación): «Mi ciudad tiene muchos lugares interesantes para visitar, como el museo de arte moderno y el parque central, donde mucha gente hace deporte los fines de semana. Personalmente, prefiero pasear por el casco antiguo porque me encanta la arquitectura histórica y los pequeños cafés donde se puede tomar algo tranquilamente.» Luego contesta cuatro preguntas del examinador sobre el texto. [15 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [10] Mark out of 10 using this course's approximation of AQA's role-play criteria: communication of the required information for each of the five bullets (task achievement), including a genuine, correctly-formed question, and appropriateness/spontaneity of the response to the unprepared bullet.
2. [15] Pronunciation and intonation (up to 5): clear, accurate Spanish vowel sounds, rr/j/ll pronounced correctly, natural sentence stress. Four compulsory questions on the text (up to 10, 2-3 each): full, accurate answers referring back to the text (e.g. what places does the town have; what does the speaker prefer and why).

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S13_Habla_juego_de_rol` exactly. Mark each answer against its mark scheme (method marks need the method shown; a multiple-choice item is worth 1 mark and needs every correct option and nothing else). 25 marks in all; a pass needs at least 15 (60%, rounded up). Record `pass` or `fail` in `syllabus_status`, with the mark and a short honest note on what was missed.

## If not passed
Name the specific misconceptions the wrong answers show, run remediation on those, then re-test with fresh items of the same types (never this exact set).
