# GCSE German (AQA 8662), Higher tier - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
Paper 1: a new passage the tutor reads aloud (or the learner reads once, at speed, if no audio is possible), with questions in English and German, plus a dictation of at least 30 words. Paper 2: a role-play, a reading-aloud text with four follow-up questions and pronunciation marks, and a photo-card discussion (description then unprepared conversation, spoken or typed in German). Paper 3: a new reading passage with comprehension questions and a translation into English of at least 50 words. Paper 4: a translation into German of at least 50 words, a structured task of about 90 words, and an open-ended task of about 150 words. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 7 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. [Paper 1 -- Sektion A, auf Englisch] (Der Tutor liest zweimal): «Mein Bruder arbeitet seit drei Jahren als Lehrer in Österreich. Er liebt das Land, obwohl er sich am Anfang schwer an das Essen und das Klima gewöhnen musste. Nächstes Jahr wird er nach Deutschland zurückkommen, um näher bei der Familie zu sein.» Answer in English: (a) What has the brother been doing, and for how long? (b) What did he find hard at first? (c) What is he going to do next year, and why? [5 marks]
2. [Paper 1 -- Diktat] (Der Tutor liest dreimal): «Es ist wichtig, dass wir mehr recyceln, um die Umwelt zu schützen.» [10 marks]
3. [Paper 3 -- Übersetzung ins Englische, mindestens 50 Wörter] Übersetze ins Englische: «Seit ich zwölf Jahre alt bin, träume ich davon, Tierärztin zu werden, weil ich Tiere über alles liebe. Meine Eltern haben mich immer unterstützt, obwohl sie wissen, dass ich viele Jahre an der Universität studieren muss. Letzten Sommer habe ich ein Praktikum in einer Tierarztpraxis gemacht und dabei bestätigt, dass es genau das ist, was ich machen möchte.» [10 marks]
4. [Paper 4 -- Übersetzung ins Deutsche, mindestens 50 Wörter] Übersetze ins Deutsche: 'Every weekend I help my grandparents in the garden, and afterwards we usually have lunch together. Last year they taught me how to grow vegetables, and now I would like to have my own garden one day.' [10 marks]
5. [Paper 4 -- Question 3 style, etwa 150 Wörter] Schreib über die Bedeutung der Technologie und der sozialen Medien in deinem Leben, deine persönlichen Erfahrungen, und wie sich die Technologie deiner Meinung nach in Zukunft verändern wird. [25 marks]
6. [Paper 2 -- Rollenspiel] Du sprichst mit einem deutschen Freund über Pläne fürs Wochenende. (1) Begrüße ihn und schlage eine Aktivität vor. (2) Frag, ob ihm diese Aktivität gefällt. (3) Erkläre, warum dir diese Aktivität gefällt. (4) Beantworte die Frage des Prüfers. (5) Schlag eine Uhrzeit vor, dich zu treffen. [10 marks]
7. [Paper 2 -- Fotokarte, gehobenes Niveau] Thema: die Umwelt. Foto A: eine Gruppe Freiwilliger, die Müll am Strand aufsammelt. Foto B: eine Straße mit vielen Autos und viel Abgas. Beschreib beide Fotos und beantworte dann: (1) Welche Umweltprobleme machen dir am meisten Sorgen? (2) Was tust du persönlich, um die Umwelt zu schützen? (3) Glaubst du, dass junge Leute den Klimawandel ernst nehmen? (4) Was sollten die Regierungen in Zukunft tun? [25 marks]

## Answer key for the ready-made items (tutor only)
1. [5] B2 (a) working as a teacher in Austria; for three years; B1 (b) getting used to the food and the climate; B2 (c) come back to Germany next year; to be closer to the family.
2. [10] Sinn (up to 5): it is important that we recycle more, in order to protect the environment, fully conveyed. Rechtschreibung (up to 5): every word correct, including the umlaut and subordinate-clause word order (wichtig, dass wir mehr recyceln, um die Umwelt zu schützen -- verb-final in the dass-clause, zu-infinitive at the end of the um...zu clause).
3. [10] Award 1 mark per correctly conveyed section: Since I was twelve years old; I have dreamed of becoming a vet; because I love animals more than anything; My parents have always supported me; although they know that I will have to study for many years at university; Last summer I did work experience at a vet's practice; and confirmed that it is exactly what I want to do.
4. [10] B1 Jedes Wochenende helfe ich meinen Großeltern im Garten; B1 und danach essen wir normalerweise zusammen zu Mittag; B1 Letztes Jahr haben sie mir beigebracht, Gemüse anzubauen; B1 und jetzt möchte ich eines Tages meinen eigenen Garten haben; B1 correct present tense with verb-second word order; B1 correct dative (meinen Großeltern); B1 correct perfect tense (haben ... beigebracht); B1 correct use of möchte + conditional meaning; B2 natural, accurate German overall.
5. [25] AQA Higher Q3 style, marked on AQA's real grid: AO2 content (up to 15) -- a lot of information conveyed in relation to the task, regular successful development of ideas with a clear, developed and justified personal opinion, clear communication, both bullet points covered; AO3 range and accuracy (up to 10) -- good variety of vocabulary, regular attempts at complexity of language and structure, successful references to more than one time frame (attempted subjunctive/conditional, e.g. würde, wäre), and grammatical control (errors classified as major = affects communication, minor = does not).
6. [10] Mark out of 10 using AQA's real role-play criteria (2 marks AO2 per task): for each of the five tasks, 2 marks if the message is conveyed without ambiguity, 1 if partially/ambiguously conveyed, 0 if not conveyed -- including a genuine, correctly-formed question, and an appropriate, spontaneous response to the unprepared bullet.
7. [25] Mark out of 25 using AQA's real photo card grid: response to the content of the photos (up to 5, AO2) -- both photos mentioned, at least one thing said about each; unprepared conversation (up to 15, AO1) -- quite a lot of information conveyed, regular good development of responses, judged on task achievement and spontaneous interaction, by far the largest component; accuracy of language (up to 5, AO3), including pronunciation and range of structures.

## Grading
Mark reading and listening against answer keys; mark the dictation on sense and exact spelling; mark translations section by section against the scheme; mark each writing task against AQA's real AO2 (content)/AO3 (range and accuracy) grids, and the speaking tasks against AQA's real AO1/AO2/AO3 split. A pass needs at least 50% of the 200 marks overall and at least 40% on each paper. AQA's grade boundaries change every series, so the course does not claim a predicted grade.

## Outcome
- **Pass:** record `exam_status: "passed"`. The course is complete.
- **Not yet:** leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the weakest skills, offer targeted review, and retry with fresh materials.
