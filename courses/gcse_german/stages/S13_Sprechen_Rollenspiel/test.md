# S13_Sprechen_Rollenspiel - Test: Paper 2 Speaking: role-play and reading aloud

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: reading comprehension in German, translation both ways, a grammar exercise and a piece of extended writing or speaking, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no dictionary and no hints. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. [Paper 2 -- Rollenspiel] Du bist in einem Touristenbüro in Österreich. (1) Begrüß und sag, welche Information du über die Stadt brauchst. (2) Frag, um wie viel Uhr das Museum öffnet. (3) Beschreib einen Ort, den du gern besuchen würdest. (4) Beantworte die Frage des Prüfers. (5) Frag, ob es eine Ermäßigung für Studenten gibt. [10 marks]
2. [Paper 2 -- Lautes Vorlesen, gehobenes Niveau] Lies laut vor (mindestens 50 Wörter, im Voraus vorbereitet): «Meine Stadt hat viele interessante Orte zu besuchen, wie das Museum für moderne Kunst und den Stadtpark, wo viele Leute am Wochenende Sport treiben. Persönlich gehe ich lieber in der Altstadt spazieren, weil mir die historische Architektur und die kleinen Cafés gefallen, in denen man in Ruhe etwas trinken kann.» Beantworte danach vier Fragen des Prüfers zum Text. [15 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [10] Mark out of 10 using AQA's real role-play criteria (2 AO2 marks per task): communication of the required information for each of the five tasks without ambiguity, including a genuine, correctly-formed question, and a spontaneous, appropriate response to the unprepared bullet.
2. [15] Pronunciation and intonation (up to 5, AO3): clear, accurate German vowel and consonant sounds (ch, r, umlauts), natural sentence stress. Four compulsory questions on the text (up to 10, AO1): full, well-developed answers referring back to the text (e.g. what places the town has; what the speaker prefers and why).

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S13_Sprechen_Rollenspiel` exactly. Mark each answer against its mark scheme (method marks need the method shown; a multiple-choice item is worth 1 mark and needs every correct option and nothing else). 25 marks in all; a pass needs at least 15 (60%, rounded up). Record `pass` or `fail` in `syllabus_status`, with the mark and a short honest note on what was missed.

## If not passed
Name the specific misconceptions the wrong answers show, run remediation on those, then re-test with fresh items of the same types (never this exact set).

Record the pass and move on to S14_Sprechen_Fotokarte.
