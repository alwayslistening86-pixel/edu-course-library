# S14_Travel_Tourism_Extended - Lesson: Travel and tourism — Higher-tier future, the pronoun y and sequencing actions

## Goal
The learner uses the simple future for holiday plans, the pronoun y, and après avoir/être + past participle to sequence past actions.

## Syllabus items taught here
- AQA-FR-T3.1-simple-future-H - Simple future for travel plans (Higher): use the simple future of regular verbs for holiday plans
- AQA-FR-T3.1-pronoun-y-H - Pronoun y (Higher): use y to replace a place already mentioned
- AQA-FR-T3.1-apres-avoir-H - après avoir + infinitive (Higher): use après avoir/être + past participle to sequence two past actions

## How to teach this
Ask: 'Where will you go next year, and what will you do there, in what order?' — this draws out the future tense, y, and a sequencing structure together.

#### AQA-FR-T3.1-simple-future-H Simple future for travel plans (Higher)
The simple future states a firm holiday plan: l'année prochaine, je visiterai le Maroc; nous resterons une semaine. As in earlier topics, alternate this with the near future (je vais aller) across a response for range, rather than using only one.

#### AQA-FR-T3.1-pronoun-y-H Pronoun y (Higher)
Y replaces a place already mentioned (there), sitting before the verb: "Tu es allé en Espagne?" "Oui, on y est allé en train" (Yes, we went there by train) — avoiding the clumsy repetition of "en Espagne." Y is especially useful in travel narratives, where a place is often named once and referred to repeatedly afterwards.

#### AQA-FR-T3.1-apres-avoir-H après avoir + infinitive (Higher)
Après avoir/être + past participle sequences two completed actions by the same subject, equivalent to English "after doing X": après avoir visité le musée, nous avons mangé dans un café (after visiting the museum, we ate in a café). Use avoir or être as the appropriate auxiliary for the first action exactly as in the ordinary perfect tense, and remember this structure needs the same subject for both actions.
