# S17_Environment_Core - Lesson: The environment and where people live — vocabulary, description, advice and comparison

## Goal
The learner describes their local area and gives environmental advice using il y a/il y avait, il faut/on doit, place prepositions and comparatives.

## Syllabus items taught here
- AQA-FR-T3.3-vocab - The environment and where people live: vocabulary: home and housing, town vs countryside, pollution, climate change, recycling, public transport
- AQA-FR-T3.3-il-faut-devoir-F - il faut / on doit + infinitive: give environmental advice with il faut and on doit + infinitive
- AQA-FR-T3.3-il-y-a-avait-F - il y a / il y avait: describe what there is/was in an area using il y a and il y avait
- AQA-FR-T3.3-place-prep-F - Prepositions of place: use à côté de, en face de, près de accurately to describe a local area
- AQA-FR-T3.3-comparatives-F - Comparatives, town and country: compare town and country life using comparatives

## How to teach this
Ask: 'What's in your local area, what was there before, and what should people do to protect the environment?' This draws together il faut from the Healthy living topic with new description structures.

#### AQA-FR-T3.3-vocab The environment and where people live: vocabulary
Core vocabulary: l'environnement, la pollution, recycler (to recycle), gaspiller (to waste), le réchauffement climatique (global warming), les transports en commun (public transport), la campagne (countryside — a false-friend trap, not "campaign"), le quartier (neighbourhood), le déchet (waste/rubbish), protéger (to protect). Add housing words (un appartement, une maison, le loyer) and urban/rural comparison vocabulary (bruyant/calme, pollué).

#### AQA-FR-T3.3-il-faut-devoir-F il faut / on doit + infinitive
Environmental advice reuses il faut (it is necessary to) and on doit (we/people must) + infinitive from the Healthy living topic: il faut recycler le plastique; on doit protéger l'environnement. Negative: il ne faut pas gaspiller l'eau (you must not waste water).

#### AQA-FR-T3.3-il-y-a-avait-F il y a / il y avait
Il y a (there is/are) describes the present state of an area; il y avait (there was/were, imperfect) describes how it used to be, letting learners show change over time: il y a un parc en face de mon collège; avant, il y avait moins de voitures. Il y a peu de + noun (there is little/few) is a useful negative-flavoured variant: il y a peu de transports en commun ici.

#### AQA-FR-T3.3-place-prep-F Prepositions of place
Describe exactly where something is with fixed prepositional phrases: à côté de (next to), en face de (opposite), près de (near), loin de (far from) — each followed by de/du/de la/des as usual before the noun: en face de mon collège, à côté de la gare, près du parc.

#### AQA-FR-T3.3-comparatives-F Comparatives, town and country
Compare urban and rural life using plus/moins/aussi...que: la ville est plus bruyante que la campagne (the town is noisier than the countryside); la campagne est moins polluée. This directly supports a balanced answer weighing where the learner would prefer to live.
