# S19_Listening_and_Reading_Papers - Test: Listening and Reading papers — comprehension, dictation and translation into English

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: "Complète un exercice de compréhension (5 questions), une dictée de 20 mots, et traduis un paragraphe de 35 mots en anglais." Higher (once Foundation is secure): "Complète les mêmes tâches au niveau Higher (dictée de 30 mots, traduction de 50 mots), avec une précision quasi totale."

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S19_Listening_and_Reading_Papers` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S20_Speaking_Paper.
