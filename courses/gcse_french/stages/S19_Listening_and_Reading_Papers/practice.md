# S19_Listening_and_Reading_Papers - Practice: Listening and Reading papers — comprehension, dictation and translation into English

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. List three things to do in the reading/preparation time before a Listening exam recording starts.
2. Write a short dictation from a partner reading five simple French sentences aloud, checking your spelling and accents afterwards.
3. Identify the specific detail asked for in a sample comprehension question versus a vague answer that would lose marks.
4. Translate a six-sentence French paragraph into natural English, then check every negative, tense and named detail is present.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
