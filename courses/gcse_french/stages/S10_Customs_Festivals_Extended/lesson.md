# S10_Customs_Festivals_Extended - Lesson: Customs, festivals and celebrations — Higher-tier passive, relative clauses, en and future

## Goal
The learner uses the present passive, relative pronouns où/que, the pronoun en and the simple future to describe festivals with more sophistication.

## Syllabus items taught here
- AQA-FR-T2.2-passive-H - Present passive (Higher): form and recognise the simple present passive (les crêpes sont mangées)
- AQA-FR-T2.2-rel-ou-que-H - Relative pronouns où and que (Higher): use où and que to build longer descriptive sentences about a place or event
- AQA-FR-T2.2-en-H - Pronoun en, festivals (Higher): use en to avoid repeating a quantity already mentioned
- AQA-FR-T2.2-simple-future-H - Simple future, festivals (Higher): use the simple future of regular verbs for a family's future celebration plans

## How to teach this
Ask: 'Describe the city or place where you celebrated, what is done there, and what will happen next year' — this pulls in a relative clause, a passive and a future tense together.

#### AQA-FR-T2.2-passive-H Present passive (Higher)
The present passive (être + past participle, agreeing with the subject) describes an action done to the subject without naming who does it: les cadeaux sont ouverts le matin (the presents are opened in the morning); les crêpes sont mangées le 2 février. Compare with the active on + present taught in the previous stage — both describe customs, but the passive shifts focus onto the object.

#### AQA-FR-T2.2-rel-ou-que-H Relative pronouns où and que (Higher)
Où (where/when) introduces a clause about a place or time already mentioned: la ville où nous avons fêté Noël (the city where we celebrated Christmas). Que (that/which, direct object) was introduced in the Identity topic and applies equally here: la tradition que je préfère (the tradition that I prefer). Using either correctly, rather than two short separate sentences, is a clear Higher-tier marker.

#### AQA-FR-T2.2-en-H Pronoun en, festivals (Higher)
En replaces a quantity already mentioned, avoiding repetition: "Tu manges des crêpes à la Chandeleur?" "Oui, on en mange beaucoup" (Yes, we eat a lot of them). It sits directly before the conjugated verb.

#### AQA-FR-T2.2-simple-future-H Simple future, festivals (Higher)
The simple future of regular verbs states a firm future plan for a celebration: nous fêterons son anniversaire en famille l'année prochaine; on organisera un grand repas. As in Education and work, this sits alongside the near future for variety across a Higher response.
