# S01_Identity_Relationships_Core - Practice: Identity and relationships with others — core present tense, possessives, agreement

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write three sentences describing your family using avoir, être and at least one possessive adjective each.
2. Correct the errors: "Ma frère est intelligent" and "J'ai une sœur sympas."
3. Describe a friend's personality using at least three different agreeing adjectives.
4. Translate into French: "My stepmother is very kind and my half-brother is talkative."

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
