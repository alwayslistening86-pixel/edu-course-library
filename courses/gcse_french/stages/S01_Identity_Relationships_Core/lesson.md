# S01_Identity_Relationships_Core - Lesson: Identity and relationships with others — core present tense, possessives, agreement

## Goal
The learner describes self, family and friends using avoir/être, regular -er verbs, possessive adjectives and adjective agreement.

## Syllabus items taught here
- AQA-FR-T1.1-vocab - Identity and relationships: vocabulary: family members, friendship, adult relationships (marriage, partnership), personality and describing people, getting on/falling out
- AQA-FR-T1.1-avoir-etre-F - Present tense of avoir and être, and regular -er verbs: use avoir and être correctly in the present, including for age, and conjugate regular -er verbs
- AQA-FR-T1.1-poss-adj-F - Possessive adjectives: choose mon/ma/mes etc. by the gender and number of the noun owned, not the owner
- AQA-FR-T1.1-adj-agree-F - Adjective agreement: agree adjectives in gender and number with the noun they describe

## How to teach this
Ask: 'Describe your best friend in three French sentences: who they are, what they are like, why you get on.' Note every gap in vocabulary or verb form as you go, then teach this stage's items to fill them.

#### AQA-FR-T1.1-vocab Identity and relationships: vocabulary
Learners need family words beyond the basics: les parents, le beau-père (stepfather), la belle-mère (stepmother), la demi-sœur (half-sister), le demi-frère (half-brother), les grands-parents, un enfant unique (only child). Friendship and relationship language: un ami/une amie, un copain/une copine, le meilleur ami/la meilleure amie, s'entendre bien avec, se disputer avec, le mariage, le partenaire, être célibataire (to be single), divorcé(e). Personality adjectives: drôle, sympa, fidèle (loyal), jaloux/jalouse, égoïste, gentil(le), bavard(e) (talkative). These words sit inside AQA's Appendix 2 prescribed vocabulary list for this topic; teach them as a set the learner drills with flashcards, then use immediately in full sentences rather than as an isolated list, since GCSE marks reward vocabulary used accurately in context, not recited alone.

#### AQA-FR-T1.1-avoir-etre-F Present tense of avoir and être, and regular -er verbs
Two irregular verbs carry huge weight in this topic. Avoir (to have): j'ai, tu as, il/elle/on a, nous avons, vous avez, ils/elles ont — used for age (j'ai quinze ans, NOT je suis quinze ans) and possession. Être (to be): je suis, tu es, il/elle/on est, nous sommes, vous êtes, ils/elles sont — used for identity and description (je suis fils unique). Regular -er verbs (the largest group, e.g. aimer, détester, habiter, adorer) drop -er and add -e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent: j'aime, tu aimes, il aime, nous aimons, vous aimez, ils aiment. Drill both irregulars until automatic, since almost every sentence in this topic needs one of them.

#### AQA-FR-T1.1-poss-adj-F Possessive adjectives
Possessive adjectives agree with the noun owned, not with the owner — the single most common error English speakers make here. Mon/ma/mes (my), ton/ta/tes (your, informal), son/sa/ses (his/her/its), notre/notre/nos (our), votre/votre/vos (your, formal/plural), leur/leur/leurs (their). Son père can mean 'his father' or 'her father' — context decides, not the ending. Before a feminine noun starting with a vowel, use mon/ton/son instead of ma/ta/sa for pronunciation (mon amie, not ma amie). Practice: 'mon frère, ma sœur, mes parents, son père (whoever the owner is), notre famille, leurs enfants.'

#### AQA-FR-T1.1-adj-agree-F Adjective agreement
Adjectives agree in gender and number with the noun they describe: add -e for feminine (sympa stays sympa as it already ends in -a; but grand → grande), add -s for plural (drôle → drôles), add -es for feminine plural (fidèle → fidèles). Irregular patterns to learn by heart: gentil → gentille, jaloux → jalouse, beau → belle. Most descriptive adjectives follow the noun (une amie sympa) but a small set of common ones precede it (un bon ami, une belle famille, un grand frère, un petit enfant). Worked example: 'J'ai une sœur intelligente et deux frères drôles' shows feminine singular, then masculine plural agreement in the same sentence.
