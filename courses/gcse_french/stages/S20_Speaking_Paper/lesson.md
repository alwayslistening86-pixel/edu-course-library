# S20_Speaking_Paper - Lesson: Speaking paper — role-play, reading aloud and the photo card

## Goal
The learner performs the three Speaking tasks: a role-play, reading aloud with follow-up questions, and a photo card discussion with unprepared conversation.

## Syllabus items taught here
- AQA-FR-P2-role-play - Speaking Paper: role-play: complete a five-task role-play, giving a clear message for each task within the supervised preparation time
- AQA-FR-P2-reading-aloud - Speaking Paper: reading aloud and questions: read a short prepared text aloud accurately and answer four compulsory follow-up questions, with pronunciation marked separately
- AQA-FR-P2-photo-card - Speaking Paper: photo card and conversation: discuss two photos from a theme with detail, opinion and personal experience, then sustain an unprepared conversation

## How to teach this
Ask: 'In a real conversation, what makes an answer feel developed rather than just a fact?' Speaking is marked live (recorded by the teacher), after 15 minutes of supervised preparation — use this to set expectations for how the whole paper works before drilling each task.

#### AQA-FR-P2-role-play Speaking Paper: role-play
The role-play is worth 10 marks: five short tasks based on a given card, each needing a clear, comprehensible message rather than perfect grammar — a simple, correctly understood answer scores better than an ambitious but garbled one. Prepare during the 15-minute supervised preparation by predicting the likely question for any task marked with a question mark, and always give a real, specific answer rather than dodging the question.

#### AQA-FR-P2-reading-aloud Speaking Paper: reading aloud and questions
Worth 15 marks: read a short prepared text aloud (pronunciation and intonation marked separately from the four compulsory follow-up questions that come after it) then answer four unseen but related questions. Practise reading aloud specifically for the sounds that trip up English speakers: silent final consonants, nasal vowels (-on, -en, -in), and the "r" sound. The four questions typically extend the text's topic, so revise the topic vocabulary thoroughly, not just the printed text itself.

#### AQA-FR-P2-photo-card Speaking Paper: photo card and conversation
Worth 25 marks, the largest single task: discuss two photos from one theme (say something about both, then add a detail, an opinion and a link to personal experience for each), followed by an unprepared conversation on a related but different theme. Never give a one-word or single-sentence description — examiners specifically reward development (detail + opinion + personal link) over a longer but flatter description. The unprepared conversation rewards genuine spontaneous interaction, so practise responding to questions you have not pre-scripted.

## Explicitly not here
Writing (Paper 4) follows in the final skills stage.
