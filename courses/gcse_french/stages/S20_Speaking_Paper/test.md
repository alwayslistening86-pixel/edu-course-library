# S20_Speaking_Paper - Test: Speaking paper — role-play, reading aloud and the photo card

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Foundation: "Complète un jeu de rôle de cinq tâches, lis un texte à voix haute avec deux questions, et décris une photo avec un détail et une opinion." Higher (once Foundation is secure): "Complète les trois tâches au niveau Higher, avec une conversation non préparée plus longue et plus spontanée."

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S20_Speaking_Paper` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S21_Writing_Paper.
