# S20_Speaking_Paper - Practice: Speaking paper — role-play, reading aloud and the photo card

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Prepare and perform a five-task role-play card with a partner or recording yourself.
2. Read a short French paragraph aloud, focusing on nasal vowels and silent endings, then answer two follow-up questions.
3. Describe a photo fully: one sentence of description, one detail, one opinion, one personal link.
4. Have an unscripted two-minute conversation on a Theme 2 or 3 topic with a partner.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
