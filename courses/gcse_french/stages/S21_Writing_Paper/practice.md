# S21_Writing_Paper - Practice: Writing paper — Foundation and Higher task types across both tiers

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Write a five-sentence photo description of an imagined scene.
2. Respond to a five-bullet Foundation task, checking every bullet is covered.
3. Complete a five-item grammar gap-fill, checking every accent.
4. Translate a short English paragraph into French, then a short French paragraph into English.
5. Write a 150-word Higher response to two open bullet points, using at least two time frames.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
