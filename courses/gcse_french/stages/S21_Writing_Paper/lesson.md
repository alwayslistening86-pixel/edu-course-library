# S21_Writing_Paper - Lesson: Writing paper — Foundation and Higher task types across both tiers

## Goal
The learner completes every AQA 8652 Writing task type: photo description, structured bullet tasks, gap-fill, translation both ways, and the extended/open-ended tasks.

## Syllabus items taught here
- AQA-FR-P4-photo-desc-F - Writing Paper: photo description (Foundation): write five simple sentences describing a photo
- AQA-FR-P4-5-bullet-F - Writing Paper: five-bullet structured task (Foundation): respond to five bullet points with short accurate sentences
- AQA-FR-P4-gap-fill-F - Writing Paper: grammar gap-fill (Foundation): complete five sentences with the exact correct word form, including accents
- AQA-FR-P4-translation-en-fr - Writing Paper: English-to-French translation: translate an English passage into accurate French, keeping the tense and meaning of each element
- AQA-FR-P4-extended-structured-F - Writing Paper: extended structured task (Foundation): cover all set bullet points in an extended response using a range of tenses and justified opinions
- AQA-FR-P4-3-bullet-H - Writing Paper: three-bullet structured task (Higher): cover three bullet points in a developed response using more than one time frame
- AQA-FR-P4-150-open-H - Writing Paper: 150-word open-ended task (Higher): write a 150-word response covering two bullets with a wide range of structures, opinions and time frames

## How to teach this
Ask: 'Why does missing one bullet point in a five-bullet task cost more than one small grammar mistake?' Writing is worth 25% overall (Foundation 50 marks/70 minutes, Higher 50 marks/75 minutes) and AQA's AO2 levels are capped by content coverage before accuracy is even considered — this is the single most important exam-technique fact for this paper.

#### AQA-FR-P4-photo-desc-F Writing Paper: photo description (Foundation)
Write five simple, accurate sentences describing a photo: who/what is shown, where, what they are doing, and simple description. Keep sentences short and grammatically safe (present tense, simple structures) since this task rewards accuracy over ambition — a short correct sentence outscores an ambitious one with an error.

#### AQA-FR-P4-5-bullet-F Writing Paper: five-bullet structured task (Foundation)
Respond to five given bullet points with a short, accurate sentence for each — every bullet must be addressed, since AO2 content marks are capped by coverage before accuracy is considered. Number your response mentally against the five bullets while planning, and check every one is answered before writing, not just the ones that feel easiest.

#### AQA-FR-P4-gap-fill-F Writing Paper: grammar gap-fill (Foundation)
Complete five sentences with the exact correct word form, including accents — an answer with the right idea but a missing accent or wrong verb ending scores zero for that gap, since this task tests precise grammatical accuracy, not comprehension. Worked example: "Mon frère ___ seize ans" with options a/as/est — the correct answer is "a" (avoir for age), not "est."

#### AQA-FR-P4-translation-en-fr Writing Paper: English-to-French translation
Translate an English passage into accurate French, keeping the tense and meaning of every element — ticks are awarded element by element (a correct time expression, a correct verb tense, a correct noun phrase), so a single missing or wrong element loses only that tick rather than the whole sentence. Use only tenses and structures you are confident you can form correctly, since an ambitious but wrong structure scores no better than a simple correct one.

#### AQA-FR-P4-extended-structured-F Writing Paper: extended structured task (Foundation)
A longer structured task (around three bullet points) requiring extended sentences rather than short answers: cover every bullet, aim for at least two time frames (e.g. past and future, not only the present), and include a justified opinion where relevant, since these are the features AQA's Foundation-tier AO2/AO3 levels specifically reward.

#### AQA-FR-P4-3-bullet-H Writing Paper: three-bullet structured task (Higher)
The Higher-tier structured task covers three bullet points in a more developed response than the Foundation equivalent, expected to show a wider range of tenses, structures and vocabulary, plus justified opinions, while still fully covering every bullet.

#### AQA-FR-P4-150-open-H Writing Paper: 150-word open-ended task (Higher)
The 150-word task covers two open bullet points with the widest possible range: aim for at least two, ideally three, time frames (past, present, future or conditional), justified and nuanced opinions, and a variety of connectives and complex structures (relative clauses, the passive, the subjunctive is not required at GCSE but complex tenses are rewarded). Top-band answers connect ideas fluently rather than listing isolated facts; occasional minor slips do not lose the top band, but consistently wrong tenses or endings do.

## Explicitly not here
This is the final content stage. The cumulative exam follows, combining all four papers.
