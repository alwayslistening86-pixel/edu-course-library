# S14_Inequality_Innovation_and_Environment - Practice: Inequality, innovation and environmental economics

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working or a full paragraph before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Explain the difference between absolute and relative poverty in one sentence each. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 absolute poverty: below a fixed real subsistence standard, can fall to zero as development occurs; B1 relative poverty: below a threshold defined relative to a country's own income distribution (e.g. 60% of median income), persists even as absolute living standards rise.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish an attempt before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
