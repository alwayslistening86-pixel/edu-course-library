# Economics (BSc/BA Hons) — Open University module-framework structure, QAA Economics Benchmark graded - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
A single comprehensive paper drawing questions from every stage, weighted roughly evenly across the three blocks (microeconomics and quantitative tools; macroeconomics and statistics; applied statistical modelling and applied/policy economics), plus one extended policy-evaluation question. Questions are original; write fresh ones rather than reusing stage tests. The ready-made questions below are a starter bank; the tutor writes the rest.

## 5 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. A monopolist faces demand P = 200 - 2Q and MC = 20. Find the profit-maximising price and quantity, and calculate the deadweight loss relative to a perfectly competitive outcome (where P = MC). [6 marks]
2. MPC = 0.7. Calculate the multiplier and the change in equilibrium income from a £30bn fall in autonomous investment. [3 marks]
3. IS: Y = 600 - 15r. LM: Y = 350 + 10r. Find equilibrium r and Y. [4 marks]
4. A sample of n=64 has sample mean £520 and population sigma=£64. Construct a 95% confidence interval for the population mean. [4 marks]
5. [Policy evaluation, extended answer] The government is deciding between a carbon tax and a cap-and-trade scheme to meet a legally binding national emissions target for 2035. Using cost-benefit analysis, discounting, and the price-vs-quantity distinction from environmental economics, write a reasoned recommendation. [15 marks]

## Answer key for the ready-made items (tutor only)
1. [6] M1 TR=PQ=(200-2Q)Q, MR=200-4Q; M1 set MR=MC: 200-4Q=20; A1 Q=45, P=200-2x45=110; M1 competitive outcome: P=MC, 200-2Q=20, Q=90; M1 DWL = 0.5x(90-45)x(110-20); A1 = 0.5x45x90=£2,025.
2. [3] M1 multiplier = 1/(1-0.7) = 3.33 (to 2dp); A1 fall in Y = 30 x 3.33 = £100bn (to nearest £bn); B1 correctly signed as a fall.
3. [4] M1 600-15r=350+10r; M1 250=25r; A1 r=10; A1 Y=600-150=450.
4. [4] M1 SE=64/8=8; M1 margin=1.96x8=15.68; A1 CI=(504.32, 535.68); B1 correct interpretation (about 95% of such intervals over repeated sampling contain the true mean).
5. [15] Level 3 (11-15): clear, well-applied use of the price/quantity distinction (cap-and-trade guarantees the quantity target; a tax gives price certainty but not quantity certainty), explicit discussion of discounting long-horizon climate costs/benefits, and a reasoned recommendation that engages with genuine uncertainty and trade-offs (e.g. cost-effectiveness vs target certainty, revenue recycling, distributional effects). Level 2 (6-10): relevant theory applied with some development but limited synthesis or evaluation. Level 1 (1-5): mostly descriptive, theory stated but not applied to the specific policy choice.

## Grading
Mark short and calculation answers against the mark schemes; mark the extended evaluative answer by levels (knowledge and application of theory; correct use of evidence/data; a reasoned, appropriately qualified judgement). A pass needs at least 60% of the marks overall and a genuine attempt at every block. Report the total, the percentage per block and the weakest topic areas.

- **Not yet:** leave `exam_status: "available"`, name the weakest topic areas, offer targeted review, and retry with fresh questions.
