Give the whole test at once, with no hints and no calculator unless the question is a genuinely complex calculation. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. State the three factors that distinguish one market structure from another. [3 marks]
2. Explain two ways a competitive market benefits consumers. [4 marks]
3. Explain the characteristics of an oligopoly, and why firms in an oligopoly are described as interdependent. [4 marks]
4. Discuss the impact of a monopoly on consumers. [6 marks]
5. Which best describes a firm operating in a highly competitive market? Choose every correct option.
   A. a price taker, unable to significantly influence the market price
   B. a price maker, able to set its own price with little consequence
   C. a firm that faces no competition at all
   D. a firm guaranteed high long-run profits

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 the number of producers/firms; B1 the degree of product differentiation; B1 the ease of entry for new firms.
2. [4] B1 lower prices -- intense competition between many similar firms keeps prices down; B1 correctly explained; B1 greater choice -- many firms compete for custom, offering more variety; B1 correctly explained.
3. [4] B1 an oligopoly is a market dominated by a small number of large firms; B1 with high barriers to entry for new firms; B1 interdependent means each firm's decisions (e.g. on price) must take into account how its rivals are likely to react; B1 correctly explained, e.g. a price cut by one firm is likely to be matched by rivals, so firms often compete on non-price factors instead.
4. [6] Level 2 (4-6): balanced discussion of negative impacts (higher prices, restricted output/choice, less incentive to innovate given no competitive pressure) alongside possible positives (economies of scale from size could, in principle, be passed on as lower costs; possible standardisation/reliability) with a reasoned overall judgement. Level 1 (1-3): one-sided or limited discussion. 0: no relevant discussion.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
