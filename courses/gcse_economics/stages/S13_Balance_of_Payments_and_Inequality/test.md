## Test items
1. Distinguish between the balance of trade and the balance of payments. [2 marks]
2. A country's exports of goods and services total £380bn and its imports total £415bn over a year. Calculate the current account balance, showing your working, and state whether this is a surplus or a deficit. [3 marks]
3. Explain one possible consequence of a country running a persistent, large current account deficit. [2 marks]
4. Explain two causes of income inequality. [4 marks]
5. Discuss the effectiveness of progressive taxation as a way to reduce income inequality. [6 marks]
6. A current account deficit occurs when: Choose every correct option.
   A. the value of imports (and other outflows) exceeds the value of exports (and other inflows)
   B. the value of exports exceeds the value of imports
   C. government spending exceeds tax revenue
   D. the exchange rate rises

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 the balance of trade covers only trade in goods (exports minus imports of goods); B1 the balance of payments is the fuller record of all economic transactions with the rest of the world, including trade in services and other flows.
2. [3] M1 380-415; A1 = -£35bn; B1 a deficit of £35bn.
3. [2] B1 e.g. the country is effectively financing the gap by borrowing from/selling assets to the rest of the world, which is not sustainable indefinitely; B1 correctly explained.
4. [4] B1 differences in wages, reflecting differences in skills/education/qualifications (linking to the labour market); B1 correctly explained; B1 differences in wealth/inheritance, or unemployment, or unequal access to education/opportunity; B1 correctly explained.
5. [6] Level 2 (4-6): balanced discussion, e.g. progressive taxation directly reduces post-tax income inequality by taking a higher share from higher earners, and can fund redistributive spending on benefits/public services; but very high rates could in principle discourage work/investment or encourage avoidance, and taxation alone does not address underlying causes such as unequal access to education, with a reasoned overall judgement. Level 1 (1-3): one-sided or limited discussion. 0: no relevant discussion.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
