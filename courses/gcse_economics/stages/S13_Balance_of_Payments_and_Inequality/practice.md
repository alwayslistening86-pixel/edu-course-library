# S13_Balance_of_Payments_and_Inequality - Practice: Balance of payments and income/wealth inequality

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A country's exports total £600bn and its imports total £570bn over a year. Calculate the current account balance and state whether this is a surplus or a deficit. [2 marks]
2. State one government policy that could help reduce a persistent current account deficit. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] M1 600-570 = £30bn; A1 a surplus of £30bn.
2. [1] B1 e.g. supply-side policies to make exports more competitive / a weaker exchange rate (any one credible policy).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
