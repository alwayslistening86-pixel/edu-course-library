# S06_Productivity_and_Economies_of_Scale - Practice: Production, productivity and economies of scale

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A factory produces 12,000 units a month with 40 workers. After training, it produces 15,000 units a month with the same 40 workers. Calculate labour productivity (units per worker per month) before and after training. [2 marks]
2. Identify the type of economy of scale being described: 'a large supermarket chain negotiates a lower price per tonne for flour because it buys such large quantities.' [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 before: 12,000/40 = 300 units per worker; B1 after: 15,000/40 = 375 units per worker.
2. [1] B1 purchasing/bulk-buying economies of scale.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
