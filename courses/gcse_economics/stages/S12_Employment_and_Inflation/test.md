## Test items
1. A country's labour force is 30,000,000, of whom 1,500,000 are unemployed. Calculate the unemployment rate, showing your working. [2 marks]
2. Distinguish between cyclical unemployment and frictional unemployment, giving an example of each. [4 marks]
3. A basket of goods that cost £180 in the base year costs £189 this year. Calculate the CPI this year and the rate of inflation, showing your working. [3 marks]
4. Distinguish between demand-pull and cost-push inflation. [3 marks]
5. Explain two consequences of a sustained period of high inflation for an economy. [4 marks]
6. Which of these is an example of cost-push inflation? Choose every correct option.
   A. a sharp rise in global oil prices raising firms' production costs, which they pass on as higher prices
   B. a fall in interest rates increasing consumer spending faster than supply can keep up
   C. a rise in the exchange rate making imports cheaper
   D. a fall in unemployment with no change in wages or costs

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] M1 1,500,000/30,000,000; A1 = 5%.
2. [4] B1 cyclical unemployment is caused by a downturn in the economic cycle/a recession, e.g. widespread job losses across many industries in a recession; B1 correctly explained; B1 frictional unemployment is short-term, while someone moves between jobs, e.g. a graduate taking a few weeks to find their first job; B1 correctly explained.
3. [3] M1 CPI = 189/180 x 100 = 105; A1 inflation rate = (189-180)/180 x 100 = 5%; B1 correct method shown.
4. [3] B1 demand-pull inflation occurs when aggregate demand grows faster than the economy's ability to supply, pulling prices up; B1 cost-push inflation occurs when rising production costs (e.g. wages, energy, raw materials) push firms to raise prices; B1 both correctly and clearly contrasted.
5. [4] B1 reduced purchasing power of money, hitting those on fixed incomes (e.g. many pensioners) hardest; B1 correctly explained; B1 loss of international competitiveness if domestic inflation is higher than trading partners', or reduced business investment due to uncertainty; B1 correctly explained.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
