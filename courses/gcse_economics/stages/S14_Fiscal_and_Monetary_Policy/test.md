## Test items
1. Distinguish between a budget deficit and a budget surplus. [2 marks]
2. A government's spending is £650bn and its tax revenue is £670bn in a year. Calculate the size of the budget deficit or surplus, showing your working, and state which it is. [2 marks]
3. Explain how expansionary fiscal policy could be used to reduce unemployment, and one drawback of using it. [4 marks]
4. Explain how the Bank of England could use monetary policy to reduce inflation that is above target. [3 marks]
5. Explain why monetary and fiscal policy objectives can sometimes conflict with each other. [3 marks]
6. If the Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee wants to reduce inflation that is above the 2% target, it is most likely to: Choose every correct option.
   A. raise the base interest rate
   B. cut the base interest rate
   C. increase government spending
   D. cut income tax

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 a budget deficit is where government spending exceeds tax revenue (must borrow to cover the gap); B1 a budget surplus is where tax revenue exceeds government spending.
2. [2] M1 670-650 = £20bn; A1 a budget surplus of £20bn.
3. [4] B1 cutting taxes and/or raising government spending increases demand in the economy; B1 higher demand encourages firms to produce more and take on more workers, reducing unemployment; B1 drawback, e.g. this widens the budget deficit/adds to the national debt; B1 correctly explained (accept: risks raising inflation if overdone).
4. [3] B1 the Monetary Policy Committee raises the base interest rate; B1 this makes borrowing more expensive and saving more attractive, reducing consumer spending and business investment; B1 lower demand in the economy helps bring inflation back down towards the 2% target.
5. [3] B1 e.g. cutting interest rates/expansionary fiscal policy to boost growth and cut unemployment can also raise inflation; B1 while raising interest rates/contractionary fiscal policy to control inflation can slow growth and raise unemployment; B1 so a policy that helps one objective can work against another.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
