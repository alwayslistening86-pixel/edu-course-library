# S15_Supply_Side_and_Externality_Policies - Practice: Supply-side policies and correcting externalities

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Identify one supply-side policy a government could use to raise long-run productivity, and explain one advantage of it. [2 marks]
2. State one government policy that could be used to encourage more of a good with a positive externality, such as vaccination. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 e.g. investment in education and training; B1 advantage correctly explained, e.g. raises workers' skills/productivity, improving long-run competitiveness.
2. [1] B1 e.g. a subsidy, or direct government provision (free vaccination).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
