# S11_Growth_and_Government_Objectives - Practice: Government economic objectives and economic growth

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the UK government's four main economic objectives. [4 marks]
2. Nominal GDP rises from £500bn to £520bn while prices rise by 2% over the same period. Calculate real GDP in year 2 and the approximate real growth rate. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B1 full employment; B1 price stability (low, stable inflation); B1 economic growth; B1 a healthy/stable balance of payments.
2. [3] M1 real GDP = 520/1.02 = £509.8bn (accept rounding); A1 real growth = (509.8-500)/500 x 100 = 1.96% (accept ~2%); B1 correctly lower than the 4% nominal growth figure.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
