# S10_Interest_Rates_and_Government_Finance - Practice: Interest rates and government income and expenditure

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. £2,000 is saved at a simple interest rate of 4% per year for 3 years. Calculate the total interest earned and the final balance. [2 marks]
2. Explain, using an example, the difference between a direct tax and an indirect tax. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 interest = 2,000 x 4% x 3 = £240; B1 final balance = 2,000+240 = £2,240.
2. [2] B1 a direct tax is paid directly by the individual/firm it is levied on, e.g. income tax; B1 an indirect tax is levied on spending and can be passed on in price, e.g. VAT.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
