# S09_Market_Failure_and_Externalities - Practice: Market failure and externalities

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Identify whether the following is a negative or positive externality, and whether it is a production or consumption externality: 'A factory's chemical waste pollutes a river used by a fishing community downstream.' [2 marks]
2. State one way government could intervene to reduce the market failure caused by a firm's carbon emissions. [1 mark]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 negative externality; B1 production externality.
2. [1] B1 e.g. a tax on carbon emissions / regulation setting an emissions limit / a subsidy for cleaner technology (any one appropriate method).

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
