# S05_Costs_Revenue_Profit - Practice: Cost, revenue and profit for producers

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A firm sells 200 units of a good at £15 each. Its fixed costs are £900 and its variable cost is £4 per unit. Calculate: (a) total revenue; (b) total cost; (c) profit. [4 marks]
2. State one fixed cost and one variable cost a bakery might have. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] M1 (a) TR = 200 x 15 = £3,000; M1 (b) TVC = 200 x 4 = £800; A1 (b) TC = 900+800 = £1,700; A1 (c) profit = 3,000 - 1,700 = £1,300.
2. [2] B1 fixed cost, e.g. rent/business rates/insurance; B1 variable cost, e.g. flour/ingredients/packaging.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
