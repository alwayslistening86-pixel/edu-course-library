# S17_Trade_Agreements_and_Globalisation - Practice: Free-trade agreements and globalisation

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. Define a tariff and state one likely effect of a country imposing one on imported cars. [2 marks]
2. State one benefit and one drawback of globalisation for workers in a less developed country. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 a tariff is a tax on imports; B1 effect, e.g. it raises the price of imported cars, making domestically-produced cars relatively more competitive/discouraging imports.
2. [2] B1 benefit, e.g. new jobs/foreign investment/technology transfer raising incomes; B1 drawback, e.g. risk of exploitation/low wages/poor working conditions.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
