# S18_Money_and_Financial_Sector - Practice: The role of money and the financial sector

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. State the four functions of money. [4 marks]
2. Explain why money held in a bank current account still counts as money, even though it is not physical cash. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [4] B1 medium of exchange; B1 unit of account; B1 store of value; B1 means of deferred payment.
2. [2] B1 because the definition of money is based on the functions it performs, not its physical form; B1 a current account balance can act as a medium of exchange/unit of account/store of value/means of deferred payment, e.g. spending by card.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
