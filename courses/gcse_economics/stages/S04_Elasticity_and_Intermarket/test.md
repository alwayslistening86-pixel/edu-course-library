## Test items
1. Distinguish between a substitute good and a complementary good, giving one example of each. [2 marks]
2. A 8% rise in the price of a good leads to a 4% fall in quantity demanded. Calculate the price elasticity of demand (show your working) and state whether demand is elastic or inelastic. [3 marks]
3. Explain two factors that make demand for a good more price elastic. [4 marks]
4. A 25% rise in price of a good leads to a 10% rise in quantity supplied. Calculate the price elasticity of supply (show your working) and state whether supply is elastic or inelastic. [3 marks]
5. Explain why supply of fresh strawberries is likely to be more price inelastic in the very short run than supply of factory-made furniture. [3 marks]
6. A firm facing inelastic demand for its product wants to increase total revenue. Which action should it take? Choose every correct option.
   A. raise the price
   B. cut the price
   C. leave price unchanged and hope demand rises anyway
   D. it is impossible to affect revenue by changing price

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 substitutes can replace one another in use, e.g. tea and coffee; B1 complements are usually consumed together, e.g. cars and petrol.
2. [3] M1 PED = -4% / 8%; A1 PED = -0.5 (accept 0.5); B1 inelastic, since the magnitude (0.5) is less than 1.
3. [4] B1 the availability of close substitutes -- consumers can easily switch away if price rises; B1 correctly explained; B1 the good being a luxury rather than a necessity -- consumers can more easily do without it; B1 correctly explained (accept: takes up a large share of income; more time available to find alternatives).
4. [3] M1 PES = 10% / 25%; A1 PES = 0.4; B1 inelastic, since the magnitude (0.4) is less than 1.
5. [3] B1 strawberries take a growing season to produce more of, and cannot be quickly increased even if price rises; B1 furniture factories can often increase output faster (e.g. more shifts, some spare capacity); B1 so strawberry supply responds less to a price change in the short run -- more inelastic.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
