# S04_Elasticity_and_Intermarket - Practice: Intermarket relationships and price elasticity of demand and supply

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. The price of butter rises by 15%. State, with a reason, what is likely to happen to the demand for margarine (a substitute). [2 marks]
2. A 10% fall in the price of a good causes a 25% rise in quantity demanded. Calculate the price elasticity of demand and state whether demand is elastic or inelastic. [3 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 demand for margarine rises/increases (shifts right); B1 because consumers switch away from the now more expensive substitute, butter, towards margarine.
2. [3] M1 PED = 25% / -10%; A1 PED = -2.5 (accept 2.5); B1 elastic, since the magnitude (2.5) is greater than 1.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
