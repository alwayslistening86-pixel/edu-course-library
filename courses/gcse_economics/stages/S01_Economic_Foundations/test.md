# S01_Economic_Foundations - Test: Economic foundations: needs, wants, factors of production and choice

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of AQA's papers: multiple-choice, short-answer, calculation and extended-response questions, with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints and no calculator unless the question is a genuinely complex calculation. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Distinguish between a need and a want, giving one example of each. [2 marks]
2. State the three key economic decisions every economy must make. [3 marks]
3. Identify the four factors of production and the reward each earns. [4 marks]
4. Explain what is meant by the basic economic problem, and define opportunity cost. [3 marks]
5. A worker can spend Saturday doing paid overtime (earning £120) or attending a friend's wedding. Explain, using the concept of opportunity cost, what is given up by choosing to work. [2 marks]
6. Which economic group organises land, labour and capital to produce goods and services, aiming chiefly at profit? Choose every correct option.
   A. producers
   B. consumers
   C. government
   D. the Bank of England

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [2] B1 a need is essential for survival, e.g. food; B1 a want improves quality of life but is not essential, e.g. a new phone.
2. [3] B1 what to produce; B1 how to produce it; B1 for whom to produce (who receives it).
3. [4] B1 land -- rent; B1 labour -- wages; B1 capital -- interest; B1 enterprise -- profit.
4. [3] B1 wants are unlimited but resources (factors of production) are limited/scarce, so choices must be made; B1 opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative given up; B1 when a choice is made.
5. [2] B1 the opportunity cost is attending the wedding (the next best alternative given up); B1 correctly applies the concept, not simply restating the £120 earned.
6. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
