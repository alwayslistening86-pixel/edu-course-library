# S01_Economic_Foundations - Practice: Economic foundations: needs, wants, factors of production and choice

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A local council can spend its remaining budget this year on either resurfacing roads or building a new library, but not both. Explain the opportunity cost of choosing to resurface the roads. [2 marks]
2. State the factor of production, and its reward, being described: 'the farmland a wheat farmer grows crops on'. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative given up; B1 here, the opportunity cost of resurfacing the roads is the library that is given up/not built.
2. [2] B1 land; B1 rent.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
