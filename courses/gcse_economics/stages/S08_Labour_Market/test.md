## Test items
1. Explain how the wage rate for a particular job is determined. [3 marks]
2. Explain two reasons why wages differ between different jobs. [4 marks]
3. Distinguish between gross pay and net pay. [2 marks]
4. A worker has a gross annual salary of £40,000. Using a tax-free personal allowance of £12,570 and a 20% income tax rate on earnings above it, and National Insurance charged at 8% of earnings above the same threshold, calculate: (a) the taxable amount; (b) income tax due; (c) National Insurance due; (d) net pay. [5 marks]
5. Which of these would most directly cause a rise in the equilibrium wage for a particular job? Choose every correct option.
   A. a fall in the number of people qualified to do that job (a fall in labour supply)
   B. a rise in the number of people qualified to do that job
   C. a rise in income tax rates
   D. a fall in demand for the good that job produces

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] B1 by the interaction of the demand for that labour (from firms) and the supply of that labour (from workers able/willing to do the job); B1 the wage settles where the quantity of labour demanded equals the quantity supplied; B1 a change in either demand or supply changes the equilibrium wage.
2. [4] B1 differences in required skills/training -- jobs needing rare, expensive-to-acquire skills have low supply relative to demand, raising wages; B1 correctly explained; B1 compensating differentials for dangerous/unpleasant/unsociable work -- higher pay needed to attract enough workers; B1 correctly explained (accept differences in productivity/value to the employer).
3. [2] B1 gross pay is total earnings before any deductions; B1 net pay is what is actually received after deductions such as income tax and National Insurance.
4. [5] M1 (a) taxable = 40,000-12,570 = £27,430; A1 (b) tax = 20% x 27,430 = £5,486; A1 (c) NI = 8% x 27,430 = £2,194.40; A1 (d) net pay = 40,000 - 5,486 - 2,194.40 = £32,319.60; B1 correct method: tax and NI are both charged only on earnings above the personal allowance threshold, not on the full salary.
5. Correct: A (exactly these options, no others)
