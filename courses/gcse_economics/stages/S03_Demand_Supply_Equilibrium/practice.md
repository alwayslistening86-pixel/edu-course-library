# S03_Demand_Supply_Equilibrium - Practice: Demand, supply and equilibrium price

## Goal
Low-stakes practice: the learner attempts each question with full working before seeing the model answer. Mistakes are expected and corrected here, before any grading.

## Practice items
1. A new film releases and becomes hugely popular, but the cinema does not change its ticket price. Explain, using the correct term, what happens to demand for tickets, and whether this is a shift or a movement along the demand curve. [2 marks]
2. The cost of steel used to build cars rises sharply. Explain the effect this has on the supply curve for cars. [2 marks]

## Answers (for the tutor; reveal only after a genuine attempt)
1. [2] B1 demand for tickets increases/rises (shifts right), due to a change in tastes/popularity, not price; B1 this is a shift of the whole curve, not a movement along it (because price did not change).
2. [2] B1 supply of cars falls/decreases; B1 the whole supply curve shifts left, because production is now less profitable at every price.

## How to run it
One question at a time. Let the learner finish a genuine attempt (including full working for any calculation) before offering a hint; then walk through the model answer and have them redo any step they missed.

## When to move to test
When the learner gets a new item of each type right without prompting.
