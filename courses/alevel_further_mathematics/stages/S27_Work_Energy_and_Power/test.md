# S27_Work_Energy_and_Power - Test: Work, energy and power

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's Further Mathematics papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A cyclist and bike of total mass 80 kg ride up a slope inclined at 5° against a resistance of 30 N. The cyclist's power is 400 W. Find the maximum speed. [4 marks]
2. A 3 kg particle is projected at 8 m/s up a rough slope at 20° with μ = 0.25. Use the work-energy principle to find the distance travelled before it stops. [5 marks]
3. A light spring of natural length 0.4 m and modulus 60 N is compressed by 0.1 m and used to launch a 0.2 kg ball horizontally on a smooth surface. Find the launch speed. [3 marks]
4. A variable force F = 6x^2 N acts along the x-axis on a particle moving from x = 1 to x = 2. Find the work done. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 driving force = 30 + 80g sin 5°; A1 98.33 N; M1 v = 400/F; A1 4.07 m/s.
2. [5] M1 KE lost = 96 J; M1 GPE gained 3g d sin 20°; M1 friction work 0.25 x 3g cos 20° x d; A1 96 = d(3g sin 20° + 0.75g cos 20°); A1 d = 5.66 m.
3. [3] M1 EPE = 60 x 0.01/(2 x 0.4) = 0.75 J; M1 (1/2)(0.2)v^2 = 0.75; A1 v = root7.5 ≈ 2.74 m/s.
4. [2] M1 ∫ 6x^2 dx; A1 [2x^3] = 14 J.
