# S30_Circular_Motion_and_Variable_Force - Test: Circular motion and variable forces

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's Further Mathematics papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. A particle of mass 0.3 kg on a light inextensible string of length 0.8 m moves in a horizontal circle as a conical pendulum with the string at 40° to the vertical. Find the tension and the angular speed. [5 marks]
2. A particle is attached to a light inextensible string of length 1.2 m and moves in a vertical circle. At the lowest point its speed is 8 m/s. Find its speed at the top and determine whether the string stays taut there. [5 marks]
3. A particle of mass 0.5 kg moves in a straight line against a resistance of 2v^2 N. Its initial speed is 10 m/s. Find its speed after travelling 1 m. [4 marks]
4. A bend of radius 60 m is banked at 20°. Find the speed at which a car has no tendency to slip sideways. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] M1 T cos 40° = 0.3g; A1 T = 3.84 N; M1 T sin 40° = 0.3(0.8 sin 40°)ω^2; A1 ω^2 = g/(0.8 cos 40°); A1 ω = 4.00 rad/s.
2. [5] M1 energy: v^2 = 64 - 2g(2.4); A1 v^2 = 16.96, v = 4.12 m/s; M1 needs v^2 ≥ gr = 11.76; A1 16.96 ≥ 11.76; A1 so the string stays taut: the tension at the top is m(v^2/r - g) = 4.33m N > 0 for a particle of mass m kg.
3. [4] M1 0.5v dv/dx = -2v^2; M1 separates: ln v = -4x + c; A1 v = 10e^(-4x); A1 10e^(-4) ≈ 0.183 m/s.
4. [3] M1 tan 20° = v^2/(60g); A1 v^2 = 214.0; A1 v = 14.63 m/s.
