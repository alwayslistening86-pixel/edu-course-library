# S04_Complex_Loci - Test: Loci and regions in the Argand diagram

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's Further Mathematics papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Find the cartesian equation of the locus |z - 4| = |z + 2i| and describe it. [4 marks]
2. Sketch the region {z : |z - 2 - 2i| ≤ 2} ∩ {z : arg z ≥ π/4}. [4 marks]
3. A locus is given by arg(z + 1) = π/3. Find the point where it meets the imaginary axis. [3 marks]
4. z lies on the circle |z - 3 - 4i| = 2. Find the least and greatest values of |z|. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] M1 (x - 4)^2 + y^2 = x^2 + (y + 2)^2; A1 -8x + 16 = 4y + 4; A1 y = 3 - 2x; B1 the perpendicular bisector of the points 4 and -2i.
2. [4] B1 circle centre (2, 2), radius 2, solid; B1 half-line from O at π/4 (through the centre); B1 correct half of the disc indicated (above/left of the line); B1 clear indication of included boundaries.
3. [3] M1 half-line from -1 at angle π/3; M1 rises tan(π/3) = root3 per unit; A1 i root3.
4. [3] M1 the distance from O to 3 + 4i is 5; A1 least 3; A1 greatest 7.
