# S16_Polar_Coordinates - Test: Polar coordinates

## How to run this
A real checkpoint in the style of OCR's Further Mathematics papers: structured questions with marks shown. Give the whole test at once, with no hints; the learner shows full working and may use a calculator. Then mark against the mark schemes below and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Sketch r = 3sin 2θ for 0 ≤ θ ≤ π/2 and find the area of the loop. [5 marks]
2. Convert x^2 + y^2 - 6y = 0 to polar form and describe the curve. [3 marks]
3. The curves r = 2cos θ and r = 1 meet at two points. Find their polar coordinates. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [5] B1 loop in the first quadrant, maximum r = 3 at θ = π/4; M1 (1/2)∫9sin^2 2θ dθ; M1 sin^2 2θ = (1 - cos 4θ)/2; A1 correct integration; A1 9pi/8.
2. [3] M1 r^2 = 6r sin θ; A1 r = 6sin θ; B1 circle centre (0, 3), radius 3.
3. [2] M1 cos θ = 1/2; A1 (1, π/3) and (1, -π/3).
