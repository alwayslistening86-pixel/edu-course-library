# S28_Impulse_Momentum_and_Restitution - Test: Impulse, momentum and restitution

## How to run this

## Test items
1. Sphere A (mass 3 kg) moving at 4 m/s collides directly with sphere B (mass 1 kg) moving towards it at 2 m/s. e = 0.6. Find the velocities after impact and the loss in kinetic energy. [7 marks]
2. A ball of mass 0.2 kg moving with velocity (5i + 2j) m/s receives an impulse of (-2i + 1.2j) N s. Find its new velocity and speed. [3 marks]
3. A smooth sphere hits a smooth fixed wall at 6 m/s at an angle of 30° to the wall. e = 0.4. Find its speed after impact and the angle its path makes with the wall. [4 marks]
4. A force F = (4 - t) N acts on a 2 kg particle, initially at rest, for 0 ≤ t ≤ 4. Find the impulse and the final speed. [3 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [7] M1 momentum: 12 - 2 = 3v_A + v_B; M1 restitution: v_B - v_A = 0.6 x 6 = 3.6; A1 4v_A = 6.4; A1 v_A = 1.6 m/s; A1 v_B = 5.2 m/s; M1 KE before 24 + 2 = 26 J, after 3.84 + 13.52 = 17.36 J; A1 loss 8.64 J.
2. [3] M1 0.2v = 0.2(5i + 2j) + (-2i + 1.2j); A1 v = -5i + 8j; A1 speed root89 ≈ 9.43 m/s.
3. [4] M1 parallel component 6cos 30° unchanged; M1 perpendicular 6sin 30° x 0.4 = 1.2; A1 speed 5.33 m/s; A1 angle 13.0°.
4. [3] M1 ∫ (0 to 4) (4 - t) dt; A1 8 N s; A1 4 m/s.
