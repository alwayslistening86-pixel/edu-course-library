# S1_Persuasive_Techniques - Test: Recognising persuasive techniques

## How to run this
A real checkpoint. Give the whole test at once, with no hints, then mark against the answer key and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Read: 'You deserve better. You deserve a library that is warm, welcoming and open all week.' Name two techniques used, quoting the words for each. [4 marks]
2. Explain the effect of the rule of three in that extract. [3 marks]
3. Explain why simply naming a technique does not earn full credit. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [4] B1 direct address, quote 'You deserve better'; B1 rule of three, quote 'warm, welcoming and open'; B1 each technique correctly named; B1 each supported by a quotation
2. [3] B1 the list of three adjectives sounds complete and persuasive; B1 it makes the library seem fully good; B1 the reader is encouraged to agree
3. [2] B1 the effect on the reader must be explained; B1 because the purpose of a technique is what it does to the reader
