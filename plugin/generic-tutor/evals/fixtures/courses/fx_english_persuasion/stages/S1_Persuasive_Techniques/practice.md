# S1_Persuasive_Techniques - Practice: Recognising persuasive techniques

## Goal
The learner applies the ideas to simple cases, with mistakes expected and corrected.

## Practice scenarios
1. 'Isn't it time we all did something?' Name the technique and its effect. - expect: rhetorical question; makes the reader agree and feel included
2. 'Fast, fair and free.' What is the technique and why use it? - expect: rule of three; sounds complete and memorable
3. 'Heartbreaking scenes of abandoned puppies.' What is the effect? - expect: emotive language; pity, prompting action

## How to run it
One scenario at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.
