# S1_Persuasive_Techniques - Lesson: Recognising persuasive techniques

## Goal
The learner identifies common persuasive techniques in a short text and explains the effect each is meant to have.

## Syllabus items taught here
- EP1.1 — name rhetorical questions, the rule of three, emotive language and direct address
- EP1.2 — explain what effect a technique is meant to have on the reader

## How to teach this
Read two slogans aloud and ask which one sticks, and why. What is the writer doing with the words?

Once the learner has had a go, introduce the core ideas plainly:
- A rhetorical question is asked for effect, not for an answer.
- The rule of three groups three items to sound complete and memorable.
- Emotive language chooses words that trigger feelings; direct address speaks to 'you'.
- Naming the technique earns little unless the effect on the reader is explained.

## Suggested flow
1. Open with the question above.
2. Explain the core ideas in plain words.
3. Give two low-stakes examples for pattern recognition only.
4. Check the learner can explain the idea back before moving to practice.
