# S2_Writing_a_Persuasive_Paragraph - Lesson: Writing a persuasive paragraph

## Goal
The learner writes a short persuasive paragraph with a clear point, a reason, and at least two deliberate techniques.

## Syllabus items taught here
- EP2.1 — state a clear point and support it with a reason
- EP2.2 — choose techniques on purpose to suit the audience

## How to teach this
Ask the learner to persuade a head teacher to start school an hour later. What is their one main point, and who exactly are they writing for?

Once the learner has had a go, introduce the core ideas plainly:
- Start with one clear point, then give a reason, then an example or consequence.
- Choose techniques to suit the reader: a head teacher needs reasons more than slogans.
- Finish with a line that tells the reader what to do or think.

## Suggested flow
1. Open with the question above.
2. Explain the core ideas in plain words.
3. Give two low-stakes examples for pattern recognition only.
4. Check the learner can explain the idea back before moving to practice.
