# S2_Writing_a_Persuasive_Paragraph - Test: Writing a persuasive paragraph

## How to run this
A real checkpoint. Give the whole test at once, with no hints, then mark against the answer key and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Write a paragraph of 4 to 6 sentences persuading your head teacher to allow a weekly lunchtime club of your choice. [8 marks]
2. Name the two techniques you used and the effect you intended for each. [4 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [8] B2 one clear point stated at the start; B2 at least one reason and one example; B2 at least two techniques used deliberately and suited to a head teacher; B1 a closing line that asks for action; B1 clear, accurate sentences
2. [4] B1 first technique correctly named; B1 its intended effect explained; B1 second technique correctly named; B1 its intended effect explained

## Grading
