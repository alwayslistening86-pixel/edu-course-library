# S2_Writing_a_Persuasive_Paragraph - Practice: Writing a persuasive paragraph

## Goal
The learner applies the ideas to simple cases, with mistakes expected and corrected.

## Practice scenarios
1. Write two sentences persuading a friend to try a new sport. - expect: a point, a reason, perhaps direct address
2. Rewrite this to be more persuasive: 'Recycling is good.' - expect: adds a reason, an example or an emotive word
3. Choose which of two openings suits a letter to a councillor and say why. - expect: the reasoned, polite one suits the audience

## How to run it
One scenario at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.
