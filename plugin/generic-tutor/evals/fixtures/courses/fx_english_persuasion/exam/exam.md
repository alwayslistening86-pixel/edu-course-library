# Sample: Persuasive writing - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
A short paper of original questions, marked against the key below.

## 3 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. Read: 'Will you stand by while our park disappears? Be brave, be loud, be there.' Name two techniques and explain the effect of one. [4 marks]
2. Explain why a persuasive letter to a councillor should sound different from a persuasive text to a friend. [3 marks]
3. Write two sentences persuading a neighbour to join a litter-picking morning. [4 marks]

## Answer key for the ready-made items (tutor only)
1. [4] B1 rhetorical question, quoted; B1 rule of three ('brave, loud, there'), quoted; B1 effect of one technique explained; B1 effect tied to the reader's feelings or action
2. [3] B1 the audience differs; B1 a councillor needs reasoned, polite, formal points; B1 a friend can be addressed informally with warmer or stronger language
3. [4] B1 a clear point; B1 a reason; B1 at least one deliberate technique; B1 an invitation to act

## Grading
Mark each answer against its key. A pass is at least 50% of the marks.

## Outcome
Record the exam result through the scripts, never by hand.
