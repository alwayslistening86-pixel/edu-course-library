# S2_Percentages_of_Amounts - Practice: Percentages of amounts and percentage change

## Goal
The learner applies the ideas to simple cases, with mistakes expected and corrected.

## Practice scenarios
1. Find 15% of 240 pounds. - expect: 10% is 24, 5% is 12, total 36 pounds
2. A bike costing 200 pounds rises in price by 10%. What is the new price? - expect: 200 x 1.1 = 220 pounds
3. A 60 pound top is reduced by 25%. What is the sale price? - expect: 60 x 0.75 = 45 pounds

## How to run it
One scenario at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.
