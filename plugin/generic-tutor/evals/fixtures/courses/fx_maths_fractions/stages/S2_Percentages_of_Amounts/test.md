# S2_Percentages_of_Amounts - Test: Percentages of amounts and percentage change

## How to run this
A real checkpoint. Give the whole test at once, with no hints, then mark against the answer key and this stage's entry in `rubric.json`.

## Test items
1. Find 35% of 120 pounds. [3 marks]
2. A train fare of 40 pounds goes up by 15%. Work out the new fare. [3 marks]
3. A phone's price falls from 250 pounds to 200 pounds. Work out the percentage decrease. [4 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [3] M1 10% = 12 and 5% = 6 (or 35/100 x 120); M1 3 x 12 + 6; A1 42 pounds
2. [3] M1 15% of 40 = 6 (or 40 x 1.15); M1 added to 40; A1 46 pounds
3. [4] M1 change = 50; M1 50/250; M1 x 100; A1 20%
