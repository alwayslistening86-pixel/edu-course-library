# S2_Percentages_of_Amounts - Lesson: Percentages of amounts and percentage change

## Goal
The learner finds a percentage of an amount and works out a percentage increase or decrease.

## Syllabus items taught here
- MF2.1 — find a percentage of a quantity without a calculator for easy percentages
- MF2.2 — increase or decrease an amount by a percentage

## How to teach this
Ask: a coat costs 80 pounds and is in a 25% sale. How would you find the sale price, and could you check it two different ways?

Once the learner has had a go, introduce the core ideas plainly:
- 10% is one tenth, 1% is one hundredth; build other percentages from these.
- A percentage increase multiplies by 1 plus the percentage as a decimal; a decrease multiplies by 1 minus it.
- Percentage change is the change divided by the original amount, times 100.

## Suggested flow
1. Open with the question above.
2. Explain the core ideas in plain words.
3. Give two low-stakes examples for pattern recognition only.
4. Check the learner can explain the idea back before moving to practice.
