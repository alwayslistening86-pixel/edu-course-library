# S1_Adding_Fractions - Lesson: Adding fractions with different denominators

## Goal
The learner adds two fractions by finding a common denominator and gives the answer in simplest form.

## Syllabus items taught here
- MF1.1 — find a common denominator of two fractions
- MF1.2 — add fractions and simplify the result

## How to teach this
Ask: if you eat 1/2 of a pizza and your friend eats 1/3, how much has gone? Why can't we just add the tops and the bottoms?

Once the learner has had a go, introduce the core ideas plainly:
- Fractions can only be added when the pieces are the same size, so the denominators must match.
- Find a common multiple of the denominators, rewrite each fraction, then add the numerators only.
- Simplify the answer by dividing top and bottom by a common factor.

## Suggested flow
1. Open with the question above.
2. Explain the core ideas in plain words.
3. Give two low-stakes examples for pattern recognition only.
4. Check the learner can explain the idea back before moving to practice.
