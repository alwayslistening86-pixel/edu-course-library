# S1_Offer_and_Acceptance - Practice: Offer and acceptance

## Goal
The learner applies the ideas to simple cases, with mistakes expected and corrected.

## Practice scenarios
1. A shopkeeper puts a bicycle in the window marked 90 pounds. Is that an offer? - expect: generally an invitation to treat
2. Sam says 'I will sell you my guitar for 100 pounds.' Ria replies 'I will give you 80.' Is there a contract? - expect: no: counter-offer ends the original offer
3. Ria then says 'Fine, 100.' Is the agreement formed? - expect: yes if Sam has not withdrawn and the acceptance is communicated

## How to run it
One scenario at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.
