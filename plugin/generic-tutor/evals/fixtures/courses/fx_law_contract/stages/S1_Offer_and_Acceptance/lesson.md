# S1_Offer_and_Acceptance - Lesson: Offer and acceptance

## Goal
The learner distinguishes an offer from an invitation to treat and recognises when acceptance is complete.

## Syllabus items taught here
- LC1.1 — tell an offer from an invitation to treat in a short scenario
- LC1.2 — recognise valid acceptance and when it takes effect

## How to teach this
Ask: a shop window shows a jacket marked 50 pounds. Is the shop bound to sell it to you at that price? What would change your answer?

Once the learner has had a go, introduce the core ideas plainly:
- An offer shows a willingness to be bound on stated terms; an invitation to treat only invites offers.
- Acceptance must match the offer's terms and be communicated to the offeror.
- A counter-offer ends the original offer instead of accepting it.
- These are generic principles of the common-law tradition, taught here only as a sample; no real jurisdiction's rules are being stated.

## Suggested flow
1. Open with the question above.
2. Explain the core ideas in plain words.
3. Give two low-stakes examples for pattern recognition only.
4. Check the learner can explain the idea back before moving to practice.
