## Test items
1. Dev advertises his used laptop in a shop window as 'Laptop, 300 pounds'. Mira walks in and says 'I accept.' Using IRAC, advise whether a contract exists. [6 marks]
2. Explain why a counter-offer is not an acceptance. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 issue: offer or invitation to treat; B1 rule: a display or advert is generally an invitation to treat; B2 applies it: Dev's notice invites offers, so Mira's 'I accept' is itself an offer; B1 Dev is free to accept or refuse; B1 conclusion: no contract yet
2. [2] B1 it changes the terms so it is a new offer; B1 it ends the original offer
