## Test items
1. Ola paints Ben's fence on Saturday. On Sunday Ben says 'I will pay you 60 pounds for that.' Using IRAC, advise whether Ola can enforce the promise. [6 marks]
2. Give one reason an agreement between two friends to share a lift is presumed not to create legal relations. [2 marks]

## Answer key (for the tutor only)
1. [6] B1 issue: is there consideration for Ben's promise; B1 rule: consideration must be given in exchange, past consideration is not good; B2 applies it: the painting was finished before the promise; B1 so Ben's promise is a gift; B1 conclusion: not enforceable
2. [2] B1 social or domestic agreements are presumed not to be intended as binding; B1 because people do not usually expect to sue each other over them
