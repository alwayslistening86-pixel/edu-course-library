# S2_Consideration_and_Intention - Lesson: Consideration and intention to create legal relations

## Goal
The learner explains consideration and intention and applies them to short everyday scenarios.

## Syllabus items taught here
- LC2.1 — explain consideration as something of value given by each side
- LC2.2 — explain the presumptions about social and commercial agreements

## How to teach this
Ask: you promise to drive a friend to the airport for nothing. Can they sue if you do not turn up? What if you promise to deliver 100 chairs for money?

Once the learner has had a go, introduce the core ideas plainly:
- Consideration is something of value that each side gives; a promise to give a pure gift is not enforceable.
- Past consideration, given before the promise, does not usually count.
- Social and family agreements are presumed not to be legally binding; commercial agreements are presumed to be.
- These are generic principles taught as a sample only.

## Suggested flow
1. Open with the question above.
2. Explain the core ideas in plain words.
3. Give two low-stakes examples for pattern recognition only.
4. Check the learner can explain the idea back before moving to practice.
