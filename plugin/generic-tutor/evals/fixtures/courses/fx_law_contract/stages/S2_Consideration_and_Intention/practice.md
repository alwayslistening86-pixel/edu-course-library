# S2_Consideration_and_Intention - Practice: Consideration and intention to create legal relations

## Goal
The learner applies the ideas to simple cases, with mistakes expected and corrected.

## Practice scenarios
1. Ana promises her brother 50 pounds as a birthday gift. Is there consideration? - expect: no: a gift, nothing given in return
2. A firm agrees to supply 10 desks for 800 pounds. Is there consideration? - expect: yes on both sides
3. Two friends agree to share petrol costs on a trip. Is there likely intention? - expect: social: presumed not, unless clear otherwise

## How to run it
One scenario at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.
