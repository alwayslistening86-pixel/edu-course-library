# Sample: Contract basics - Cumulative Exam

## Unlock condition
Only available once every stage in `stage_ladder` has a passed test.

## Format
A short paper of original problem and short-answer questions, marked against the key below. Generic common-law principles, taught as a sample only.

## 3 ready-made items (write the rest fresh, never reusing stage-test items)
1. A bookshop displays a novel marked 12 pounds. A customer takes it to the till and offers 12 pounds. Using IRAC, advise whether the shop must sell. [6 marks]
2. Explain why a promise to give a gift is generally not enforceable. [3 marks]
3. Leo says 'I will sell you my bike for 150.' Mia replies 'Would you take 120?' Leo says no. Mia then says 'Then I accept 150.' Using IRAC, advise whether a contract exists. [6 marks]

## Answer key for the ready-made items (tutor only)
1. [6] B1 issue: is the display an offer; B1 rule: a display is generally an invitation to treat; B2 applies it: the customer's offer at the till can be accepted or refused; B1 the shop need not sell; B1 clear conclusion
2. [3] B1 consideration is needed; B1 a gift gives nothing in exchange; B1 so there is nothing to enforce
3. [6] B1 issue: effect of a counter-offer; B1 rule: a counter-offer ends the original offer; B2 applies it: 'Would you take 120?' may only be a request for information, but Leo's refusal confirms the 150 offer stayed open, so Mia's later acceptance can still be valid, unless the question was a counter-offer; B1 the better view depends on whether the question was a counter-offer or an enquiry; B1 conclusion with that reasoning

## Grading
Mark each answer against its key. A pass is at least 50% of the marks.
