# S14_Skills_Legal_Writing - Practice: Course Skills: professional legal writing conventions

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Draft a short attendance note for a telephone call in which a client instructs you to proceed with sending a letter before claim, including a clear action point.
2. Draft the opening and summary sections of a client letter reporting the outcome of a straightforward piece of research, putting the headline answer first.
3. Rewrite this sentence in plain English: "It is not improbable that the counterparty's non-performance of its contractual obligations may, in certain circumstances, give rise to a right of termination."
4. Draft the same short piece of advice twice: once as it would appear in an attendance note for the file, and once as it would appear in a client letter, and compare the register used in each.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
