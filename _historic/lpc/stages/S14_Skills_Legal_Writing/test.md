# S14_Skills_Legal_Writing - Test: Course Skills: professional legal writing conventions

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: "You have just finished a call with a client in which they instructed you to accept a settlement offer of 15,000 pounds, subject to written confirmation of payment terms within seven days." Task: draft a properly structured attendance note recording the call, then draft a short client letter confirming the outcome of the call and the agreed next steps, applying plain-English conventions throughout and clearly matching the register in each document to its intended reader.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S14_Skills_Legal_Writing` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S15_Skills_Drafting.
