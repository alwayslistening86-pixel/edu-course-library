## Test scenario
Scenario: "Your client completed the purchase of a leasehold flat eight days ago. The lease requires landlord's consent to assign and a notice of assignment with a 50-pound fee within one month of completion; a further mortgage was taken out on completion; and your client has not yet paid SDLT." Task: list, in order, every step still outstanding, state the SDLT filing deadline that applies and by when it must be met, and explain what could go wrong for your client if the notice of assignment and the Land Registry application are both left too late.
