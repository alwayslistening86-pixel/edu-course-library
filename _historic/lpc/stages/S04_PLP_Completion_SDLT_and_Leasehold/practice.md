# S04_PLP_Completion_SDLT_and_Leasehold - Practice: Completion, Stamp Duty Land Tax, post-completion registration and leasehold extras

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Explain to a client, correctly, when they will actually own their new house at law, and why that differs from the date they hand over the money.
2. Calculate, in outline, the SDLT position for a first-time buyer completing today on a residential purchase, and separately for a buyer purchasing an additional buy-to-let property at the same price.
3. List, in the correct order, the post-completion steps a buyer's solicitor must take, and explain what an OS1 search protects against.
4. A client is buying a leasehold flat. List the leasehold-specific steps that a freehold purchase would not require.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
