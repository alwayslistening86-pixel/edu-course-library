# S12_Skills_Advocacy - Practice: Course Skills: oral advocacy on an unseen brief

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Prepare and deliver a thirty-second opening for an unseen bail application, stating who you appear for, the order sought, and a one-sentence case theory.
2. Take a short unseen fact pattern and build a two-to-four-point skeleton, strongest point first, each supported by a rule or fact.
3. Role-play a judge interrupting mid-submission with a direct question your prepared script does not answer, and practise stopping to answer it directly.
4. Practise the correct forms of address and how to refer to an opposing solicitor versus an opposing barrister in formal submissions.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
