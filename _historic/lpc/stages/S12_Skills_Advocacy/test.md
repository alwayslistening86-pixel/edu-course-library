# S12_Skills_Advocacy - Test: Course Skills: oral advocacy on an unseen brief

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Unseen brief: "You act for a defendant applying to adjourn a case management hearing because a key witness statement has not yet been obtained through no fault of the defendant." Deliver a short structured submission: state who you appear for and the order sought, give a one-sentence case theory, make two to three supporting points in strength order addressing the obvious counter-argument (prejudice to the claimant from delay), and respond appropriately if the judge interrupts to ask how long the adjournment should be.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S12_Skills_Advocacy` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S13_Skills_Practical_Legal_Research.
