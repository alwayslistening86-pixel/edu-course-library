## Test scenario
Scenario: "A company is making one of four similar roles redundant, has selected an employee who recently disclosed a disability and raised concerns about inaccessible facilities, using selection criteria applied only to that employee and without any consultation, and that employee now wants to bring a claim just over three months after being told of the decision, having only recently contacted ACAS." Task: assess whether the redundancy process itself is likely to be fair, identify any discrimination and/or reasonable adjustments issues raised by the facts, and advise on the tribunal time limit position given when ACAS was contacted, explaining what the employee needs to establish to bring a claim in time.
