# S19_EMP_Redundancy_Discrimination_and_Tribunal_Procedure - Practice: Elective, Employment Law: redundancy, discrimination, reasonable adjustments and tribunal procedure

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A company selects one employee for redundancy from a pool of three doing similar work, using criteria that were never explained or applied to the other two. Assess whether the process is likely to be fair.
2. A workplace policy requiring all staff to work full Saturday shifts disadvantages employees observing a particular religious day of rest. Identify the type of discrimination potentially in issue and explain the test that applies.
3. A disabled employee's substantial disadvantage from an inaccessible workplace has never been actively addressed by their employer. Explain the nature of the duty the employer may have breached.
4. An employee wants to bring an unfair dismissal claim four months after dismissal, having spent part of that time in ACAS Early Conciliation. Advise on the time limit position.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
