## Test scenario
Scenario: "A manufacturer supplies goods to a distributor under a contract that is silent on retention of title. The distributor's sales agent regularly agrees informal price variations with customers that head office has never formally authorised but has also never corrected, and a new market entrant has begun trading under a very similar unregistered brand name to the distributor's own long-established, well-known unregistered trading name." Task: advise on the effect of the missing retention of title clause, explain whether the sales agent's price variations are likely to bind the distributor and on what basis, and advise the distributor on a possible passing-off claim against the new entrant, applying all three elements of the test.
