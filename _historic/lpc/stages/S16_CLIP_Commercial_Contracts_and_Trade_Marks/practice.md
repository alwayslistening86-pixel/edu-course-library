# S16_CLIP_Commercial_Contracts_and_Trade_Marks - Practice: Elective, Commercial Law and IP: commercial contracts, and trade marks and passing off

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A supplier delivers goods under a contract with a retention of title clause, and the retailer becomes insolvent before paying. Advise the supplier on its position.
2. Distinguish, using a short example, an agent, a distributor and a franchisee acting for the same manufacturer.
3. A sales representative's actual authority to agree discounts is withdrawn, but the customer is not told and reasonably continues to believe the representative can still agree them. Advise on whether the principal is bound.
4. A small business starts trading under a name confusingly similar to an established, unregistered local competitor's trading name. Advise the competitor on a possible claim, applying the relevant test.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
