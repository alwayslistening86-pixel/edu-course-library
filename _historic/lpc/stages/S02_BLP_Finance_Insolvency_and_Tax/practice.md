# S02_BLP_Finance_Insolvency_and_Tax - Practice: Raising finance, corporate insolvency, and the taxation of businesses

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A growing company needs finance and is deciding between issuing new shares and taking a secured bank loan. Explain the practical consequences of each for existing shareholders and for the company's freedom to deal with its assets.
2. A lender takes a fixed charge over a company's freehold premises and a floating charge over its stock. Explain the difference in effect while the company is trading normally, and what "crystallisation" means.
3. On liquidation, a company has 200,000 pounds of floating charge realisations, a 30,000-pound prescribed part, and 250,000 pounds owed to its floating charge holder. Calculate what the floating charge holder actually recovers from that fund and explain why.
4. Compare, in outline, how a sole trader and a private limited company are each taxed on 100,000 pounds of annual trading profit, including what happens if the company then pays that profit out as a dividend.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
