# S02_BLP_Finance_Insolvency_and_Tax - Test: Raising finance, corporate insolvency, and the taxation of businesses

## How to run this

## Test scenario
Scenario: "A company has failed to pay a 21,000-pound debt to an unsecured trade creditor and is now in liquidation. It has a fixed charge over its warehouse (proceeds 80,000 pounds, secured debt 60,000 pounds), a floating charge (realisations 150,000 pounds, secured debt 200,000 pounds), liquidator's costs of 15,000 pounds, and a 25,000-pound prescribed part for unsecured creditors." Task: set out, in the correct statutory order, how the fixed charge proceeds and the floating charge realisations are distributed, state what (if anything) the unsecured trade creditor recovers, and briefly explain how the position would have differed had the company instead entered administration before this point.
