# S11_Skills_Interviewing_and_Advising - Test: Course Skills: interviewing and advising a client

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Unseen scenario: "A client attends about a dispute with a builder who left work unfinished after being paid a deposit. Run the full interview: open it, gather the facts including what has actually been paid and agreed, identify what the client actually wants (which may not be simply 'suing the builder'), give balanced plain-English advice with at least two realistic options, and close with agreed next steps." Assess for structure, client-handling and accuracy of technique rather than for legal correctness alone.

## Grading
Apply `rubric.json`'s `stage_rubrics.S11_Skills_Interviewing_and_Advising` criteria exactly. Record `passed` or `not_passed`, with a short honest note on strengths/weaknesses, into this course's micro-profile.

## If not passed
Name the specific weak or missing step, offer to revisit practice, then re-test with a new question of the same type.

## On pass
Record the pass and let the learner know they can move to S12_Skills_Advocacy.
