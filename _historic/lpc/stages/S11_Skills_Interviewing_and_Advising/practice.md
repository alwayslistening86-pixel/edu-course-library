# S11_Skills_Interviewing_and_Advising - Practice: Course Skills: interviewing and advising a client

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Script your own opening lines for an unseen client interview, covering greeting, role, structure of the meeting and client care basics, and rehearse them aloud.
2. Practise funnelling from an open question to closed follow-up questions on a scenario where a client says only "my neighbour has done something to our fence."
3. A client opens by saying they want to sue their former employer. Draft the question you would ask to test whether that is really their objective, and two alternative objectives it might turn out to be.
4. Draft a plain-English summary of a simple legal position (for example, a straightforward breach of contract) that a lay client with no legal background could follow, offering two realistic options.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
