## Test scenario
Scenario: "A worker has been engaged for three years under a contract describing them as an independent contractor, but they work fixed shifts set by the employer, cannot send a substitute, and have just been dismissed on the spot with no notice and no prior warning or investigation, after a single disputed incident." Task: assess their likely employment status and explain your reasoning, identify any wrongful dismissal claim and how damages would be assessed, and assess whether an unfair dismissal claim is realistically available, addressing both the potentially fair reason and the reasonableness/procedure requirement.
