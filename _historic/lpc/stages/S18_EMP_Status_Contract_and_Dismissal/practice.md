# S18_EMP_Status_Contract_and_Dismissal - Practice: Elective, Employment Law: employment status, contract terms, wrongful dismissal and unfair dismissal

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A business engages someone on a contract stating they are "self-employed," but requires them to work fixed hours personally, with no right to send a substitute. Assess their likely employment status and explain why.
2. Identify three possible sources of the term "one month's notice" in a given employment relationship, and how you would establish which actually applies.
3. An employee is dismissed with no notice at all, in clear breach of their contractual entitlement to three months' notice. Advise on their wrongful dismissal claim and how damages would likely be assessed.
4. An employer dismisses an employee for repeated lateness after two verbal warnings but no formal investigation or right of appeal. Assess the fair-reason and reasonableness/procedure elements of a likely unfair dismissal claim.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
