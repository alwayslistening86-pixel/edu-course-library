# S10_WAE_Wills_Intestacy_and_Estate_Administration - Practice: Wills and Administration of Estates: valid wills, intestacy, obtaining the grant, IHT and administration

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A will was signed by the testator alone, with the two witnesses signing it later that same day when the testator was not present. Assess whether it is validly executed and explain why.
2. A testator marries after making a will that makes no mention of the forthcoming marriage. Explain what happens to that will and why.
3. A client dies without a will, survived by a cohabiting partner of twenty years and two adult children, but no spouse. Explain who inherits and confirm whether the cohabitant has any automatic entitlement.
4. A deceased's estate is worth substantially more than the nil-rate band, and the whole estate passes to the surviving spouse. Explain the inheritance tax position and why.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
