# S10_WAE_Wills_Intestacy_and_Estate_Administration - Test: Wills and Administration of Estates: valid wills, intestacy, obtaining the grant, IHT and administration

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: "A client's mother has died. She left a will appointing the client as sole executor, made three years before her death and unaffected by any later marriage, but the family has found a note in her handwriting, unsigned and unwitnessed, apparently trying to leave a specific painting to a grandchild." Task: confirm whether the will itself is validly executed on the facts given, explain whether the unsigned, unwitnessed note has any legal effect on its own, advise on the correct type of grant to apply for and why, and outline the sequence the client, as personal representative, must now follow to administer the estate properly.
