## Test scenario
Scenario: "A solicitor is instructed to act for a company on a share sale. The solicitor personally holds a small shareholding in the buyer, learned during a previous matter for a different client that the target company has an undisclosed liability, and has not yet sent the new client any client care information." Task: identify every conduct issue in the facts, classify each conflict correctly, explain how the confidentiality/disclosure clash should be resolved, and state what client care steps are still outstanding and why they matter.
