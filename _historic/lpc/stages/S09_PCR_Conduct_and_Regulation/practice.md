# S09_PCR_Conduct_and_Regulation - Practice: Professional Conduct and Regulation: Principles, conflicts, confidentiality, client care, AML and accounts

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A solicitor is asked to act in a transaction where they personally hold shares in the buyer company. Classify the type of conflict and explain the correct next step.
2. A firm acts for both a landlord and a prospective tenant negotiating the same lease. Explain whether this is permissible and, if so, under what conditions.
3. A solicitor learns something confidential from Client A that would be directly relevant to advice they are giving Client B. Explain which duty prevails and what the firm must do.
4. Explain what client care information must be given to a new client at the outset, and what happens if the client later wants to complain.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
