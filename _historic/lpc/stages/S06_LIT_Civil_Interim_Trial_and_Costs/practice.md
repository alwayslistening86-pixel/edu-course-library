# S06_LIT_Civil_Interim_Trial_and_Costs - Practice: Civil litigation: disclosure, interim applications, Part 36 offers, trial, costs and enforcement

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A defendant has an internal memo that damages its own defence. Advise on the disclosure obligation and the professional conduct risk of withholding it.
2. A claimant applies for summary judgment against a defendant who has filed only a bare denial with no supporting facts. Apply the summary judgment test to this scenario.
3. A defendant makes a Part 36 offer of 20,000 pounds; the claimant rejects it and is awarded 17,000 pounds at trial. Explain the likely costs consequences.
4. A judgment debtor owns a car, has money in a bank account, and is in steady employment, but has ignored the judgment. Advise on two enforcement options and why you would choose them.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
