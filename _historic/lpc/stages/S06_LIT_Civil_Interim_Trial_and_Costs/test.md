## Test scenario
Scenario: "Your client obtained judgment for 30,000 pounds against a defendant who has not paid. Before trial, the defendant made a Part 36 offer of 25,000 pounds which your client rejected, and standard disclosure earlier in the case revealed an email that weakened your client's own case but was disclosed anyway." Task: explain the costs consequences of rejecting the Part 36 offer given the trial outcome, confirm the disclosure obligation was correctly followed and why it mattered to comply even though the email was unhelpful, and recommend at least two realistic enforcement methods against the non-paying defendant.
