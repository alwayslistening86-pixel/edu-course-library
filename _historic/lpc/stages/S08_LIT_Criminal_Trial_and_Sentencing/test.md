# S08_LIT_Criminal_Trial_and_Sentencing - Test: Criminal litigation: classification of offences, mode of trial, trial process and sentencing

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: "Your client is charged with an either-way offence, has no previous convictions, and is uncertain whether to accept the Magistrates' jurisdiction or elect Crown Court trial if it is offered." Task: explain the classification of the offence and the plea-before-venue/allocation procedure that will determine venue, explain the burden and standard of proof that will apply whichever court hears the case, and advise, in outline, on the sentencing considerations relevant if your client is ultimately convicted after a guilty plea versus after a contested trial.
