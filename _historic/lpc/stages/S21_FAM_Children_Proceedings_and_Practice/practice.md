# S21_FAM_Children_Proceedings_and_Practice - Practice: Elective, Family Law: private law children proceedings, the welfare principle, parental responsibility and practice points

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Two parents disagree about where their eight-year-old child should live. Explain why the child's own wishes are relevant but not necessarily decisive, applying the welfare checklist.
2. An unmarried father is not named on the birth certificate. Explain whether he has parental responsibility and, if not, how he could acquire it.
3. A client wants to resolve child arrangements without going to court. Explain what you would advise about mediation, and what would make it unsuitable.
4. A client discloses, during an initial interview about children arrangements, that they are frightened of their former partner. Explain how this should change your approach to the matter.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
