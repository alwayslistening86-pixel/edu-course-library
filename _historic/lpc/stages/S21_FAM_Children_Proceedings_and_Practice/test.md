## Test scenario
Scenario: "Two separated parents disagree about where their six-year-old should live during the school week; the father was never married to the mother and is not named on the birth certificate; the mother has raised, for the first time in your meeting, that she is afraid of the father's temper, though there is no formal finding of abuse; and both parents initially said they wanted to 'just go to court and get it sorted.'" Task: advise on whether the father currently has parental responsibility and how he could acquire it if not, apply the welfare checklist to identify which factors are likely to be most significant here, and advise on how the safeguarding disclosure should change your approach to process (including the suitability of mediation) and to the substantive welfare analysis.
