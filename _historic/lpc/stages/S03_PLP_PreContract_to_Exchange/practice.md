# S03_PLP_PreContract_to_Exchange - Practice: Conveyancing procedure from instructions to exchange of contracts

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. List the three registers making up a registered title and state what each one would tell you about a restrictive covenant affecting the property.
2. A client is buying a Victorian terraced house near a former industrial site. List the searches you would run and explain why each is relevant.
3. Draft, in outline, the key terms you would expect in the Special Conditions of a contract for the sale of a leasehold flat with 90 years remaining on the lease.
4. Explain to a lay client, in plain terms, what changes for them the moment contracts are exchanged.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
