# S03_PLP_PreContract_to_Exchange - Test: Conveyancing procedure from instructions to exchange of contracts

## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: "Your client is buying a semi-detached house with a right of way over next door's driveway benefiting the property, and a mortgage burdening the seller's title. The seller has not yet returned the completed TA6 form." Task: identify which register each of the two matters (the right of way and the mortgage) would appear in, list the searches and enquiries you still need before advising exchange, and explain what legal effect exchanging contracts now, rather than waiting for the TA6, would have on your client's position.
