# S05_LIT_Civil_PreAction_and_Starting_a_Claim - Practice: Civil litigation: pre-action conduct, starting a claim, limitation and track allocation

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A client has a straightforward breach of contract claim worth 8,000 pounds against a supplier who has not responded to a letter before claim. Explain what you must check and do before issuing proceedings.
2. Draft, in outline, the sequence and purpose of the statements of case for a contract claim where the defendant intends to dispute liability entirely.
3. A client's cause of action in contract accrued five years and eight months ago. Advise on limitation and any urgency this creates.
4. Explain, for a claim worth 60,000 pounds involving several factual and expert issues, which track it is likely to be allocated to and why that matters for costs.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
