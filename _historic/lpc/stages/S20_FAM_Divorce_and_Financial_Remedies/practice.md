# S20_FAM_Divorce_and_Financial_Remedies - Practice: Elective, Family Law: divorce/dissolution procedure and financial remedies on relationship breakdown

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A client's paperwork refers to "decree nisi" and specific fault-based grounds. Explain what this tells you about which procedural regime applies to their case.
2. Apply the statutory checklist factors to a short marriage with no children and broadly equal earning capacity, and explain which factors are likely to matter most.
3. Compare, for a 25-year marriage with substantial surplus assets, how the needs approach and the sharing approach might produce different starting points.
4. Explain to a client why the court might prefer a lump sum payment now over ongoing monthly maintenance payments, and when that preference might not apply.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
