## Test scenario
Scenario: "A couple are ending a 20-year marriage. One party gave up a well-established career to care for the children and run the home, the family has a house worth substantially more than either party's individual needs require, and the paperwork already filed refers to 'irretrievable breakdown' with no fault-based facts pleaded." Task: confirm which procedural regime applies to this divorce and explain the remaining procedural stages, apply the statutory checklist factors to identify which are most significant here, explain which of needs, sharing and compensation are realistically in play and why, and advise on whether a clean break is likely to be appropriate on these facts.
