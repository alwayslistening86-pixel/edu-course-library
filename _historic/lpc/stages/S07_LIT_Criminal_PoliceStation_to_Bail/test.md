## How to run this
Real checkpoint, not more practice. Present the scenario once, let the learner submit a full answer, then grade against this stage's entry in `rubric.json` -- never grade without it.

## Test scenario
Scenario: "Your client has been arrested on suspicion of burglary, is being interviewed under caution, and has an explanation for their presence near the scene that they are reluctant to give the police but plans to raise at trial." Task: advise on the right to legal advice and the detention clock currently running, advise clearly on the risk of staying silent about the explanation given the s.34 adverse inference rule, and, assuming charge follows, advise on the likely bail position and what, if anything, could displace the presumption of bail on these facts.
