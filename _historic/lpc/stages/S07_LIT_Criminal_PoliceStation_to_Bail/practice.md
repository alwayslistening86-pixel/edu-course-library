# S07_LIT_Criminal_PoliceStation_to_Bail - Practice: Criminal litigation: the police station stage, interview, charge and bail

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. A client is arrested and the custody officer asks if they want a solicitor. Explain what you would advise them to do and why, before any interview takes place.
2. A client has an alibi they choose not to mention at interview but wants to rely on at trial three months later. Explain the risk this creates and why.
3. Draft, in outline, the factors you would raise to a court to support a bail application for a defendant charged with an either-way offence with no previous convictions.
4. Explain two grounds on which the presumption of bail could be displaced for a defendant charged with a serious assault.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
