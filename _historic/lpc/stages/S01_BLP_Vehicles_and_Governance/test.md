## Test scenario
Foundation-style scenario: "Anjali and Priya run a small design studio as an unincorporated partnership and are worried about personal liability as the business grows. They also want to know who can decide to take on a new client contract, and who would need to agree to bring in a third partner as a director of a company if they incorporate." Answer: (a) recommend incorporation as a private limited company and explain why it changes their liability position; (b) explain that day-to-day contract decisions sit with the board by ordinary board resolution; (c) explain that bringing in a new director is usually a board decision (appointment) but explain what shareholder-level decision, if any, might also be relevant, and identify one CA 2006 director's duty each partner-turned-director must now observe.
