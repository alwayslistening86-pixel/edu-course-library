# S13_Skills_Practical_Legal_Research - Practice: Course Skills: practical legal research methodology

## Goal
Learner starts applying the method to short, low-stakes questions -- mistakes expected and corrected before any grading happens.

## Practice scenarios (use or adapt as needed)
1. Turn the instruction "can my client stop paying under this contract?" into a precise, answerable research question.
2. For a question about statutory redundancy pay calculation, identify the right starting source type and explain why.
3. Take the vague search term "unfair contract" and refine it into a more precise legal search term, explaining your reasoning.
4. Explain how you would check whether a five-year-old case you have found is still good law before relying on it in advice.

## How to run it
Present one question at a time. Let the learner attempt it fully, showing working or reasoning, before correcting. Offer a worked example only after a genuine attempt.

## When to move to test
Once the learner can handle a new, similar question correctly without step-by-step prompting.
